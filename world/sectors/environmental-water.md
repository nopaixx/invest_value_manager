## Resumen Ejecutivo

Water treatment and environmental services is a secular growth sector driven by water scarcity, tightening regulations, and industrial process optimization. The global water treatment chemicals market is valued at approximately $40B in 2026, growing at 4-6% CAGR. The sector is characterized by high recurring revenue (consumables model), strong switching costs (embedded equipment + proprietary chemistries), and regulatory moats. However, premium valuations reflect these qualities — finding entry points requires patience.

Key trends: (1) Industrial digitalization driving service-based models, (2) AI/IoT integration for water monitoring, (3) Tightening EPA/EU water discharge standards, (4) Growing semiconductor/data center water demand as a new growth vertical.

## Riesgos Sectoriales
1. **Oil price sensitivity**: Raw material costs (chemicals derived from oil/gas) — currently elevated
2. **Regulatory risk**: Standards could be loosened under deregulation push
3. **Customer concentration**: Food & beverage, hospitality cyclicality
4. **China slowdown**: Industrial water demand tied to manufacturing
5. **Premium valuations**: Quality names trade at 30-40x earnings consistently
