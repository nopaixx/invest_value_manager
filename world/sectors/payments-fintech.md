## Resumen Ejecutivo

El sector de pagos mantiene su posicion como uno de los negocios de mayor calidad en los mercados: margenes operativos 57-67% (los mas altos del S&P 500), ROICs sostenidos >40%, y un duopolio Visa-Mastercard que procesa ~90% de transacciones globales fuera de China. El TAM de pagos digitales sigue creciendo a CAGR ~11%, con value-added services (VAS) emergiendo como segundo motor de crecimiento (Visa VAS +28% YoY en Q1 2026).

Desarrollos clave desde ultima actualizacion (Feb 4):

1. **Visa Q1 FY2026 (Jan 30):** Revenue +15% YoY a $10.9B. VAS revenue +28% a $3.2B. Cross-border +11%. Payments volume +8% a ~$4T. Resultados solidos que validan la tesis de calidad.

2. **UK MIF Pass-On Ruling (Feb 18, 2026):** La CAT fallo que V/MA NO demostraron que los merchants pasaron el MIF overcharge a consumidores. Esto AUMENTA la exposicion a damages en Trial 3 (H2 2027). Impacto potencial de billones de GBP.

3. **PayPal CEO ouster (Feb 3):** Board forzo salida de Alex Chriss, reemplazado por Enrique Lores (ex HP CEO, board member desde 2020). Senal de insatisfaccion con ritmo de turnaround en branded checkout.

4. **Adyen H2 2025 (Feb 12):** Revenue +17% YoY pero guidance 2026 de 20-22% growth decepciono (consensus 22.8%). Stock -20% el dia. EBITDA margin ~53% estable.

5. **Iran crisis / risk-off:** V/MA han caido ~10-15% desde 52wH por selloff general. V ahora a P/E ~28x (vs 31x en Feb). MA a P/E ~30x (vs 33x). Compresion de multiplos NO por deterioro fundamental sino por macro.

6. **Visa interchange settlement:** Propuesta de $38B en ahorros para merchants pendiente de aprobacion judicial. Preliminary approval expected early-mid 2026, final approval late 2026 / early 2027. Reduciria interchange 10bp por 5 anos. Merchants (NRA, NACS, Walmart) objetan como insuficiente.

7. **DOJ vs Visa (debit antitrust):** Caso activo. Discovery dispute escalating (Mar 31 hearing over Treasury documents). Fact discovery NOT yet underway. Trial not expected until late 2027-2028. Riesgo estructural pero timeline largo.

8. **UK PSR Cross-Border Fee Cap Upheld (Jan 15, 2026):** High Court ruled PSR has power to cap cross-border interchange fees, rejecting V/MA/Revolut legal challenge. Current fees 1.15-1.5% (post-Brexit 5x increase) could return to pre-Brexit 0.2-0.3%. PSR now designing cap rate and timeline. GBP 150-200M annual cost to UK merchants at current rates. ADDITIONAL regulatory headwind on top of MIF Trial 3.

9. **EU Wero Payment System Scaling Rapidly:** Wero reached 52M users (up from 47M in Mar) across Belgium, France, Germany. Worldpay/Global Payments joined EPI as Principal Member (Mar 24). MoU with EuroPA Alliance connecting ~130M users across 13 countries. NFC POS payments scheduled 2026-2027. iDEAL (Netherlands) migration by 2027. Full A2A alternative to V/MA for EU domestic/intra-EU solidifying. Medium-term structural threat to V/MA EU domestic volumes, though cross-border and non-EU remain firmly V/MA territory.

10. **Tariff Impact on Cross-Border Payments:** Liberation Day tariffs (Apr 2, 2025) one-year anniversary confirms: 90-95% tariff pass-through to consumers, +$600/yr avg household, GDP -1% (~$300B output loss). Goldman cut 2026 discretionary cash inflow growth to 4.2% (from 5.1%). Lower-income discretionary growth collapsed to 0.8%. Peak consumer price pressure Apr-Oct 2026. Trade disruption impacting >$500B in flows (Canada, China, Mexico). V/MA net impact: inflation lifts nominal transaction values but discretionary volume growth at risk, especially lower-income cohorts. Cross-border B2B corridors (Visa B2B Connect, Mastercard Move) face competitive pressure from fintechs offering lower fees on disrupted trade routes.

11. **Oil WTI $110 (T1 verified):** Stagflation channel -- higher energy costs crowding out discretionary spend. V/MA asset-light and not directly exposed, but consumer spending is the transmission mechanism. Higher nominal values partially offset lower volumes. Net impact: slightly negative for volume growth, neutral-to-positive for revenue per transaction.

La narrativa se mantiene entre duopolio invencible vs amenazas fintech/regulatorias. La novedad es que las valuaciones se han comprimido FURTHER por tariff + oil headwinds. V a ~$303 esta ahora solo 6% por encima de nuestra entry zone ($270-285) -- the closest it has been since we started tracking. ADYEN has crashed to EUR 844 (-52% from 52wH), becoming increasingly interesting at P/E 25x.

## Subsectores

### 1. Payment Networks (V, MA)
- **Modelo:** Toll-booth model, cobran 0.1-0.3% por transaccion + VAS (creciendo a 28% YoY)
- **Moat:** Network effects, escala, brand, regulatory capture, data
- **Growth driver:** Cash-to-digital conversion, cross-border travel, VAS expansion (security, consulting, tokenization)
- **Riesgo:** Regulacion de interchange (US settlement, UK MIF damages, DOJ debit), RTP bypass para A2A, CCCA passage risk
- **UPDATE:** VAS now ~30% of Visa revenue and growing 2x faster than core payments. Strategic diversification away from interchange dependence. Tariff/oil headwinds pressuring consumer volume growth but inflation lifts nominal values.

### 2. Digital Payments / Wallets (PYPL, XYZ/Block)
- **Modelo:** Processing fees + float income + lending
- **Moat:** User base, brand (Venmo, Cash App), two-sided network
- **Growth driver:** P2P adoption, commerce enablement, crypto integration
- **Riesgo:** Competition from Apple Pay, Google Pay, bank wallets. CEO instability at PYPL.
- **UPDATE:** PayPal CEO Lores took over Mar 1. Outlining "merchant-focused" FY26 strategy with emphasis on "speed and precision." Stock $45, P/E 8.3x -- historically cheap but turnaround uncertain under 3rd CEO in 3 years. Lower-income consumer spending weakness a headwind for Venmo P2P volumes.

### 3. Payment Processing (Adyen, Stripe, Fiserv, FIS/GPN)
- **Modelo:** Per-transaction fees, value-added services
- **Moat:** Technical integration, enterprise relationships
- **Growth driver:** E-commerce, omnichannel, global expansion
- **Riesgo:** Commoditization, network orchestration
- **UPDATE:** Adyen guided 20-22% revenue growth for 2026 (below consensus 22.8%), stock -20% then continued declining to EUR 844 (-52% from 52wH). P/E now 25x. Next earnings Jun 3 (H1 2026). Q1 expected to show APAC headwinds + FX drag. GPN/Worldpay joined EPI (Wero) as Principal Member (Mar 24) -- positioning for EU A2A payments.

### 4. BNPL (Affirm, Klarna, Afterpay/Block)
- **Modelo:** Merchant fees (3-6%) + interest income
- **Moat:** Merchant integrations, consumer data
- **Growth driver:** TAM $560B in 2025 - $1T by 2031
- **Riesgo:** Credit losses (34-41% miss payments), regulation pending, funding costs
- **Nota:** Affirm at $52 P/E 64x -- speculative. Klarna IPO still pending. Commoditizing rapidly. State regulators stepping in as CFPB pulls back under new administration. Tariff-driven consumer squeeze likely INCREASES BNPL usage but also default risk.

### 5. Crypto/Stablecoin Payments
- **Modelo:** Low-fee cross-border rails
- **Moat:** First-mover (USDC, Tether), regulatory clarity post-GENIUS Act
- **Growth driver:** Circle USDC settled $4.8T in 2025. Stablecoin transfer volume $33T in 2025 (+72% YoY). Nearly half of institutions now using stablecoins. Blockchain settlements <3 min vs wire 3-5 business days.
- **Riesgo:** Regulation still evolving, volatility, trust, competition from CBDCs
- **UPDATE:** MA acquiring BVNK for up to $1.8B (largest stablecoin deal ever). Visa stablecoin settlement run rate $3.5B, expanded to 40+ countries. V/MA executives explicitly state little product-market fit for everyday consumer POS stablecoins -- smart co-option strategy intact. Stablecoins remain cross-border disruption play, NOT consumer checkout threat.

**UK MIF Ruling (Feb 18, 2026) -- MATERIAL DEVELOPMENT:**
The Competition Appeal Tribunal ruled that V/MA did not prove merchants passed on interchange fee overcharges to consumers (with only 3 narrow exceptions). This was Trial 2 of the umbrella proceedings. The finding means V/MA face HIGHER damages in Trial 3 (H2 2027), as the "pass-on defence" was their primary argument to reduce damages. Exposure could be in the billions of GBP. V/MA granted appeal (Mar 17) on the underlying liability ruling -- this is procedurally positive as it delays and could potentially overturn, but the pass-on finding itself stands for now.

**CFPB Rollback:**
The Trump administration effectively gutted the CFPB. The larger participant rule for digital payments supervision was REPEALED by Congress. The digital payments interpretive rule was RESCINDED. This is NET POSITIVE for V/MA/PYPL/Block -- less regulatory burden, lower compliance costs. However, state regulators are stepping into the gap.

## AI Impact on Payments

AI is reshaping the payments sector in three key ways:

1. **Fraud detection (NET POSITIVE for V/MA):** AI reducing false positives by 83% according to industry surveys. V/MA have the largest datasets globally for training fraud models -- a moat reinforcement. GenAI fraud (deepfakes, synthetic identity) creating $40B risk by 2027 but incumbents best positioned to combat it.

2. **Payment orchestration (NET RISK for V/MA):** AI enabling merchants to intelligently route transactions across cheapest available rails. This could pressure V/MA fees over 3-5 years by increasing competition between rails.

3. **Customer experience (NEUTRAL):** AI-powered personalization, chatbot-based checkout, predictive analytics. Benefits everyone in the ecosystem.

**Assessment:** AI is more TAILWIND than HEADWIND for V/MA. Their data advantage in fraud detection reinforces the moat. The orchestration risk is real but medium-term (3-5 years).

## Tesis de Inversion: V/MA como Quality Compounders

### Por que V/MA son Quality Compounders (QS 80-86)

1. **ROIC Spread Extraordinario:** 25-40% ROIC vs ~9% WACC = 15-30pp+ spread
2. **Moat Duradero:** Network effects + switching costs + regulatory licenses + data moat (AI-enhanced)
3. **FCF Conversion:** Near-100% FCF conversion, minimal capex needs
4. **Growth Runway:** Only 15-20% of global transactions are digital + VAS as second growth engine
5. **Capital Allocation:** Consistent buybacks + dividend growth + strategic acquisitions
6. **VAS diversification:** Visa VAS +28% YoY, now ~30% of revenue. Reduces dependence on interchange.
7. **Stagflation resilience:** Asset-light, no commodity exposure, inflation lifts nominal values. Among the most resilient businesses to stagflation.

### Pero la valuacion importa (y los riesgos son crecientes)

| Metrica | V Actual | MA Actual | V Entry Zone | MA Entry Zone |
|---------|----------|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| P/E | ~28x | ~30x | <25x | <28x |
| P/FCF | ~26x | ~29x | <25x | <30x |
| EV/EBIT | ~22x | ~25x | <20x | <25x |

**El problema (refined Apr 2026):** V/MA at P/E 28-30x still implies near-perfection. FOUR active risks:
1. UK MIF Trial 3 damages (H2 2027) -- pass-on defense FAILED, appeal granted but timeline extended.
2. DOJ debit antitrust (discovery escalating, trial 2027-2028) -- structural remedies possible.
3. CCCA -- if attached to must-pass legislation, passage probability could spike. Durbin Amendment precedent showed real margin impact on debit.
4. Tariff/oil consumer spending squeeze -- Apr-Oct 2026 peak pressure window.

These are NOT existential threats (V/MA will survive and thrive), but they could result in meaningful one-time charges and structural margin pressure in UK/US debit. The market is partially pricing this -- V/MA P/E compression from 31-33x to 28-30x reflects macro + regulatory discount.

**Estrategia (UPDATED Apr 2026):** V at $303 is now only 6% from entry zone. This is the closest V has been since we started tracking. If any of these catalysts trigger further weakness -- Q2 earnings volume miss, CCCA legislative progress, DOJ discovery adverse ruling -- V enters our zone this quarter. Monitor V WEEKLY.
