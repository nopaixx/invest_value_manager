## Estructura Competitiva

### Information Services
**Concentracion:** Oligopolio per subsector (each niche has 1-3 dominant players)
**Barreras de entrada:** VERY HIGH
- Proprietary databases built over decades (ISO data, credit histories, legal precedents)
- Regulatory mandates (FICO scores, insurance statistical agents)
- Embedded in customer workflows (switching costs)
- Network effects (analyst consensus, benchmark indices)
- AI reinforces moat: proprietary data is INPUT to AI models, not disrupted by them

### TIC
**Concentracion:** Top 3 (SGS, BVI, ITRK) hold ~23% global share
**Barreras de entrada:** HIGH
- Accreditations and lab infrastructure
- Reputation and trust in certification marks
- Regulatory requirements for third-party testing
- Global laboratory networks (thousands of locations)
- EU AI Act (Aug 2026) creates NEW testing/certification demand for AI systems

### Employee Benefits

- Regulatory licenses per country, network of merchants, technology platform
- BUT: interoperability mandates in Brazil (from Nov 2026) WILL lower switching costs
- Fee caps in Italy erode pricing power (EUR ~120M annual EBITDA impact)
- Turkey antitrust probe could signal further regulatory scrutiny in emerging markets
- **Competitive update (Apr 7):** Pluxee Q1 FY2026 showed +9% organic revenue, Employee Benefits +11.6% organic, LatAm +14.3%. Acquired Skipr (Belgium/France mobility) and ProEves (India).
