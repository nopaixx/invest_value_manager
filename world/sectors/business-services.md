## Resumen Ejecutivo

El sector Business Services es extremadamente heterogeneo, abarcando desde BPO/contact centers hasta information services, testing/inspection/certification (TIC), professional consulting, y specialty business services. El factor comun es que son servicios B2B o B2B2C que no producen bienes fisicos.

**Tesis principal ACTUALIZADA (2026-03-17)**: El sector esta TRIFURCADO:

1. **Information Services / Data Analytics** (WIDE moat, high ROIC, recurring) -- Verisk, RELX, Wolters Kluwer, Gartner, FactSet, S&P Global, Moody's. Quality compounders del sector. Capital-light, datos propietarios, switching costs. La mayoria sigue 30-65% por debajo de 52w highs. El SaaSpocalypse selloff se ha MODERADO pero no revertido — los mejores nombres (VRSK, WKL.AS) muestran resiliencia AI (WKL.AS nombrada 3x Leader en Gartner Magic Quadrant Financial Close Mar 2026). IT y FDS en nuevos minimos ($160, $207). OPORTUNIDAD SELECTIVA: el mercado sigue castigando indiscriminadamente pero los datos propietarios son INSUMOS para AI, no victimas.

2. **Testing/Inspection/Certification (TIC)** (medium moat, steady growth) -- Bureau Veritas, SGS, Intertek, Eurofins. Regulacion crea demanda estructural. BVI.PA (QS 69) corrigio desde EUR 29 a EUR 27.68, ahora -9% off 52wH. ITRK.L en 52wL (3650p), interesante por primera vez. EU AI Act (Aug 2026) y ESG reporting mandates crean demanda incremental estimada en $180B+ compliance services.

3. **BPO/Staffing/Value Traps** (low moat, AI disruption) -- Teleperformance, Concentrix, staffing agencies. Vendimos TEP.PA y FUTR.L. Sin cambios en tesis negativa.

**Posiciones actuales**: EDEN.PA (employee benefits, Tier B, 18% portfolio = LARGEST position). WKL.AS (info services, Tier B, 7.7% portfolio).

## Estructura Competitiva

### Information Services
**Concentracion:** Oligopolio per subsector (each niche has 1-3 dominant players)
**Barreras de entrada:** VERY HIGH
- Proprietary databases built over decades (ISO data, credit histories, legal precedents)
- Regulatory mandates (FICO scores, insurance statistical agents)
- Embedded in customer workflows (switching costs)
- Network effects (analyst consensus, benchmark indices)
- AI reinforces moat: proprietary data is INPUT to AI models, not disrupted by them

### TIC
**Concentracion:** Top 3 (SGS, BVI, ITRK) hold ~23% global share
**Barreras de entrada:** HIGH
- Accreditations and lab infrastructure
- Reputation and trust in certification marks
- Regulatory requirements for third-party testing
- Global laboratory networks (thousands of locations)
- EU AI Act (Aug 2026) creates NEW testing/certification demand for AI systems

### Employee Benefits
**Concentracion:** Oligopolio (Edenred ~40% France, Pluxee #2, WEX US)
**Barreras de entrada:** MEDIUM-HIGH (under pressure)
- Regulatory licenses per country, network of merchants, technology platform
- BUT: interoperability mandates in Brazil (from Nov 2026) WILL lower switching costs
- Fee caps in Italy erode pricing power (EUR ~120M annual EBITDA impact)
- Turkey antitrust probe could signal further regulatory scrutiny in emerging markets
- **Competitive update:** Pluxee (Sodexo spinoff) growing double-digit, half Edenred's revenue but concentrated in employee benefits. HelloBonnie and other digital-native challengers emerging in DACH region with Mastercard-based solutions. European gift card market $85.8B growing 7.8% — Edenred and Pluxee increasingly compete here via B2B incentives.
