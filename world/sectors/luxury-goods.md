## Resumen Ejecutivo

El sector de bienes de lujo globales (EUR ~370B mercado personal luxury goods, 2025; USD ~484B mercado global luxury goods, 2026) agrupa empresas con moats excepcionales basados en marca, escasez, pricing power y herencia multi-generacional. El sector ha sufrido un de-rating significativo en 2025-2026, con caidas del 20-40% en los principales nombres, impulsado por: (1) desaceleracion del gasto luxury en China (mayor mercado marginal, ~22% del gasto global, contraccion -3-5% en 2025 per Bain), (2) expectativas de crecimiento rebajadas post-CMD de varios players (Ferrari, Kering), (3) rotacion institucional fuera de "growth at any price" hacia value/defensivos.

Sin embargo, los fundamentales de los mejores operadores (Hermes, Ferrari, LVMH, Brunello Cucinelli) permanecen intactos: margenes expandiendo, pricing power demostrado (Ferrari paso 10% tariff hike sin impacto en demanda), y la poblacion UHNW crece ~5% anual globalmente (Knight Frank). La desconexion entre fundamentos y valoracion crea oportunidades selectivas para inversores pacientes. **MC.PA (LVMH) R3 complete Apr 7: FV EUR 430, entry EUR 355, QS 66 Tier B. DA identified structural headwinds: aspirational consumer base shrunk -70M, W&S in structural decline, ROIC declining from 15.7% to 11.7%. WATCHLIST -- compelling at EUR 355 but not yet there (current EUR 466.85).** El riesgo principal es que la desaceleracion de China sea estructural (no ciclica), lo que afectaria al crecimiento del sector durante anos.

**China outlook Q1 2026:** Bain expects mainland China personal luxury goods market to see modest growth in 2026 after -3-5% contraction in 2025 (itself recovering from -17 to -19% in 2024). Domestic spending accounted for 65% of Chinese luxury spending in 2025, reversing overseas demand rebound as weaker currency and narrowing global price gaps pushed purchases back to mainland. Second-hand luxury segment grew 15-20%. Recovery is "fragile and uneven" -- brand and category dependent. Growing middle class, improving consumer confidence, and Hainan duty-free policies are supportive. Asia-Pacific projected as fastest-growing region at 5.41% CAGR through 2031 (Mordor Intelligence).

El sub-segmento ultra-luxury (Ferrari, Hermes, Patek Philippe) es especialmente interesante: estos negocios operan con modelos de escasez deliberada (produccion < demanda) que les protege de ciclos economicos. Ferrari reporto FY2025 con FCF +50% y order book hasta fin 2027. Hermes mantiene listas de espera de 2+ anos para Birkin.

## Sentimiento de Mercado

**Sentimiento actual:** CAUTELOSO / CASTIGADO (de-rating -30% desde 2023 picos). LVMH -28.2% YTD (worse than 2008/COVID). China bottoming signals emerging but fragile. **Polarizacion K-shaped acelerando: UHNW 46-47% of spending (vs 30% in 2019). Aspirational buyer has virtually disappeared from the market.**

**Narrativa dominante:**
"China luxury spending has peaked. Growth is slowing to low-single-digits. These stocks were overvalued at 50-60x and the normalization has further to go. Tariffs add margin pressure."

**Mi contra-tesis:**
"Los mejores operadores (Hermes, Ferrari) estan protegidos por escasez deliberada y UHNW clientele que no es ciclica. La correccion de multiples es justa (de 50x a 35x), pero los fundamentos no se han deteriorado. A 30-35x para negocios con ROIC 20-40%, moats de 30+ anos, y pricing power demostrado, el sector es interesante selectivamente. China bottoming (Bain: modest growth 2026E but fragile) supports stabilization. **MC.PA R3 DA correctly identified structural headwinds (aspirational -70M, W&S decline, ROIC compression) -- FV EUR 430 at QS 66 Tier B reflects this realistically. At EUR 355 entry, E[CAGR] would be attractive as quality-at-discount, not quality compounder.**"

**Actualizacion Apr 7:** LVMH Q1 reports April 15 -- consensus is grim (F&LG -1 to -2% organic). This will set tone for entire sector. Hermes also reports Q1 revenue April 15. Kering CMD in April presents de Meo turnaround strategy. Tariff situation has improved (10% for 90 days, framework for 15% cap vs original 20%). Oil at $111 supports UHNW spending but compresses aspirational consumers further. The K-shape intensifies. Ferrari at EUR 286.75 is well below our sell price of EUR 301.60 -- we were right to sell on valuation grounds (E[CAGR] 6.6%) but the underlying business continues to execute.
