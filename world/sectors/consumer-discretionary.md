# Sector: Consumer Discretionary - Athleisure, Premium Retail & UK Homewares

> Ultima actualizacion: 2026-02-22
> Status: **NEUTRAL** - Selectivo. LULU ON PROBATION (governance crisis). Tariffs escalating COGS across sector. UK consumer showing unexpected resilience (Jan retail +4.5% YoY). US consumer confidence depressed but spending resilient.

## Resumen Ejecutivo

Consumer Discretionary / Athleisure / Premium Retail faces a complex crosscurrent environment in Feb 2026. Three dominant forces shape the sector:

**1. Tariff escalation is the #1 industry headwind.** US apparel tariffs have spiked from 13% to 54% on key sourcing hubs (China, Vietnam, India, Bangladesh, Indonesia = 63% of US textile imports). 76% of fashion executives say tariff response is the most important factor for 2026. DECK estimates $185M unmitigated COGS impact (up from $150M). LULU estimates $210M operating income reduction. Price increases of 1-3% are being passed through selectively, but margin compression is real for FY26-27.

**2. Governance crisis at LULU.** CEO Calvin McDonald departed Jan 31. Elliott Management holds ~$1B (5%) activist stake and is pushing Jane Nielsen (ex-Ralph Lauren CFO/COO) as CEO candidate. Founder Chip Wilson running simultaneous proxy fight with 3 director nominees (ex-On Running co-CEO Marc Maurer, ex-ESPN CMO Laura Gentile, ex-Activision CEO Eric Hirshberg). Interim co-CEOs (CFO Meghan Frank + CCO Andre Maestrini) running the company. Bonuses below target per Feb 11 internal meeting. Stock -45% from peak. Mar 31 Q4 earnings = MAKE-OR-BREAK.

**3. Consumer bifurcation: US weak sentiment vs resilient spending.** Michigan Consumer Sentiment at 56.6 (Feb 2026) -- near 12-year lows. BUT: actual consumer spending remains resilient, especially for affluent consumers. UK showing unexpected strength: Jan 2026 retail sales +4.5% YoY (biggest increase since Feb 2022), +1.8% MoM. UK discretionary spending +1.6% despite negative real wage growth. This divergence (weak sentiment, OK spending) favors premium brands with pricing power.

DECK (Deckers/HOKA) delivered strong Q3: revenue $1.96B (+7.1%), HOKA +18.5%, international +15%, EPS $3.33 beat. Raised HOKA guidance to mid-teens growth. Tariff headwind is real ($185M) but brand momentum offsets. DOM.L reports Mar 5 with new CFO starting Mar 16. Pipeline name DNLM.L at 976p (2.7% from 950p SO).

## Sub-sectores

### 1. Athleisure / Activewear (Foco Principal)
- **TAM:** $420-470B global, ~9% CAGR
- **Key Players:** Lululemon (LULU), Nike (NKE), Adidas (ADS.DE), On Holding (ONON), Puma (PUM.DE), Under Armour (UAA)
- **Dinamica:** LULU in governance crisis -- CEO out, Elliott activist, Wilson proxy fight. NKE turnaround slow. ONON growth premium intact. Tariffs compressing margins sector-wide.
- **Valuacion actual:** LULU P/E 13x (vs historico 40x). ONON P/E 50x. DECK P/E 16.9x.

### 2. Premium Footwear
- **Key Players:** Deckers (DECK - HOKA/UGG), Nike (NKE), Birkenstock (BIRK), On Holding (ONON), Crocs (CROX)
- **Dinamica:** DECK Q3 FY26 blowout: revenue $1.96B (+7.1%), HOKA +18.5%, UGG +4.9%. International +15%. Raised HOKA guidance to mid-teens (was low-teens). EPS $3.33 beat ($2.67-$2.88 expected). Stock +15% post-earnings to $118.69. BUT: $185M tariff COGS impact (75% sourced Vietnam). No full-year guidance due to "macroeconomic uncertainty."
- **Valuacion actual:** DECK P/E 16.9x (fair for growth profile). Near 52wH $149.55.

### 3. Premium Fashion / Apparel
- **Key Players:** Ralph Lauren (RL), Hugo Boss (BOSS.DE), PVH, Tapestry (TPR)
- **Dinamica:** RL executing well but at premium. BOSS.DE brand reset ongoing.
- **Valuacion actual:** RL P/E 24x (caro). BOSS.DE P/E 11x (interesante).

### 4. UK Homewares Retail
- **Key Players:** IKEA (#1), Dunelm (#2 specialist, 7.9% share), Next Home, B&Q
- **Dinamica:** DNLM.L H1 FY26: revenue GBP 926.3M (+3.6%), but PBT -7.5% to GBP 114M. Q2 slowed to +1.6% (vs Q1 +6.2%). FY26 PBT guided GBP 210-221M (lower end of consensus GBP 214-227M). Q3 showing improvement (Winter Sale + spring ranges). Special dividend 25p + interim 17p = returning cash. Currently 976p -- 2.7% from our 950p SO.
- **Valuacion actual:** P/E 13.4x (vs historical 16-18x). Shore Capital maintains BUY.

## Sentimiento de Mercado

**Sentimiento actual:** Bearish-to-Neutral. LULU governance crisis dominates headlines. Tariff fears overhang all US apparel. DECK is the bright spot (HOKA momentum). UK names under-the-radar.

**Narrativa dominante:**
"Tariffs will crush apparel margins. LULU is leaderless. The consumer is weakening. Only HOKA is working."

**Mi contra-tesis:**
- LULU governance crisis is TEMPORARY -- Elliott + Wilson both pushing for change. Interim co-CEOs are competent (CFO + CCO). GM 59% intact = moat intact. P/E 13x prices in permanent distress.
- Tariffs are REAL but partially mitigable (price increases, sourcing shifts, cost-sharing). Quality brands pass through to affluent consumers.
- UK consumer surprisingly resilient: Jan retail +4.5% YoY. DOM.L and DNLM.L benefit from domestic supply chains with minimal tariff exposure vs US peers.
- DECK executing exceptionally -- HOKA +18.5% with raised guidance. At $118.69 (P/E 16.9x), fairly valued but not cheap enough for our MoS requirements.
