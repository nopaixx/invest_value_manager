## Resumen Ejecutivo

**1. OIL SHOCK IS THE #1 HEADWIND (NEW, CRITICAL).** WTI surged from ~$73 to $92+ (+26% in one week) as Hormuz reached near-total halt (only 2 commercial transits in 24h). For UK consumer discretionary this creates a triple impact: (a) higher petrol at pump (currently 132.7p/litre, rising 10-15p within weeks), (b) supply chain/shipping cost inflation (+15-25% freight via non-Hormuz routes), (c) food input inflation re-acceleration (wheat, palm oil, dairy transport costs). The BoE's forecast of CPI falling to 2.1% in Q2 is now at SERIOUS RISK -- oil at $90+ could push UK CPI back toward 3.0-3.5%. DOM.L's supply chain (67% of revenue) and franchisee delivery costs are directly exposed. UK unemployment rose to 5.2% (post-pandemic high), youth unemployment surged to 16.4%. This is PRE-oil-shock data -- March readings will likely worsen as petrol prices rise. BofA sees only 1.2% UK household consumption growth in 2026, with 53% of respondents still cutting spending in anticipation of further price rises. The January retail +4.5% YoY surprise is now stale context -- oil shock reverses the trend.

**3. US CONSUMER K-SHAPED FRACTURE DEEPENING.** Michigan Consumer Sentiment still depressed. Oil at $90 acts as "stealth tax" on households. WING (Wingstop) reported first full-year SSS decline in 22 years (-3.3% FY2025), guiding flat-to-low-single-digit recovery for FY2026. US recession probability 40-55%. ## Sub-sectores

### 1. UK QSR / Pizza Delivery (PRIMARY FOCUS -- DOM.L)
- **TAM:** UK QSR market $37.6B, growing ~5.2% CAGR. Pizza delivery ~GBP 4.5-5.0B (mature, 1-2% growth)
- **Key Players:** Domino's Pizza Group (DOM.L, 54% UK pizza delivery share), Papa John's, Pizza Hut, independents
- **Dinamica:** Delivery channel leads QSR growth at 6.71% CAGR to 2031. Deliveries account for 18.3p of every GBP 1 spent at QSRs. DoorDash acquired Deliveroo for GBP 2.9B (Oct 2025) -- consolidation weakens aggregator-dependent competitors, slightly positive for DOM.L (90%+ own platform). Key trend: hyper-personalization, solo dining, AI/tech ordering systems.
- **Oil shock impact:** DIRECT and MATERIAL. DOM.L supply chain = 67% of revenue. Higher diesel/fuel flows through in 1-2 quarters. Estimated GBP 2-4M incremental annual cost if oil sustains $85-95. Franchisee delivery costs rise. UK consumer with higher petrol bills has less discretionary spend for delivered pizza.
- **NLW/NIC headwind:** GBP 12.71 NLW from Apr 2026 (+4.1%), employer NIC 13.8% to 15%. ~GBP 3M/yr direct DPG cost + franchisee margin squeeze.
- **Valuacion actual:** DOM.L P/E ~10x (vs historical 14-18x). Cheap but risks elevated.

### 2. Promotional Products (FOUR.L context)
- **TAM:** US promotional products ~$26B (PPAI). Industry described as "resilient but not expansionary" in 2025.
- **Key Player:** 4imprint (FOUR.L) -- #1 direct marketer of promotional products in US/Canada/UK
- **Dinamica:** FY2025 unaudited revenue $1.35B (vs $1.37B in FY24, -1.5%), PBT not less than $149M (vs $154M FY24). Gross margin ~32% maintained. Total order count -3%, avg order value +1%, new customer orders -12%, existing customer flat (strong retention). Described as "resilient but not expansionary" -- corporate spending tightened but not collapsed.
- **Oil shock impact:** INDIRECT. Corporate marketing budgets are early-cycle casualties in downturns. Oil-driven recession risk = corporate spending cuts = FOUR.L revenue risk. However, promotional products are relatively small-ticket B2B items with sticky demand from event-driven purchasing.
- **Valuacion actual:** To be assessed at R1. Results Mar 18 = HARD GATE.
