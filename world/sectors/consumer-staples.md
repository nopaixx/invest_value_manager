## Resumen Ejecutivo

Consumer Staples es el sector defensivo por excelencia: alimentos, bebidas, tabaco, productos del hogar y cuidado personal. Son productos que los consumidores compran independientemente del ciclo economico. El sector ha underperformed significativamente en 2024-2025 (gap de 70 puntos porcentuales vs tech en 3 anos) debido a: (1) rotacion hacia growth/AI, (2) tipos altos que penalizan yield stocks, (3) GLP-1 drugs impactando percepcion de food/beverage, y (4) amenaza de private label.

**DEFENSIVE ROTATION STALLING (Apr 2026).** Consumer staples rallied +15.5% YTD through mid-March but has pulled back sharply. XLP at $81.26, down -9.8% from 52wH ($90.14). The initial defensive rotation into staples is being OFFSET by (a) oil at $108 WTI creating severe input cost pressure on margins, (b) oral GLP-1 approval accelerating the structural headwind, and (c) tariff pass-through now hitting food prices, squeezing consumer budgets. The sector is caught between defensive flow DEMAND and fundamental margin PRESSURE.

**GLP-1 Impact (CRITICAL -- INFLECTION POINT Apr 2026):**
- **FOUNDAYO (orforglipron) FDA APPROVED Apr 1, 2026.** First oral GLP-1 pill. No food/water restrictions. Once daily. This REMOVES the injection barrier that limited adoption.
- **Pricing:** $149/month self-pay (lowest dose), $25/month with commercial insurance (savings card), $50/month Medicare Part D from Jul 1, 2026. MASSIVELY more accessible than injectable GLP-1s ($500-1000/month).
- **Clinical:** -7.5% to -11.2% weight loss at 72 weeks (vs -2.1% placebo), plus improved BP and lipids.
- **Availability:** Prescriptions accepted immediately (Apr 1), shipping from LillyDirect Apr 6, broad retail pharmacy availability following shortly.
- **WHY THIS IS THE INFLECTION POINT:** Injectable GLP-1s required doctor visits, needle comfort, cold chain. Oral pill = as easy as taking a vitamin. $149/month = affordable for middle class. Medicare coverage = accessible for 65+. Adoption curve will STEEPEN dramatically.
- **20% de hogares US** ahora incluyen un usuario GLP-1 (measured), proyectado ~30M Americans en GLP-1 por 2030 (JPMorgan). With oral option, this could EXCEED 30M.
- GLP-1 users reduce calorie intake ~30-40%. Snack consumption -40-60%. Dessert consumption -84%. Alcohol consumption -33%.
- **$12B in potential snack sales losses** over next decade (analyst estimates) -- likely CONSERVATIVE post oral approval.
- **Winners:** Protein-rich (+65% consumption in GLP-1 users), fiber-rich, functional foods, fruits/vegetables (+80%), health/specialty foods (+50%), energy drinks (functional positioning)
- **Losers:** Ultra-processed, high-calorie snacks (Mondelez, Kellogg's), sugary drinks, alcohol (NOW HIGH impact), fast food/pizza delivery
- **Spirits: UPGRADED TO HIGH IMPACT.** Oral GLP-1 + 33% alcohol reduction in users + 30M+ potential users by 2030 = STRUCTURAL demand destruction for spirits. This is NOT temporary. Pernod Ricard/Diageo "destocking recovery" narrative must contend with THIS.
- **Industry response:** PepsiCo, General Mills, Conagra, Danone investing in reformulations, protein-rich, smaller portions. CELH (energy drinks) positioned as BENEFICIARY (functional, low-calorie positioning).
