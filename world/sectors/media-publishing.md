## Resumen Ejecutivo

El sector de medios digitales y publicaciones esta en una **bifurcacion estructural acelerada**: los publishers dependientes de trafico organico de Google estan en caida libre (trafico de busqueda Google a publishers cayo -33% global en 2025, -38% en US), mientras que las empresas con modelos de subscripcion premium o data/analytics B2B estan prosperando. La disrupcion por AI se ha CONFIRMADO como permanente -- zero-click searches ahora representan el 69% de todas las queries, y los publishers esperan una caida adicional del 43% en trafico en los proximos 3 anos.

**April 8 update:** (1) **Ad spend forecasts DOWNGRADED**: WARC cut global 2026 forecast by $20B; MAGNA expects $979B (was $1T+). Tariffs (April 2 "Liberation Day" reciprocal tariffs) + recession risk cited. 9/10 ad buyers "concerned about tariff impact on spend." Cyclical events (World Cup, Olympics, midterms) provide $9B cushion but may not offset macro drag. DOUBLE HEADWIND confirmed: structural AI + cyclical tariff/recession. (2) **Future plc (FUTR.L) COLLAPSED further**: Now 275p (was 402p at last update, -32% in 21 days). Multiple analyst downgrades: JPMorgan PT cut to 750p from 1,235p; Barclays downgraded to Equal Weight; Berenberg downgraded to Hold. P/E 4.4x, yield 6.0%, market cap GBP 0.3B. Near 52wL of 262p. This is a dying business -- validates our Feb exit at 522.5p (+90% above current price). (3) **AI copyright pivot point approaching**: Anthropic $1.5B settlement (Bartz case) final approval hearing April 23. BMG vs Anthropic filed Mar 18 (music). Encyclopedia Britannica + Merriam-Webster sued OpenAI Mar 20. Supreme Court denied Thaler v. Perlmutter certiorari Mar 2 (AI-generated art not copyrightable). 2026 remains pivotal year but no definitive fair use ruling yet. (4) **Publisher AI licensing deals expanding beyond OpenAI**: News Corp signed multi-year deal with Meta ($50M/yr, Mar 4). Reach signed Amazon Nova deal (Mar 2). Licensing revenue emerging as new but MODEST income stream for major publishers. (5) **Paramount-Skydance/WBD mega-merger advancing**: WBD shareholder vote set April 23. Gulf sovereign wealth funds committed ~$24B equity (PIF $10B, QIA, L'imad). Deal expected Q3 2026 close. Creates unprecedented content consolidation. (6) **NYT at $85.86 (near 52wH $87.10)**: Q1 earnings May 13. Guided 14-17% digital sub revenue growth Q1 with bundle price hike $25->$30. Premium model CONTINUES to work. P/E 41x -- expensive, no crisis entry. (7) **RELX at $33.36 (near 52wL $27.57)**: Sold off from $56 highs with broader market. P/E 22.5x, yield 2.7%. Getting more interesting as tariff selloff compresses multiples on a business with ZERO tariff exposure.
