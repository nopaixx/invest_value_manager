## Dinamica Sectorial

### Fortalezas Estructurales
1. **Network effects**: More listings = more buyers = more listings
2. **Winner-take-all**: 1-2 viable platforms per vertical per country
3. **Pricing power**: No viable alternative for dealers/agents (though resistance growing at AUTO.L, and RMV.L class action challenges this premise legally — NOW FILED)
4. **Capex minimo**: Software platform, no physical assets
5. **Recurring revenue**: 85-95% subscription
6. **Inventory data moat (classifieds)**: AUTO.L/RMV.L own the listing data; AI cannot replicate without the inventory feed

### Riesgos Sectoriales

**1. AI / ChatGPT Disruption -- ELEVATED AND ACCELERATING (Apr 8 update)**

AI comparison is no longer theoretical — it is generating real revenue for big tech:

- **Google Gemini shopping ads LIVE**: Monetizing at same rate as traditional search ads. Direct Offers inside AI Mode.
- **OpenAI ChatGPT ads pilot**: $100M annualized revenue. $60 CPM. 800M+ weekly users. Product discovery features expanding.
- **Meta AI shopping chatbot**: Testing shopping research feature to rival ChatGPT and Gemini.
- **Walmart + Gap testing AI commerce**: Both testing in ChatGPT AND Gemini. Split on checkout integration.
- **Perplexity abandoned ads (Feb 2026)**: Pivoted to subscription ($500M target). The ad-free alternative.
- **BCG Consumer AI Disruption Index (Jan 2026)**: Auto marketplaces in "Breached" category. 33% of consumers discover brands via AI agents.
- **MONY.L ChatGPT app**: Car insurance, broadband, van, current accounts, savings LIVE. Home insurance pending. (MONY.L now sold from portfolio.)
- **RMV.L: 31 AI projects**: Accepting 2026 margin compression. Targeting 12% annual OP growth by 2030.

**Differential risk by vertical (UPDATED):**
- **Price comparison (MONY.L): HIGH risk, ACTIVELY DEFENDED.** Rate aggregation replicable. ChatGPT/Gemini shopping ads now monetizing — this is the most directly threatened vertical. Insurance/mortgages need deterministic auditable systems (regulatory moat). Less defensible for broadband/energy.
- **Car classifieds (AUTO.L): MEDIUM risk.** Inventory data moat. Dealers must LIST somewhere. AI improves discovery, cannot create listings. FB Marketplace + Marketplace Pro as low-cost alternative for independent dealers.
- **Property classifieds (RMV.L): MEDIUM-HIGH risk (upgraded from MEDIUM).** CoStar/OnTheMarket growing. GBP 1.5B class action FILED — could constrain pricing power AND distract management. If class action succeeds, pricing power thesis fundamentally damaged.
- **Adviser platform (IHP.L): LOW risk.** B2B infrastructure. AI augments, not displaces.

TAILWIND for MONY.L (though now sold from portfolio) and any price comparison platform.
