## Dinamica Sectorial

### Fortalezas Estructurales
1. **Network effects**: More listings = more buyers = more listings
2. **Winner-take-all**: 1-2 viable platforms per vertical per country
3. **Pricing power**: No viable alternative for dealers/agents (though resistance growing at AUTO.L)
4. **Capex minimo**: Software platform, no physical assets
5. **Recurring revenue**: 85-95% subscription
6. **Inventory data moat (classifieds)**: AUTO.L/RMV.L own the listing data; AI cannot replicate without the inventory feed

### Riesgos Sectoriales

**1. AI / ChatGPT Disruption -- ELEVATED, SECTOR IN ACTIVE DEFENSE MODE**

Live products and industry research confirm AI as a structural force. All major players now investing heavily:

- **BCG Consumer AI Disruption Index (Jan 2026)**: Auto marketplaces in "Breached" category. 33% of consumers discover brands via AI agents.
- **HBR Jan 2026**: Gen AI threatening platforms that dominate online travel (Expedia, Booking). Canary for all aggregators. As conversational AI answers users directly, traditional platforms risk losing gateway role.
- **ChatGPT shopping-intent**: 50.2M monthly visits in UK with shopping intent, ranking alongside biggest e-commerce sites.
- **Insurify ChatGPT app (Feb 9)**: 196M+ quote database. Insurance comparison inside ChatGPT. US-only for now.
- **MONY.L ChatGPT app (Feb 20)**: Car insurance + broadband + van insurance live, home insurance pending. First UK financial services ChatGPT app. Nearly all main products expected by end Q1 2026.
- **RMV.L: 31 live AI projects (Feb 27)**: Conversational search (3x more leads), Online Agent Valuation, Google Cloud collaboration. Guiding 3-5% OP growth in 2026 (down from 9%) due to AI investment. Revenue rebound expected post-2028; targeting 12% annual OP growth by 2030.
- **Google AI Overviews**: Ambient risk extracting comparison data directly in search results.

**Differential risk by vertical:**
- **Price comparison (MONY.L): HIGH risk but ACTIVELY DEFENDED.** Rate aggregation is algorithmically replicable. MONY.L's defense (own ChatGPT app, Price Optimiser, SuperSaveClub loyalty) is proactive. Insurance/mortgages require deterministic, auditable systems -- harder for generic AI to replicate. Less defensible for broadband/energy.
- **Car classifieds (AUTO.L): MEDIUM risk.** Inventory data moat remains. Dealers must LIST somewhere. AI improves discovery but cannot create listings. Co-Driver AI product launched for dealer listings.
- **Property classifieds (RMV.L): MEDIUM risk.** Agents must list. RMV.L investing most aggressively in AI (31 projects, accepting margin compression in 2026).
- **Adviser platform (IHP.L): LOW risk.** Transact is B2B infrastructure for financial advisers. Not a consumer-facing discovery platform. AI more likely to augment than displace.

**2. Dealer Revolt / Pricing Pushback -- APRIL 1 RESOLUTION IMMINENT**

AUTO.L's Deal Builder backlash largely addressed. Concessions made. 59 cancellations + 180+ downgrades quantified (~1.8% churn). IMDA survey Feb/Mar 2026: 165 dealers cancelled/gave notice (AUTO.L says <100). 70% of surveyed dealers reduced/downgraded packages. April 1, 2026 pricing +5.5% (down from 8% prior year).

**April 1 pricing rollout = DEFINITIVE test.** CEO Coe flagged "unusual drop-off in customers." If second wave of cancellations: thesis damaged. If smooth absorption: narrative clears. Supply shortage of 5-7yr old cars (-25-30% in 2026) potentially eases friction: dealers earn higher margins per car in tight supply, making subscription more justifiable.

**Analyst consensus remains BEARISH**: Goldman Sachs SELL (PT 777p). Jefferies and Peel Hunt Hold. Berenberg previously downgraded. Overall: 7 Buy, 7 Hold, 2 Sell. Consensus PT ~698p (down from ~860p). Avg analyst PT ~738.75p. Bears see pricing power erosion as structural; bulls see temporary noise.

**3. UK Used Car Supply Dynamics -- STRUCTURAL FACTOR**

COVID-era production losses filtering through: -25-30% drop in 5-6yr old vehicle supply in 2026, worsening to -35% by 2027. Bifurcated market:
- **Positive for AUTO.L**: Scarcity = higher per-unit value, stronger buyer engagement, price resilience. Dealers need AUTO.L more when inventory is tight.
- **Negative for independents**: Small dealers face acute stock shortages.

**4. UK Insurance Premium Cycle -- CONFIRMED TURNING POSITIVE (updated Mar 7)**

ABI Motor Insurance Premium Tracker for H1 2026 CONFIRMS double-digit YoY premium increases after -9%/-10% decline in 2025. Key data points:
- Average car insurance premium: GBP 559 (Q4 2025 base), now rising
- EV premiums 25% higher than petrol equivalents (GBP 707 vs GBP 558)
- Repair costs = 64% of claims payouts (GBP 7.5B, +12% YoY)
- Vehicle theft average claim: GBP 11,800
- Industry expects ~5% premium increase across 2026 (+~GBP 25 per policy)
- **TAILWIND for MONY.L CONFIRMED**: Higher premiums = higher revenue per switch. Reverses the 2025 headwind mechanically. Also: consumers shop harder for insurance deals when premiums rise = more comparison activity.

**5. Regulation**: DMCCA (Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act) coming into force 2025-2026 with new CMA powers (up to 10% global turnover fines). BUT focus is on big tech (Google, Apple designated SMS). No CMA investigation of classifieds/comparison platforms on radar. Subscription transparency rules (autumn 2026) could affect SuperSaveClub terms marginally. Not material.

### Iran Crisis Impact (2026-03-02, still applicable)

**Impacto DIRECTO:** LOW. Asset-light digital platforms, subscription-based revenue, no oil/commodity exposure.

**Impacto INDIRECTO:**
- **AUTO.L:** SLIGHTLY NEGATIVE. Oil spike could suppress UK car buying if sustained. But historical correlation between oil shocks and UK used car transactions is weak. April pricing event remains factor #1 by far.
- **MONY.L:** NEUTRAL to SLIGHTLY POSITIVE. Insurance premiums confirmed re-inflating faster. Also: consumers shopping harder for insurance deals in cost-of-living squeeze = more comparison activity.
- **RMV.L:** NEUTRAL. Property market uncorrelated with oil at short timeframes.
- **IHP.L:** NEUTRAL. Adviser platform AUM driven by asset prices, not oil. Market volatility from Iran could be mildly negative (lower AUM from market declines) but historically Transact benefits from volatility (more advisory activity, more flows).

No thesis change justified by Iran crisis for this sector.
