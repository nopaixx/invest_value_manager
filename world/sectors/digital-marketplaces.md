## Dinamica Sectorial

### Fortalezas Estructurales
1. **Network effects**: More listings = more buyers = more listings
2. **Winner-take-all**: 1-2 viable platforms per vertical per country
3. **Pricing power**: No viable alternative for dealers/agents (though resistance growing)
4. **Capex minimo**: Software platform, no physical assets
5. **Recurring revenue**: 85-95% subscription
6. **Inventory data moat (classifieds)**: AUTO.L/RMV.L own the listing data; AI cannot replicate without the inventory feed

### Riesgos Sectoriales

**1. AI / ChatGPT Disruption -- ELEVATED, SECTOR IN ACTIVE DEFENSE MODE**

Live products and industry research confirm AI as a structural force:

- **BCG Consumer AI Disruption Index (Jan 2026)**: Auto marketplaces in "Breached" category. 33% of consumers discover brands via AI agents. Auto + travel + news = most exposed.
- **Insurify ChatGPT app (Feb 9)**: 196M+ quote database. Insurance comparison inside ChatGPT. US-only for now.
- **MONY.L ChatGPT app (Feb 20)**: Car insurance + broadband + van insurance live, home insurance pending. First UK financial services ChatGPT app. Enterprise OpenAI agreement signed.
- **Rightmove Gemini beta (Feb 19)**: Conversational property search leveraging own inventory data.
- **Google AI Overviews**: Ambient risk extracting comparison data directly in search results.

**Differential risk by vertical:**
- **Price comparison (MONY.L): HIGH risk but ACTIVELY DEFENDED.** Rate aggregation is algorithmically replicable. MONY.L's defense strategy (own ChatGPT app, Price Optimiser, SuperSaveClub loyalty) is proactive. Management framing: "facilitator, not replicator" -- regulated financial products need deterministic, auditable systems. Valid argument for insurance/mortgages. Less valid for broadband/energy where commoditized.
- **Car classifieds (AUTO.L): MEDIUM risk.** Inventory data moat remains. Dealers must LIST somewhere. AI improves discovery but cannot create listings. 25% of car buyers already use AI in process.
- **Property classifieds (RMV.L): MEDIUM risk.** Agents must list. Rightmove integrating AI natively on own platform.

**2. Dealer Revolt / Pricing Pushback -- MODERATING, APRIL TEST AHEAD**

AUTO.L's Deal Builder backlash largely addressed. Concessions made (Reservation Request, Message Seller restored, Customer Advisory Groups). 59 cancellations + 180+ downgrades quantified (~1.8% churn). ARPR reduced to 5.5% for 2026 (from 8%).

**April 2026 pricing rollout = key test.** If second wave of cancellations: AUTO.L thesis damaged. If smooth absorption: narrative clears and re-rate potential. UBS flagged consensus revenue at risk if pricing power erodes further. Jefferies maintains Hold (PT 650p). Supply shortage of 5-7yr old cars (-25-30% in 2026) is a potential TAILWIND: dealers earn higher margins per car in tight supply, making AUTO.L subscription more justifiable.

**3. UK Used Car Supply Dynamics -- NEW STRUCTURAL FACTOR**

COVID-era production losses now filtering through: 2.5M lost new car sales creating -25-30% drop in 5-6yr old vehicle supply in 2026, worsening to -35% by 2027. This creates a bifurcated market:
- **Positive for AUTO.L**: Scarcity = higher per-unit value for dealers, stronger buyer engagement (more searches per car), price resilience. Dealers need AUTO.L more when inventory is tight.
- **Negative for independents**: Small dealers selling older vehicles face acute stock shortages.

**4. Regulacion antimonopolio**: AUTO.L's 75% market share could attract CMA attention. No active investigation found. CMA focused on digital markets (Google, Apple) and new consumer protection regime. Not a near-term risk.
