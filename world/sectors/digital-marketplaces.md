## Resumen Ejecutivo

Plataformas digitales de classifieds y price comparison con modelos marketplace (two-sided networks). Negocios con moats excepcionales (network effects, switching costs, inventory data), margenes FCF >40%, y crecimiento organico 2-10%. Valuaciones siguen comprimidas (P/E 11-22x vs 10yr avg 20-25x) por miedos a AI disruption.

Key developments since v2.6:

1. **Rightmove GBP 1B class action ADVANCING**: 170+ estate agencies expressed interest. Innsworth Capital funding. Competition Appeal Tribunal filing expected in "due course." Alleges dominance abuse via excessive fees. RMV.L 70% margins cited as evidence. If certified, discovery phase could reveal pricing mechanics across sector. Relevant read-across for AUTO.L (similar pricing power dynamics).

2. **Facebook Marketplace emerging as dealer channel**: Marketplace Pro (UK SaaS) launched AI-powered listing software for car dealers. Imports inventory from AutoTrader, Cars.com, CarGurus. FB Marketplace has 38M UK users (~70% of UK Facebook users). Still free for dealers. Not yet a serious threat to AUTO.L's professional dealer tools (no financing, no provenance data, no consumer protection) but reduces friction for small/independent dealers exploring alternatives during price revolt.

3. **AUTO.L reaches new 12-month low**: Touched 454.5p (52wL) in early Mar. Buyback continues at 469p avg (Mar 4 repurchase of 535K shares). April 1 pricing increase remains the single most important catalyst. 25 days away.

4. **CMA Strategy 2026-2029 published + DMCCA consumer protection LIVE since Apr 2025**: Focus remains on big tech (Google, Apple designated SMS). UK government consultation on CMA overhaul launched Jan 2026 (responses by Mar 31). No direct investigation of classifieds/comparison platforms, but Rightmove class action at CAT could establish precedent on pricing power = dominance abuse.

5. **Scout24 Fotocasa closed (Mar 2)**: Integration underway. 2026 guide: Rev +16-18% (6-7pp inorganic from Fotocasa). Margin pressure expected from integration.

## Dinamica Sectorial

### Fortalezas Estructurales
1. **Network effects**: More listings = more buyers = more listings
2. **Winner-take-all**: 1-2 viable platforms per vertical per country
3. **Pricing power**: No viable alternative for dealers/agents (though resistance growing at AUTO.L, and RMV.L class action challenges this premise legally)
4. **Capex minimo**: Software platform, no physical assets
5. **Recurring revenue**: 85-95% subscription
6. **Inventory data moat (classifieds)**: AUTO.L/RMV.L own the listing data; AI cannot replicate without the inventory feed

### Riesgos Sectoriales

**1. AI / ChatGPT Disruption -- ELEVATED, SECTOR IN ACTIVE DEFENSE MODE**

Live products and industry research confirm AI as a structural force. All major players investing:

- **BCG Consumer AI Disruption Index (Jan 2026)**: Auto marketplaces in "Breached" category. 33% of consumers discover brands via AI agents.
- **HBR Jan 2026**: Gen AI threatening aggregator platforms. Canary for all comparison/classified platforms.
- **ChatGPT shopping-intent**: 50.2M monthly visits in UK with shopping intent.
- **Google Gemini ads**: Google confirmed ads within Gemini AI chatbot for 2026. Could redirect discovery away from comparison platforms.
- **30% of UK adults open to AI acting as personal shopping agent** (purchasing, delivery, returns).
- **Experian ChatGPT app (Feb 12)**: 37+ carriers. US-only. Confirms AI comparison proliferating.
- **MONY.L ChatGPT app**: Car insurance, broadband, van, current accounts, savings LIVE. Home insurance pending. First UK financial services ChatGPT app.
- **RMV.L: 31 AI projects**: Accepting 2026 margin compression. Targeting 12% annual OP growth by 2030.

**Differential risk by vertical:**
- **Price comparison (MONY.L): HIGH risk, ACTIVELY DEFENDED.** Rate aggregation replicable. MONY.L defense: ChatGPT app (5+ categories), SuperSaveClub (2.1M members), Price Optimiser. Insurance/mortgages need deterministic auditable systems. Less defensible for broadband/energy.
- **Car classifieds (AUTO.L): MEDIUM risk.** Inventory data moat. Dealers must LIST somewhere. AI improves discovery, cannot create listings. FB Marketplace + Marketplace Pro emerging as low-cost alternative for independent dealers.
- **Property classifieds (RMV.L): MEDIUM risk, CoStar/OnTheMarket EMERGING + CLASS ACTION.** CoStar investing heavily (GBP 46.5M Year 1). Traffic +212%, visitors +348%, leads +76%. GBP 1B class action could constrain pricing power.
- **Adviser platform (IHP.L): LOW risk.** B2B infrastructure. AI augments, not displaces.

**2. Dealer Revolt / Pricing Pushback -- APRIL 1 IMMINENT (25 DAYS)**

AUTO.L: IMDA survey 165 dealers cancelled/gave notice (AUTO.L says <100 of 14,000). 70% of surveyed dealers reduced/downgraded packages. April 1, 2026 pricing +5.5% (down from 8% prior year). Deal Builder + Buying Signals monetization begins April 1.

**New competitive dynamic**: Marketplace Pro (UK SaaS, launched Feb 2026) enables AI-powered bulk listing on Facebook Marketplace. Imports inventory from AutoTrader. 38M UK FB Marketplace users. Free channel. Currently targets independent/small dealers. Not yet professional-grade (no financing, no provenance checks, no consumer protection). But reduces switching friction during price revolt.

**April 1 = DEFINITIVE test.** If smooth absorption: narrative clears. If second cancellation wave: thesis damaged. Used car supply shortage (-25-30% in 5-6yr old vehicles) may ease friction (dealers earn higher margins per car in tight supply).

**Analyst consensus BEARISH**: Goldman SELL. 7 Buy, 7 Hold, 2 Sell. Consensus PT ~698p.

**3. Rightmove GBP 1B Class Action -- NEW MATERIAL RISK**

Filed at Competition Appeal Tribunal (CAT). Led by Jeremy Newman (former CMA panel member). Funded by Innsworth Capital. Law firm: Scott+Scott UK. 170+ estate agencies expressed interest. Alleges dominance abuse via excessive/unfair fees. RMV.L's 70% profit margin cited as evidence of market power.

**Sector read-across**: If case proceeds to discovery and establishes that dominant marketplace pricing constitutes abuse, it creates precedent applicable to AUTO.L and potentially MONY.L. However, RMV.L's position is unique (>85% market share in property listings vs AUTO.L's ~70% in auto). Case certification is next milestone -- timeline uncertain.

**RMV.L response**: "Confident in the value we provide to our partners."

**4. UK Insurance Premium Cycle -- CONFIRMED TURNING POSITIVE**

ABI H1 2026 confirms double-digit YoY premium increases after -9%/-10% decline in 2025. UK motor insurance market GBP 13.6B (CAGR 4.33% to 2032). Key: EV premiums 25% higher than petrol (GBP 707 vs GBP 558). Industry expects ~5% premium increase across 2026. **TAILWIND for MONY.L**: higher premiums = higher revenue per switch + more comparison activity.

**Aviva/Direct Line merger (completed Jul 2025)**: Direct Line brands NOW on PCWs (Compare the Market debut). Combined 20-25% motor market share. Short-term positive for MONY.L (more products). Long-term risk: Aviva targets reduced PCW reliance via direct channels (MyAviva, GBP 100M synergy target).

**5. UK Consumer & Housing Backdrop -- MIXED**

GfK Consumer Confidence -19 (Feb 2026, down from -16 in Jan). Rising unemployment weighing. BUT housing market improving: 22% net surveyors expect sales increases, prices +2-4% in 2026. Mortgage rates easing. Net: mixed but directionally improving for property platforms.

**6. Regulation: DMCCA + CMA Evolution**

DMCCA consumer protection LIVE since Apr 2025. CMA Strategy 2026-2029 published. Focus on big tech SMS designations (Google, Apple). UK government launched consultation on CMA overhaul (Jan 2026, responses by Mar 31). Subscription transparency rules (autumn 2026) marginally affect SuperSaveClub. No direct CMA investigation of classifieds/comparison, but CAT class action (Rightmove) could establish regulatory precedent on pricing power.

**7. European Classifieds M&A Wave**: Scout24's Fotocasa/Habitaclia closed Mar 2 (EUR 153M). Validates winner-take-all thesis but introduces execution risk on international expansion.
