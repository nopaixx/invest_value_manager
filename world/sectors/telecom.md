**Nota sobre Iran War/Oil Shock (UPDATED Apr 8):**
Telecoms are relatively insulated from the Iran/oil crisis vs cyclicals because:
- Revenue is subscription-based, essential service -- churn remains low in recessions
- Contracts often indexed to inflation -- some pricing power maintained
- Network energy costs (electricity) represent ~5-7% of opex -- meaningful but not crippling
- Main transmission channel: inflation re-acceleration delays rate cuts, hurting leveraged operators (TEF, VOD) more than net-cash/low-leverage (KPN, DTE)
- Defensive characteristics = CONFIRMED fund inflow beneficiary in risk-off (Telia near ATH, KPN near 52wH, Orange near 52wH)
- Oil at $94 WTI (T1: macro_fragility.py Apr 8), 52wH $119.48 -- elevated but off peaks

**EU Digital Networks Act (DNA) - Progress Update (Apr 2026):**
The DNA proposal (Jan 21, 2026) is now in the Ordinary Legislative Procedure before EU Parliament and Council. Key provisions unchanged:
- **Indefinite spectrum licenses** (subject to periodic review)
- **"Single Passport" pan-EU authorization** -- notify in one Member State, operate everywhere
- **Office for Digital Networks (ODN)** replaces RSPG
- **Merging 4 legal acts** into one directly applicable Regulation
- **Security and Resilience**: EU-level Preparedness Plan
- **Timeline**: 12-24 months from proposal to adoption. "Many amendments expected." Implementation 2028-2029 at earliest. Long road.
