## Resumen Ejecutivo

El sector telecom europeo permanece en una paradoja: es de los sectores MAS BARATOS de Europa (P/E 7-12x vs mercado 15-18x), pero con razones estructurales que justifican parcialmente el descuento. La tesis central sigue siendo que los operadores europeos sufren fragmentacion regulatoria, competencia agresiva, y capex intensivo que comprime retornos sobre capital.

Los catalizadores identificados previamente se han CONFIRMADO pero parcialmente AGOTADO en terminos de upside inmediato:
1. **DTE.DE FY2025: BEAT + Strong 2026 Guidance** - EBITDA AL EUR 44.2B (FY2025). 2026 guide EUR 47.4B (+6% YoY). EPS EUR 2.20 targeted (+10%). Record EUR 1.00/share dividend + EUR 2B buyback. Revenue EUR 119.1B (+4.2% organic). However: stock reaction mixed -- guidance in-line, not upside surprise. Market expected more given TMUS strength.
2. **Consolidacion ejecutandose** - VodafoneThree integration advancing (10,000 MOCN sites by Mar 2026). Orange MASORANGE 100% acquisition signed, EC review pending, close expected H1 2026. TIM Sparkle sale closing Mar 2026. DNA regulatory proposal advancing through EU Parliament.
3. **Capex peak pasando** - Capex-to-sales bajando de pico 25% (2021) hacia 16% (2025-27). FCF mejora visible.
4. **Proximus CRASH** - Q4 2025 earnings DISASTER: stock dropped -19.7% to EUR 6.63 on Feb 27. Higher costs + weaker FCF growth. Recovery play thesis DAMAGED.

**Key developments since last update (Mar 18):**
1. **ECB held rates at 2.15% (Feb 5).** Mar 18-19 meeting pending -- oil at $97-99 WTI makes another hold likely. EUR appreciation may push ECB toward eventual cut if inflation stays below 2% target (1.9% forecast 2026). Leveraged operators (TEF, VOD) remain most exposed.
2. **MWC 2026 highlights:** AI-RAN gaining prominence, DTE/Orange call for "four building blocks" of digital sovereignty, sector lobbying DNA to go further.
3. **TIM Sparkle sale closing confirmed Mar 2026.** Poste Italiane became TIM largest shareholder.
4. **Morgan Stanley picks DTE + Orange as top EU telecoms** -- consensus view aligns with our prior thesis (quality operators outperform).

**Previous context (still valid): IRAN WAR + OIL SHOCK.** Oil at $92 WTI is a MIXED impact for telecoms -- on one hand, defensive/essential service characteristics protect revenue; on the other, energy costs for network operations rise and inflation re-acceleration delays rate cuts (negative for leveraged operators). Telecoms remain relatively insulated vs cyclicals but NOT immune to stagflation.

**Mejor fase del ciclo:** All-weather (defensivo)
**Beta tipico:** 0.6-0.8 (bajo)

**Nota sobre Iran War/Oil Shock (NEW):**
Telecoms are relatively insulated from the Iran/oil crisis vs cyclicals because:
- Revenue is subscription-based, essential service -- churn remains low in recessions
- Contracts often indexed to inflation -- some pricing power maintained
- Network energy costs (electricity) represent ~5-7% of opex -- meaningful but not crippling
- Main transmission channel: inflation re-acceleration delays rate cuts, hurting leveraged operators (TEF, VOD) more than net-cash/low-leverage (KPN, DTE)
- Defensive characteristics = potential fund inflow beneficiary in risk-off (already visible in KPN, TELIA near 52wH)

**Nota sobre tipos de interes (updated):**
- Oil at $92 WTI = rate cuts effectively OFF TABLE for H1 2026
- ECB Mar 18 may hold instead of expected cut (BofA pre-war expected cut, but oil changes calculus)
- Operators with 2.5-3.5x leverage (TEF, VOD) most exposed to "higher for longer"
- DTE, KPN, Orange at 2.0-2.5x -- manageable
- Only ~7% of debt refinances per year -- impact gradual, not immediate

**EU Digital Networks Act (DNA) - Progress Update (Mar 2026):**
The DNA proposal (Jan 21, 2026) is now before the European Parliament and Council. Key provisions unchanged:
- **Indefinite spectrum licenses** (subject to periodic review)
- **"Single Passport" pan-EU authorization** -- notify in one Member State, operate everywhere
- **Office for Digital Networks (ODN)** replaces RSPG
- **Merging 4 legal acts** (EECC, BEREC Regulation, Radio Spectrum Policy Programme, Open Internet Regulation) into one directly applicable Regulation
- **Security and Resilience**: EU-level Preparedness Plan for natural disasters and foreign interference
- **Telecom industry pushing for bolder action** -- Connect Europe says DNA doesn't go far enough
- **Timeline**: EU legislative process 12-24 months from proposal. Implementation 2028-2029 at earliest

### Competitivos
- **Nuevos entrantes:** Improbables (capex prohibitivo), except Iliad (low-cost disruptor)
- **Sustitutos:** OTT for communications, but not for connectivity
- **Private equity:** Buying infrastructure (towers, fibre), separating from operations
- **Competencia de fibra alternativa:** New fibre operators entering selected markets. Spain: PremiumFiber JV (MasOrange 58%, Vodafone Spain 17%, GIC 25%) + Fiberpass (Vodafone Spain + Telefonica)
- **Iran war cyberattack risk:** State-sponsored cyberattacks on network infrastructure ELEVATED. Operators with robust cybersecurity (larger ones) better positioned
