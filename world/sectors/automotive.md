## Resumen Ejecutivo

The European auto premium sector (BMW, Mercedes, VW Group, Stellantis) faces the worst confluence of headwinds since 2008. Three crises are now simultaneous:

1. **Operation Epic Fury / Hormuz closure**: Oil at $96 WTI (T1 macro_fragility Apr 8; was ~$62 pre-crisis) devastates input costs (petrochemical feedstocks +15-25%, synthetic rubber, plastics, adhesives), shipping/logistics (Hormuz near-total halt, ~9.1M b/d production shut-ins in April), and consumer demand (gasoline ~$4.30/gal crushing discretionary spending). Gulf production shut-ins expanded from 7.5M b/d in March to 9.1M b/d in April, with no resolution in sight.

2. **US tariffs (post-SCOTUS regime)**: SCOTUS struck down IEEPA tariffs Feb 20 (Learning Resources v. Trump). Section 232 25% auto tariff survives. EU-US deal at 15% for EU autos remains unratified by EU Parliament. Administration imposed 15% global surcharge under Section 122 (Feb 24, expires ~Jul 24). Net tariff on EU cars currently 15% -- better than 25% but still significant margin headwind for EU OEMs selling into US.

3. **Structural disruption from China**: China NEV penetration hit 50.8% in 2025 (16.49M NEVs sold). BYD raised 2026 export target to 1.5M units with EU sales 3x YoY. German premium brands lost 12-19% China volume in 2025. BMW offset with Europe +7.3% and Americas +5.6%, but the competitive moat continues eroding in the world's largest auto market.

The FY2025 earnings season is now COMPLETE and confirms the pain: Mercedes EBIT crashed to EUR 4.8B (from EUR 8.7B), VW operating profit halved to EUR 8.9B (margin 2.8%), Stellantis posted a EUR 22.3B net loss, and BMW held relatively better (EBT EUR 10.24B) but guides 4-6% auto margin for 2026. ALL German OEMs guide compressed margins for 2026 (3-6% vs historical 8-12%). Supplier distress accelerating -- ZF debt costs at 7%, Falkensteg forecasts 30% jump in supplier insolvencies.

The one positive: BMW Neue Klasse gaining traction. iX3 surpassed 50,000 orders. i3 sedan debuted March 18 with strong specs (440mi range, 400kW). But this is a 2027-2028 margin story, not 2026.

**Bottom line**: Status remains AVOID. Oil has worsened since last update ($96 vs $92). Tariff clarity is slightly better (15% not 25%) but still punitive. All OEMs guiding compressed margins. Supplier bankruptcy wave building. We have no positions and no plans to enter.
