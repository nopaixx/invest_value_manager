# Sector: Automotive (Premium/Luxury EU)

> Ultima actualizacion: 2026-03-18 (macro-analyst refresh — BMW i3 debut, US pre-tariff sales surge, oil $98 WTI)
> Status: AVOID (Iran oil shock + tariffs + structural disruption = triple crisis)

## Resumen Ejecutivo

The European auto premium sector (BMW, Mercedes, VW Group, Stellantis) faces the worst confluence of headwinds since 2008. Three crises are now simultaneous:

1. **Operation Epic Fury / Hormuz closure**: Oil at $92 WTI (up from ~$62 three weeks ago) devastates input costs (petrochemical feedstocks +15-25%, synthetic rubber, plastics, adhesives), shipping/logistics (Hormuz near-total halt, 3,200 ships idle), and consumer demand (gasoline costs crushing discretionary spending). If sustained >$90 for 3+ months, auto demand drops materially.

2. **US tariffs**: The EU-US tariff deal (27.5% reduced to 15% on EU autos) remains unratified by EU Parliament. Greenland-related tariffs threaten additional 10-25% on Germany, France, Netherlands. Auto supply chains face severe disruption from multi-layered tariff regimes.

3. **Structural disruption from China**: China NEV penetration hit 50.8% in 2025 (16.49M NEVs sold). BYD sold 4.6M NEVs, dethroning Tesla globally. German premium brands lost 12-19% China volume in 2025. The competitive moat is eroding fast in the world's largest auto market.

The FY2025 earnings season (Feb-Mar 2026) confirms the pain: Mercedes EBIT crashed to EUR 4.8B (from EUR 8.7B), VW operating margin compressed to ~2.8%, Stellantis posted a EUR 22.3B net loss on strategic reset charges, and BMW guides 4-6% auto margin for 2026. Supplier distress is accelerating -- ZF, Continental, Bosch all cutting tens of thousands of jobs, with analyst forecasts of a "wave of bankruptcies" in 2026.

The one positive: BMW's Neue Klasse iX3 launched production in late 2025 at Debrecen (Hungary), with demand outstripping initial estimates (second shift added early). The i3 sedan debuts March 18. If Neue Klasse delivers margin parity at scale, it could mark a turning point -- but that is a 2027-2028 story, not 2026.

**Bottom line**: Status moved from NEUTRAL to AVOID. Oil shock adds a NEW cost/demand headwind on top of existing structural challenges. P/E ratios have risen to 8-9x (from 5-7x in Feb) reflecting some hope from German fiscal stimulus and EU regulatory flexibility, but the risk/reward remains unfavorable given triple crisis and uncertain oil duration. We have no positions and no plans to enter.
