## Resumen Ejecutivo

**MATERIAL CHANGE since Feb 13: Operation Epic Fury (Feb 28-Mar 1) TRANSFORMS this sector.**

Defense & Aerospace has shifted from "structural spending upcycle at elevated valuations" to "CRISIS-ACCELERATED spending supercycle at even MORE elevated valuations." Operation Epic Fury -- US-Israel coordinated strikes on Iran, Khamenei killed, Hormuz de facto closed -- is the single most powerful defense spending catalyst since Russia's Feb 2022 invasion. European defense stocks surged 5-8% on Mar 2 alone. NATO's Hague Summit (Jun 2025) ALREADY raised the target from 2% to 5% GDP by 2035 (3.5% core defense + 1.5% defense-related). The Iran crisis makes this target politically EASIER to achieve and accelerates the timeline.

Global defense spending hit $2.63 trillion in 2025 and is rising. Europe collectively exceeded $693B in 2026, with Germany alone budgeting EUR 82.69B for the Bundeswehr and targeting 3.5% GDP by 2029. The rearmament wave is REAL, FUNDED, and ACCELERATING.

However, the valuation problem is now WORSE, not better. European defense stocks that traded at 30-85x P/E on Feb 13 have rallied further on the Iran crisis. The sector is MORE "Querido" than before. RHM.DE at 84x, BAE at 32x, Thales at 47x, SAAB at 55x -- all above their Feb 13 levels or at similar premiums. The investment opportunity remains surgical:

1. **AM.PA (Dassault Aviation)** -- Tier A quality, just reported strong FY2025 (revenue EUR 7.4B, backlog EUR 46.6B record, 2026 guide EUR 8.5B), P/E 33x = cheapest quality name. BUT near 52wH (EUR 329 vs EUR 354.8 high). Needs pullback to 15-20% MoS.
2. **BAH (Booz Allen Hamilton)** -- CONTRARIAN: P/E 11.8x, crushed by DOGE to $79.80 (near 52wL $73.93). DOGE impact WORSE than expected (Goldman downgrade to Neutral, PT slashed to $109). But $38B backlog, 22K cleared workforce, AI growth = structural moat intact. If DOGE eases, re-rating potential is massive. NOW AT OUR ENTRY ZONE ($70 target, currently $79.80 = 16% above entry).
3. **Aftermarket monopolies (TDG, HEI)** -- Moats structural not geopolitical. TDG Q1 revenue +14% to $2.3B. HEI record Q1 (EPS +13%, Flight Support +15%). Both expensive but compounders.
4. **US govcon IT** as a sub-sector remains the ONLY cheap area in defense: BAH P/E 11.8x vs sector 30-85x.

**Key risk UPDATED:** The de-escalation risk from Feb 13 ("Ukraine ceasefire could reduce urgency") is now LOWER. Iran crisis ADDS to Ukraine/NATO urgency, not substitutes for it. The risk now is that we are in the LATE stages of a defense re-rating where all the easy money has been made and valuations are stretched. A pullback would create opportunity, but buying at these levels requires exceptional selectivity.

**US Government IT Services sub-sector UPDATE:** DOGE impact deeper than expected. Goldman Sachs downgraded BAH from Buy to Neutral, slashing PT from $150 to $109. BAH stock fell to $79.80 (near 52wL). Revenue pressures from contract cuts MORE severe than analysts predicted. BUT -- and this is crucial -- the structural moat (22K cleared workforce, $38B backlog, AI capabilities) is UNAFFECTED by DOGE. DOGE is a cyclical headwind on a structural asset. When political winds shift (and they always do), the re-rating will be violent. BAH at P/E 11.8x with ROIC 23.6% in a sector trading at 30-85x is a 3x multiple gap.
