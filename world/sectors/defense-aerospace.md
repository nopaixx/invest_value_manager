**MATERIAL CHANGE since Feb 13: Operation Epic Fury (Feb 28-Mar 1) TRANSFORMS this sector.**

Defense & Aerospace has shifted from "structural spending upcycle at elevated valuations" to "CRISIS-ACCELERATED spending supercycle at even MORE elevated valuations." Operation Epic Fury -- US-Israel coordinated strikes on Iran, Khamenei killed, Hormuz de facto closed -- is the single most powerful defense spending catalyst since Russia's Feb 2022 invasion. European defense stocks surged 5-8% on Mar 2 alone. NATO's Hague Summit (Jun 2025) ALREADY raised the target from 2% to 5% GDP by 2035 (3.5% core defense + 1.5% defense-related). The Iran crisis makes this target politically EASIER to achieve and accelerates the timeline.

Global defense spending hit $2.63 trillion in 2025 and is rising. Europe collectively exceeded $693B in 2026, with Germany alone budgeting EUR 82.69B for the Bundeswehr and targeting 3.5% GDP by 2029. The rearmament wave is REAL, FUNDED, and ACCELERATING.

However, the valuation problem is now WORSE, not better. European defense stocks that traded at 30-85x P/E on Feb 13 have rallied further on the Iran crisis. The sector is MORE "Querido" than before. If DOGE eases, re-rating potential is massive.
3. **Aftermarket monopolies (TDG, HEI)** -- Moats structural not geopolitical. TDG raised FY26 guidance (revenue $9.94B, EBITDA margin 52.4%). HEI record Q1 (EPS +13%, Flight Support +15%). Both sold off with market -- TDG $1,170 (-28% from 52wH), HEI $275 (-24% from 52wH). Pullback improving entry points.
4. **US govcon IT** as a sub-sector remains the ONLY cheap area in defense: BAH P/E 12.5x vs sector 30-85x.

**Key risk UPDATED:** The de-escalation risk from Feb 13 ("Ukraine ceasefire could reduce urgency") is now LOWER. Iran crisis ADDS to Ukraine/NATO urgency, not substitutes for it. The risk now is that we are in the LATE stages of a defense re-rating where all the easy money has been made and valuations are stretched. A pullback would create opportunity, but buying at these levels requires exceptional selectivity. **NEW RISK (Apr 8):** War escalation beyond military targets to civilian infrastructure could trigger broader market panic and oil spike above $120, which would hurt EVERYTHING including defense stocks short-term (risk-off). When this gap closes -- and it WILL close -- the re-rating is 50-100%.

## Key Observations (UPDATED 8-Apr-2026)

1. **War Day 39 is the most dangerous escalation point yet.** Kharg Island strikes, civilian infrastructure threats, 8pm deadline -- the conflict is intensifying, not stabilizing. This REINFORCES the defense spending thesis but creates short-term market volatility risk. A ceasefire remains possible but probability is LOW near-term (45-day proposal rejected, 10-point counter rejected by US).

2. **BAH is now at PEAK FEAR.** Goldman downgraded to SELL. Pentagon cancelled $5.1B in contracts. Layoffs confirmed. Treasury terminated all BAH contracts. And yet -- Q3 FY26 showed EPS $1.77 (beat $1.29 by 37%), guidance RAISED to $5.95-$6.15, and FY27 pipeline is $53B (+12% YoY). The gap between narrative (destruction) and fundamentals (resilience) is the WIDEST it has been. At $84.08 (P/E 12.5x), the stock prices permanent impairment. The data says temporary disruption. This is the setup for a 50-100% re-rating when DOGE fades.

3. **Aftermarket correction creating opportunities.** TDG (-28% from 52wH) and HEI (-24%) have sold off with broader market despite raising guidance (TDG) and posting record earnings (HEI). These are structural compounders tied to flight hours and fleet aging -- not defense budgets. P/E compression from 42x/61x to 38x/55x still elevated but the trajectory is favorable.

4. **EU defense correcting from euphoria -- but not enough.** RHM.DE from EUR 2,008 to EUR 1,531 (-24%), SAAB from SEK 749 to SEK 622 (-17%). P/E compression is happening as earnings grow (RHM from 84x to 69x) but still far above historical 15-25x range. The correction makes them LESS dangerous but NOT cheap.

5. **SAF.PA emerges as best value in the sector.** P/E 16.5x for a jet engine DUOPOLIST (CFM56/LEAP with GE). FCF EUR 3.9B growing to EUR 4.4-4.6B. "Outstanding" FY2025. This is cheaper than most consumer staples companies. Worth formal QS scoring -- may be the hidden gem of defense/aerospace.

6. **Tariffs are a NON-EVENT for defense.** Chapter 98 exemption covers military materials. EU defense firms have US manufacturing presence. Breaking Defense reports impact "remains muted" one year after Liberation Day. This was a fear that did not materialize.

7. **UK defence spending trajectory confirmed.** GBP 62.2B (2025/26) rising to GBP 79.7B (2027/28). Labour committed 2.6% GDP from Apr 2027, funded by cutting ODA. This is FUNDED and REAL, supporting BA.L, BAB.L, and UK defense supply chain.

8. **Oil at $110 WTI is a net POSITIVE for defense.** Higher oil = energy security urgency = more defense spending. Militaries face higher fuel costs but governments face higher political pressure to secure energy supply chains. Defense budgets rise in oil crises -- always have.
