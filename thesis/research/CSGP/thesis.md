## 3. Moat Assessment

### Moat Type: WIDE (Commercial) + NARROW (Residential)

**Commercial Real Estate Data (WIDE moat):**

1. **Proprietary Data (40 years, $5B+ cost to replicate):** CoStar employs ~6,000 researchers who physically verify property data across 8.5M+ commercial properties. This database took 40 years to build. No competitor has anything comparable. The data compounds -- every year of new data makes the database more valuable and harder to replicate.

2. **Network Effects (Self-reinforcing):** More data attracts more users. More users generate more transaction data. More transaction data improves analytics. This flywheel has been running for decades.

3. **Switching Costs (Extreme):** Brokers, appraisers, and lenders build workflows around CoStar data. Their models, their presentations, their compliance processes all reference CoStar. Switching requires re-training staff, rebuilding models, and losing continuity of historical analysis. 93% renewal rate confirms.

4. **Regulatory Moat:** CoStar data is used as the standard in many regulatory and legal proceedings. Courts, municipalities, and government agencies reference CoStar data. This quasi-official status is almost impossible for a competitor to replicate.

**Apartments.com (WIDE moat):**

- #1 multifamily marketplace in US. 99% monthly renewal rate.
- Network effects: largest listing inventory attracts renters, which attracts landlords.
- Super Bowl marketing investment has built brand awareness ($2B+ invested over years).

**Homes.com (NARROW/BUILDING moat):**

- 108M monthly unique visitors, 31K agent subscribers.
- Still small vs Zillow (50%+ of market). Agent-centric model is differentiated but unproven at scale.
- Moat potential is there (data + network effects) but not yet established.

**Matterport (NARROW moat):**

- Leader in 3D digital twins. 14M+ spaces digitized. 177 countries.
- Enhances listings across all CoStar platforms. Vertical integration play.
- Not yet proven as standalone moat within CoStar ecosystem.
