## Business Understanding

### Model de Negocio

MSCI is a financial data infrastructure monopoly organized in four segments: **Index** (57% of revenue, 76.4% EBITDA margin), **Analytics** (23%, 47.9% margin), **Sustainability & Climate** (11%, 36.3% margin), and **Private Assets** (9%, 24.9% margin).

The core business model is a **toll on global passive investing**. MSCI licenses 240,000+ proprietary indexes to fund managers who create ETFs, mutual funds, and mandates benchmarked against them. Revenue comes from:
- **Recurring subscriptions** (73% of revenue, 94.4% retention): Predictable, contract-based
- **Asset-based fees** (25% of revenue): Scales automatically with AUM growth (market + flows)
- **Non-recurring** (3%): One-off projects and data sales

The Index segment alone generated $1.79B in FY2025 with 76.4% EBITDA margin — effectively a license to print money. Each dollar of AUM benchmarked to MSCI indexes generates ~2.41 basis points in annual fees. With $2.34T in ETF AUM linked to MSCI (up 35.7% YoY), the asset-based fee run rate reached $852M.

### Unit Economics

- **Revenue per employee**: ~$500K (6,268 employees, $3.1B revenue) — exceptional
- **FCF margin**: 46.5% — near best-in-class across all industries
- **Marginal cost of licensing**: Near zero — each new ETF benchmarked to MSCI adds pure margin
- **Capital intensity**: Low (capex 4% of revenue, rising to 5% FY2026 guidance)
- **88% of subscription revenue from multi-product clients** — deep cross-sell and switching cost

### Por Que Esta Barata (It Is NOT Cheap)

MSCI is not cheap. At 35.5x P/E and 27.4x EV/EBIT, the market fully prices the monopoly quality.

**Why the stock trades -11% from 52wH ($626):**
