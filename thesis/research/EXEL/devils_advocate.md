Short interest at 14.2% of float with 9.3 days to cover confirms meaningful institutional skepticism. The thesis survives as a speculative option play on zanzalintinib, but NOT as a Tier A quality compounder purchase at current prices.

## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. Patent Protection Through 2030-2031

- **FA assumption:** "Patent protection through 2030 (MSN) and 2031 (Teva, Cipla settlements). EP0 ruled in favor of formulation patent."
- **Evidence against:** The COMPOSITION OF MATTER patent (US 7,579,473) expires **August 14, 2026** -- only 5 months from now. The 2030 date relies entirely on malate salt patents ('439, '440, '015) that MSN is appealing. A Federal Circuit reversal would allow generic entry as early as late 2026. The thesis conflates "District Court ruled in favor" with "protection is secure" -- appeals are a real risk. Morningstar explicitly rates EXEL as NO MOAT precisely because of this patent timeline.
- **Severity:** **HIGH**
- **Resolution suggested:** The thesis must explicitly model the probability of MSN winning the appeal (estimated 20-35% based on patent litigation success rates at Federal Circuit). If MSN wins, the stock drops 20%+ to the $28-32 range. This binary risk is not priced into the FA's FV.

### 2. FV of $50 vs Market $41 vs Morningstar $25-46

- **FA assumption:** Weighted FV of $50 based on OEY + DCF + EV/EBIT
- **Evidence against:** Morningstar's original FV was $18.90 (NO MOAT rating), later raised to $25.10, and most recently to $45.83. The FA's $50 sits ABOVE even Morningstar's latest upward revision. Analyst consensus clusters at $44.36-$47.56. The FA is at the high end of the consensus range, which means there is NO informational edge -- the FA's FV is the consensus FV, just rounded up. Per Error #49: "If my FV converges to consensus without independent reasoning, I have NO informational edge."
- **Severity:** **HIGH**
- **Resolution suggested:** The FA's FV should be stress-tested against Morningstar's NO MOAT framework. The $50 FV implicitly assumes patent protection holds AND zanzalintinib succeeds. If either fails, FV drops to $25-35 range. The probability-weighted FV should account for these binary paths.

### 3. Q4 2025 Revenue Miss and Below-Consensus 2026 Guidance

- **FA assumption:** Revenue growing at +11% YoY guided, with further acceleration from zanzalintinib
- **Evidence against:** Q4 2025 revenue MISSED consensus ($598.7M vs $616.2M). FY2026 guidance midpoint ($2.575B) came in BELOW consensus ($2.665B). Discounts and allowances surged 25% to $889M, outpacing 20% gross revenue growth. This signals 340B program utilization and government rebate pressure that the thesis barely mentions. The thesis projects $2,850M in FY2027 revenue, but if the base (FY2026) starts below consensus, FY2027 projections are too aggressive.
- **Severity:** **MODERATE**
- **Resolution suggested:** Reduce FY2026 revenue to guidance midpoint $2.575B. Apply haircut of 3-5% to FY2027-2029 projections to account for rebate pressure trend.

### 4. Short Interest 14.2% of Float (Not Mentioned in Thesis)

- **FA assumption:** Thesis does not mention short interest at all
- **Evidence against:** Short interest is 14.2% of float with 9.3 days to cover. This is ELEVATED for a healthcare company. Short interest decreased 5.6% MoM (from 27.9M to 26.3M shares) but remains very high. Insider Monkey flagged EXEL as "among large-cap stocks insiders and short sellers are dumping like crazy." This institutional skepticism from informed short sellers is material information the thesis ignores.
- **Severity:** **MODERATE**
- **Resolution suggested:** Add short interest monitoring to kill conditions. If SI rises above 18% of float, it signals intensifying institutional bear conviction and should trigger review.

### 5. Zanzalintinib OS Benefit is Clinically Modest

- **FA assumption:** Zanzalintinib has "positive Phase 3 OS data" and is a potential "second franchise drug"
- **Evidence against:** The OS benefit was 10.9 months vs 9.4 months -- a 1.5-month median OS improvement. While statistically significant (HR 0.80, p=0.0045), this is a MODEST clinical benefit. For context, oncology drugs with transformative commercial success typically show 3-6+ month OS improvements or meaningful quality-of-life gains. A 1.5-month OS benefit may limit pricing power, payer adoption, and peak sales estimates. The $875M-$5B peak sales range in the thesis spans a 5.7x range -- this enormous spread reveals genuine uncertainty about commercial potential. The regorafenib comparator is a LOW bar (standard 3L CRC is a weak competitor). Against stronger treatment lines, zanzalintinib may not be competitive.
- **Severity:** **MODERATE**
- **Resolution suggested:** Weight peak sales estimates toward the lower end ($875M-$1.5B) rather than mid-range. The thesis assigns 50% probability to $500-800M peak by 2029 in the base case -- this is reasonable but the probability of the bear case (limited uptake) should be higher given the modest OS benefit.
