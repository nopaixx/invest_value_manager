# Counter-Analysis: UBER (Uber Technologies, Inc.)

## Fecha: 2026-03-21

## Resumen Ejecutivo

The R1 thesis presents Uber as a dominant platform at historically cheap multiples (16x P/E) with an impressive operating leverage story. While the profitability trajectory is genuine, the thesis contains **material vulnerabilities** in three areas: (1) the QS adjustment of +17 points is the largest in the system's history and relies on subjective reframing of what "quality" means, (2) the AV disruption risk is framed optimistically as "Uber becomes the AV orchestrator" when recent evidence shows Uber and Waymo are heading toward direct competition, and (3) the FV of $82 was derived by overriding the anti-bullish-bias protocol that produced $73-75. At $73.89 today, the stock is essentially AT the protocol-derived FV, meaning there is no margin of safety. The thesis survives as a high-quality WATCHLIST candidate, but the entry price needs to be lower.

---

## Phase 0.5: Calibration

**Market Anchor:** At $73.89, the market implies 2.9% FCF growth for 5 years. Historical 3Y revenue CAGR is 17.7%. The enormous gap (-14.8pp) suggests either: (a) the market sees massive structural risk the thesis underestimates, or (b) genuine undervaluation. The answer likely involves AV disruption being priced aggressively.

**DA Historical Accuracy:** 25 total corrections, avg -15.7%, median -13.0%. All negative. No outcomes measured yet. I must distinguish genuine risks from reflexive conservatism.

**Anchoring Principle:** The market at $73.89 is pricing Uber as if FCF will barely grow. The FA must prove WHY the market is wrong. The FA's case (AV orchestrator thesis, operating leverage) is plausible but requires scrutiny.

## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. QS Adjustment +17 Points (45 to 62): "Uber is Tier B"

- **FA's Claim:** Tool score of 45 understates quality because of inflection dynamics (negative EPS base year, short ROIC history, wrong sector comparison).
- **Evidencia en contra:** A +17 adjustment is unprecedented in this system. Historical adjustments range from +2 (META) to +8 (VRSN market position). The FA's reasoning is individually plausible but collectively aggressive:
  - ROIC 11% is barely above WACC (10.5%) -- a spread of only +0.5pp. This is NOT exceptional. The FA notes it is "improving" but 3 years of data is insufficient to establish persistence.
  - EPS inflection from -$4.64 to +$4.82 is impressive, but scoring the TRAJECTORY rather than the LEVEL means we're giving credit for being unprofitable in the past.
  - Market position 8/8 is correct but does not change the financial reality captured in the other components.
  - Capital Allocation 0/10 is honestly scored -- no dividends, 0.5% insider ownership, CEO sold 450K shares ($43M) in 2025 alone with 0 buys.
- **Severidad:** **HIGH** -- If QS stays at 45 (Tier C), required MoS jumps from 20-25% to 30-40%, making the stock uninvestable at $74. Even at 55 (Tier B minimum), the thesis changes materially.
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Accept +10 adjustment (to 55, Tier B floor) rather than +17. Market position correction is valid (+8), but the financial metrics don't support full Tier B quality. ROIC persistence is unproven, capital allocation is poor. Use QS 55-58 as realistic adjusted score.

### 2. "AVs Will NEED Uber as the Demand Platform"

- **FA's Claim:** Uber is positioning as the AV orchestration layer. Deals with Nvidia (100K robotaxis), Waymo, Waabi, Nuro, BYD. Even in an autonomous future, someone needs to manage demand.
- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - **Waymo partnership is FRAYING:** Recent reporting confirms Uber and Waymo are moving from partnership to direct competition in Dallas, London, and San Francisco. Waymo has SURPASSED Lyft in gross bookings in San Francisco. The "platform partnership" narrative is already breaking down.
  - **Uber is shifting to ASSET OWNERSHIP:** The Rivian deal ($1.25B for 10K robotaxis), Lucid investment ($300M+), Nuro investment ($300M+) represent Uber BUYING vehicles, not remaining asset-light. This fundamentally alters the capital intensity assumption. The thesis says "Asset-light marketplace model. Capex/Revenue ~1%." This will change.
  - **The orchestrator thesis is untested:** There is no precedent for a demand aggregator successfully transitioning to AV orchestration. The closest analogy (travel aggregators vs airlines) shows that when the supply side consolidates, the aggregator's power diminishes.
  - **Consumer Watchdog report:** Uber's California ballot initiative would limit robotaxi liability for accidents -- suggesting Uber is positioning to avoid, not embrace, the regulatory framework that governs safe AV deployment.
- **Severidad:** **HIGH** -- The AV orchestrator thesis is the core bull case at current valuation. If Uber becomes an AV operator (capital intensive) rather than an AV platform (asset light), margins could compress rather than expand. The Rivian/Lucid investments are EARLY signs of this shift.
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Model a scenario where Uber's AV transition requires $5-10B cumulative capex (vehicle purchases, insurance, maintenance), compressing FCF margins from 20% to 15% during 2027-2030. This reduces bull case FV from $125 to $95-100.

### 3. Fair Value Override ($82 vs Protocol-Derived $73-75)

- **FA's Claim:** The 60/40 bear/base anti-bullish-bias protocol is "overly punitive for a company with ACCELERATING profitability."
- **Evidencia en contra:** The S202 protocol exists because of documented systematic bullish bias (10/10 positions, avg +24.1% overestimation). The FA's reasoning for the override:
  - "Accelerating profitability" -- but ROIC is flat at 11% (2024-2025), not accelerating.
  - "Market implies only 3.4% growth" -- this IS the market telling us something. 3.4% implied growth means the market sees structural risk to the business model.
  - "Using standard 50/50 for a Tier B compounder with strong momentum" -- this is exactly the kind of override the S202 protocol was designed to prevent.
  - The protocol-derived FV range is $73-78, which means at $73.89 the stock is FAIRLY VALUED, not undervalued.
- **Severidad:** **CRITICAL** -- The FA acknowledged that the protocol produces FV at current price, then overrode it to create apparent MoS. This is a textbook example of the bullish bias the protocol was designed to correct. DA accuracy tracker shows avg correction -15.7%. If I apply historical avg correction to $82, post-DA FV would be ~$69.
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Use $75 as FV (midpoint of protocol range). At current price $73.89, there is essentially NO MoS. Entry should be at $63-65 for meaningful MoS (~15-20%).

### 4. CEO Selling Pattern

- **FA's Claim:** "Low insider ownership but not dumping."
- **Evidencia en contra:** Dara Khosrowshahi sold 450,000 shares in 2025 (Sept), worth ~$43M, with ZERO purchases. Over the past 5 years: 2 buys, 9 sells. This is a consistent pattern of monetization, not alignment.
- **Severidad:** **MODERATE** -- CEO selling is common in tech and some are likely 10b5-1 plans. But 9 sells vs 2 buys over 5 years, combined with 0/10 Capital Allocation score, weakens the "aligned management" narrative.
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Note as risk factor but not thesis-invalidating. Verify whether sells are 10b5-1.

### 5. SBC Understated as Risk

- **FA's Claim:** SBC declining from 5.6% to 3.5% of revenue. "Dilution ~2%/year is manageable."
- **Evidencia en contra:** The thesis uses two different SBC figures. In the margin table: SBC/Revenue 3.5%. But per Macrotrends/AlphaQuery data, Uber's SBC for the twelve months ending September 2025 was $4.516B -- that is ~8.7% of FY2025 revenue, not 3.5%. The meta-reflection correctly notes that "true economic FCF" subtracting SBC is closer to $8B, making EV/FCF effectively 20x, not 16x.
  - At 20x EV/FCF (economic), Uber is no longer "historically cheap." It's in line with mature tech platforms.
  - The 2% annual dilution compounds to ~24.7% over a 10-year forecast -- nearly a quarter of economic value destroyed for existing shareholders.
- **Severidad:** **HIGH** -- The headline P/E of 16x and FCF yield of 6.4% are misleading if SBC is $4.5B, not $1.8B. The thesis may be using quarterly run-rate vs TTM data inconsistently.
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Clarify which SBC figure is correct. If $4.5B TTM, revise all per-share economics accordingly. EV/FCF should be quoted post-SBC for honest comparison.
