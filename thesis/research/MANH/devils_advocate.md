## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. Revenue Deceleration is Structural, Not Temporary (RETAINED from R2)

- **Severity: HIGH**
- **Prior R2 assessment:** 3 years of declining growth (21.1% to 12.2% to 3.7%). Management guides 5-7%.
- **Update:** FY2026 guidance reaffirmed at $1.133-1.153B (5-7% growth). No upward revision. Maintenance and license attrition is a ~4pp headwind baked into guidance. Cloud revenue guided +21% to $492M, but this adds only ~$84M of revenue while legacy declines eat ~$45M.
- **Professional services** (38% of revenue): guided +3% for FY2026 after declining -4% in FY2025. Recovery is uncertain in a tariff-driven slowdown when implementation timelines stretch.
- **The "customer liquidation" headwind:** Q4 cloud revenue included a $1.3M headwind from customer liquidation, with $2.5M annualized not embedded in guidance. This is a new risk vector -- customers going out of business.
- **Resolution:** Growth will stabilize at 5-8%, not re-accelerate to 10-12%. R3's 8-10% assumption remains generous.

### 2. CEO Transition Risk is Material and Unresolved (RETAINED from R2)

- **Severity: HIGH**
- **Update:** Eric Clark has been CEO for ~13 months. Still no clear evidence of strategic differentiation or commercial acceleration under his leadership. FY2026 guidance is steady but uninspiring. Zero conference presentations or public strategic vision statements found beyond boilerplate "Agentic AI" messaging.
- **Insider activity update:** Only stock grants visible in insider transactions. ZERO open-market purchases by ANY insider at -42% from ATH. The 52-week low of $127.86 was hit in recent weeks. Insiders had ample opportunity to demonstrate conviction. They did not.
- **Smart money signal:** Fundsmith (quality compounder fund) holds 288K shares ($50M) as 3.2% of their portfolio. This is a positive quality signal but Fundsmith's position predates the CEO transition and may reflect inertia rather than fresh conviction.

### 3. Multiple Compression Risk at P/E 40x for 5-7% Grower (RETAINED from R2)

- **Severity: HIGH**
- **Update:** The stock trades at P/E 40.1x, EV/EBIT 29.9x. For a company guiding 5-7% revenue growth, this is a significant premium that requires EARNINGS growth to exceed revenue growth via margin expansion. This is the thesis, but SBC at 10.3% and rising erodes the economic margin expansion.
- **Comparables:** Enterprise software companies growing 5-7% typically trade at P/E 20-30x. MANH's premium is justified by quality (ROIC, moat, cloud-native) but the premium has compressed from P/E 60x+ at ATH to 40x. Further compression to 25-30x (which would be NORMAL for this growth) would imply $90-108 per share.
- **Morgan Stanley's $165 target** with "equal weight" effectively says the stock is fairly valued at current levels with limited upside. Redburn's downgrade to Neutral says the same.

### 4. Tariff-Driven Capex Freeze Risk (NEW -- NOT IN PRIOR R2)

- **Severity: MODERATE-HIGH**
- **Evidence:** Trump's reciprocal tariff regime (effective Apr 2, 2026) creates unprecedented supply chain disruption for MANH's customer base:
  - **Retail customers** (major segment: Walmart, Target, Hy-Vee): Facing margin pressure from tariffs on imported goods. Likely response: delay non-critical IT capex, including WMS implementation projects.
  - **3PL/logistics providers** (major segment): Facing volume uncertainty as supply chains are reorganized. Implementation projects may be paused pending clarity.
  - **Manufacturing/CPG customers:** Reshoring creates long-term WMS demand but SHORT-TERM creates project delays as companies re-evaluate supply chain topology.
  - **Professional services** (38% of revenue): Implementation projects are the first to be delayed in uncertainty. FY2025 already showed -4% services decline. Tariff uncertainty could push FY2026 services negative again.
- **Counter-argument:** MANH's cloud subscription revenue is contracted and recurring (~37%). But NEW bookings (which drive future growth) are discretionary and vulnerable.
- **Impact:** If Q1 2026 shows bookings deceleration attributable to tariff uncertainty, the 5-7% growth guide becomes optimistic, not conservative.

### 5. Rising Short Interest as Informed Signal (UPDATED -- stronger than R2)

- **Severity: MODERATE**
- **Prior R2:** Short interest 4.7% of float, +12.4% MoM.
- **Update:** Short interest 5.7% of float, +19.4% MoM (Feb 27 data). Shares short increased from 2.1M to 2.5M. Days to cover: 2.3.
- **Interpretation:** This is a 21% increase in short interest in one month. Informed investors are actively building short positions. While 5.7% SI is not extreme, the ACCELERATION is the signal. Two consecutive months of double-digit SI increases suggest institutional conviction against the stock.
- **No short positions** in our FCA/AMF/CONSOB regulatory databases (US stock, not European-regulated shorts).
