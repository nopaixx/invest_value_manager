## Business Understanding

### Modelo de Negocio

SS&C Technologies provides mission-critical software and software-enabled services to the global financial services industry. Founded in 1986 by Bill Stone (CEO, 14% insider ownership), the company has executed 46+ acquisitions to build a comprehensive platform spanning:

1. **Fund Administration & Services (~55% of revenue):** End-to-end fund admin for hedge funds, PE, mutual funds. NAV calculation, investor reporting, compliance, transfer agency. Serves 18,000+ funds, $2.6T+ AUA. Extremely sticky -- switching fund admin providers is operationally risky and expensive.

2. **Software Licensing & Maintenance (~20%):** Portfolio management (Advent Geneva/APX), accounting (SunGard), compliance (Sylvan), healthcare (DomaniRx JV). Recurring license + maintenance contracts with 90%+ retention.

3. **Software-as-a-Service (~25%):** Cloud-hosted versions of their software suite. Growing as clients migrate from on-premise. Black Diamond wealth platform, Eze trading system.

**Revenue mix:** ~95%+ recurring (subscription + services + maintenance). Organic growth 4.8% FY2025. Total growth 6.6% including acquisitions.

**Key insight:** SS&C is NOT a pure SaaS company. It is a financial infrastructure company that provides software AND runs operations for financial institutions. This is why GM is 48% not 70%+ -- the services component is labor-intensive but generates massive switching costs.

### Unit Economics

- **Client retention:** ~95%+ (management-reported)
- **FCF Margin:** 23.0% (FY2025) -- expanding from 17.5% (FY2022)
- **OCF to Net Income:** 2.2x -- high quality earnings
- **Capex/Revenue:** 4.4-4.8% (guided 2026) -- relatively asset-light
- **Capital intensity:** Light-to-medium (software + services, minimal fixed assets)
- **LTV/CAC:** Not disclosed, but 95%+ retention on multi-year contracts with high switching costs implies very favorable economics

### Por Que Esta Barata

**Narrativa del mercado:**
1. **Leverage concern:** 2.8x net debt/EBITDA with $7.5B gross debt. Market fears refinancing risk in high-rate environment.
2. **Organic growth "only" 4.8%:** Seen as a slow-grower in tech. Market wants 15%+ organic.
3. **Acquisition-dependent model:** 46+ acquisitions raises integration risk concerns. Morningstar downgraded moat to "none" recently.
4. **Macro uncertainty:** Fund admin services tied to AUM levels and capital markets activity.
5. **SBC creep:** SBC/Revenue rising from 2.4% (2022) to 4.1% (2025).

**Mi contra-tesis:**
1. **Leverage is rapidly declining.** OCF $1.74B (FY2025, +26% YoY). Net leverage down from 3.6x to 2.8x. Company paid $360M+ debt in 2025. At current OCF trajectory, leverage reaches 2.0-2.5x by YE2026 -- well within comfortable territory. Debt is mostly Term Loan B at SOFR+200, not distressed pricing.
2. **Organic growth is accelerating.** Q4 organic growth 5.3% vs full year 4.8%. Calastone adds network effect (4,500 clients, $250B/month processed). AI products (AI Agents, DomaniRx) are new revenue vectors. 2026 guidance implies 5.1% organic at midpoint.
3. **Acquisition model creates moat, not risk.** 30 years of acquisition execution. Goodwill has NOT been impaired. Each acquisition adds product breadth + cross-sell. The resulting platform is nearly impossible to replicate organically. Bill Stone has done this for 40 years.
4. **Capital markets activity recovering.** Alternative fund AUM growing 8-10% annually. Regulatory complexity increasing globally (more compliance = more SS&C revenue).
5. **SBC is manageable.** 4.1% SBC/revenue is moderate for tech. Share count roughly flat (dilution offset by buybacks -- $340M authorized Q4 2025).
