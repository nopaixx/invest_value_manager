### 3. Domain Registration Growth Narrative Weakening

- **FA's Claim:** Domain base growing 2.6% YoY, .com "returned to growth" in 2025.
- **Evidencia en contra:** VeriSign management themselves acknowledged a structural shift: "The post-pandemic boom in website creation has officially ended." Entrepreneurs are launching directly on TikTok, Instagram, or Shopify subdomains rather than buying a .com domain. The stock crashed 8.1% on this disclosure.
  - New gTLDs grew 21% YoY vs .com's 2.6% -- but gTLDs have only ~32% renewal rates ("churn and burn" dynamic).
  - ICANN's new gTLD round (2026) will introduce hundreds of additional extensions, further fragmenting the market.
  - The domain industry itself describes .com's growth as "plateaued relative to the broader market."
  - Web3 blockchain domains (.crypto, .eth) remain fringe but represent conceptual alternative to ICANN-controlled DNS.
- **Severidad:** **MODERATE** -- .com is not dying, but 2.6% volume growth is at the low end of management's 1.5-3.5% guidance range. Secular headwinds (social media as website substitute, AI-generated web presence) are real and growing.

## Independent Bear-Case Valuation

**Method: DDM (Dividend Discount Model) -- different from FA's OEY/EV-EBIT**

VeriSign as a "regulated monopoly" is best compared to utility-like businesses that return cash via dividends and buybacks.

- Current dividend: $3.27/share (1.4% yield)
- Dividend growth: 5.2% (last increase, Feb 2026)
- Buyback yield: ~2.5% (share count declining ~2.5%/yr)
- Total shareholder yield: ~3.9%
- Ke: 8.4% (per thesis WACC components)
- DDM FV = D1 / (Ke - g) = $3.44 / (8.4% - 5.2%) = $107/share (pure dividend)

This is absurdly low because VRSN pays out only 26% of FCF as dividend. The rest goes to buybacks.

**Adjusted Total Return model:**
- Total shareholder return = FCF yield (4.4% at $241) + revenue growth (5-7%) + margin expansion (0-1%)
- Bear total return: 4.4% + 5% + 0% = 9.4%
- To justify $241 at 8.4% Ke: 9.4% > 8.4%, so stock is very slightly above Ke -- nearly fairly valued.

**EV/EBIT bear case:**
- Normalized EBIT: $1,121M (FY2025 actual)
- Bear multiple: 17x (below current 20.5x, reflecting slow growth + political risk)
- Bear EV: $19.1B
- Net debt: $1.2B
- Bear equity: $17.9B / 92.5M shares = **$193/share**
