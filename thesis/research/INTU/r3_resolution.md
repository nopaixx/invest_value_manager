### 2. SaaSpocalypse exposure (DA HIGH) — PARTIALLY ACCEPTED
QuickBooks per-seat pricing IS exposed to enterprise consolidation/AI headcount reduction. But SMB (Intuit's core) is LESS vulnerable than enterprise — small businesses don't fire their accountant because of AI. Growth 8%→7% adjustment.

### 3. Credit Karma concentration risk (DA HIGH) — ACCEPTED
Credit Karma revenue declined in rate-sensitive environment. Acknowledged as cyclical weakness. Not structural but adds earnings volatility.

### 4. QS Tier A → Tier B (DA recommendation) — ACCEPTED
QS 72 Tier B is correct. SBC dilution + Credit Karma cyclicality + growth deceleration = not Tier A compounder quality. MoS requirement increases to 20-25%.
