## Business Understanding

### Model of Business

**Problem solved:** Neurocrine develops treatments for under-addressed neurological, psychiatric, and endocrine disorders. Two commercial products:

1. **INGREZZA (valbenazine)** — First and only FDA-approved treatment specifically for tardive dyskinesia (TD), a debilitating involuntary movement disorder caused by long-term antipsychotic use. ~800,000 diagnosed TD patients in the US; only ~25% treated. INGREZZA commands ~60% market share in a duopoly with Teva's Austedo (~40%).

2. **CRENESSITY (crinecerfont)** — First-in-class treatment for classic congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH), a rare genetic disorder. First new treatment in 70 years. Orphan Drug designation (7-year market exclusivity). $301M revenue in first year (launched mid-2025), already reaching ~10% of the classic CAH population.

**Revenue structure:** 100% product revenue. ~88% INGREZZA, ~12% CRENESSITY (FY2025). Recurring/refill prescriptions = high predictability. Outsourced manufacturing = asset-light.

### Unit Economics

- Gross margin: 98.2% (among the highest in all pharma — pure drug economics, outsourced manufacturing)
- Operating margin: 22.3% (expanding from 16.7% in 2022, with room to grow as CRENESSITY scales)
- FCF margin: 26.2% (up from 21.7% in 2022 — consistent improvement)
- R&D spend: ~30% of revenue (high — reflects active Phase 3 pipeline)
- Capital intensity: Asset-light (outsourced manufacturing, ~600 person sales force)

### Why It Is Cheap

**Current price $131.60 vs 52wH $160.18 (-18%). P/E 28x trailing, ~22x forward FY2026E.**

The market's concerns:

1. **DOJ civil investigative demand (Aug 2025) + FDA warning letter (Sep 2025)** regarding INGREZZA marketing practices. This is the PRIMARY overhang. The market fears fines, restricted marketing, or worse. My counter-thesis: DOJ CIDs in pharma are common and typically resolve with consent decrees or modest fines ($50-200M range for marketing violations). The warning letter is about promotional materials, not product safety. INGREZZA's clinical data is robust (57.8% remission at 24 weeks). Risk is manageable but real — included in bear case.

2. **INGREZZA price erosion** — guided ~4-5% annual price decline to maintain formulary access. The market fears this accelerates, especially if INGREZZA enters IRA Medicare negotiation (possible 2027-2028). Counter: volume growth (double-digit) more than offsets pricing. Penetration is only ~25% of diagnosed patients. TAM expanding as awareness grows.

3. **Valbenazine generic risk** — patent protection extends to mid-2030s (13 years). Generic risk is NOT imminent. The Phase 2 trial of NBI-1065890 (next-gen VMAT2) is a lifecycle management strategy.

4. **Pipeline binary risk** — osavampator (MDD) and direclidine (schizophrenia) Phase 3 data in 2027. If both fail, growth post-INGREZZA peak is limited. Counter: CRENESSITY is already scaling ($301M year one) and is pipeline-independent.

5. **Macro/risk-off** — broader pharma selloff in crisis environment. NBIX actually defensive (neuropsychiatry = non-cyclical demand).

**Value Trap Checklist: 0/10 factors present.** No secular decline, no disruption, no management destruction, no balance deterioration, no insider selling red flags (selling is routine/10b5-1 pattern likely), no dividend cuts, no market share loss, ROIC>WACC consistently, FCF always positive, minimal goodwill.
