## DA Challenges — Disposition

### 1. DOJ Investigation Scope (HIGH) — **ACCEPTED**

DA is right that "manageable" undersells this. Dual CID + FDA warning letter in 2 months with record FCA enforcement ($6.8B FY2025) is not routine. Comparable settlements: $60M-$323M. But NBIX's revenue ($2.9B) and INGREZZA concentration (88%) mean even a moderate consent decree restricting promotional activity could reduce new patient starts 10-15%.

**Resolution:** Bear case DOJ: $300-400M fine + 6-12 months restricted promotion. Model new patient acquisition -10% in affected period. Bear FV revised from thesis $109 to **$100**.

### 2. QS +4 Adjustment / Tier Classification (MODERATE) — **ACCEPTED**

DA correctly argues: being #1 in a duopoly with zero switching costs (doctor swaps INGREZZA → Austedo trivially) doesn't merit 8/8 market position. MELI has network effects. TW has regulatory moats. NBIX has... a slightly better side-effect profile. That's not a moat in the market-position sense.

**Resolution:** QS 74-75 = Tier B. Market position 5/8 (not 8/8). Net adjustment +1 (was +4). Tier B MoS requirement: 20-25%. At $131.60 vs FV $155, MoS was 15.1% = INSUFFICIENT for Tier B. This alone requires entry revision.

### 3. CRENESSITY Peak Sales (MODERATE) — **PARTIALLY ACCEPTED**

Atumelnant Phase 2 at 88% efficacy is a real competitive threat 3-4 years out. But CRENESSITY has orphan exclusivity (7 years from Sep 2024 = Sep 2031) and first-mover advantage in a tiny market (20K US patients). Peak sales should be haircut but not gutted.

**Resolution:** CRENESSITY peak US sales: $700M (was thesis implied ~$1B+). 2027 estimate: $500M (was $700M). Haircut 15-20% per DA recommendation. Still meaningful revenue diversification away from INGREZZA.

### 4. INGREZZA Share Erosion (MODERATE) — **PARTIALLY ACCEPTED**

Austedo XR (once-daily, IRA-priced) is a real competitive threat. Model gradual erosion 60% → 55% by 2028. But INGREZZA's patent wall to 2038 prevents generic entry — the erosion is branded competition, not cliff.

**Resolution:** INGREZZA 2027 revenue: $2.9B (was $3.0B). 2028: $3.0B (was $3.2B). Modest 3-5% haircut from Austedo XR pressure.

### 5. FV Methodology Override (MODERATE) — **ACCEPTED**

DA correctly identified the three-step scenario blending with manual "round up." The anti-bullish protocol should produce the FV, not be overridden.

**Resolution:** Clean recalculation below.

### 6. CEO Transition (LOW) — **DISMISSED**

Internal succession, 23-year veteran, board continuity. Non-issue. DA agrees.
