## Resumen Ejecutivo

The FA thesis is well-constructed and correctly identifies ROL as a Tier A quality compounder with a WIDE moat. The WATCHLIST verdict at $36 entry is defensible. My challenges center on three areas: (1) a MAJOR insider sale and secondary offering ($1.14B, 20M shares by the Rollins family trust) that the thesis did NOT capture, which represents a structural shift in family ownership and alignment, (2) the persistent premium multiple question -- ROL has NEVER traded below 30x P/E in the last decade, raising the question of whether my/FA's 28-33x bear/base P/E assumptions are achievable or structural misunderstanding of this stock's scarcity premium, and (3) goodwill at 43.8% of assets and rising from aggressive M&A ($310M FY2025 + Fox $350M + Saela) creates an under-appreciated acquisition integration risk. The thesis FV of $36 is reasonable if multiples compress, but the stock may NEVER reach $36 absent a severe market-wide correction.

## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. "38.3% family ownership = strong long-term alignment"

- **Evidencia en contra:** THIS IS THE MOST CRITICAL FINDING. The R. Randall Rollins Voting Trust executed a SECONDARY OFFERING selling 20 MILLION shares at $57.50/share, totaling approximately $1.14 BILLION. This is a massive ownership reduction -- ~4.2% of shares outstanding in a single transaction. Additionally, Executive Chairman Emeritus Gary Rollins sold $1.2M worth of stock at $63.26/share (the biggest individual insider sale in the last year). The thesis noted "net shares purchased is -59.2M over tracking period" and "sustained net selling pattern" but did NOT capture the $1.14B secondary offering.
- **Implications:** (a) The family is monetizing at premium valuations -- they believe the stock is fully priced, (b) The 365-day lock-up suggests this was an orderly, planned reduction -- not panic selling, (c) Family ownership has dropped from ~40%+ to potentially 34-36% range after this offering, (d) Institutional float increased meaningfully, which could increase volatility on any future earnings miss.
- **The thesis characterizes insider selling as "routine liquidations" -- a $1.14B secondary offering is NOT routine. It is the largest family monetization event in Rollins' history.**
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** The committee must assess whether this represents: (a) simple estate planning/diversification (benign, given 60+ year family holding), or (b) a signal that the family believes the stock is overvalued and is reducing exposure. Either way, the "38.3% family ownership = alignment" pillar is weakened. The thesis should document actual current family ownership post-offering.

### 2. "50.5x P/E cannot be sustained indefinitely"

- **Evidencia en contra -- BOTH DIRECTIONS:**
  - **FOR the thesis:** At 50.5x P/E on 7-8% organic growth + 3% M&A, the stock is priced as if it were a 15-20% grower. This is objectively expensive. Alpha Spread estimates intrinsic value at $33 (overvalued 41%). My DCF tool base case: $21.77 (severely overvalued).
  - **AGAINST the thesis:** ROL has NEVER traded below 30x P/E in the last decade. Since 2016, the P/E has been consistently in the 40-55x range. The thesis bear case assumes 28x P/E, which has literally never happened in modern history. If I use 35x P/E as "bear" (the lowest it has actually traded at), the bear FV rises from $31.36 to $39.20 -- significantly higher than my anti-bias result.
  - The "structural premium" argument is not baseless: (a) ROL is the ONLY pure-play publicly-traded pest control company of quality, (b) Rentokil is struggling with Terminix integration, (c) AI disruption risk is near-zero, (d) the business is genuinely non-cyclical. Scarcity premiums can persist for DECADES.
- **Severidad:** MODERATE (challenges the thesis in BOTH directions -- FV could be too low OR too high)
- **Resolucion sugerida:** The committee should acknowledge the structural premium but not anchor to it. Use 32-35x P/E as realistic bear/base (not 28x/33x), which gives FV range of $36-$42. The thesis's $36 is at the low end of this adjusted range.

### 3. "Goodwill at 43.8% of total assets is not dangerous yet"

- **Evidencia en contra:** Goodwill has risen from 39.9% (2022) to 43.8% (2025), a trajectory of ~1pp per year. FY2025 acquisitions totaled $310M, plus the Fox acquisition (~$350M) and Saela. At this pace, goodwill will exceed 50% of assets by 2028-2029. The thesis KC #4 sets 60% as the kill threshold -- but this is very generous. Most quality-conscious investors would flag 50%+ goodwill.
- **Integration risk is real:** M&A volumes +27.6% YoY in 2024 means ROL is acquiring at an ACCELERATING pace. Each acquisition adds goodwill and integration complexity. The Fox and Saela acquisitions are LARGER than typical tuck-ins, requiring more integration effort. If any major acquisition write-down occurs, it would be the first in ROL's history and would shock the market.
- **Deferred revenue growth deceleration:** 9.0% -> 4.9% -> 3.8% vs 11% revenue growth. This COULD indicate that M&A-acquired customers have shorter contract terms or lower retention than organic customers -- a signal that acquisition quality may be declining as ROL fishes in a more-picked-over pond.
- **Severidad:** MODERATE
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Lower the goodwill KC from 60% to 55% of total assets. Add a KC for "major acquisition write-down >$200M" (lower than thesis's $500M, which is too lenient for a company with $650M FCF).

### 4. "E[CAGR] at market is too low to justify purchase"

- **Evidencia en contra:** The FA's OEY analysis produces a spread of only +3.95pp vs WACC at current price -- the WEAKEST of any Tier A evaluated. The FA correctly identifies this. However, the FA's bear OEY FV targets a 7pp spread, which is MORE conservative than any Tier A purchase in our history (ADBE: 7.5pp, NVO: 11pp, LULU: 9pp -- all at PURCHASE, not fair value targets). A 5pp spread target for FV assessment would give $38.63 (the FA's own base OEY FV). The final $36 FV is between the mechanical $32 and the base $38.63, which is reasonable but leans conservative.
- **Severidad:** LOW
- **Resolucion sugerida:** FV of $36 is within the reasonable range. No adjustment needed.

### 5. "Climate tailwind extending pest seasons"

- **Evidencia en contra:** Climate change effects on pest control are a MILD tailwind, not a transformational one. The thesis correctly notes this but assigns it as a bull case driver. Counter-evidence: (a) Q4 2025 saw weather HEADWINDS (cold/wet conditions reduced pest activity), demonstrating that climate effects are VARIABLE, not unidirectional, (b) Extended pest seasons also increase labor costs (more service visits, more overtime), (c) Climate change could shift pest populations in ways that require different expertise (tropical pests moving north), creating NEW competitor opportunities in niche segments.
- **Severidad:** LOW
- **Resolucion sugerida:** No adjustment needed. Climate is a minor factor either way.

## META-REFLECTION

### Dudas/Incertidumbres
- The "structural premium" question for ROL is genuinely unresolvable with available data. If ROL ALWAYS trades at 40-55x P/E, then my bear FV of $41 (35x P/E) may itself be too bearish. But this is circular reasoning -- "it's always been expensive so it will always be expensive" is exactly the kind of narrative that breaks during regime changes.
- The $1.14B family secondary offering could be interpreted as either bearish (family thinks stock is overvalued) or neutral (estate planning, 60+ years of concentrated wealth, diversification is rational). I lean bearish given the timing at premium valuations, but I cannot prove intent.
- The FA adjusted the mechanical anti-bias FV from $32 to $36 with reasonable justification (7pp OEY spread too aggressive for Tier A with WIDE moat + SM convergence). This adjustment is within acceptable range but adds subjectivity.
