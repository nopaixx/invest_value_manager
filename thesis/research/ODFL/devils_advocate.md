## Resumen Ejecutivo

The FA thesis correctly identifies ODFL as a best-in-class LTL operator and, critically, correctly concludes the stock is OVERVALUED at $195 (now $183.92). The thesis verdict is WATCHLIST at $135-140 entry. My primary challenge is NOT to the verdict (which I agree with) but to the **Fair Value estimate of $135**, which I believe is STILL too generous given (1) the freight recession is deeper and longer than the thesis assumed, (2) normalized earnings anchoring to 2022-2024 averages includes a peak year inflated by Yellow's closure, and (3) the thesis assigns a 25x P/E multiple to normalized earnings of a cyclical business still in trough. My independent bear-case FV is $95-110.

## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. "Normalized EBIT of $1.645B is representative of mid-cycle earnings"

- **Evidencia en contra:** The 2022-2024 average is distorted upward by FY2022 ($1.899B EBIT), which was an anomalous year: post-COVID freight boom PLUS Yellow Corporation closure (July 2023) that handed ODFL a one-time capacity windfall. Yellow held ~12% LTL market share. Some of that share gain is permanent, but the pricing power it enabled was partly one-time. A more conservative mid-cycle EBIT using 2023-2024 average would be ~$1.518B. Using 2019-2024 through-cycle (pre-COVID through current) would be lower still.
- **Q1 2026 data confirms continued weakness:** Revenue per day -3.3% YoY in February 2026, LTL tons per day -6.8%, LTL shipments per day -7.0%. The freight recession is now in its 4th year -- this is not a normal trough.
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Use $1.45-1.50B as normalized EBIT (2023-2024 average adjusted for Yellow windfall dissipation). This alone reduces EV/EBIT FV from $119 to $105-110.

### 2. "25x P/E is conservative for a WIDE moat compounder"

- **Evidencia en contra:** 25x P/E on normalized EPS of a cyclical industrial is NOT conservative -- it is at the high end of what quality industrials command at mid-cycle. The thesis acknowledges ODFL "historically traded 25-35x" but this history includes a period of secular market share gains from Yellow that may not repeat. Comparable quality industrials: Waste Management trades at 28x (less cyclical, utility-like), Illinois Tool Works at 22x, Parker Hannifin at 22x. ODFL's cyclicality should command a DISCOUNT to those, not a premium. Wolfe Research has an Underperform rating with PT $138 (now cut to $138 from $141).
- **Analyst consensus:** Average PT $182, but consensus is HOLD, and price is trading AT consensus -- meaning no upside from the sell-side's own estimates.
- **Severidad:** MODERATE
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Use 22-23x normalized P/E (quality premium but cyclical discount). This gives $122-128 on $5.55 normalized EPS.

### 3. "Freight cycle expected to turn in H2 2026"

- **Evidencia en contra:** Industry experts describe the current freight environment as "the softest in a generation or longer." The ISM Manufacturing PMI contracted for 10 consecutive months through December 2025. February 2026 data shows tonnage STILL declining at -6.8% YoY. C.H. Robinson and industry sources project LTL tonnage to stay negative YoY for H1 2026, with only modest recovery in H2. Tariff uncertainty, rising oil prices ($97-99 Brent), and potential recession risk from Hormuz crisis add NEW headwinds not present when the thesis was written. The thesis base case assumes revenue recovery to $6.2B by FY2027 -- this requires a sharp reversal that current data does not support.
- **Macro headwinds post-thesis:** Oil at $97-99 (vs ~$85 at thesis date), Hormuz crisis ongoing, tariff uncertainty, FOMC stagflation dilemma. These are net NEGATIVE for freight demand.
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Assign higher probability to bear case (30% vs 20%). Extend the trough assumption through most of 2026 rather than assuming H2 inflection.

### 4. "ODFL gains market share through cycles"

- **Evidencia en contra:** While true historically, the Yellow closure in July 2023 was a ONE-TIME event that redistributed ~12% of LTL market share. ODFL was a major beneficiary. Going forward, share gains must come from taking customers from FedEx Freight, XPO, Saia, and ABF -- all of which are investing in capacity and service quality. Saia is aggressively expanding its service center network. XPO has improved its operating ratio significantly. The competitive landscape post-Yellow is TIGHTER, not easier. ODFL's February 2026 data (-7.0% shipments/day) suggests market share gains are NOT accelerating in the current environment.
- **Severidad:** MODERATE
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Model 0-1pp share gains per year (not the 1-2pp embedded in thesis growth assumptions). This is more conservative but realistic given competitive dynamics.

### 5. "Short interest at 9.1% reflects bears who see valuation disconnect"

- **Evidencia en contra:** The thesis correctly identified this but UNDERWEIGHTED it. 9.1% short interest with 8.1 days to cover on a $38.5B market cap company is significant. This is not retail shorts -- these are institutional bets. The short thesis is straightforward: cyclical stock trading at peak multiples during trough earnings, with no evidence of imminent recovery. The February tonnage data validated the short thesis. Partners Group (institutional) has been REDUCING holdings. The smart money signal here is bearish.
- **Severidad:** MODERATE
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Weight the short interest signal more heavily in the risk assessment. Consider it a TIMING indicator that the market has already priced the recovery.

## META-REFLECTION

### Dudas/Incertidumbres
- The "right" normalized EBIT for a cyclical business during a 4-year recession is genuinely uncertain. My $1.45B may be too conservative if Yellow share gains prove more durable than I assume. The FA's $1.645B may be too optimistic if the post-Yellow competitive landscape is tighter.
- ODFL's pricing power (+4.9% yields into a -6.8% volume decline) is genuinely extraordinary and may justify a higher multiple than my bear case allows. Few cyclicals can raise prices 5% while losing 7% of volume.
- I could not find concrete evidence that the Yellow share gains are reversing. If they are permanent, my normalized earnings haircut is excessive.
