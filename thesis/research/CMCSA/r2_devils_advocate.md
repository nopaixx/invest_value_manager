### 8. Wireless Growth: Revenue Driver or Margin Diluter?

**R1 assumption:** Wireless at $4.5B revenue growing 30%/yr is a "convergence play."

**Evidence requiring nuance:**

- Xfinity Mobile is an MVNO on Verizon's network. MVNO economics are structurally inferior to MNO (network owners). Xfinity Mobile pays wholesale rates to Verizon, limiting margin expansion.
- The "free wireless lines" promotional strategy (2025-2026) means much of the 9.3M subscriber base is on free/promotional plans. Converting free-to-paid is "mission critical" per industry analyst Verdict.
- Wireless EBITDA contribution is not separately disclosed. Given MVNO economics + free lines, EBITDA margin is likely 5-15% (vs 40%+ for broadband).
- At $4.5B revenue with 10% EBITDA margin: $450M EBITDA contribution. Growing to $7.7B by 2028E at 15% margin: $1.15B. Meaningful but NOT transformative for a $37B EBITDA company.
- The R1 values wireless at 3.0x revenue ($13.5B) -- which seems aggressive for an MVNO that has never reported standalone profitability.
- Through a new partnership with Charter, Comcast secured T-Mobile 5G access for 2026 -- this INCREASES wholesale costs (paying T-Mobile now too) while potentially improving network quality.

**The real value of wireless is CHURN REDUCTION**, not revenue. Bundled broadband + wireless customers churn ~50% less. This is a defensive tool, not a growth engine.

**Severidad: LOW-MODERATE**

### Interpretacion

**MODERATE-STRONG COUNTER:** The thesis has legitimate structural appeal (theme parks ARE valuable, post-Versant IS cleaner, buybacks at 7.5x ARE accretive) but OVERSTATES the margin of safety through multiple compounding optimistic assumptions. The most damaging findings are:

1. **No informational edge** -- post-DA FV converges to consensus. We are not seeing something the market cannot see.
2. **FCF anchor is wrong** -- historical 3yr avg is $12.7B, not $15B. The R1 normalizes DOWN from $19.2B but should normalize UP from $12.7B.
3. **Parks 14x is the single most aggressive SoP assumption** -- above Disney, above industry, for a still-ramping smaller operator.
4. **Correlated risks** -- broadband/ARPU/FWA are three expressions of one structural problem. Tail risk is fatter than the thesis models.

### What Survives

- Post-Versant RemainCo IS genuinely cleaner.
- Theme parks ARE underappreciated at consolidated multiples. But at 11-12x (not 14x).
- Wireless growth IS real and provides broadband churn reduction.
- Dividend 4.3% at 18% payout IS well-covered and provides income floor.
- Buybacks at 7-8x P/FCF ARE highly accretive.
- The stock IS cheap on most metrics. The question is: cheap ENOUGH for the risks?

- **DOCSIS 4.0 wildcard.** I could not find reliable deployment data or performance benchmarks. Comcast says roughly half its footprint will have mid-split by end of 2026, with DOCSIS 4.0 to follow. If it delivers multi-gig speeds cheaply, the broadband bear case weakens materially. This is the biggest upside risk to my analysis.
- **Post-Versant EBITDA estimation.** FY2025 consolidated results include Versant through Jan 2, 2026. My estimates of "clean" RemainCo EBITDA ($33-35B) are derived, not reported. Q1 2026 will provide the first actual data point.
- **Parks recession probability.** With tariff uncertainty and recession probability at 20-40%, the theme parks sensitivity ($3.8B -> $2.3B in recession) could be more material than I've weighted. I did not assign explicit recession probability in my scenarios.
- **Peacock strategic optionality.** If Comcast successfully merges Peacock with another streamer, the combined entity could reach profitability faster. My $2.5B weighted value may be too low if strategic options materialize. But "buy or sell" from a former exec is speculation, not certainty.

- I do not have access to Comcast's Q1 2026 earnings data (late April). Broadband trajectory is the single most important variable and I can only extrapolate from Q4 2025.
- Theme park comparable multiples are limited because Universal is not a public pure-play. My 11-12x is triangulated from United Parks, Disney, and Dealstream benchmarks.
- MVNO economics for Xfinity Mobile are not publicly disclosed at the segment level. My 10-15% EBITDA margin estimate is directional.
- I relied on narrative_checker for FCF history, which confirmed $12.5-13.0B range for 2022-2024 but does not break down sub-components.

### Sugerencias para el Sistema
- **FCF normalization should be MANDATORY** when FCF margin deviates >200bps from 3yr trailing average. The DCF tool and quality_scorer both use headline FCF, which was dangerously misleading ($19.2B vs ~$14B sustainable).

- **Governance discount protocol:** For dual-class with >25% voting / <5% economic, minimum 13-15% discount. Currently applied ad hoc.
