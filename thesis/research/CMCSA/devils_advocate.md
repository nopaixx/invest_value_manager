# Devil's Advocate: CMCSA (Comcast Corporation)

## Fecha: 2026-03-21

## Resumen Ejecutivo

The R1 thesis (FV $39.37, later corrected to $33 post-R3) presents CMCSA as an undervalued conglomerate with a hidden parks gem and a broadband cash cow under temporary siege. After independent investigation, I find the thesis survives scrutiny on parks and capital returns but has material weaknesses in three areas: (1) broadband erosion is accelerating, not stabilizing -- "Cord Cutting 2.0" is a structural trend with FWA capacity doubling, (2) Peacock losses WIDENED in Q4 with $27B in NBA commitments and no breakeven timeline, and (3) our edge over consensus is negligible -- post-R3 FV of $33 is within the consensus PT range of $27-$37. The SO at $28 provides adequate downside protection but the risk-reward at current $29.02 is marginal.

**NOTE: Two prior DAs already exist (r2_devils_advocate.md and counter_analysis.md). This is the FORMAL devils_advocate.md per naming contract (S276). The R3 resolution already incorporated those two prior DAs. This DA serves as the UPDATED assessment given 3 weeks of new data (price decline from $30.93 to $29.02, new broadband data, Versant trading data).**

## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. Broadband Erosion Will "Stabilize" in H2 2026

- **FA Assumption:** Q3 loss of 104K was "best of year," suggesting stabilization. DOCSIS 4.0 + wireless bundling will arrest decline. Recovery expected H2 2026 as pricing migration laps.
- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - Q4 loss WORSENED to 181K (74% worse than Q3's "stabilization"). The stabilization narrative was premature.
  - "Cord Cutting 2.0" is accelerating: Comcast + Spectrum lost 1.11M internet customers in FY2025 and are tracking to lose 1M+ again in 2026 ([CordCuttersNews](https://cordcuttersnews.com/comcast-spectrum-are-in-trouble-as-over-1-million-customers-canceled-their-internet-in-2025-as-2026-becomes-the-year-of-cord-cutting-2-0/)).
  - FWA capacity has DOUBLED -- Big 3 (T-Mobile/Verizon/AT&T) now have room for 32M FWA customers vs current 12% penetration. FWA priced at $35-50 vs cable ARPU $74+ ([LightReading](https://www.lightreading.com/cable-technology/comcast-faces-arpu-pressure-as-broadband-losses-start-to-stabilize)).
  - Amazon home internet launching widely in 2026, Starlink improving for rural.
  - AT&T fiber targeting 40M locations by year-end 2026 (from ~29M in 2025).
  - New Street Research: "no return to positive broadband subscriber adds this decade."
  - Goldman downgraded to Neutral ($30 PT), BNP downgraded to Underperform ($27 PT) -- both citing broadband specifically.
  - Analysts forecast ARPU turning NEGATIVE in Q1-Q2 2026, with EBITDA pressure peaking Q2 2026 ([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/analyst-stock-ratings/reiteration/26/01/50275500/comcast-broadband-losses-deepen-as-competition-intensifies)).
- **Severidad:** **HIGH**
- **Resolucion sugerida:** The R3 already tightened broadband multiple from 5.5x to 5.0x and KC from 300K to 250K losses/quarter. These corrections were appropriate. However, the thesis still assumes H2 2026 stabilization. The committee should gate execution on Q1 2026 broadband data: if losses >200K AND ARPU negative, the bear case is becoming the base case.

### 2. Normalized FCF of $14-15B is Achievable

- **FA Assumption:** FY2025 $19.2B headline FCF is inflated by ~$4B of one-timers. Normalized sustainable FCF is $14.5-16B (R1) / $14B (R3).
- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - Management explicitly guided "significantly lower" 2026 FCF -- they did NOT say $14-15B. They said "significantly lower than $19.2B."
  - 2026 is "largest broadband investment year" -- capex rising materially for DOCSIS 4.0 rollout + network upgrades.
  - Historical 3yr FCF average is $12.7B (per DA2 in R3). The $14B R3 number is still ABOVE historical average.
  - Broadband ARPU turning negative + sub losses = DOUBLE headwind on the EBITDA that generates most FCF.
  - Peacock NBA payments ($2.5B/yr) started Q4 2025 and are a NEW cash drain not in historical comparisons.
  - If sustainable FCF is $13B (not $14B), then at 8.5x P/FCF = $110.5B equity = $30.80/share. At current $29.02, virtually NO upside.
- **Severidad:** **MODERATE**
- **Resolucion sugerida:** The $14B R3 figure is reasonable but optimistic. A $13-14B range is more honest. The committee should use $13.5B as the conservative anchor and $14.5B as base.

### 3. Theme Parks at 12x EBITDA is Justified

- **FA Assumption:** Universal theme parks deserve 12x EBITDA (R3 corrected from R1's 14x). Only 2 US operators at scale (Disney + Universal). Epic Universe is generational asset.
- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - Epic Universe had a genuine positive impact: Q4 2025 revenue +22%, EBITDA crossed $1B for first time ([ThemeParkInsider](https://www.themeparkinsider.com/flume/202601/12285/)). This supports the FA's thesis. However:
  - Epic Universe is still NOT at full capacity -- execs say full ops expected by late 2026 ([AttractionsMagazine](https://attractionsmagazine.com/universal-epic-universe-capacity-future-2026/)). The $3.8B 2026E EBITDA includes ramp assumptions.
  - United Parks (Six Flags/Cedar Fair) trades at ~10x. Disney Parks at ~12-14x. 12x for Universal is at the HIGH end of the relevant peer range.
  - Theme parks are cyclically sensitive -- a recession would compress EBITDA 20-30% AND compress multiples from 12x to 8-10x. Combined effect: parks value from $42B to $22-28B.
  - Parks are only ~20% of total CMCSA EBITDA. Even if parks are worth $42B, broadband decline overwhelms.
- **Severidad:** **LOW**
- **Resolucion sugerida:** The R3 correction to 12x was well-calibrated. Parks thesis is the STRONGEST part of the CMCSA case. No further correction needed unless recession materializes.

### 4. Our Edge Over Consensus is Meaningful

- **FA Assumption:** "SoP lens on parks" provides edge. FCF normalization discipline provides edge. Thesis FV $39.37 (R1) / $33 (R3) is above consensus.
- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - Post-R3 FV of $33 is WITHIN the consensus PT range ($27-$37, mean $34.90). Our FV is BELOW the consensus mean. We have NO positive informational edge.
  - The SoP lens on parks is widely shared -- every sell-side analyst does SoP on CMCSA. This is NOT a differentiated insight.
  - Goldman ($30), BNP ($27) are BELOW our bear case ($26.26). Two sophisticated institutional analysts are MORE bearish than our worst case.
  - 53% of analysts rate HOLD. This is a contested, well-covered stock with 26+ analysts. Finding alpha here requires knowing something specific that 26 analysts do not.
  - Dodge & Cox (3.1% ownership) is the strongest bull signal, but they are deep-value generalists with long holding periods -- they could hold through 30% further decline.
- **Severidad:** **HIGH**
- **Resolucion sugerida:** This is the fundamental challenge. Error #49 (anchoring to consensus) is in effect -- our FV IS the consensus. The committee should acknowledge thin edge and require LARGER MoS (25%+ from post-R3 FV of $33 = entry at $24.75) or gate on specific data (Q1 broadband + FCF normalization).

### 5. Peacock Will Show "Meaningful Improvement" in 2026

- **FA Assumption:** Peacock losses will narrow. 44M subs at scale. Ad revenue growing.
- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - Q4 2025 Peacock loss was $552M, WIDER than Q4 2024's $372M (+48% deterioration) ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/comcast-q4-cable-revenue-wider-peacock-losses-1236644283/)).
  - NBA deal cost $2.7B/yr for 11 years -- this is a NEW $2.5B+ annual cash drain that did not exist before Q4 2025.
  - Cumulative Peacock losses exceed $10B with no management breakeven guidance.
  - Netflix and Amazon continue outspending on content, creating a "prisoner's dilemma" where opting out means irrelevance.
  - Some analysts suggest Peacock's losses could push Comcast toward merger or acquisition ([AwfulAnnouncing](https://awfulannouncing.com/peacock/losses-comcast-next-move-merger-acquisition-nbc.html)).
  - CFO Armstrong's claim that Peacock has "reached meaningful scale" is management optimism (Level 3 source -- opinion, not data).
- **Severidad:** **MODERATE**
- **Resolucion sugerida:** R3 correctly valued Peacock at $4B (0.75x revenue) vs R1's $8.1B (1.5x). This captures the risk. Peacock is a binary outcome -- the $4B valuation assumes option value only. No further correction needed.
