## Issue-by-Issue Resolution

### 1. Theme Parks Multiple: 14x → 12x

**FA:** 14x on $3.8B 2026E EBITDA = $53.2B
**DA1:** 11-12x. **DA2:** 10-11x. Industry benchmarks: 6-8x standard, 10x United Parks, 12-14x Disney.

**CIO Resolution: 12x on $3.5B = $42B**

Reasoning:
- Universal is the ONLY competitor to Disney at scale. This IS premium. 6-8x is standard parks, not elite duopoly.
- But Universal is smaller than Disney, less international, less diversified IP. 14x = Disney premium on a non-Disney asset.
- Epic Universe is genuine generational asset (not priced elsewhere), but still ramping — use $3.5B conservative 2026E, not $3.8B.
- 12x splits the difference: premium over industry (6-8x), discount to Disney (14x), aligned with United Parks (10x) + premium for duopoly + Epic.
- **Impact: -$11.2B EV = -$3.12/share vs FA**

### 2. FCF Normalization: $15.25B → $14.0B

**FA:** $14.5-16B midpoint $15.25B
**DA1:** $14.0B base. **DA2:** $13.5B (historical 3yr avg $12.7B).

**CIO Resolution: $14.0B**

Reasoning:
- Historical 3yr avg $12.7B is the ANCHOR. The FA needs to JUSTIFY why normalized is $15B, not the other way around.
- Parks EBITDA grew from $2.9B to $3.1B (+$200M). At ~60% flow-through to FCF: +$120M.
- Wireless growing 30%/yr but MVNO economics (paying Verizon wholesale) limit FCF contribution.
- Broadband ARPU turning negative + sub losses = FCF HEADWIND of $500M-1B/yr.
- Net: $12.7B + $0.8B parks + $0.5B wireless - $0.0B Peacock (still losing) - $0.5B broadband erosion = $13.5-14.5B. Midpoint $14.0B.
- Management guided "significantly lower" 2026 FCF. $14B is realistic, $15B is optimistic.

Reasoning:

- **Impact: -$5.3B EV difference vs FA (2pp × $265B gross EV)**

### 5. Peacock Valuation: $8.1B → $4B

**FA:** 1.5x revenue = $8.1B.
**DA1:** $0 base. **DA2:** 1.0x = $5.4B.

**CIO Resolution: $4.0B**

Reasoning:
- 44M paid subs at scale IS real. The subscriber base has value as distribution asset for NBCU content.
- BUT: $10B+ cumulative losses, Q4 loss WIDENED, no breakeven date, NBA $27B committed.
- $0 is too harsh — there's strategic value (retention tool, ad revenue growing, sports distribution).
- $8.1B is too generous — assigns positive value to a unit that may NEVER profit standalone.
- $4B = ~0.75x revenue. Reflects option value discounted for ongoing cash burn.
- **Impact: -$4.1B EV = -$1.14/share vs FA**

### 6. Broadband Multiple: 5.5x → 5.0x

**FA:** 5.5x EBITDA.
**Both DAs:** 4.5-5.0x.

**CIO Resolution: 5.0x**

Reasoning:
- Q4 loss worsened (181K vs 104K Q3). "Stabilization" narrative NOT confirmed.
- New Street: no return to positive adds this decade. FWA capacity doubled.
- But broadband STILL generates $18-20B EBITDA. Even declining, it's massive cash flow.
- Charter at 5.8x with similar challenges. 5.0x reflects the slightly worse trajectory.
- **Impact: -$8B EV = -$2.23/share vs FA**
