## Deep Dive on Top 3 Risks

### 1. AI Disruption (15% probability, HIGH impact)

**The bull case for AI disruption:**
- AI can generate a complete website in minutes (demonstrated by multiple tools)
- Social media pages + AI chatbots could replace the need for a traditional website
- "Do businesses even need websites?" is a legitimate long-term question
- AI coding tools (Cursor, Replit, Bolt) make it trivial to build custom web apps

**Why I think the risk is overstated for GoDaddy specifically:**
- GoDaddy's target market (micro-SMBs: pizza shops, hair salons, plumbers) is the LAST market that will DIY-code with AI. They want turnkey solutions
- The domain + email + hosting bundle is not threatened by AI website builders -- it's the infrastructure layer
- GoDaddy IS adapting: Airo drives 50% of new paid subscriptions. They are the AI play for their customer segment
- Even if website builder revenue goes to zero, the Core Platform ($3.1B/year) is unaffected
- Historical precedent: Wix and Squarespace have existed for 15+ years. They haven't killed GoDaddy because the target markets are different (prosumer/designer vs. micro-SMB)

**Kill condition trigger:** A&C segment revenue growth turns negative (currently +14%)

### 2. FCF Margin Sustainability (20% probability, HIGH impact)

**The concern:**
- FCF margin went from 22.5% to 31.8% in 3 years -- is this sustainable or peak margin?
- R&D/Revenue declining from 19.4% to 17.0% -- is this efficiency or underinvestment?
- Starboard-driven cost cuts may have a shelf life
- SBC at 6.4% of revenue is real dilution that partially offsets buybacks

**Analysis:**
- Operating margin expansion (12.6% to 23.0%) was achieved through genuine operating leverage, not just cost-cutting
- Capex/Depreciation of 0.2x means GoDaddy is spending almost nothing on capital -- this is sustainable for a software/platform business
- The key risk is that R&D cuts eventually hurt product competitiveness. Monitor: product NPS, Airo adoption rates, competitor feature parity
- Even if FCF margin reverts to 25% (a 680bps compression), FCF on $5.2B revenue = $1.3B -- still yielding 10.2% at current price

**Kill condition trigger:** FCF margin drops below 25% for 2 consecutive quarters

### 3. SMB Recession (35% probability, MEDIUM impact)

**The concern:**
- Oil at $92 WTI creates stagflationary pressure on US consumers and small businesses
- Small businesses are the first to cut discretionary spending
- GoDaddy's growth-driver segment (A&C, commerce, website builder) is more discretionary than domains

**Analysis:**
- Domain renewals ($10-15/year) are among the lowest-cost business expenses -- unlikely to be cut even in recession
- Website builder subscriptions ($10-25/month) are more vulnerable but still low absolute cost
- During COVID (the most severe SMB shock in decades), GoDaddy's revenue actually GREW (2020: +11.5%, 2021: +14.8%). Micro-SMBs went online during crisis, boosting demand
- The A&C deceleration may partly reflect SMB caution, but 14% growth in a challenging macro environment is still strong

**Kill condition trigger:** Not a kill condition per se, but monitor Core Platform for negative growth (would signal domain churn)

## Insider Alignment Risk (Structural)

**The facts:**
- Insider ownership: 0.7% (very low)
- CEO Aman Bhutani joined 2019 from Tripadvisor. Total comp ~$20M/year (mostly RSUs)
- CFO Mark McCaffrey joined 2022
- No founder involvement (GoDaddy founded by Bob Parsons, who exited long ago)
- Private equity legacy: Silver Lake + KKR were major holders, have largely exited

**Assessment:**
- This is a professional-manager-run company, not a founder-led one. Typical of ex-PE companies
- The 0.7% insider ownership means management's wealth is NOT significantly tied to share price performance
- Starboard (7.8%) provides external alignment/pressure, but Starboard's interest is financial engineering and value extraction, not long-term business building
- **This is the weakest aspect of the investment case.** In a quality compounder, you want management whose net worth rises and falls with the stock. Here, the CEO's wealth is in his annual RSU grants, not accumulated ownership

**Mitigation:** Starboard's 7.8% stake provides activist oversight. Institutional ownership at 104% means the shareholder base is engaged. But there is no substitution for genuine insider ownership alignment.

## Scenario-Specific Risk: Iran War / Oil Shock

**Direct impact:** Negligible. GoDaddy's business is US-domestic, digital, subscription-based, with no supply chain exposure to Middle East.

**Indirect impact:**
- SMB spending softens if oil stays >$90 for 3+ months
- Risk-off compresses multiples for all growth stocks
- Dollar strength (DXY stable at ~99) is neutral for USD-denominated business

**Assessment:** GDDY is among the least Iran/oil-sensitive stocks in the universe. The current selloff is primarily earnings-driven (guidance miss) compounded by general risk-off, not macro-specific.
