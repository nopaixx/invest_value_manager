## Business Understanding

### What Does Pegasystems Do?

Pegasystems provides a **unified low-code platform** that combines Business Process Management (BPM), Customer Relationship Management (CRM), and AI-powered decisioning for large enterprises. Think of it as the operating system for complex enterprise workflows.

**Problem solved:** Large enterprises (banks, insurers, telcos, government agencies) have complex, interconnected workflows that span departments and systems. Legacy systems create inefficiency, compliance risk, and inability to adapt. Pega's platform lets them automate, optimize, and modernize these processes without ripping and replacing everything.

**Pega's differentiation:**
1. **Workflow-anchored AI**: While competitors use LLM prompts for AI agents, Pega anchors agents in pre-validated workflows. Critical for regulated industries (banks, insurers, government) that need auditability.
2. **Blueprint product**: AI-powered application design tool creating 1,000+ app designs/week. 98% of opportunities involve replacing NON-Pega legacy systems -- dramatically expands TAM.
3. **Unified platform**: BPM + CRM + AI decisioning in one platform vs stitching together Salesforce + ServiceNow + UiPath.
4. **Customer sticky**: Deep integrations into mission-critical processes (loan origination, claims processing, KYC). Switching costs are VERY high.

**Moat Assessment: NARROW-leaning-WIDE (20/25)**
- Switching costs: 9/10 (enterprise implementations take 6-18 months, deeply embedded)
- Data/IP advantage: 4/5 (proprietary decisioning models, workflow libraries)
- Network effects: 2/5 (partner ecosystem growing but not self-reinforcing)
- Brand/position: 5/5 (Forrester Leader, trusted by 60+ of top 100 global banks)

### AI Strategy Assessment

**Pega GenAI Blueprint:** This is NOT marketing fluff. It's a product that generates $0 today but creates 1,000+ application designs per week. 2. **KC#2: Operating margin reverses below 10%** (currently 16.3%)
