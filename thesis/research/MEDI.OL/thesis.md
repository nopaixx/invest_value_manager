## Business Understanding

### Model de Negocio

**Problem solved:** During CABG (coronary artery bypass grafting) surgery, surgeons create bypass grafts but have no objective way to verify graft quality intraoperatively. Historically, 55% of procedures rely on finger palpation -- a subjective, unreliable method. Graft failure leads to repeat surgery, heart attacks, or death.

**Solution:** Medistim's MiraQ system combines Transit Time Flow Measurement (TTFM) with high-resolution ultrasound imaging in a single device. This gives surgeons objective, real-time data on graft quality DURING surgery, allowing immediate revision if needed. The REQUEST study showed 25% of patients had surgical procedure changes made using combined TTFM + imaging.

**Revenue model:**
- **~66% recurring** (flow probes = consumables, pay-per-use, service contracts)
- **~34% capital equipment** (MiraQ systems)
- **~16% third-party distribution** (Scandinavia only, lower margin)

**Revenue by segment (FY2025 est.):**
- Cardiac: ~65% of own products (~NOK 380M)
- Vascular: ~19% (~NOK 110M, growing fastest at +43% H1 2025)
- Third-party distribution: ~16% (~NOK 110M)

**Revenue by geography (FY2025):**
- Americas: ~33% (largest, +36% FY2025 growth)
- EMEA: ~35% (+8%)
- APAC: ~18% (+41%)
- Scandinavia distribution: ~14% (+2%)

### Unit Economics

- **Gross margin: 79.6%** (expanding -- 76.8% in 2021 to 79.6% in 2024, likely >80% in FY2025)
- **Operating margin: 28.0%** (FY2025, UP from 23.6% in 2024 -- recovery from investment year)
- **FCF margin: ~21%** (FY2024, likely improving with revenue scale)
- **ROIC: 34.2%** (declining from 47.8% peak but still exceptional)
- **ROIC-WACC spread: +24.0pp** (massive value creation)
- **Capital intensity: Asset-light** (probes are consumables, R&D-intensive not capex-intensive)
- **Installed base = annuity stream**: Each MiraQ system placed creates years of recurring probe sales

### Margin Analysis: Why Operating Margin Declined 2022-2024, Then Recovered

Key finding: The operating margin decline from 29% (2022) to 23.6% (2024) was driven by:
1. **Investment in direct sales operations**: Transitioning from distributors to direct sales in key markets (China, now Japan) front-loads SG&A costs
2. **R&D expansion**: MiraQ INTUI platform launched Dec 2024, probe redesign, software innovation
3. **Working capital buildup**: NOK 35M inventory increase for supply chain security
4. **FX headwinds**: NOK strengthening against USD/EUR in 2023-2024

FY2025 EBIT margin recovery to 28.0% (from 23.6%) CONFIRMS these were investment costs, not structural margin erosion. The operating leverage is now manifesting as revenue scales.

### Why It's Cheap (-26% from 52wH)

**Market narrative:** Small-cap Norwegian medtech with limited analyst coverage. Slowing growth in 2023-2024 (7.2%, 6.7% revenue growth) after post-COVID rebound. P/E at 27.7x on stale 2024 EPS (NOK 5.67). Flat EPS 2023 vs 2024 suggested growth stalling.

**My contra-thesis:** The 2024 slowdown was a deliberate investment year (direct sales buildout, R&D, Japan preparation). FY2025 proves this definitively: +24.4% revenue, +50% EBIT. The market is pricing the stock on trailing 2024 data. With FY2025 EPS likely NOK 8.50-9.50 (estimating from EBIT NOK 196M, ~22% tax, 18.3M shares), forward P/E is ~23-25x -- much more reasonable for a monopoly compounder with 80% GM and 34% ROIC.

**Value trap checklist: 0/10** -- No value trap factors present. Industry growing, no secular decline, no management destruction, net cash, no insider selling, no dividend cuts, gaining market share, ROIC >> WACC, FCF consistently positive, minimal goodwill.

**Edge:** The market is anchored to 2024 trailing data showing flat EPS. FY2025 results (released recently) show massive acceleration. Limited analyst coverage means the re-rating may be slow, creating a window. Additionally, Japan direct sales (Mar 2026) and vascular expansion are not priced in.

### Growth Drivers (ordered by impact)

1. **US penetration (highest impact):** Only ~40% technology adoption in the US, Medistim's largest market. 92% market share of the penetrated portion. Runway: 40% to 80% penetration = revenue doubles from US alone over 5-10 years.

2. **Vascular expansion:** +43% YoY in H1 2025. Larger addressable market than cardiac. PATENT clinical trial underway. Surgeons currently use Doppler/angiography (inferior). Medistim offers quantitative flow data without radiation/contrast media.

3. **Japan direct sales (Mar 2026):** 90% of CABG in Japan already uses Medistim. Going direct captures distributor margin (typically 30-40% of end price). Pure margin expansion.

4. **Installed base upgrades:** Converting flow-only systems to MiraQ (flow + imaging). Higher ASP + more probe types = revenue uplift per procedure.

5. **India/emerging markets:** 100,000+ CABG/year in India alone, most using finger palpation. Massive untapped market.

6. **Pricing power:** US price increases of ~9% being implemented beyond tariff offset. Monopoly pricing power.

7. **MiraQ INTUI software platform (Dec 2024):** Pricing uplift opportunity, data analytics, potential for recurring software revenue.
