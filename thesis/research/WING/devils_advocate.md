## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. "Revenue will grow 10-13% driven by 13-15% unit growth + flat-to-positive SSS"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - FY2025 revenue growth already decelerated to 11.4% (from 36% in FY2024, 28.7% in FY2023)
  - Domestic SSS was -3.3% for FY2025, with Q4 at -5.8% (ACCELERATING decline)
  - TD Cowen forecasts SSS -0.5% for 2026, citing proprietary survey data showing core customer demographics (younger, lower-income, Hispanic) remain under financial pressure
  - Broadening of weakness from Hispanic/low-income to middle-income cohorts
  - International SSS was -4.3% (FY2025) -- the international thesis is also under pressure
  - FCF was FLAT in FY2025 ($105.7M vs $105.6M) despite 11.4% revenue growth -- operating leverage is NOT materializing
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Revenue growth projections should be 8-10%, not 10-13%. SSS recovery is NOT assured and may take longer than 2 quarters. Committee should demand evidence of SSS stabilization before approving entry.

### 2. "FCF margins will expand from 15.2% to 17.5% over 5 years"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - FCF margin has DECLINED: 17.6% (2023) -> 16.9% (2024) -> 15.2% (2025)
  - OCF/Net Income dropped to 0.9x in FY2025 (below 1.0 = earnings quality concern)
  - SBC/Revenue creeping up: 1.2% (2022) -> 3.4% (2023) -> 3.5% (2024) -> 3.6% (2025)
  - The FA projects margin expansion based on operating leverage, but FY2025 showed NEGATIVE operating leverage on the FCF line despite 11.4% revenue growth
  - Goodwill/Assets increased from 10.4% to 12.1% in FY2025 (potential M&A or write-up)
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Assume FCF margins flat at 15-16%, not expanding. The declining trend is empirical, not theoretical.

### 3. "The franchise model insulates Wingstop from commodity risk"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - While technically correct (franchisor collects royalties on gross sales), the FA misses the SECOND-ORDER effect: if wing costs spike, franchisee profitability deteriorates, franchisees reduce expansion, unit growth slows
  - HPAI (bird flu) is a material omission. The 2022 outbreak spiked wing prices to $3.80/lb. Current prices are $1.12/lb -- near historic lows. HPAI outbreaks are recurring (2015, 2022, potentially 2025-2026). A spike to $2-3/lb would crush franchisee economics
  - Chicken wing prices currently low ($1.12/lb) but this IS the cycle -- poultry is a commodity cycle. The thesis was written near the trough, creating a false sense of cost security
  - If franchisee AUV drops below $1.7M AND wing costs rise, franchisee cash-on-cash returns drop from 70% to potentially 30-40%, destroying the "95% reinvestment rate" that underpins the entire growth thesis
- **Severidad:** MODERATE (franchisor IS partially insulated, but second-order effects are real)
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Kill condition needed: "If bone-in wing prices exceed $2.50/lb for 2+ consecutive quarters AND net new unit openings decelerate >30% YoY"

### 4. "QS 76 (Tier A) accurately reflects quality"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - The QS tool gave 0/10 on leverage (4.9x ND/EBITDA). The FA argues this is "WBS debt with lower risk profile" but still chose not to adjust. The REAL problem: Wingstop has NEGATIVE shareholder equity (-$703M). ROIC is artificially inflated on equity basis.
  - The FA admitted market position score was 0/8 (tool default) and considered adjusting +5 but did not. This is inconsistent -- if the tool gave 0 on a metric that should be 5-8, the QS is understated on one dimension and overstated on another.
  - The risk assessment scored WING as HIGH overall risk. A QS 76 Tier A with HIGH risk is contradictory -- Tier A implies "lower risk of permanent capital loss" but the risk assessment says otherwise.
  - The moat assessment is NARROW (not WIDE), which is atypical for Tier A.
- **Severidad:** MODERATE
- **Resolucion sugerida:** QS should remain 76 (tool output correct), but the Tier A classification should carry heavy caveats. This is a HIGH-RISK Tier A -- more appropriate MoS is 25-35%, not the 10-15% Tier A typical range.

### 5. "The stock at $246 (at time of R1) is 17% below FV of $296"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - Stock has since dropped to $203 (down 18% from R1 analysis date) -- the market disagreed
  - Reverse DCF: market at $203 implies 38.5% FCF growth. Historical is 26.4%. Even at $203, the market prices above-historical growth
  - DCF asymmetry ratio: 0.62x (unfavorable -- more downside than upside)
  - Equal-weight expected return from DCF tool: -12.1%
  - DCF bear case from tool: $14.58 (extreme, but directionally correct that standard DCF fails for WING)
  - Even the FA's own analysis showed bear case FV of $195-$210, meaning current price of $203 is IN the bear case range
  - Short interest: 15.5% of float (elevated, declined from 17.3% but still significant bearish conviction)
- **Severidad:** CRITICAL
- **Resolucion sugerida:** FV of $296 is overstated. My independent analysis (see below) produces $155-$195 range. Even the FA's own bear case ($195) is near the current price.
