## Top 5 Riesgos Criticos

### 1. Multiple Compression on a Still-Expensive Stock (CRITICAL)

- **Categoria:** Valoracion
- **Descripcion:** WING trades at P/E 40x on TTM earnings after a 36% decline from $388. The stock peaked at P/E 157x. Despite the correction, 40x is still extremely elevated for a company posting NEGATIVE same-store sales growth (-3.3% FY2025, -5.8% Q4 2025). The multiple implicitly prices in a strong recovery that is NOT yet visible in the data. The 2026 guidance of flat-to-low-single-digit comps is ASPIRATIONAL, supported by an unproven loyalty program (Club Wingstop launching end of Q2 2026).
- **Evidencia:**
  - P/E 40x vs restaurant peer group average ~18-22x
  - First SSS decline in 22 years signals potential trend change, not blip
  - Q4 2025 SSS -5.8% was WORSE than Q3 (-5.6%), showing ACCELERATION of decline
  - At 31x EV/FY26E EBITDA, premium to ALL restaurant peers
  - Analyst downgrades cite "too optimistic" guidance
- **Probabilidad:** Alta -- the market is forward-looking and comps data is deteriorating
- **Impacto si materializa:** If P/E normalizes from 40x to 25-30x (still premium), stock drops 25-37%. At a 25x P/E on current EPS of $3.72, stock = $93. Even at 30x = $112. Current price $246 implies significant downside to more normal multiples IF growth doesn't re-accelerate.
- **Mitigante:** Stock already corrected 36% from peak. Goldman maintains $335 target. Earnings beat in Q4 (+$0.16 vs consensus). If loyalty program works, comps recovery would justify premium.
- **Kill condition?:** YES -- If P/E remains >35x AND domestic SSS remains negative for 2+ consecutive quarters in 2026.

### 2. Same-Store Sales Decline May Be Structural, Not Cyclical (HIGH)

- **Categoria:** Fundamental
- **Descripcion:** Wingstop's 22-year SSS growth streak ended in 2025. Management attributes this to consumer weakness in Hispanic/low-income demographics and lapping 2 years of industry-leading growth. But the decline BROADENED across geographies and to middle-income consumers. This pattern -- initial weakness in a segment that broadens -- is a classic early-stage structural demand problem, not just tough comps.
- **Evidencia:**
  - FY2025 domestic SSS: -3.3% (first decline in 22 years)
  - Q4 2025: -5.8% (worse than Q3 -5.6% -- accelerating)
  - Weakness started in Hispanic/low-income, broadened to middle-income
  - Buyer penetration only 19% (vs Raising Cane's 24%, KFC 29%) -- ceiling may be near
  - Low diner frequency is undermining SSS despite menu innovation
  - 2026 guidance: flat to low-single-digit positive. If this misses, narrative breaks.
- **Probabilidad:** Media -- recovery is possible with loyalty program, but no hard evidence yet
- **Impacto si materializa:** Revenue growth decelerates to mid-single digits (unit growth only). FCF growth stalls. Multiple de-rates aggressively. 30-40% further decline from current price.
- **Mitigante:** Club Wingstop loyalty program showing 50% enrollment, +7% frequency in pilot. AI smart kitchens reducing labor cost. International comps remain positive. Management has track record of innovation (thigh introduction, digital-first model).
- **Kill condition?:** YES -- If domestic SSS remains negative through Q2 2026 (after loyalty launch).

### 3. Franchisee Profitability Squeeze Threatens Unit Growth Engine (HIGH)

- **Categoria:** Fundamental
- **Descripcion:** Wingstop's growth model depends entirely on franchisees investing in new units. Franchisee unit economics require $2M+ AUV to be attractive. With SSS declining, commodity costs volatile, and labor inflation ongoing, franchisee four-wall margins are under pressure. If franchisee profitability deteriorates enough, new unit openings will slow -- and unit growth is the ONLY growth engine when SSS are negative.
- **Evidencia:**
  - AUV $2.1M (target $3M) -- but SSS declining 3-5% compresses actual volumes
  - Franchisees pay 6.5% royalty + 5.5% advertising = 12% off gross sales
  - Bone-in wing prices were $3.22/lb at peak (84% increase), now $1.64 but volatile
  - 493 net new units in 2025 shows no CURRENT slowdown in development
  - But pipeline commitments (~2,300) were made when SSS were +20%. Conditions have changed.
  - Sustained SSS decline erodes the unit economics that drive franchisee reinvestment
- **Probabilidad:** Media -- not yet visible in development pipeline, but leading indicator would be commitments declining
- **Impacto si materializa:** If unit growth declines from 15-19% to <10%, revenue growth drops to low single digits. Stock multiple collapses as growth premium evaporates. 40-50% decline.
- **Mitigante:** Digital-first model (72.2% digital sales) reduces franchisee labor cost. Thigh diversification reduces commodity exposure. Small footprint = lower capex per unit. International provides growth diversification.
- **Kill condition?:** YES -- If net new domestic unit openings drop below 250/year (vs 375 in 2025).

### 4. Competitive Intensification in Chicken Category (HIGH)

- **Categoria:** Competitivo
- **Descripcion:** The chicken category is experiencing unprecedented competition. Raising Cane's grew faster than Wingstop in 2025 and has higher buyer penetration. Chick-fil-A is the dominant player. McDonald's, Wendy's, and others are adding chicken to menus. Wingstop's differentiation (flavor, wings specifically) may be narrower than the market assumes. The "fried chicken war" is a real threat to same-store sales.
- **Evidencia:**
  - Raising Cane's buyer penetration: 24% vs Wingstop 19%
  - McDonald's chicken items expanding aggressively
  - Chick-fil-A still dominant (#1 by consumer spend)
  - Seeking Alpha downgrade specifically cites "commoditization of hyper-customization advantage"
  - Multiple analyst downgrades cite competitive pressure
  - Every QSR chain adding chicken = price competition + consumer choice explosion
- **Probabilidad:** Alta -- competition is a FACT, not a prediction
- **Impacto si materializa:** Permanent SSS headwind of 2-4%. Pricing power erosion. Franchisee ROI squeeze.
- **Mitigante:** Wingstop's flavor-forward positioning is differentiated. Digital-first model creates data advantage. Brand loyalty exists (NBA partnership, cultural cachet). International markets have less competition.
- **Kill condition?:** NO as standalone. Becomes kill condition when combined with #2 (sustained SSS decline).

### 5. Negative Equity and Leverage in a Declining-Comps Environment (HIGH)

- **Categoria:** Financiero
- **Descripcion:** Wingstop has -$686M shareholder equity and $1.2B total debt (Net Debt/EBITDA 4.9x). This is common for asset-light franchise models during growth phases. However, the combination of negative equity + declining comps is DANGEROUS. The company issued $500M in 5.858% notes in Dec 2024 partly to fund buybacks -- buying back stock at P/E 100x+ with borrowed money while comps are declining is value-destructive capital allocation.
- **Evidencia:**
  - Total debt: $1.2B. Cash: $197M. Net debt: $1.1B
  - Net Debt/EBITDA: 4.9x (elevated for franchise model; typical range 2-4x)
  - Interest coverage: 7.9x (adequate but not comfortable given declining comps)
  - $500M new debt issued Dec 2024 at 5.858% to fund buybacks
  - CEO sold $5M in stock at $370; CFO sold $2M (-39% of holdings). Management selling while company borrows to buy back.
  - Shareholder equity: -$686M (entirely from buyback-funded capital return)
- **Probabilidad:** Media -- not imminent covenant risk, but leverage amplifies downside
- **Impacto si materializa:** If comps remain negative and revenue growth slows to <5%, interest burden becomes significant (~$43M/year guided). Dividend could be cut. Credit rating pressure. Stock dilution if debt needs to be refinanced at higher rates.
- **Mitigante:** FCF of $106M (2024) covers interest 2.5x. Asset-light model means minimal capex needs. Debt maturities likely not immediate.
- **Kill condition?:** YES -- If Net Debt/EBITDA exceeds 6.0x OR interest coverage falls below 5.0x.

## Riesgos Adicionales (No en Top 5, pero Material)

### 6. Consumer Demographic Vulnerability

- **Descripcion:** Wingstop over-indexes to Hispanic and low-income consumers -- the demographics most impacted by the current economic environment. Management admitted weakness started in these cohorts and broadened.
- **Probabilidad:** Alta
- **Impacto:** Medio (already partially reflected in SSS decline)
- **Mitigante:** Menu at multiple price points. Brand aspires to "everyday occasion" positioning.

### 7. Insider Selling Pattern

- **Descripcion:** ZERO insider purchases in 2025. CEO sold $5M at $370, CFO sold $2M (-39% of holdings) at $374. All insiders only selling. This is NOT a signal of confidence at current prices.
- **Probabilidad:** N/A (factual observation)
- **Impacto:** Medio -- confirms that management does not consider the stock cheap at $250+
- **Mitigante:** Sales were at significantly higher prices ($337-$377); current price 33% below.

### 8. BIPA Class Action (Voiceprint Privacy)

- **Descripcion:** Class action alleging Wingstop's AI phone ordering (ConverseNow) captured customer voiceprints without consent, violating Illinois BIPA.
- **Probabilidad:** Media (of settlement/judgment)
- **Impacto:** Bajo -- limited to Illinois; settlement likely $5-20M range. Not existential.
- **Mitigante:** Could modify AI ordering practices to comply.

### 9. Tariff Risk on Chicken Supply (Indirect)

- **Descripcion:** US chicken is primarily domestic, so direct tariff impact is limited. However, tariffs on feed inputs, packaging, and equipment could raise costs indirectly. More importantly, broader tariff-driven inflation pressures the low/middle-income consumer who is Wingstop's core demographic.
- **Probabilidad:** Baja (direct); Media (indirect via consumer pressure)
- **Impacto:** Medio
- **Mitigante:** Domestic supply chain for primary protein. Menu engineering flexibility.

### 10. 2026 Guidance "Too Optimistic" Risk

- **Descripcion:** Multiple analysts flagged 2026 guidance as overly optimistic. Flat-to-low-single-digit SSS growth requires a REVERSAL from -5.8% Q4 trajectory. This implies +6-8pp swing in a single quarter with no proven catalyst (loyalty launches end of Q2).
- **Probabilidad:** Media-Alta
- **Impacto:** Alto -- guidance miss would trigger another leg down in the stock
- **Mitigante:** If loyalty program achieves pilot metrics at scale (+7% frequency), guidance is achievable.

## Kill Conditions Sugeridas

Based on risk assessment findings, the following kill conditions should be included in any thesis:

1. **KC#1 (SSS Structural Decline):** Domestic SSS remains negative through Q2 2026 (post-loyalty-launch). If the loyalty program cannot reverse the trend, the decline is structural.

2. **KC#2 (Unit Growth Stall):** Net new domestic unit openings fall below 250/year (vs 375 in 2025). This signals franchisee loss of confidence in unit economics.

3. **KC#3 (Leverage Deterioration):** Net Debt/EBITDA exceeds 6.0x OR interest coverage falls below 5.0x. Leverage is already elevated at 4.9x; further deterioration in a franchise model is a red flag.

4. **KC#4 (Guidance Miss):** FY2026 domestic SSS comes in below -2% (i.e., management's flat-to-positive guidance missed by >200bp). Would indicate management has lost visibility on the business.

5. **KC#5 (Franchisee Churn):** Reported closures exceed 3% of domestic system in any 12-month period. Currently negligible, but rising closures would signal economic distress.

## Riesgo Agregado

- **Numero de riesgos HIGH+CRITICAL:** 6 (1 CRITICAL + 5 HIGH)
- **Riesgos correlacionados?** SI -- Risks #1, #2, #3, #5, and #6 are ALL linked. Consumer weakness (#6) drives SSS decline (#2), which pressures franchisee economics (#3), which threatens unit growth (the remaining growth engine), which de-rates the multiple (#1). The leverage (#5) amplifies all of these. This correlation means risks don't diversify -- they compound.
- **Risk Score Final: HIGH**

### Justification for HIGH (not VERY HIGH):
While there is one CRITICAL risk (multiple compression) and five HIGH risks, the mitigating factors are real:
- The asset-light franchise model is genuinely capital-efficient (FCF $106M, 17% margin)
- ROIC at 39.8% with 27.4pp spread over WACC is exceptional
- The loyalty program pilot data is promising (50% enrollment, +7% frequency)
- International growth (15-16% unit growth) provides diversification
- Stock has already corrected 36% from peak

The difference between HIGH and VERY HIGH is that the business model is NOT broken -- the VALUATION may be broken relative to the current trajectory. At the right price, Wingstop could be a compelling investment. At P/E 40x with negative comps, the risk-reward is unfavorable.

**Price at which risk assessment would shift to MEDIUM:** P/E 20-25x (stock ~$75-93 range) would price in the current headwinds appropriately and create genuine margin of safety. Current price of $246 provides essentially ZERO margin of safety against the identified risks.

## 🔄 META-REFLECTION

### Dudas/Incertidumbres
- **Loyalty program efficacy at scale:** The Club Wingstop pilot data (+7% frequency, 50% enrollment) is the single most important variable for the bull case. Pilots often don't replicate at national scale. I have no way to independently verify these numbers -- they come from management (Nivel 2 source with clear incentive to be optimistic).
- **International unit economics:** I could not find detailed four-wall economics for international franchisees. If international margins are significantly lower, the 10,000-unit global target may be a mirage.
- **Receivables anomaly:** The 47% receivables growth vs 36% revenue growth flagged by narrative_checker.py is likely explained by rapid unit openings (franchise fees receivable from new franchisees). However, without access to the 10-K detail, I cannot confirm this is benign. It deserves scrutiny in the fundamental analyst's work.

### Riesgos que Podrian Estar Subestimados
- **Buyback value destruction:** I classified this as part of Risk #5, but it may deserve its own HIGH classification. Borrowing $500M at 5.858% to buy back stock at P/E 100x+ while your core business metric (SSS) is declining is textbook bad capital allocation. If SSS don't recover, this debt-funded buyback program will look disastrous in hindsight.
- **Consumer demographic shift:** Wingstop's core customer being disproportionately low-income/Hispanic in a macro environment where the "bottom half of the economy is already in recession" (per world current_view.md) is a more persistent headwind than a cyclical blip.

### Discrepancias con Thesis
- No thesis exists yet. However, the fundamental analyst should be WARNED: the bull case requires believing that (a) loyalty program replicates pilot at scale, (b) SSS reverse from -5.8% to positive within 2 quarters, and (c) P/E 40x is justified for a company with negative comps. All three of these are uncertain.

### Sugerencias para el Sistema
- **Franchise model leverage template:** Companies with negative equity due to buyback-funded capital return (WING, SBUX, MCD, DECK, etc.) need a specific analytical framework that distinguishes between "healthy franchise leverage" and "value-destructive leverage." Net Debt/EBITDA alone is insufficient -- need to also track debt-funded buyback yield vs cost of debt.
- **Consumer demographic risk factor:** For QSR/consumer names, the income quintile of the core customer base should be a standard risk assessment input. Wingstop's over-indexing to Hispanic/low-income consumers is a critical risk that a standard QS wouldn't capture.

2. Should the receivables anomaly (47% vs 36% revenue growth) be investigated further before proceeding to R1? It's likely benign but could indicate franchise fee recognition issues.
