## Adjudication

### 1. Fair Value: $296 → $240 (-19%)

R2 correctly identifies three devastating FV challenges:
- **Market prices in 42% FCF growth** — our $296 requires EVEN MORE optimism than the market
- **FY2025 FCF was FLAT** — zero operating leverage despite 11.4% revenue growth
- **Our FV ($296) is below sell-side consensus ($325-340)** — Error #49 territory, no informational edge

However, R2's bear FV ($137) is too aggressive:
- Uses 25x EV/EBIT — punishes a company still growing units 15-19%
- Uses FY2025 OE without projecting FY2026 improvement
- P/E 30x is appropriate for a no-growth QSR, not a franchise model with 10-year runway

**Resolution: $240**
- OEY method: FY2026E OE ~$115M (conservative 9% growth from $105.6M) at 1.9% yield → $219/share
- Unit growth premium: +$20 (modest; reflects discounted 5,500-6,000 target vs R1's 7,000)
- Weighted: $240
- This is 14% below R1, 75% above R2 bear, and critically **at parity with market ($237)** — meaning we have near-zero MoS at current price, consistent with R2's "no edge" finding

### 2. Entry Price: $215 → $190 (-12%)

R2's strongest tactical point: **R1's own entry ($215) produces E[CAGR] 11.8% — below the 12% Tier A threshold.** This is incoherent. The thesis contradicts itself.

**Resolution: $190**
- MoS vs $240 FV: 20.8% (appropriate for Tier A with elevated uncertainty)
- E[CAGR] at $190: ($240/$190)^(1/3) - 1 + 8% growth = 8.0% + 8% = ~16%
- Provides genuine cushion vs DA bear range ($137-$180)
- E[CAGR] at $200: ~14.5% (still above 12% threshold — reasonable aggressive entry)

### 3. SSS Trajectory: UNRESOLVED — Gate on Q1 FY2026

Neither R1 nor R2 can prove whether the SSS decline is cyclical or structural. The data:
- R1 evidence (cyclical): 2-year stack still positive, comp lapping +20%
- R2 evidence (structural): Q4 WORSE than Q3 (-5.8% vs -5.6%), broadening from Hispanic/low-income to middle-income, RBC flags weak Q1

**Resolution: DO NOT RESOLVE. Gate the investment on Q1 FY2026 data (May 2026).**
- If Q1 domestic SSS > -2%: cyclical argument gains credibility. Entry at $190 justified.
- If Q1 domestic SSS -2% to -4%: ambiguous. Hold watchlist.
- If Q1 domestic SSS < -4%: structural thesis gains evidence. ARCHIVE.

### 4. FCF Stagnation: R2 WINS

FCF: $105.7M → $105.6M. Zero growth on 11.4% revenue growth. This is the single most important data point from R2. The R1 thesis did not address this. An asset-light franchise model should convert revenue growth into FCF growth. The fact that it didn't in FY2025 suggests:
- Smart Kitchen capex absorbed operating leverage
- SBC acceleration (1.2% → 3.6% of revenue) diluting real economics
- Interest expense from leveraged buybacks consuming cash flow

This directly damages the OEY growth assumption in R1. Projecting FY2026 OE growth at 9% (vs R1's implicit 12%) is a more defensible base case.

### 5. Capital Allocation: R2 WINS

Borrowed $500M at 5.86% to buy back stock at P/E 100x+. CEO sold $5M. CFO sold $2M (-39% of holdings). Zero insider purchases in 2025. Director sales at $260-$261.

This is NOT aligned with shareholder interests. Management is deleveraging personal risk while leveraging the company. R1 did not adequately weight this as a negative quality signal.

### 6. Unit Growth Target: Discounted to 5,500-6,000

R2 correctly notes that the 7,000-unit target is management aspiration with historical precedent showing aggressive franchise targets often unmet. Pipeline committed at +20% SSS may not convert in a negative-SSS environment.

Splitting the difference: 5,500-6,000 achievable units (vs R1's 7,000). This reduces the long-term growth runway by ~15-20% but still supports meaningful unit growth for 8-10 years.
