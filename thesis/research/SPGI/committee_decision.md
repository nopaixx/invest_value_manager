SPGI is a $127B financial infrastructure monopolist with 53 years of dividend growth. The quality-adjusted risk is lower for SPGI despite lower E[CAGR].

3. **ALFA.L is NOT at entry.** Current price 173.80p is 5.3% above the 165p entry. SPGI at $419.70 is AT the $420 trigger. Deploying into what's available NOW (contingent on Q1 gate) is better than waiting for ALFA.L to drop.

4. **Basket priority.** Data & Analytics Monopolies basket has TW + WKL.AS but needs its anchor (SPGI). ALFA.L would join UK Digital Platforms, which already has IHP.L + MONY.L (2 positions). Basket health argues for SPGI.

5. **ALFA.L should be pursued INDEPENDENTLY.** If ALFA.L drops to 165p and receivables gate clears, it should be bought with separate capital (e.g., from MONY.L rotation, given MONY.L is flagged as first rotation candidate).

## META-REFLECTION

### Doubts About This Decision
- **E[CAGR] 12.1% is the minimum acceptable for Tier A.** If the gate clears but price is $425 instead of $420, E[CAGR] drops to ~11.7% (below threshold). The entry discipline must be STRICT: $420 or below, not "$420-ish."
- **No informational edge (Error #49 acknowledged).** This is a "buy quality at fair price" decision, not an alpha-generating insight. The alpha comes from patience and discipline, not from seeing what others miss. This is inherently a lower-conviction thesis than EDEN.PA (27.1% E[CAGR]) or IHP.L (20.4%).
- **Financial Services sector at 56.6%.** While the businesses are uncorrelated in reality, a systemic financial crisis (2008-style) WOULD hurt most of them simultaneously. The concentration deserves ongoing monitoring.

### Weaknesses in the Analysis
- **No standalone moat_assessment.md or risk_assessment.md.** The full buy pipeline (R1 parallel) should have included moat-assessor and risk-identifier agents. These were integrated into the R1 thesis but not as standalone files. For a position this significant (basket anchor), standalone assessments would be more rigorous.
- **Mobility spinoff not modeled.** The Form 10 in Q2 2026 will change SPGI's composition. A sum-of-parts post-spinoff analysis should be done before the Q1 gate clears, so we know whether RemainCo at $380-400 (ex-Mobility value) is still attractive.
- **Smart money context not refreshed.** The TCI Fund (Chris Hohn) adding is cited from February data. A fresh smart_money.py stock-profile before deployment would be prudent.

### Suggestions for Improvement
- **Pre-deployment checklist for gated positions:** When a gated SO approaches execution, run: (1) price_checker, (2) smart_money stock-profile, (3) constraint_checker, (4) verify gate conditions from earnings data. Create this as a standard pre-flight for gated positions.
- **Model Mobility spinoff BEFORE Q1 gate date.** If we wait until Q1 earnings to model the spinoff, we'll be rushing the analysis during decision time.
