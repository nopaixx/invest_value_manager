## Moat Assessment

### Moat Rating: WIDE

**1. Regulatory Duopoly (Ratings) -- IMPENETRABLE**
- Credit ratings are REQUIRED by regulation for bond issuance (SEC Rule 17g, Basel III, Solvency II)
- Only 10 NRSROs exist, but issuers effectively MUST have S&P + Moody's (dual-rating requirement is market convention)
- ~80% combined market share has been stable for 40+ years
- New entrant barriers: decades of credibility, regulatory approval, client relationships
- **Margin: 62% adjusted operating** -- pricing power is near-absolute
- **Kill scenario: Regulatory change removing NRSRO designation** -- probability <5% in 10yr

**2. Index Monopoly (Indices) -- NEAR-IMPENETRABLE**
- S&P 500 is the DEFINITION of the US stock market -- $16.6T+ AUM tracking S&P DJI indices
- Switching cost is not just technical -- it's IDENTITY. A fund tracking "S&P 500" cannot switch to "MSCI USA"
- Revenue grows automatically with market appreciation (asset-linked fees)
- ETD revenue growing 20% (derivatives on indices = high margin, growing)
- **Margin: 69% operating** -- highest in entire company
- **Kill scenario: Passive investing reversal** -- probability <5% in 10yr

**3. Benchmark Monopoly (Commodity Insights/Platts) -- WIDE**
- Platts is THE reference price for oil (Dated Brent), LNG, metals, petrochemicals
- Embedded in commercial contracts worth trillions globally
- Switching cost: would require renegotiating millions of existing contracts
- Energy transition creates NEW benchmark needs (carbon credits, hydrogen, renewables)
- **Margin: 45% adjusted operating**

**4. Data Lock-In (Market Intelligence + Mobility) -- NARROW-WIDE**
- Capital IQ is embedded in analyst workflows -- high switching costs but not insurmountable (Bloomberg competes)
- Carfax brand dominance in vehicle history
- With Intelligence acquisition deepens corporate data moat
- **This is the weakest segment competitively** -- Bloomberg and AI tools pose real long-term risk
- **Margin: 32% adjusted operating** (lowest, but improving)

### Moat Duration Estimate

- Ratings + Indices: 15-20+ years (regulatory + network + brand)
- Commodity Insights: 10-15 years (benchmark lock-in)
- Market Intelligence: 5-10 years (competitive but high switching costs)
- Mobility: 5-10 years (likely to be spun off, Form 10 in Q2 2026)
