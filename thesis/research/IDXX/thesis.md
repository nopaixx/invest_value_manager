## Business Understanding

### What IDEXX Does

IDEXX is the global leader in companion animal (pets) veterinary diagnostics and software. The company solves a critical problem: veterinarians need fast, accurate diagnostic results to treat sick animals, and pet owners increasingly demand human-quality healthcare for their companions.

**Revenue model: Razor-Razorblade (highly recurring)**
- **Instruments (razors):** IDEXX places diagnostic analyzers (Catalyst, ProCyte, SediVue, inVue Dx) into veterinary clinics, often via multi-year commitment programs
- **Consumables (blades):** Each test run on those instruments generates recurring revenue — this is ~85% of CAG Diagnostics revenue
- **Reference Labs:** IDEXX operates the largest global network of veterinary reference laboratories
- **Software (Cornerstone, Neo):** Practice management software creates additional stickiness
- **Water Testing (LPD):** ~8% of revenue — environmental water testing (separate business)

**Revenue Mix (FY2025, $4.3B total):**
| Segment | Revenue | % Total | Growth |
|---------|---------|---------|--------|
| CAG Diagnostics recurring | ~$3.1B | ~72% | +8-9% organic |
| CAG Instruments | ~$230M | ~5% | Volatile (placement-driven) |
| Water (LPD) | ~$350M | ~8% | +6-8% |
| Other/Livestock | ~$620M | ~15% | Mid-single digits |

### Unit Economics

- **Installed base:** ~250,000+ clinics globally with IDEXX instruments
- **Revenue per clinic:** Growing annually as more tests per visit, new modalities (Cancer Dx, inVue)
- **Customer retention:** Extremely high (multi-year commitments, switching costs are massive — retraining staff, workflow disruption, data migration)
- **FCF Margin:** 24.2% (FY2025) — exceptional and expanding
- **Capital intensity:** Low-medium (Capex/Depreciation ~1.0x, capex ~5% of revenue)

### Moat Assessment

**Moat Width: WIDE**

1. **Switching costs (PRIMARY):** Veterinary clinics build entire workflows around IDEXX instruments. Switching means retraining staff, disrupting daily operations, migrating patient data. Multi-year placement commitments lock customers in. This is the strongest moat component.

2. **Network effects (reference labs):** Largest global network of ~90+ reference labs means fastest turnaround times, widest test menu. Scale advantage compounds — more tests = more data = better algorithms = better products.

3. **Proprietary technology:** Protected assay chemistries, proprietary algorithms, 3,000+ patents. The IDEXX Cancer Dx (liquid biopsy for canine lymphoma) and inVue Dx (cellular analyzer for in-clinic cytology) represent new modalities that competitors cannot replicate quickly.

4. **Scale + distribution:** 3,000+ direct sales reps globally. No competitor has this direct relationship depth with veterinarians.

5. **Regulatory barriers:** FDA CVM oversight of veterinary diagnostics creates meaningful barriers for new entrants.

**ROIC persistence:** 47-54% ROIC over 4 years (tool shows 4yr data), ROIC-WACC spread of +40pp. This is among the highest ROIC spreads I've analyzed in the portfolio universe.

### Innovation Cycle (2025-2026)

- **inVue Dx:** ~6,400 placements in 2025 (blew past initial 4,500 target). Planning 5,500 in 2026. Transformational — brings cytology analysis to point-of-care.
- **Cancer Dx:** First-ever blood test for canine lymphoma. Expanding to mast cell tumors mid-2026. Opens entirely new diagnostic category.
- **FNA on inVue Dx:** Fine needle aspirate cytology at point of care. Controlled launch began Dec 2025. Combines with Cancer Dx for integrated cancer diagnostic workflow.

These innovations are incremental TAM expansion — not just share gains. Cancer diagnostics for companion animals barely existed before IDEXX.

**My view:** The business quality is exceptional (QS 83 Tier A), but the market already pays a full premium. There is no informational edge at current prices.

## Valuation

### Method 1: Owner Earnings Yield (Tier A Primary, 60% weight)

```
FCF (FY2025): $1.04B
Depreciation: ~$200M
Maintenance Capex: ~$220M (D&A x 1.1)
Capex/D&A = 1.0x → Owner Earnings ≈ FCF = $1.04B

Owner Earnings Yield = $1.04B / $46.9B = 2.22%
Sustainable Growth = 9.5% (revenue 9% + slight margin expansion)
OEY + Growth = 2.22% + 9.5% = 11.7%

vs WACC ~10% → spread = +1.7pp (thin but positive)
```

For a Tier A compounder, OEY + Growth of 11.7% is MARGINAL — below the 12% threshold where deployment is compelling. The market is pricing in almost all of the value creation.

**OEY-implied Fair Value:** At a target OEY+Growth of 13% (minimum for compelling entry):
- Required OEY = 13% - 9.5% = 3.5%
- FV = $1.04B / 3.5% = $29.7B market cap
- FV per share = $29.7B / 80M shares = ~$371

At 12% target:
- Required OEY = 12% - 9.5% = 2.5%
- FV = $1.04B / 2.5% = $41.6B
- FV per share = ~$520

**Range: $371-520.** Midpoint $445.

### Method 2: Forward P/E with Anti-Bullish-Bias (40% weight)

Using FY2026E EPS midpoint: $14.55

- IDXX historical 5yr average P/E: ~50x (elevated by ZIRP era)

- Comparable quality compounders (MSCI, MCO, SPGI) trade at 30-40x
