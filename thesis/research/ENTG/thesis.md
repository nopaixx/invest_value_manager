## Business Understanding

### Model

Entegris provides mission-critical materials, chemicals, and contamination control solutions for semiconductor manufacturing. The business operates through two segments:

**Materials Solutions (MS) — ~44% of revenue ($362M Q4):** CMP slurries and pads, specialty chemicals, advanced deposition materials, selective etch chemistries. These are consumable materials that are used up during chip manufacturing — every wafer needs them, creating recurring demand tied to wafer starts.

**Advanced Purity Solutions (APS) — ~56% of revenue ($465M Q4):** Liquid filtration, gas filtration, microenvironments (wafer handling), and fluid handling systems. These products ensure the ultra-pure environment required for advanced chipmaking (contamination at parts-per-trillion levels).

**Key insight:** Entegris is a consumables business in semiconductors. Unlike equipment makers (ASML, AMAT) whose revenue is lumpy and tied to capex cycles, Entegris revenue is tied to wafer STARTS (volume-driven) and node COMPLEXITY (content-per-wafer driven). This makes revenue more stable through cycles.

### Revenue Model
- ~80% consumables/materials (recurring with wafer production)
- ~20% equipment/systems (higher capex sensitivity)
- ~60% from advanced nodes (7nm and below) — highest content per wafer
- ~30% from memory (~equal DRAM/NAND)
- Significant switching costs: materials qualified into production for 12-24 months

### Unit Economics
- Content per wafer INCREASES with node shrinks (more filtration steps, more CMP passes, more specialty chemicals at 2nm vs 5nm)
- Management claims "double-digit increase in NAND content per wafer" from layer count transitions (250 -> 300+)
- 2x POR wins at N2 vs N5 = content growth accelerates at leading edge

### Margin Structure
- Gross margin: 44.4% FY2025 (43.8% Q4), trending from 42.5% (2022) — improving
- Operating margin: 14.3% GAAP (depressed by $470M+ amortization from CMC acquisition)
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 27.7% Q4
- FCF margin: 12.4% FY2025 (16.3% Q4) — rapidly improving as capex declines

### Capital Requirements
- Capex: $299M in 2025 (9% of revenue), declining to ~$250M in 2026
- Management: can deliver "$1B+ incremental revenue with limited further investment"
- This is the KEY inflection: heavy investment cycle is ENDING
