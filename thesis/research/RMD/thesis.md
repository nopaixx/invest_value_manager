## Business Understanding

### What ResMed Does

ResMed designs, manufactures, and sells medical devices and cloud-based SaaS solutions for the treatment of sleep-disordered breathing (primarily obstructive sleep apnea/OSA), COPD, and other chronic respiratory conditions. The core product is CPAP (Continuous Positive Airway Pressure) devices and masks.

**Problem solved:** OSA affects an estimated 936 million adults globally (aged 30-69), with approximately 80% undiagnosed. Untreated OSA causes hypertension, stroke, heart failure, diabetes, and cognitive decline. CPAP is the gold-standard first-line treatment for moderate-to-severe OSA. The undiagnosed population represents a massive runway for growth.

**Revenue model (Q2 FY2026 actuals, $1.42B quarterly):**
- **Devices (flow generators):** $726M quarterly (~51%). One-time sale of AirSense 11 CPAP/BiPAP machines. Replacement cycle 3-5 years. +9% YoY.
- **Masks & accessories:** $530M quarterly (~37%). HIGH recurring -- masks replaced every 3-6 months. Resupply automation drives repeat purchases. +16% YoY (strongest segment).
- **Software/SaaS (Residential Care):** $167M quarterly (~12%). Brightree (DME provider management), MatrixCare (long-term care), AirView (clinician platform, 28M+ patients), myAir (patient app, 8.8M users). +7% YoY. Margins >70%.

**Revenue mix (H1 FY2026: $2.76B, +10% YoY):**
- US/Canada/LatAm: ~59% ($835M Q2)
- Europe/Asia/Other: ~41% ($421M Q2)
- Recurring/resupply: estimated 55-60% of total revenue

### Unit Economics

- **Customer acquisition:** Primarily through sleep labs, pulmonologists, and primary care physicians. Patients are PRESCRIBED RMD devices -- minimal direct CAC. GLP-1 prescriptions are now SURFACING undiagnosed OSA patients through cardiometabolic screening.
- **LTV:** Device sale ($500-1,500) + 5-10 years of mask resupply ($200-400/year) + software fees = estimated LTV $3,000-5,000 per patient.
- **LTV/CAC:** Extremely favorable. Essentially zero marketing cost per patient acquired through medical prescription channel.
- **Payback:** Immediate on device sale; resupply is high-margin recurring stream.
- **FCF Margin:** 32.1% (FY2025), among the highest in medtech
- **Capital intensity:** ASSET-LIGHT. Capex/Depreciation declining from 0.8x to 0.4x. Minimal capital needs for growth.

(a) GLP-1 prescriptions trigger health assessments that DIAGNOSE previously undiagnosed OSA -- 80% of OSA is undiagnosed, (b) GLP-1 patients are health-motivated and more likely to comply with treatment, (c) ~40% of OSA is NOT obesity-related (central apnea, anatomical factors), (d) GLP-1 adherence drops significantly after year 1 with weight regain, (e) medical guidelines maintain CPAP as first-line treatment even with GLP-1, (f) CHEST 2025 data: OSA patients on GLP-1 have 20% GREATER mortality benefit vs non-OSA patients -- this drives more, not less, diagnosis and treatment. ## Moat Assessment: WIDE

### Moat Sources
