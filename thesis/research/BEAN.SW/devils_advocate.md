## Executive Summary

The R1 thesis (dated 2026-02-20) called BEAN.SW a "FANTASY entry" at CHF 903 with base FV CHF 470 / bull FV CHF 680. Since then, the stock has crashed 28% to CHF 652, bringing it within striking distance of the bull-case entry (CHF 612). The crash was driven by: (1) sell-the-news reaction to FY2025 results with EPS miss (-3% vs consensus), (2) US tariff shock (39% on Swiss imports), (3) broader Swiss market selloff, and (4) multiple compression from extreme levels. The R1 thesis was directionally correct that the stock was overvalued. However, at CHF 652 vs CHF 903, the valuation picture has materially changed and the thesis needs updating. The business quality is confirmed (EBIT +28.6%, margins expanded), but NEW headwinds have emerged (tariffs, FCF deterioration from elevated capex, gross margin dip) that the R1 did not anticipate. The stock is LESS overvalued but still expensive on an absolute basis (P/E 44x, EV/EBIT 35.5x). The DA bear case at CHF 360-420 suggests limited margin of safety even at current prices.

## Key Assumptions Challenged

### 1. "Data Center Cooling Is a Structural Shift, Not Cyclical"

**R1 Claim:** Data center cooling grew from 10% to ~18% of sales in 18 months, representing a "structural shift."

**Counter-Evidence:**
- Data center capex IS accelerating ($600B+ in 2026, +36% YoY) -- this SUPPORTS the thesis near-term
- However, the R1 itself noted hyperscaler capex follows boom-bust patterns. Current spending has a Darwinian quality: "supply constrained, not demand constrained" per hyperscaler CEOs. This is textbook late-cycle language.
- Only 25% of AI initiatives have delivered expected ROI. Only $25B in AI services revenue vs $450B in AI capex spending in 2025. This ROI gap cannot persist indefinitely.
- Belimo management confirmed data centers accounted for "slightly less than half" of total group sales growth in 2025. If data center demand normalizes, Belimo's growth drops from 23% back to ~10-12%.
- The data center liquid cooling market is projected 28.7% CAGR to 2033 -- but Belimo faces real competition: Danfoss, Siemens, Schneider Electric, Emerson are all active in this segment. Belimo is NOT the monopolist in data center cooling that it is in traditional HVAC actuators.

**Severity: MODERATE.** The near-term demand is real and supported by evidence. The risk is medium-term (2-3 years) if hyperscaler ROI disappointment triggers capex pullback. The R1 already identified this risk correctly.

**Resolution:** Data center contribution should be modeled with a decay function: 18% growth for 2 years, tapering to 8-10% by year 4-5. This is roughly what R1 base case assumes.

---

### 2. "FY2025 Confirmed Exceptional Growth"

**R1 Claim:** FY2025 showed 23.3% revenue growth, margin expansion, exceptional year.

**Counter-Evidence (NEW data not available at R1):**
- **EPS MISSED consensus by 3%:** CHF 14.77 actual vs CHF 15.17 expected. Dividend also missed (CHF 10.00 vs CHF 10.34 consensus). This is the FIRST signal that the market's expectations were running ahead of delivery.
- **Gross margin actually DECLINED:** 61.6% in FY2024 to 60.7% in FY2025. The R1 narrative of "4 consecutive years of expansion" is now broken. This is a subtle but important signal -- at peak demand, margin should be expanding from operating leverage, not compressing.
- **FCF DROPPED 26%:** From CHF 132M (FY2024) to CHF 98M (FY2025) despite 18.7% revenue growth. FCF margin collapsed from 14.0% to 8.7%. This is driven by elevated capex (CHF 87M, up from CHF 63M, +38% YoY) and working capital absorption.
- **Capex/Depreciation surged to 2.8x** (from 2.1x). Company says capex will remain elevated in 2026.
- **Receivables grew 22.4% vs revenue 18.7%** -- the gap widened from R1's observation (14.3% vs 9.9% in FY2024). Two consecutive years of receivables outgrowing revenue is a yellow flag for collection quality.
- **Inventory grew 27% vs revenue 18.7%** -- even more concerning. Inventory build ahead of tariff implementation? Or demand deceleration signal?

**Severity: HIGH.** The FCF deterioration fundamentally changes the valuation math. R1 used CHF 132M as FCF base. The actual FY2025 FCF is CHF 98M -- 26% lower. This mechanically reduces all DCF-based fair values by ~20-25%. The OEY calculation needs complete redo.

**Resolution:** All valuation methods must be recalculated using CHF 98M as FCF base, not CHF 132M. The margin expansion narrative needs revision given the gross margin decline.

---

### 3. "US Tariffs Are a Major Headwind" (NEW -- Not in R1)

**R1 Claim:** Not addressed (R1 predates tariff announcements).

**New Evidence:**
- Trump imposed 39% tariff on Swiss exports -- well above the 31% rate initially announced in April
- Belimo generates 50% of revenue from Americas. Most products manufactured in Switzerland.
- Company implemented 7.9% average price increase effective January 2026, but 39% tariff far exceeds this
- Management acknowledged "new U.S. tariffs ranging from 10% to 39%" as headwind
- Q1 2026 margins will absorb deferred tariff costs shifted through supply chain timing
- Company guided mid-teens growth for 2026 (deceleration from 23%) with EBIT margin "above 20%"

**Impact Assessment:**
- If 50% of revenue (Americas) faces effective 20-30% cost increase from tariffs that cannot be fully passed through, EBIT margin could compress 200-400bps
- Belimo's pricing power is real (products are <1% of project cost), but a 39% tariff is not a normal cost increase -- it's punitive and may trigger customer pushback or sourcing alternatives
- Honeywell and JCI have US manufacturing -- tariffs give them a COST ADVANTAGE vs Swiss-manufactured Belimo for the first time
- Company is expanding US production capacity but this takes 2-3 years to materially shift manufacturing mix

**Severity: HIGH.** This is a material risk that the R1 could not have anticipated. A 39% tariff on a company with 50% Americas revenue and Swiss manufacturing is structurally negative. The mitigation (price increases, US capacity expansion) takes time. Near-term (2026-2027) margin compression is likely.

**Resolution:** Committee must factor tariff headwind into FV. Base case should assume 150-250bps EBIT margin compression in 2026. Monitor whether Belimo accelerates US production ramp.

---

### 4. "P/E 62-65x Is Egregious" (R1 claim -- now 44x)

**R1 Claim:** At CHF 903, P/E was 62-65x, wildly overvalued.

**Updated Reality:**
- At CHF 652, P/E is now 44x on FY2025 EPS of CHF 14.77
- EV/EBIT is 35.5x on FY2025 EBIT of CHF 233M
- The 28% price drop has compressed multiples by ~30%

**Is 44x reasonable?**
- For a Swiss industrial growing 7-9% historically: NO. Peer multiples: Halma 35x EBIT, Spirax 25x, Atlas Copco 30x
- For a Swiss industrial growing 15%+ with data center tailwind: ARGUABLE but still premium
- R1 base FV of CHF 470 implies P/E ~32x on FY2025 EPS. This requires the stock to fall another 28% from current levels.
- R1 bull FV of CHF 680 implies P/E ~46x -- the stock is currently BELOW the bull FV

**Severity: MODERATE.** The extreme overvaluation has partially corrected. At 44x P/E, Belimo is expensive but within the range seen for exceptional quality industrials during growth inflection points. The R1 bull case of CHF 680 is now only 4% above current price, suggesting the market is pricing somewhere between R1 base and bull.

---

### 5. "CHF Strength Compresses Translated Earnings"

**R1 Claim:** Mentioned as medium sensitivity risk.

**Counter-Evidence:**
- CHF has strengthened vs EUR and USD in 2026
- Management noted 4.5% currency headwind on FY2025 revenue
- With 50% Americas revenue, every 10% USD weakness vs CHF reduces reported revenue by ~5% and EBIT by similar magnitude
- The 23.3% local currency growth was only 18.7% in CHF terms -- a 4.6pp drag

**Severity: LOW.** This is a known and permanent feature of any Swiss exporter. Belimo has managed this for decades. The FX drag is real but not thesis-changing. R1 already noted it.

## Independent Bear-Case Valuation

### Method: EV/EBIT with Bear Assumptions

**Assumptions:**
- FY2025 EBIT: CHF 233M (actual)
- Normalized EBIT (adjusting for 150bps tariff margin compression in 2026): CHF 215M
- Bear multiple: 25x EBIT (Spirax-level, industrial quality peer at lower growth)
- Add: net cash CHF 70M
- Shares: 12.3M

**Calculation:**
- EV = CHF 215M x 25x = CHF 5,375M
- Equity = CHF 5,375M + CHF 70M = CHF 5,445M
- FV per share = CHF 5,445M / 12.3M = **CHF 443**

### Cross-check: FCF-based

- FCF FY2025: CHF 98M (actual, depressed by capex)

### Interpretation

**MODERATE COUNTER:** The R1 thesis was directionally correct and prescient -- the stock has indeed fallen significantly. The business quality assessment (QS 80, Tier A) remains valid. However:

1. The R1 valuation needs updating with actual FY2025 data (FCF CHF 98M, not estimated CHF 165-175M; EPS CHF 14.77; EBIT CHF 233M; gross margin 60.7%)

## Recommendation to Investment Committee

**Before approving any position, the committee should resolve:**

1. **Recalculate FV using actual FY2025 data.** The R1 used estimated FY2025 numbers that were optimistic on FCF (estimated CHF 165-175M vs actual CHF 98M). All three valuation methods need rebasing.

2. **Quantify tariff impact.** At 39% tariff on Swiss exports, with 50% Americas revenue and Swiss manufacturing, what is the realistic EBIT margin impact? Can Belimo pass through 100% via pricing? Historical evidence suggests partial pass-through at best in year 1.

3. **Monitor Q1 2026 results.** Management explicitly warned tariff costs will hit Q1 margins. Wait for this data point before entry.

4. **Set realistic entry.** At CHF 652, MoS vs FA base (CHF 470) is -39% (overvalued). MoS vs DA bear (CHF 420) is -55%. Even recalibrating upward for data center growth, entry below CHF 500 seems necessary for adequate MoS.

5. **Assess data center position competitively.** Belimo is #1 in HVAC actuators but NOT #1 in data center liquid cooling. Danfoss, Siemens, Schneider, Emerson all compete. The moat may be narrower in the fastest-growing segment.
