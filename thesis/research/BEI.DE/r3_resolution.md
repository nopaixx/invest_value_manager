## Resolution of Key Conflicts

### 1. Fair Value: EUR 99 → EUR 87 (-12.1%)

**R1 method:** EV/EBIT 14.5x on normalized EBIT + OEY at 5% growth → EUR 99
**DA bear:** Forward EV/EBIT 11.5x + DCF bear → EUR 73
**Resolution:** Forward EV/EBIT at 12.5x on EUR 1,350M FY2026E EBIT + OEY at 2.5% growth

Rationale for EUR 87:
- FY2026 EBIT ~EUR 1,350M (margin 13.5% on EUR 10,000M revenue, per guidance)
- EV/EBIT 12.5x (split between R1's 14.5x and DA's 11.5x — growth stalled, not dead)
- EV = EUR 16,875M + net cash EUR 2,200M = equity EUR 19,075M
- Per share: EUR 19,075M / 221M = EUR 86.3 ≈ EUR 87
- Cross-check: OEY at EUR 800M FCF / 4.5% target yield = EUR 17,778M equity = EUR 80/share
- Weighted: 60% EV/EBIT (EUR 87) + 40% OEY (EUR 80) = **EUR 84** blended
- **Resolved FV: EUR 87** (using EV/EBIT as primary — FCF margin too low for OEY to be reliable)

### 2. Quality Score: Maintain QS 70 Tool (Tier B), remove +5 adjustment

**R1:** QS 70 → 75 (Tier A) with +5 for NIVEA #1 position
**DA:** Questioned adjustment given NIVEA growth collapse
**Resolution:** NIVEA's +0.9% growth and multi-year recalibration undermine the premium for #1 position. The market position exists but isn't generating compounder-level returns. QS stays at **70 Tier B**.

### 3. Growth: 5% → 2.5%

**R1:** 5% organic growth
**DA:** 2.4% actual, guided flat-to-slightly-positive
**Resolution:** 2.5% blended (Derma +10-12%, NIVEA flat, La Prairie flat, tesa +2%). Conservative but realistic for next 2 years. Recovery to 4%+ requires NIVEA recalibration success (2027+).

### 4. Entry Point: EUR 85-90 → EUR 72-75 (GATED)

At EUR 84.10 with FV EUR 87:
- MoS = 3.4% → **INSUFFICIENT** for Tier B (need ~20-25%)
- E[CAGR] = (87/84.10)^(1/3) - 1 + 2.5% + 1.2% = 1.1% + 3.7% = **4.8%**
- 4.8% E[CAGR] is BELOW both Tier A (12%) and Tier B (15%) thresholds
- **This is NOT buyable at current prices**

New entry: EUR 72-75 would give:
- MoS: 14-17% (still thin for Tier B, but with net cash fortress)
- E[CAGR]: (87/73)^(1/3) - 1 + 2.5% + 1.2% = 6.0% + 3.7% = 9.7%
- Still below 15% Tier B threshold. Need EUR 65 for E[CAGR] >12%.

**HARD GATE: Do NOT buy until H1 2026 results (August 2026) confirm either:**
(a) NIVEA growth returning (>3% organic), OR
(b) Derma growth sustaining (>10%), OR
(c) Margin trajectory restored (guided up for H2 2026)

## META-REFLECTION

### What Changed My Mind
The DA correctly identified that reaching the R1 entry target was NOT an automatic buy signal when the fundamentals deteriorated. The R1 assumed NIVEA would recover; it didn't. The R1 assumed margin expansion; management guided margins down. The stock is "cheap" relative to the old thesis but fairly valued relative to the new reality.

### Lesson for the System
**Standing orders need dynamic re-validation when the thesis changes.** If BEI.DE had an SO at EUR 85, it would have triggered on a WEAKER fundamental picture than when set. DNLM.L R4 re-validation (same session) is the correct pattern: when SO triggers, verify thesis is still valid before executing.
