## Business Understanding

### Modelo de Negocio

Beiersdorf is a 143-year-old German consumer staples company organized in two segments:

**Consumer (83% of sales, EUR 8.2B FY2025):**
- **NIVEA** (EUR 5.5B, 56%): World's #1 skincare brand. Mass market. Deodorant, body care, lip care, face care, sun care. FY2025 organic growth collapsed to **+0.9%** from +9.0% in FY2024. Monthly market share losses of 20-120bps in Western Europe through Jan 2026 (NielsenIQ data). Management undertaking "adjusted strategy" to "restore balanced portfolio and greater local relevance."
- **Derma / Eucerin+Aquaphor** (EUR 1.5B, 15%): Dermocosmetics via pharmacy channel. **+11.7% organic growth** — 5th consecutive year of double digits. The one bright spot.
- **La Prairie** (EUR 478M, 5%): Ultra-luxury skincare. **-4.5% organic** full year, though Q4 recovered to +3.8%. China exposure is the key swing factor.
- **Health Care / Hansaplast** (EUR 299M, 3%): Plasters and wound care. +9.3% organic — best year in company history.

**tesa (17% of sales, EUR 1.7B):**
- Industrial adhesive tapes and solutions. Electronics, automotive, construction.
- **+1.8% organic growth.** Automotive dragged by EV transition volatility.
- Higher EBIT margin (~16%) than Consumer (~13.7%).
- B2B with high switching costs (1,000+ adhesive solutions in automotive, 130+ tapes per EV).

**Revenue model:** Consumable/repeat purchase. 2.4% organic growth group (FY2025). FX neutral reported growth was ~0%.

### Unit Economics

- **Gross Margin: 57.7%** (FY2025) — down 80bps from 58.5% peak (FY2024). Still +19.7pp vs sector median. Signal: pricing power exists but weakening.
- **Operating Margin: 14.0%** (ex-special factors, FY2025). Up 10bps from FY2024. Guided DOWN slightly for FY2026. Low for a brand company — L'Oreal 20%+, P&G 22%, Henkel ~15%.
- **FCF Margin: 3.3%** (FY2025). TERRIBLE. Collapsed from 8.1% in FY2024. This is the critical issue.
- **Capital intensity: Medium.** Capex EUR 463M (4.7% of revenue). Capex/D&A 1.4x (normalizing from investment cycle).
- **R&D: 3.7% of revenue** (EUR 365M). Increasing — positive for innovation.

### FCF Collapse Analysis (THE CRITICAL QUESTION)

FY2025 FCF collapsed from EUR 794M to EUR 322M (-59%). Root causes from the annual report:

| Driver | FY2024 | FY2025 | Delta | Impact |
|--------|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| Gross Cash Flow | EUR 1,269M | EUR 1,075M | -EUR 194M | Operating cash generation declined |
| Working Capital Outflow | -EUR 38M | -EUR 290M | -EUR 252M | **PRIMARY DRIVER** |
| ...of which receivables | | +EUR 161M increase | | Channel mix? Timing? |
| ...of which payables | | -EUR 132M decrease | | Paid suppliers faster |
| Operating Cash Flow | EUR 1,231M | EUR 785M | -EUR 446M | |
| Capex | EUR 463M | EUR 463M | ~flat | |

**Verdict on FCF:** The collapse is primarily working capital driven (EUR 252M swing), not structural. Receivables grew 6.6% vs 0% revenue — this is a RED FLAG that could indicate channel stuffing, aggressive trade terms, or simply timing. Payables declined EUR 132M — potentially lost supplier leverage. Gross cash flow also declined EUR 194M, reflecting weaker operating performance.

**Is it temporary?** Probably partially. Working capital swings can reverse. But the receivables/revenue gap (6.6% vs 0%) is concerning and requires H1 2026 monitoring. If the pattern persists, it signals structural deterioration in cash conversion.

**Normalized FCF estimate:** Using 3-year average FCF margin of 5.3% on EUR 9.9B revenue = EUR 525M. This is the realistic baseline, not the EUR 794M peak or EUR 322M trough.

### Por Que Esta Barata

**Current price: EUR 75.54.** 52wH: EUR 127.45 (-40.8% from high). 52wL: EUR 71.10.

**Narrativa del mercado (why it's down 41% from high):**
1. **NIVEA growth collapse:** From +9% organic (2024) to +0.9% (2025). Western Europe losing share monthly (NielsenIQ data). Mass-market skincare facing private label pressure and premiumization trend (CeraVe, La Roche-Posay eating into Nivea's space).
2. **La Prairie structural weakness:** -4.5% full year. China luxury consumption still weak. Hainan/travel retail channel disrupted.
3. **FCF collapse:** EUR 794M to EUR 322M. Working capital blowout. Gross cash flow declining.
4. **Weak 2026 guidance:** Flat-to-slightly-growing revenue, margins DECLINING. Berenberg expects -30bps margin, UBS expects -70bps for Consumer.
5. **Tariff risk:** Trump tariffs on EU goods could impact transatlantic trade (Beiersdorf has 15%+ Americas revenue).
6. **Oil at $95+:** Input costs for packaging (petrochemicals), transport rising.
7. **Morgan Stanley downgrade:** Cut Beiersdorf + Henkel to "underweight" on margin and market share loss risk.

**Mi Contra-Tesis:**
1. **Derma is the future, NIVEA is being recalibrated.** Derma grew +11.7%, now EUR 1.5B (18% of Consumer). Pharmacy channel = structural moat vs mass-market competitors. NIVEA's adjustment is painful but management acknowledges it.
2. **FCF collapse is mostly working capital timing.** Receivables and payables swings can reverse in H1 2026. Normalized FCF ~EUR 500-550M on current revenue is realistic.
3. **Net cash EUR 2.4B + EUR 750M buyback at depressed prices.** Buying back 4-5% of shares at EUR 75 is value-accretive. Net cash provides massive downside protection.
4. **Stock at 10-year low.** At EUR 75.54, trading at levels not seen since 2017. This is pricing in permanent impairment of a 143-year-old brand portfolio.
5. **NIVEA still gained share in FY2025 despite growth collapse.** CEO said "Beiersdorf remained the fastest growing skin care company globally." Growth deceleration reflects category slowdown, not competitive loss.
