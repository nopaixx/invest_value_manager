# Counter-Analysis: BEI.DE (Beiersdorf AG)

## Fecha: 2026-03-06

## Resumen Ejecutivo

The R1 thesis for Beiersdorf as a quality compounder at EUR 85-90 entry has been MATERIALLY WEAKENED by FY2025 results and the devastating 2026 guidance. The stock has fallen to EUR 82.54 -- below the R1's entry target -- but for WORSE reasons than the R1 anticipated. NIVEA's growth collapsed to +0.9% (R1 expected cyclical recovery), La Prairie continued declining (-4.5%, NOT stabilizing), and critically, management guided 2026 EBIT margins BELOW 2025, reversing the margin expansion narrative that was the core of the R1 thesis. The thesis survives only partially: Derma growth remains strong (+11.7%), the balance sheet is a fortress, and the business is not structurally broken. However, the R1's fair value of EUR 99 is significantly too high given the new reality.

## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. "Growth deceleration FY2025 is cyclical, not structural"
- **R1 claim:** NIVEA gained market share even in Q3 2025 weakness. September 2025 bounced to +7.8% organic. Growth would normalize post-2025.
- **Evidencia en contra:** FY2025 NIVEA organic growth was just +0.9% for the full year. Nominal NIVEA sales FELL from EUR 5.6B (2024) to EUR 5.5B (2025). Management is now guiding 2026 as "flat to slightly positive" organic growth -- NOT a recovery. The CEO acknowledged a "difficult environment" in mass-market skincare. Management explicitly said the NIVEA "recalibration" will extend through 2026 AND 2027. This is NOT a one-quarter blip -- it is a multi-year structural challenge in the mass-market segment.
- **Key quote:** Beiersdorf is repositioning NIVEA "toward body care, deodorants and more accessible face-care products" -- this is a strategic pivot, not a cyclical recovery.
- **Severidad:** **HIGH** -- The core growth narrative that justified waiting for entry has been invalidated. The recovery the R1 expected at FY2025 results did not materialize.

### 2. "La Prairie is stabilizing (Q3 2025 +1.6%)"
- **R1 claim:** La Prairie returned to growth in Q3 2025 (+1.6%). If China recovery materializes, this is upside optionality at 30%+ EBIT margin.
- **Evidencia en contra:** Full year La Prairie was STILL declining at -4.5% organic, reaching EUR 478M (down from EUR 509M in 2024 and EUR 530M in 2023). The Q3 improvement was seasonal/one-off. Q4 2025 showed 3.8% organic growth, but the full year confirms continued weakness. Management noted "disruptions in the retail landscape negatively impact Q1 2026." The luxury skincare recovery is fragile and NOT confirmed.
- **Mitigating factor:** China e-commerce +36% and China overall +3% in Q4 are genuine positive signals. But La Prairie is now only EUR 478M (~5% of group) and the trajectory over 2 years is negative.
- **Severidad:** **MODERATE** -- La Prairie is showing some green shoots but is not yet stabilizing on a full-year basis. The R1's optimistic "stabilizing" claim was premature.

### 3. "Margin expansion from 13.9% toward 15%+"
- **R1 claim:** EBIT margin expanding from 11.8% (2020) to 13.9% (2024), trajectory toward 15%+ by FY2028. Management guided +50bps for FY2025 Consumer segment.
- **Evidencia en contra:** FY2025 EBIT margin reached 14.0% -- only +10bps improvement, not the +50bps originally guided (management trimmed to +20bps during the year, then barely delivered). CRITICALLY: 2026 guidance is for margin SLIGHTLY BELOW 2025 levels. This means margin will DECLINE in 2026 due to:
  - Raw material cost increases
  - Unfavorable FX
  - Limited fixed cost leverage on flat/declining revenue
  - Maintained marketing spend (cannot cut during brand repositioning)
- The margin expansion story is PAUSED at best, REVERSED at worst. Reaching 15%+ by 2028 now requires a recovery that has not begun.
- **Severidad:** **HIGH** -- Margin expansion was a core valuation driver. A margin decline in 2026 directly reduces fair value and invalidates the R1's base case trajectory.

### 4. "FCF improving rapidly (EUR 249M to EUR 794M)"
- **R1 claim:** FCF tripled from EUR 249M (2022) to EUR 794M (2024). Capex normalizing. FCF margin could reach 9-10%.
- **Evidencia en contra:** The FY2025 annual report shows Gross Cash Flow of EUR 1,075M but capex INCREASED to EUR 463M (from EUR 437M in 2024). H1 2025 FCF was EUR 501M vs EUR 555M H1 2024 -- a decline. FCF margin of ~8% is likely flat or slightly down for FY2025 given flat revenue and higher capex. The rapid FCF improvement story was a one-off normalization from depressed 2022-2023 levels, not a sustainable trend.
- **Severidad:** **MODERATE** -- FCF is still healthy at ~EUR 800M+, but the trajectory is flattening, not accelerating as the R1 implied.

### 5. "QS 75 (Tier A) with +5 adjustment for market position"
- **R1 claim:** Tool scored 70 (Tier B), adjusted to 75 (Tier A) for NIVEA's #1 position.
- **Evidencia en contra:** NIVEA's growth of +0.9% and the multi-year "recalibration" challenge this premium. The #1 brand position by volume does not automatically translate to pricing power when the mass-market skincare segment is under pressure from D2C brands and intense competition. Revenue CAGR is ~4% over 10 years -- this is not a quality compounder growth profile. ROIC spread of +5.7pp is MODEST compared to true quality compounders (ADBE 20pp+). FCF margin at 8% is below quality compounder standards. The stock should be evaluated as Tier B (QS 70), not Tier A.
- **Severidad:** **MODERATE** -- The +5 adjustment is debatable. At current prices, whether it is Tier A or Tier B matters less than the fundamental outlook, but it affects sizing and MoS expectations.

**R1 used:** Normalized EBIT at 14.5x multiple.
**DA uses:** Forward FY2026 EBIT at sector-trailing multiple.

**Assumptions:**
1. FY2026 revenue: EUR 9,900M (flat organic, slight FX benefit -- in line with guidance)
2. FY2026 EBIT margin: 13.5% (slightly below 14.0% per guidance, with raw material + FX headwinds)
3. FY2026 EBIT: EUR 1,337M
4. Multiple: 12x EV/EBIT (current sector trailing for consumer staples with no growth -- below the 12-16x range per valuation skill, reflecting zero growth + margin decline)
5. Adjustments: -1x for growth deceleration below market, +0.5x for net cash quality = 11.5x

```
EV = EUR 1,337M x 11.5 = EUR 15,376M
```
