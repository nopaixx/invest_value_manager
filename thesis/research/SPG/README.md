# Simon Property Group (SPG) — Investment Analysis

## What is this?

This folder contains a complete investment analysis of **Simon Property Group** (NYSE: SPG), the world's largest retail real estate investment trust (REIT). The analysis was done using a formal multi-stage pipeline designed to be rigorous and adversarial — not just finding reasons to buy, but actively trying to find reasons NOT to buy.

## The Documents

### 1. `thesis.md` — The Investment Thesis (R1)
**What it is:** The core analysis. Written by a fundamental analyst agent that scores the company on quality (0-100), estimates fair value, identifies growth drivers, and defines kill conditions (automatic sell triggers).

**Key findings:**
- Quality Score: 72/100 (Tier B — good but not great)
- Fair Value: ~$158-160
- Current price: ~$190 — meaning it's **overvalued** right now
- Entry price: $140 (where it becomes interesting)

### 2. `moat_assessment.md` — Competitive Advantage
**What it is:** An independent evaluation of the company's competitive moat — how defensible is the business? Written separately from the thesis to avoid confirmation bias.

**Key finding:** WIDE moat. Simon owns irreplaceable locations (you can't build a new mall next to theirs), has 96.4% occupancy, and generates returns well above cost of capital. The moat is real.

### 3. `risk_assessment.md` — Risk Identification
**What it is:** A dedicated search for what could go wrong. Categorizes risks by probability and impact. Looks for things the thesis might be ignoring.

**Key risks:** $29.2 billion debt, e-commerce secular decline, anchor tenant attrition, oil/recession impact on consumer spending.

### 4. `devils_advocate.md` — The Bear Case (R2)
**What it is:** An adversarial challenge to the thesis. A "devil's advocate" agent deliberately attacks every assumption the thesis makes and searches for evidence against buying.

**Key finding:** MODERATE COUNTER. Growth may be overstated, no real edge for us as investors (well-covered by Wall Street), and the entry price is too high. The DA reduced the fair value estimate.

### 5. `r3_resolution.md` — Conflict Resolution (R3)
**What it is:** Where the bull case (thesis) and bear case (DA) are reconciled. Each disagreement is resolved with a decision: accept, reject, or compromise.

**Result:** Fair value adjusted to $158 (from $160). Entry price $140. Growth rate 4%.

### 6. `committee_decision.md` — Investment Committee (R4)
**What it is:** The final gate before buying. Checks 10 mandatory requirements (quality score, valuation, risk management, portfolio fit, etc.). A single failure can block the investment.

**Result: WATCHLIST (CONDITIONAL)** — meaning "interesting but don't buy yet." Two hard failures:
1. No sector view document exists for real estate (required by our process)
2. Expected return (13.3%) is below our minimum threshold (15%)

## How to Read These Documents

**Start with `committee_decision.md`** — it gives you the verdict and why.

**Then read `thesis.md`** — the full investment case with numbers.

**Then `devils_advocate.md`** — the counter-argument.

**Then `r3_resolution.md`** — how we reconciled the two.

The `moat_assessment.md` and `risk_assessment.md` are supporting documents that feed into the thesis.

## Why This Process Exists

Most investors buy based on a single opinion: "I think this stock is cheap." This pipeline forces us to:

1. **Analyze** (R1) — build the case
2. **Attack** (R2) — try to destroy it
3. **Resolve** (R3) — find the truth between bull and bear
4. **Gate** (R4) — check 10 requirements before committing money

Historically, our devil's advocate step corrects fair values downward by ~17% on average. Without it, we'd overpay for most investments.

## Bottom Line

SPG is a world-class business at a price that doesn't give us enough margin of safety. At $140 (-26% from current) it becomes interesting. Until then, it stays on the watchlist.
