## Resolution of FA vs DA Disagreements

### 1. FFO Growth 5% vs 3.1% (DA HIGH) — ACCEPTED
DA is right. 2026 guidance midpoint $13.13 implies 3.1% growth from FY2025 $12.73. The FA's 5% includes dividend growth and NOI escalation assumptions that are forward-looking, not current. Refinancing headwinds ($0.25-0.30/share interest expense increase already guided) compress near-term FFO growth.

**Resolution: Expected Growth 5% → 4% (3-5% range, midpoint). This is generous to the FA — current guidance is 3.1%.**

### 2. Entry $170 Above 60/40 FV (DA HIGH) — ACCEPTED
The DA correctly identifies that $170 > $160 (60/40 FV). Buying above our own anti-bullish FV is inconsistent with the methodology. The FA used the $181 EV for entry calibration, but the 60/40 exists specifically to prevent bullish bias.

**Resolution: Entry $170 → $140. At $140, MoS = 11.4% vs $158 R3 FV. E[CAGR] = ~13.3%. This clears the 12% Tier B threshold and provides genuine MoS for a cyclical, leveraged REIT.**

### 3. Re-rating Component Speculative (DA HIGH) — ACCEPTED
At $170, re-rating to $181 was speculative. At $140, convergence to $158 is more realistic (13% upside over 3yr = 4.0%/yr). This is reasonable for a WIDE moat REIT trading at a discount.

**Resolution: Use $158 as convergence target, not $181.**

### 4. No Informational Edge (DA HIGH) — ACCEPTED PARTIALLY
Our FV ($158) is below consensus PT ($206) by 23%. We're NOT seeing value the market misses — we're applying stricter methodology. The edge, if any, is discipline: waiting for a pullback to $140 that consensus investors won't demand.

**Resolution: Accept thin edge. Position sizing should reflect: Tier B, 4% growth, MODERATE conviction.**

### 5. WIDE Moat Confirmed (DA LOW) — ACCEPTED
Both FA and DA agree moat is WIDE. DA correctly notes ROIC "widening" is distorted by EPS anomaly. Normalized ROIC spread ~3-4pp, still positive. The moat is real but NOT widening.

**Resolution: WIDE moat confirmed. Use normalized 3yr ROIC (~14%) not 2025 (17.7%).**

### 6. Governance Red Flags (DA MODERATE) — NOTED
$61.4M CEO compensation + 11.1% Say-on-Pay 2023 + Saks $100M write-off. These are real flags. David Simon is a legendary operator but the board needed shareholder revolt to fix comp. Family employment adds opacity.

**Resolution: Not a kill condition but -1 conviction. MODERATE → MODERATE-LOW conviction until governance improves or price provides more MoS.**

### 7. Correlated Risk Chain (DA MODERATE) — ACCEPTED
Oil + tariffs + recession all hitting consumer-facing REITs simultaneously. This is the key timing risk. The business survives a recession (96% occupancy, A rating, $9B liquidity), but FFO could drop 5-10%.

**Resolution: Entry at $140 (vs $170) provides buffer for this scenario. At $140, even a 10% FFO decline produces P/FFO of ~12x — still reasonable for the #1 retail REIT.**
