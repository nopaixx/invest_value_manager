## 2. Resolution — Point by Point

### 2.1 Pipeline Probability: DA wins — CRITICAL methodological flaw

The R1 treated "all 4 pipeline products succeed" as the base case. The DA correctly calculated P(all 4) = 27%. This is a BULL case, not a base case.

Proper probability-weighted approach:

| Products succeeding | Probability | Revenue impact (2028) |
|-------------------|-------------|----------------------|
| 4 of 4 | 27% | $1.9B (R1 bull) |
| 3 of 4 | 38% | $1.6B |
| 2 of 4 | 25% | $1.3B |
| 1 of 4 | 8% | $1.1B |
| 0 of 4 | 2% | $0.9B (decline) |

**Probability-weighted 2028 revenue:** (0.27 × 1.9) + (0.38 × 1.6) + (0.25 × 1.3) + (0.08 × 1.1) + (0.02 × 0.9) = 0.513 + 0.608 + 0.325 + 0.088 + 0.018 = **$1.55B**

This is 18% below the R1's base case $1.9B. The FV must reflect this.

**Resolution:** Use $1.55B probability-weighted 2028 revenue as base, not $1.9B.

### 2.2 CEO + President Departure: DA wins — CRITICAL R1 omission

The R1 completely missed that:
- CEO Brian Markison retired Dec 31, 2025
- President Paul Blanchfield also departed
- Mary Anne Heino (former CEO 2015-2024) is interim
- Permanent CEO search ongoing

A company executing its most critical strategic transition (single-drug to multi-product) with interim leadership is a material execution risk. The leadership vacuum makes pipeline execution LESS certain, not more.

**Resolution:** Add KC#10 (permanent CEO not appointed by Q3 2026 → execution risk escalates). Reduce pipeline probabilities by ~5pp each to account for leadership uncertainty.

### 2.3 PYLARIFY Decline: DA mostly wins — structural, not cyclical

- FY2025: -6.5%. Q4: -9.7%. Trend is accelerating down.
- Telix at $804M revenue with "sticky" customer relationships is a formidable, not marginal, competitor.
- The R1's "trough in 2026" assumption requires PYLARIFY to stabilize, but no evidence supports stabilization.

**Resolution:** Model PYLARIFY declining -8% to -10% annually through 2028, not flattening. This reduces the base from which pipeline products must grow.

### 2.4 PNT2003 Revenue: DA wins — overestimated

- Hatch-Waxman litigation outcome ~50/50 (not 60% favorable)
- Curium also developing generic Lutathera
- Realistic peak revenue: $70-150M (not $200-300M)
- Expected value: $50-75M (probability-weighted)

**Resolution:** PNT2003 contributes $50M probability-weighted in 2028 projections, not $150M.

### 2.5 Valuation: DA wins — both methods use same pipeline assumption

The DA correctly identified that the R1's DCF and EV/EBIT methods both rely on the same pipeline success assumption, making them NOT independent. Using trailing EBIT (what we KNOW) as a cross-check is more honest.

**Resolution:** Primary method = trailing EV/EBIT with pipeline probability overlay. DCF as secondary cross-check only (25% weight, not 60%).

### 2.6 Class Action: DA wins — material omission

Securities class action (Feb-Aug 2025 period) + Kahn Swick investigation (Jan 2026) = legal overhang. Not thesis-breaking for pharma, but legal costs + management distraction during critical pipeline execution period.

**Resolution:** Add KC#11 (settlement >$100M or adverse ruling). Factor ~$30-50M estimated legal cost/settlement into valuation.

## 8. Verdict

**WATCHLIST — binary transition at the wrong price.**

LNTH is a genuine transition story: from single-drug (PYLARIFY, declining) to multi-product radiopharmaceutical platform (OCTEVY approved, zanzalintinib PDUFA Dec, MK-6240 Aug, PNT2003 Jun litigation). The DA found 2 CRITICAL + 3 HIGH gaps that materially changed the thesis:

1. Pipeline "base case" was actually P(all 4) = 27% — a bull case
2. CEO + President departed during most critical transition
3. PYLARIFY decline accelerating (Q4 -9.7%), not stabilizing
4. Telix is $804M competitor, not marginal entrant
5. PNT2003 peak revenue overestimated 2-3x

The business is real: $350M FCF, net cash, #1 PSMA PET position, pipeline optionality. But at $78, the stock prices in most of the bull scenario while the base case (3-of-4 success, $1.55B revenue 2028) supports only $65-70.

At $50, the stock prices in meaningful pessimism and provides 23% MoS. The 52wL ($47.25) shows this price has been reached — it's not fantasy.

**Do not advance to R4 at current price.** Resume if: (a) price reaches $55 area, (b) permanent CEO appointed, AND (c) PYLARIFY Q1 shows stabilization.

## META-REFLECTION

### Incertidumbres
- OCTEVY was approved Mar 29 (after the DA). Commercial launch revenue in Q1-Q2 2026 will be the first real data point on pipeline execution. If OCTEVY ramps fast (>$30M first 6 months), it de-risks the transition thesis and FV could rise to $70-75.
- The 70/30 patent probability for PYLARIFY (holds vs collapses Aug 2026) is the single most important binary. If MSN wins Federal Circuit appeal, FV drops to $35-40 regardless of pipeline. This resolution should come in 2026 — it's a hard gate.
- Telix's growth trajectory ($804M → $970M) is the competitive risk that's hardest to model. If Telix reaches $1.2B by 2027, PYLARIFY decline accelerates beyond -10%/yr.

### Sugerencias
- LNTH should NOT be treated as a quality compounder. It's a special situation/transition story with binary outcomes. QS 62 Tier B is borderline — any further deterioration in ROIC (already 45%→16%) would push it to Tier C.
- The "pharma binary" tag suggested in the EXEL R3 applies equally here. Binary pharma names need separate MoS treatment regardless of QS.
