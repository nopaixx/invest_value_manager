## Business Understanding

### What Lantheus Does

Lantheus manufactures and distributes radiopharmaceutical diagnostics -- radioactive imaging agents used in PET and SPECT scans to detect cancer and other conditions. The company operates at the intersection of nuclear medicine, molecular imaging, and oncology.

**Revenue breakdown FY2025:**
| Product | Revenue | % Total | YoY Change |
|---------|---------|---------|------------|
| PYLARIFY (PSMA PET, prostate cancer) | $989M | 64% | -6.5% |
| DEFINITY (cardiac imaging) | $330M | 21% | +3.9% |
| Neuraceq (amyloid PET, Alzheimer's) | $51M | 3% | New (acquisition) |
| Other/Precision Diagnostics | $170M | 11% | Various |
| **Total** | **$1,540M** | **100%** | **+0.5%** |

### How It Makes Money

1. **Manufacturing moat**: Radiopharmaceuticals have extremely short half-lives (F-18 has 110-minute half-life). This means: (a) you cannot stockpile inventory, (b) you need cyclotron facilities NEAR the point of use, (c) distribution logistics are critical. Lantheus has built a network of manufacturing/distribution facilities that is extremely hard to replicate.

2. **Regulatory moat**: FDA-approved PET tracers require years of clinical trials and regulatory approval. PYLARIFY was the first FDA-approved F-18 PSMA PET imaging agent (May 2021). New entrants face 5-7 year development timelines.

3. **Reimbursement moat**: CMS/Medicare reimbursement for PYLARIFY is established. New competitors must navigate the 340B pricing system and reimbursement pathways.

### Unit Economics
- Gross margin: 61.1% (stable, +6pp vs sector median of 55%)
- FCF margin: 22.6% (strong for pharma)
- Capital intensity: Medium (cyclotron facilities require investment but not asset-heavy like traditional pharma manufacturing)
- R&D/Revenue: 11.5% (investing in pipeline)
- SBC/Revenue: 5.6% (rising -- monitor)

### Why It Is Cheap

**The market narrative: PYLARIFY is a one-product company facing peak revenue.**

Evidence supporting the narrative:
1. PYLARIFY revenue DECLINED 6.5% in FY2025 ($989M vs $1.058B in FY2024)
2. Q4 2025 PYLARIFY revenue declined 9.7% YoY -- accelerating decline
3. Competition intensifying: Illuccix (Blue Earth), other PSMA tracers entering market
4. 340B pricing resets creating pricing pressure
5. ROIC declining from 45.7% (2023) to 16.2% (2025)
6. 2026 guidance of $1.4-1.45B implies further TOTAL revenue decline (-3% to -6%)
7. Stock dropped from $111 to $47 (57% drawdown) before recovering to $72

**My counter-thesis:**

1. **The $1.4-1.45B guidance is CONSERVATIVE by design.** It excludes revenue from PYLARIFY TruVu (approved TODAY, launching Q4 2026), OCTEVY (PDUFA Mar 29), and MK-6240 (PDUFA Aug 13). Management's 2028 target of $1.8B revenue implies 10%+ CAGR from 2026 base, which requires pipeline execution.

2. **PYLARIFY TruVu changes the economics.** Approved today (March 7, 2026). Same diagnostic agent but with enhanced stability at higher radioactive concentrations. This means larger batch sizes, broader geographic reach, improved supply chain. It LOWERS manufacturing costs and EXPANDS addressable market. This is not a new product -- it is PYLARIFY with better unit economics.

3. **Pipeline is diversifying beyond prostate cancer:**
   - **OCTEVY** (PDUFA Mar 29): Oncology imaging from Evergreen acquisition. New diagnostic category.
   - **MK-6240** (PDUFA Aug 13): Alzheimer's disease imaging. TAM expansion into neurology as Alzheimer's disease-modifying therapies (Lecanemab, Donanemab) create demand for amyloid/tau PET imaging.
   - **PNT2003** (Lutathera generic, tentative approval received Mar 2, 2026): First THERAPEUTIC product. Full approval pending Hatch-Waxman litigation resolution (June 2026). Lutathera generated ~$1B for Novartis in 2024. Even 20-30% market share = $200-300M incremental revenue.

4. **FCF is real and substantial.** $354M FCF in FY2025, even in a declining revenue year. At $4.9B market cap, that is 7.2% FCF yield. The company used $300M for buybacks + $629M for acquisitions (Evergreen + Life Molecular Imaging) to build the pipeline. Capital allocation is aggressive but focused on building the next growth wave.

5. **Balance sheet is clean.** Net debt/EBITDA 0.7x. $359M cash + $750M undrawn revolver. Interest coverage 16.9x. They can fund the pipeline transition.

6. **The market is pricing 0.7% FCF growth forever.** Reverse DCF shows the current price implies near-zero growth. If Lantheus can grow FCF at even 5% (well below the 9.8% historical CAGR), fair value is ~$89. The asymmetry ratio is 3.45x favorable.

**Manual WACC calculation:**

- Capital structure: ~89% equity, ~11% debt

I will use 9.0% WACC (conservative) for DCF to account for pipeline execution risk.
