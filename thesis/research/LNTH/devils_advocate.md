# Devil's Advocate: LNTH (Lantheus Holdings)

## Date: 2026-03-23

## Calibration Anchor
- **Market price:** $76.43 implies 1.2% FCF growth (reverse DCF at 9.0% WACC, 2.5% terminal)
- **Historical FCF CAGR:** 9.8% (4yr)
- **FA thesis FV:** $88 (5% FCF growth assumption)
- **DA historical pattern:** Average correction -15.7%. Median -13.0%. 25/25 corrections negative.

**Anchor:** The market at $76.43 says LNTH is a low-growth specialty pharma company whose core product is in decline and whose pipeline is unproven. The FA must demonstrate why the market is wrong -- not merely assume it.

---

## Executive Summary

**The thesis has MATERIAL gaps that undermine confidence in the $88 FV.** The R1 completely missed the CEO departure (Nov 2025), the securities class action lawsuit, and the accelerating competitive threat from Telix ($804M revenue in FY2025, targeting ~$970M in 2026). The PYLARIFY decline is likely structural, not cyclical, and the pipeline probabilities are optimistic. However, the balance sheet is genuinely clean, FCF is real, and at $76 the market is pricing a dire scenario. The thesis survives as a speculative transition story but not at $88 FV.

**Verdict: MODERATE-STRONG COUNTER** -- 2 CRITICAL, 3 HIGH findings. FV should be revised downward to $70-75 range.

## Assumptions Challenged

### 1. "2026 Is the Trough Year -- Revenue Rebounds to $1.63B in 2027 and $1.90B in 2028"

- **Evidence against:** This is the CORE assumption and it requires ALL FOUR pipeline products to deliver on schedule. FDA diagnostic approval rates are high (~80-90%), but the thesis assigns 60-70% probability to each catalyst while treating the combined scenario as the "base case." The probability of ALL FOUR succeeding: 0.70 x 0.65 x 0.60 x 1.0 = 27%. That makes the "base case" actually a "bull case" in probability terms.
- **PYLARIFY decline is ACCELERATING:** Full year -6.5%, but Q4 was -9.7% YoY. Q3 PSMA imaging revenue grew only 1% (3% volume offset by -2% pricing). If Q1 2026 shows -12% to -15% PYLARIFY decline, the 2026 guidance of $1.40-1.45B is at risk.
- **Telix is growing AGGRESSIVELY:** Telix Pharmaceuticals hit $804M in FY2025 revenue and is guiding ~$970M for 2026. Telix has a "two-track" strategy using Illuccix for aggressive commercial contracting plus Gozellix for the reimbursed segment. UBS notes Telix's customer service makes their offering "sticky" and radiologists are "reluctant to switch back." This is NOT a weak competitor.
- **Severity:** **CRITICAL**
- **Resolution:** The investment committee must probability-weight the revenue projection. If 2028 revenue is $1.5B (2 of 4 products succeed) instead of $1.9B, DCF drops significantly. Demand scenario analysis with 1-of-4, 2-of-4, 3-of-4, and 4-of-4 pipeline success outcomes.

### 2. "PYLARIFY Has a Manufacturing and Logistics Moat Due to Short Half-Life"

- **Evidence against:** Telix reached $804M revenue in FY2025 -- they ALREADY replicated the logistics network. Telix has cyclotron partnerships and distribution infrastructure across the US. The "moat" of short half-life distribution is not unique to Lantheus; it is a BARRIER TO ENTRY that Telix has already crossed. Furthermore, Telix's Illuccix is a Ga-68 kit-based product that uses on-site generators, potentially EASIER to distribute than F-18 cyclotron-dependent products.
- **Illuccix pass-through reimbursement:** Illuccix pass-through expired July 2025, which means reimbursement has now normalized. However, Telix launched Gozellix with pass-through status (October 2025), giving them a reimbursement advantage for their NEWER product.
- **Severity:** **HIGH**
- **Resolution:** The moat needs to be downgraded from "manufacturing moat" to "incumbent advantage" -- real but narrowing. Telix is not a startup; they are an $800M revenue competitor with momentum.

### 3. "Fair Value is $88 Based on 5% Growth DCF + 15x Forward EV/EBIT"

- **Evidence against:** The DCF uses 9.0% manual WACC (tool gives 3.6% due to negative beta). While 9.0% is reasonable as a manual override, the growth assumption of 5% is the midpoint of the 5-7% range, which REQUIRES pipeline execution. Without pipeline: PYLARIFY declines 8-10% annually, DEFINITY grows 3-4%, other products are flat. Organic growth WITHOUT pipeline is actually -3% to -5%.
- **The EV/EBIT method uses forward 2027 estimates at 15x multiple.** But using forward earnings in a multiple-based approach AND growing them in a DCF is double-counting the growth optionality. The reconciliation gives 60% weight to DCF and 40% to EV/EBIT -- both rely on the same optimistic pipeline assumptions. This is not two independent methods; it is one assumption with two wrappers.
- **Current EV/EBIT is 16.0x** -- the market is ALREADY paying a premium multiple relative to the 10-14x specialty pharma range. The thesis suggests the stock is cheap, but at 16x current EBIT, it is valued at the TOP of the pharma range.
- **Severity:** **HIGH**
- **Resolution:** Use trailing EBIT (not forward) for the multiples method to provide a genuinely independent check. At 12x trailing EBIT ($330M normalized), EV = $3.96B, equity = $3.7B, FV = ~$54/share. This provides a floor, not a target.

### 4. "The Securities Class Action and CEO Departure Are Not Material"

- **Evidence against:** The R1 thesis DOES NOT MENTION either the securities class action lawsuit or the CEO departure. These are material omissions.
- **Securities class action:** Filed for the period Feb 26 to Aug 5, 2025, alleging Lantheus made "false and misleading statements" about PYLARIFY's revenue outlook and minimized competition and pricing risks. A separate investigation by Kahn Swick & Foti was announced Jan 31, 2026. While pharma class actions are common and often settle for modest amounts, the EXISTENCE of litigation creates: (a) legal costs, (b) management distraction during critical pipeline execution, (c) potential settlement payments.
- **CEO departure:** Brian Markison retired Dec 31, 2025. President Paul Blanchfield ALSO departed. Mary Anne Heino (former CEO 2015-2024) is interim CEO. A permanent CEO search is ONGOING. This means the company is executing its most critical strategic transition (single-product to multi-product) with interim leadership and a leadership vacuum at the President level. This is a material risk for execution.
- **Severity:** **CRITICAL**
- **Resolution:** Both items must be added to the thesis. The CEO transition must be added as a kill condition: "If permanent CEO not appointed by Q3 2026, execution risk escalates materially." Legal costs and settlement provisions must be estimated.

### 5. "PNT2003 (Lutathera Generic) Opens $200-300M Revenue Opportunity"

- **Evidence against:** Full approval depends on the 30-month Hatch-Waxman stay expiring in June 2026, which itself depends on the patent litigation outcome with Novartis. Novartis has deep legal resources and has ALSO sued Curium over a similar product. Generic pharma patent litigation outcomes are historically ~50/50. If Lantheus loses, PNT2003 cannot launch until the patent expires (potentially years later).
- **Even if approved:** Lutathera's US revenue is approximately $600-700M (Novartis does not break out by geography precisely). A generic would likely capture 20-30% share at 60-70% of brand price, implying $70-150M in revenue -- not the $200-300M the thesis projects. Generic radiopharmaceuticals face the same manufacturing/logistics barriers, and Novartis will likely compete on price and bundling.
- **Curium is ALSO developing a Lutathera alternative** via 505(b)(2) pathway, meaning even the generic market may fragment.
- **Severity:** **HIGH**
- **Resolution:** Reduce PNT2003 probability-weighted revenue. At 50% litigation win probability and $100M peak revenue (more conservative share + pricing assumptions), the expected value is $50M -- far less than the $150M projected for 2028 in the thesis base case.

## Independent Bear-Case Valuation

**Method: EV/EBIT on TRAILING normalized EBIT (different from FA's forward-looking DCF)**

Assumptions:
- EBIT: $317M actual FY2025 (not forward; using what we KNOW, not what we HOPE)
- Multiple: 11x (low end of specialty pharma range, reflecting: declining core product, interim leadership, class action overhang, pipeline uncertainty)
- Net debt: $260M
- Shares: 64.5M (diluted)

Calculation:
- EV = $317M x 11 = $3,487M
- Equity = $3,487M - $260M = $3,227M
- FV/share = $3,227M / 64.5M = **$50**

**Cross-check with DCF bear:** Tool bear case at 2% growth, 10.5% WACC = $71.25. But the tool's "bear" uses FCF of $350M which ASSUMES current FCF is sustainable. If PYLARIFY declines 15% and pipeline underdelivers, FCF could drop to $250-280M. At $265M FCF, 10.5% WACC, 2% terminal: FV ~$53.
