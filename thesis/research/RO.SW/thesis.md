## What Changed Since Original R1 (Feb 20, 2026)

### Price: CHF 376 --> CHF 321 (-15%)
- **Cause**: 100% US pharma tariff executive order (Apr 2), general market derating, oil/war macro
- **Switzerland carved out at 15% tariff** (vs 100% default) due to US-Swiss trade deal
- **Roche + Novartis pledged $73B combined US investment** over 5 years for onshoring
- **Diagnostics division MORE exposed** to tariffs (instruments/tests manufactured in Switzerland)
- **Implementation: July 31, 2026** for large pharma companies

### Pipeline: SIGNIFICANTLY IMPROVED
1. **Fenebrutinib (MS)**: 3 positive Phase III studies since R1
   - FENhance 1 (Nov 2025): Positive in relapsing MS
   - FENtrepid (Feb 2026): First drug in >decade to reduce disability in PPMS vs Ocrevus. Non-inferior + numerically 12% better than Ocrevus
   - FENhance 2 (Mar 2026): Positive in relapsing MS vs teriflunomide
   - **BUT**: Safety concern -- 1.4% fatal cases vs 0.2% Ocrevus (assessed unrelated but regulators will scrutinize)
   - **NET**: MAJOR catalyst materialized. Fenebrutinib is now the clear Ocrevus lifecycle successor
2. **Giredestrant (breast cancer)**:
   - FDA accepted NDA (Feb 2026), PDUFA date: Dec 18, 2026
   - persevERA trial MISSED primary endpoint (Mar 2026) -- did not improve PFS in broad population
   - BUT: evERA and lidERA positive -- giredestrant + everolimus reduced risk 44% (ITT) / 62% (ESR1-mutated)
   - NET: Mixed but regulatory path OPEN in ESR1-mutated subpopulation
3. **CT-388 (obesity)**:
   - Phase III (Enith1/Enith2) initiated Q1 2026 as planned
   - Phase II showed 22.5% weight loss at 48 weeks -- competitive with Wegovy/Zepbound
   - **NEW COMPETITIVE THREAT: Lilly's orforglipron** (oral GLP-1) approved Apr 1, 2026 at $149/month
   - CT-388 is injectable, 2-3 years from market. Oral GLP-1s are NOW. This REDUCES CT-388's peak sales potential
   - CT-388 differentiation must come from combo therapy (petrelintide) and potentially superior efficacy
   - **NET**: CT-388 still viable but competitive landscape TOUGHER than Feb. Reduce bull case accordingly

### Biosimilar Erosion: Confirmed Largely Behind
- Avastin CHF 1.0B (-17% YoY) -- well advanced erosion
- Herceptin CHF 1.0B (-22% YoY) -- well advanced erosion
- Combined legacy trio (Avastin/Herceptin/Rituxan) declining ~CHF 0.7B/yr -- unchanged from R1
- **NEW: Perjeta CHF 3.0B (-13%)** -- biosimilar competition starting 2026-27, but Phesgo (+48%) is lifecycle defense
- HER2 franchise expected to peak FY2026 at ~CHF 9B then decline

### Diagnostics: Stable, Not Exciting
- FY2025: +2% CER (-3% CHF). China pricing reforms still weighing
- Diagnostics Day May 12, 2026 in London -- potential catalyst for rerating segment
- 53 new tests, 6 digital solutions, 2 instrument platforms launched in 2025
- AI sequencing world record (full human genome in <4 hours)
- **Tariff exposure**: Diagnostics instruments MORE exposed to US tariffs than pharma drugs

### Macro Context
- Oil at $97-100, Hormuz crisis ongoing, recession probability 40-55%
- Pharma = DEFENSIVE -- healthcare spending is necessity
- CHF structural headwind persists (~50% revenue in USD)
- US pharma tariffs 15% for Switzerland (manageable, not catastrophic)
- **Positive**: Oil ceasefire talks resuming, recession probability may be peaking

## META-REFLECTION

### Incertidumbres/Dudas
- Fenebrutinib 1.4% fatal rate vs 0.2% Ocrevus: investigators assessed as unrelated to treatment, but regulatory scrutiny is CERTAIN. If FDA requires additional studies or restricts label, the Ocrevus lifecycle defense weakens significantly. This is the single biggest uncertainty in the thesis
- The 15% Swiss pharma tariff seems manageable, but the executive order allows escalation to 100% in 4 years if onshoring commitments are not met. This is a long-tail regulatory risk that could materially change the thesis
- CT-388 vs oral GLP-1s (orforglipron at $149/month): injectable vs oral is a meaningful convenience gap. CT-388 may need to show CLEARLY SUPERIOR efficacy (>25% weight loss vs orforglipron's ~15%) to justify injectable delivery. The combo with petrelintide is the differentiation path but Phase III data is 2+ years away
