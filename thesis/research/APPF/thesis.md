## Business Understanding

### What AppFolio Does

AppFolio provides a cloud-based property management platform for residential property managers, primarily serving the SMB segment (typically 50-5,000 units). The platform handles:
- **Core Solutions (~35% of revenue):** Property management software (accounting, leasing, maintenance, communication) on a per-unit subscription basis
- **Value-Added Services (~65% of revenue):** Electronic payments processing, tenant screening, insurance services, and other usage-based offerings

The business solves a critical problem: residential property management is operationally complex (maintenance, tenant communication, accounting, compliance, leasing) and traditionally managed with fragmented tools or legacy software. AppFolio provides a unified, modern platform that becomes the operating system of the property management business.

### Revenue Model

| Component | ~% Revenue | Type | Characteristics |
|-----------|-----------|------|-----------------|
| Core subscriptions | ~35% | Recurring per-unit | Predictable, sticky, grows with unit growth |
| Electronic payments | ~40% | Transactional | Per-transaction fees, ACH + credit card |
| Screening services | ~15% | Usage-based | Per-application fees |
| Insurance & other | ~10% | Usage-based | Renter's insurance, utility management |

**Key metric:** Revenue per unit is the true ARPU driver. As customers adopt more Value+ services, ARPU expands even without price increases. 96% of customers now use at least one AI-powered feature (Realm-X).

### Unit Economics

- **CAC:** Not publicly disclosed, but customer acquisition is primarily direct sales + marketing. S&M expense ~15% of revenue
- **Estimated LTV/CAC:** Very high. Once a property manager migrates 500+ units to AppFolio (weeks/months of data migration, staff training), switching costs are enormous. Implied retention >95%
- **ARPU growth:** Revenue grew 20% in FY2025 while units grew 8% -- implying ~12% ARPU expansion from deeper product adoption
- **Payback period:** Likely <18 months given gross margins >63%

## Competitive Moat

### Switching Costs (PRIMARY moat)

Property managers who adopt AppFolio migrate ALL their operational data: tenant records, lease agreements, maintenance history, financial records, vendor relationships. Migration takes weeks/months and requires staff retraining. Once embedded, the pain of switching is enormous. Implied customer retention >95%.

### Network Effects (SECONDARY moat)

- Tenant-facing portal creates bilateral stickiness (residents use the app for payments, maintenance requests)
- Data from 9.4M units improves AI models (Realm-X gets smarter with more data)
- Vendor marketplace creates ecosystem lock-in

### AI as Moat Amplifier

Realm-X is not just a feature -- it's a structural deepener of switching costs:
- **Realm-X Leasing Performer:** AI agent that responds to inquiries, schedules showings
- **Realm-X Maintenance Performer:** Diagnoses maintenance issues via image, creates work orders
- **Realm-X Flows:** Workflow automation that standardizes processes
- Users save 10 hours/week. Filling vacancies 5.2 days faster. Renewal rates +20%. NOI +2.8%

Once property managers build workflows around these AI agents, switching becomes even harder.

### Moat Risk

- **RealPage** (acquired by Thoma Bravo, now private): Larger customer base, strong analytics. But focuses on enterprise/large multifamily
- **Yardi:** Broad platform, strong in commercial. Legacy architecture disadvantage in AI
- **Buildium (RealPage subsidiary):** Competes directly in SMB segment. Less innovative
- **New entrants:** Could build modern PM software, but data migration + customer base = 3-5 year runway minimum
