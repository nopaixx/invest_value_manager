## Resumen Ejecutivo

The DECK thesis survives adversarial scrutiny but with SIGNIFICANT conditions. The core financial quality is genuine (ROIC 106%, GM 58%, net cash $1.7B, FCF $958M). However, the stock is now approaching our entry zone for reasons that are PARTIALLY thesis-deteriorating: HOKA US DTC is declining, tariff headwinds are larger than initially estimated ($150M COGS impact vs thesis's $25M net), short interest is rising (+14% MoM), and the competitive landscape has intensified. The R3 FV of $125 may need further revision downward to $115-120 given new evidence on tariff magnitude and HOKA deceleration. At $101.71, the market is pricing -1.5% FCF growth (per reverse DCF) which is excessively pessimistic, but the thesis's 8.5% base growth may also be optimistic given Q4 FY2025 HOKA growth slowed to 10% and DTC turned negative.

**The critical question for the committee: Is the stock cheap because the market is wrong, or cheap because it sees HOKA peaking?**

## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. HOKA Growth: 8.5% Base Assumption is Still Too Optimistic

**R3 assumes:** 8.5% blended revenue growth (HOKA mid-teens decelerating to low-teens, UGG mid-single-digits).

**Evidence in contra (updated March 2026):**

The growth deceleration has ACCELERATED since the R1/R2 analysis in February:

| Quarter | HOKA Growth | UGG Growth | Blended |
|---------|------------|-----------|---------|
| Q1 FY26 | +24.2% | +13.0% | +20.0% |
| Q2 FY26 | +11.1% | +10.1% | +14.0% |
| Q3 FY26 | +18.5% | +7.0% | +7.1% (total company) |
| Q4 FY25 (prior year) | +10% | +3.6% | ~7% est |

HOKA US DTC turned negative: Q4 FY25 -1.6%, Q2 FY26 -0.8%. This is the LEADING INDICATOR of brand health per the Under Armour analog. When DTC declines while wholesale remains positive, it signals consumer cooling BEFORE channel inventory adjusts.

**HOKA run specialty market share is now under pressure:** Per SGB Media, HOKA sales have fallen at run specialty retailers while Nike rebounds and Topo surges. Brooks and HOKA are neck-and-neck at ~23% each in adult running footwear -- HOKA is no longer clearly gaining share.

**The maximalist cushion trend is commoditizing:** New Balance, Nike (Vomero), On (Cloudmonster), ASICS, and Adidas ALL now offer max-cushion alternatives. HOKA's differentiation is eroding. Business of Fashion's analysis "Why HOKA Is Slowing and On Keeps Growing" identifies HOKA's failure to innovate beyond max-cushion as the core competitive issue.

**My revised estimate:** 6-7% blended growth is more realistic for FY27-28, not 8.5%. HOKA at low-teens (optimistic) + UGG flat-to-low-single-digits + tariff-driven demand destruction = 6-7%.

- **Severity:** HIGH
- **Resolution suggested:** Committee should model 6-7% base growth, not 8.5%. This reduces FV from $125 to approximately $110-115.

### 2. Tariff Impact is MUCH Larger Than Thesis States

**R1 thesis stated:** "$110M impact estimated FY26" with management having "reduced impact from $75M to $25M net."

**Updated evidence (March 2026):**

Deckers now anticipates **$150-200M increase in COGS** for FY2026 from tariffs. Vietnam (where 75%+ of DECK shoes are made) faces 20% tariffs, China 30%. This is NOT the $25M net impact the thesis claimed.

Management expects to offset ONLY HALF through price increases and cost-sharing, leaving **$75-100M unmitigated impact**. This represents:
- 150-200bps gross margin compression (vs thesis's 90bps estimate)
- ~$0.50-0.70 EPS headwind on ~$6.50 base EPS

**Price increase risk:** HOKA shoes at $150-200 are already at the premium end. Passing $10-15 price increases to consumers in a weakening consumer environment risks accelerating demand destruction. On Holdings and Nike offer alternatives at similar or lower prices.

- **Severity:** HIGH
- **Resolution suggested:** Revise margin assumptions. GM of 56-56.5% (not 57%) for FY26-27. This compounds with lower growth to reduce FV.

**Key difference the thesis relies on -- UGG as floor -- is now weakening:** UGG revenue growth decelerated from 13% (Q2 FY26) to 3.6% (Q4 FY25), and management guided UGG roughly FLAT for Q4 FY26. UGG DTC showed "major deterioration" per analyst commentary. The cash cow is showing signs of fatigue.

- **Severity:** MODERATE (maintained, not upgraded because UGG still generates massive cash flow regardless of growth)
- **Resolution suggested:** Tighten the KC. Instead of "HOKA <10% for 2 consecutive quarters," use "HOKA <10% for 1 quarter AND US DTC negative for 3 consecutive quarters." The DTC signal is earlier and more reliable.

### 5. Consumer Discretionary in Macro Headwind

**Current macro environment (March 2026):**
- Tariff uncertainty creating consumer confidence drag
- US consumer sentiment weakening
- Fed on hold, no near-term rate cuts to stimulate spending
- Oil prices elevated (adding to consumer cost pressures)
- Premium discretionary spending most vulnerable in slowdowns

DECK sells $150-250 shoes. In a consumer pullback, these are deferrable purchases. The thesis acknowledges this (beta 1.15, "NEUTRAL-CAUTIOUS" macro fit) but does not adequately price it into the growth assumptions.

**UGG seasonal risk:** UGG's seasonal Q3 peak (Nov-Dec) will occur against this macro backdrop. If UGG has a weak holiday season in late 2026, the combined HOKA deceleration + UGG weakness narrative could drive the stock significantly lower.

- **Severity:** MODERATE
- **Resolution suggested:** Factor into scenario weighting. Bear scenario probability should be 30-35%, not 25%.
