## Business Understanding

### What Nasdaq Does

Nasdaq operates across three segments:

**1. Capital Access Platforms ($2.14B, 41% of revenue)**
- **Index:** $827M (+17% YoY). Licenses the Nasdaq-100, Nasdaq Composite, and 1,200+ indices. ETP AUM record $882B with $99B net inflows in 2025. Revenue is AUM-linked (fee on assets) + derivatives trading royalties. This is the highest-quality revenue stream -- pure toll-booth economics.
- **Data & Listing Services:** ~$1.3B. Sells market data feeds to trading firms, financial institutions. 4,800+ listed companies pay annual listing fees ($50K-$250K/yr). Data is non-discretionary for market participants.

**2. Financial Technology ($1.85B, 35% of revenue)**
- **Anti-Financial Crime (Verafin):** $331M (+22% YoY). AI-powered fraud detection and AML compliance for 2,600+ banks. Enterprise signings quadrupled YoY. Fastest-growing segment.
- **Regulatory Technology (AxiomSL, from Adenza):** $428M (+12% organic). Regulatory reporting and risk management for banks. Mission-critical compliance software.
- **Capital Markets Technology (Calypso, from Adenza):** Part of broader FinTech. ARR $975M growing 14% organically. Trading and risk management platforms for capital markets firms.

**3. Market Services ($1.2B net, 23% of revenue)**
- Exchange operations: US equities, options, Nordic/Baltic exchanges.
- Revenue tied to trading volumes -- more cyclical than Solutions.
- Market share: #2 US equities (~20% share), #1 US options.

### Unit Economics

- **Subscription/recurring revenue:** ~80% of Solutions revenue is recurring (subscriptions, index licensing fees, data feeds, listing fees).
- **Switching costs:** Extremely high. AxiomSL regulatory reporting is embedded in bank workflows. Verafin AML systems integrate with core banking. Calypso trading platforms are mission-critical. Index licensing -- ETFs cannot easily switch benchmarks.
- **Revenue per employee:** ~$640K (8,300 employees, $5.2B revenue). High productivity.
- **Capex/Revenue:** Very low (0.3-0.4x of depreciation). Asset-light model.
- **FCF margin:** 24.1% (2025). Stable at 23-25% over 4 years. FCF $2.0B.

## Moat Assessment

### WIDE Moat -- Multi-layered

1. **Exchange Monopoly/Oligopoly:** US equity exchanges are a duopoly (NYSE + Nasdaq). Starting a new exchange requires SEC approval, massive technology investment, and network effects (liquidity begets liquidity). Barriers: effectively infinite.

2. **Index Licensing Lock-in:** The Nasdaq-100 and Nasdaq Composite are irreplaceable benchmarks. $882B in ETP AUM is tied to these indices. ETF providers (Invesco QQQ alone has $300B+) cannot switch to a different index -- the brand IS the product. Revenue grows automatically with AUM (market appreciation + inflows). This is the purest toll-booth in finance.

3. **Regulatory Technology Moat:** AxiomSL regulatory reporting is embedded in bank workflows across 110+ countries. Banks cannot easily switch regulatory reporting systems -- the switching cost is measured in years and regulatory risk. Compliance is non-discretionary.

4. **Anti-Financial Crime Network Effects:** Verafin processes data from 2,600+ banks. The more banks on the platform, the better the fraud detection AI performs (network effects on data). Larger banks joining creates flywheel: more data = better detection = more banks join. Enterprise signings quadrupled YoY.

5. **Data Moat:** Market data feeds are proprietary. NDAQ owns the data for orders/trades on its exchanges. Competitors cannot replicate this data -- it only exists because trades happen on Nasdaq venues.

**Moat durability:** 15+ years for exchange/index. 10+ years for regulatory tech. 8+ years for anti-financial-crime (AI competition possible but data advantage is durable).

## Risk Assessment

### Primary Risks

1. **Leverage risk (HIGH):** Net debt/EBITDA 2.9x. If EBITDA growth stalls or interest rates stay elevated, deleveraging timeline extends. Interest coverage 7.1x is adequate but not comfortable. Goodwill $15.2B (46% of assets) means any impairment would be large.

2. **Integration execution risk (MEDIUM):** Adenza was $10.5B acquisition. 42 cross-sells to date but full synergy realization still 2-3 years out. If AxiomSL/Calypso client retention disappoints or cross-sell pipeline stalls, growth narrative breaks.

3. **Index AUM concentration (MEDIUM):** $882B AUM growing with market. A sustained bear market (-30%+) would reduce AUM and index licensing revenue by ~$100-150M. 70% of index revenue is AUM-linked.

4. **Regulatory risk (LOW-MEDIUM):** Exchange regulation is generally protective (barrier to entry), but SEC could impose fee caps or change market structure (e.g., 24/7 trading, tick size changes, competing exchanges granted preferential access).

5. **Anti-financial-crime competition (LOW):** Verafin faces competition from LexisNexis (RELX), Actimize (NICE), SAS. However, Verafin's data network effect (2,600+ banks) is a significant moat. Enterprise tier-1 bank penetration is the growth vector -- if Verafin fails to win large bank deals, 22% growth normalizes to 10-12%.

6. **Macro/geopolitical (MEDIUM):** War in Middle East + oil crisis + potential recession affects trading volumes (Market Services, 23% of revenue). However, volatility actually HELPS exchange volumes (more trading = more revenue). Net effect ambiguous.
