## Business Understanding

### What ADP Does

ADP solves the most operationally complex compliance problem every business faces: paying employees correctly, on time, and in compliance with federal, state, and local tax regulations across 11,000+ US jurisdictions. This is a "must-have" -- you cannot operate a business without it.

### Revenue Model

| Segment | FY2025 Rev | % Total | Description |
|---------|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Employer Services (ES)** | ~$14.1B | 68% | Cloud-based payroll, HR, benefits, talent, time, compliance |
| **PEO Services** | ~$6.5B | 32% | Co-employment: ADP becomes employer of record for HR, benefits, workers' comp |

**Revenue composition:**
- ~85% recurring/subscription (PEPM + platform fees)
- ~15% PEO pass-throughs (zero-margin but creates deep lock-in)
- Float income: ~$1.31B/year (investing ~$37B avg daily client funds)

### Unit Economics

```
Revenue Per Client: ~$20,000/year (blended SMB to enterprise)
Retention Rate: 92.1% (FY2025) -- implies ~8% annual churn
Implied LTV: ~$250K per client lifetime
Est. CAC: ~$15-20K (sales force + channel)
LTV/CAC: ~12-15x (exceptional)
Payback Period: ~1 year
```

### Why the Business Model is Exceptional

1. **Regulatory moat**: 11,000+ tax jurisdictions, 75+ years of accumulated compliance knowledge. Impossible to replicate.
2. **Switching costs**: Migrating payroll is operationally terrifying. One error = employees don't get paid. 92% retention proves this.
3. **Data network effect**: 42M workers dataset enables ADP National Employment Report, ADP Pay Insights, AI-powered benchmarking.
4. **Float income**: $37B avg daily client funds at 3.4% yield = $1.31B pure-margin income. Scale advantage.
5. **PEO lock-in**: ADP becomes legal employer of record. Switching away is even harder than changing payroll software.

### Margin Structure and Trends

```
Gross Margin:     42.6% (2022) -> 44.7% (2023) -> 45.4% (2024) -> 46.0% (2025)  [+340bps/3yr]
Operating Margin: 23.1% (2022) -> 25.0% (2023) -> 25.8% (2024) -> 26.3% (2025)  [+320bps/3yr]
FCF Margin:       15.4% (2022) -> 20.2% (2023) -> 18.7% (2024) -> 21.4% (2025)  [+600bps/3yr]
SBC/Revenue:      1.2% -> 1.2% -> 1.3% -> 1.3%  [Stable, minimal dilution]
```

**Critical DA correction (prior R2):** A substantial portion of margin expansion is driven by float income recovery ($400M -> $1.3B from near-zero to elevated rates). Float income is ~24% of operating profit, NOT "6% of EPS" as the original R1 framed it. If rates normalize to 2-2.5%, approximately half the margin expansion reverses.

### Capital Requirements

- **Asset-light**: Capex ~3% of revenue, declining (capex/depreciation 3.2x -> 2.7x)
- **Working capital**: Net source of cash (client prefunding)
- **Goodwill**: 6.1% of assets (rising from 3.6% due to WorkForce Software acquisition, but still low)
- **OCF/Net Income**: 1.1-1.2x consistently (high-quality earnings)

## Why Is It Cheap? (-36% from 52-Week High)

### Market Narrative (The "Triple Squeeze")

1. **Employment stagnation (CRITICAL)**: PPC (pays per control) flat for all of FY2026. ADP employment report: Feb 2026 only +63K private jobs. Professional services shed 30K. This is ALREADY happening.
2. **AI / SaaSpocalypse narrative**: $800B software selloff dragged ADP. "If AI replaces workers, fewer paychecks" narrative.
3. **Interest rate uncertainty**: Float income at $1.31B depends on rates. Fed at 3.50-3.75%, oil crisis complicates cuts. Each 100bp = ~$350-400M float impact.
4. **Multiple compression**: P/E from 30x+ to 20.2x. Morningstar downgraded moat to NARROW (was WIDE). FV cut from $297 to $231.

### My Counter-Thesis

**On employment stagnation:**
- PPC flat is already priced at 20.2x P/E. If PPC recovers even modestly (+1%), revenue growth accelerates to 7-8%.
- ADP has pricing power: routinely raises prices 2-3% without retention impact.
- Revenue growth of 6% DESPITE flat PPC shows pricing power + new client wins are working.

**On AI/SaaSpocalypse:**
- Payroll is REGULATORY COMPLIANCE, not discretionary software. You cannot eliminate the need to pay employees.
- AI reduces headcount (fewer paychecks) but does NOT eliminate the payroll function. Each remaining employee still needs payroll.
- ADP is USING AI offensively: ADP Assist (anomaly detection, compliance automation) + AI Agent Marketplace (Mar 2026) + 42M worker dataset for AI training.
- Per-seat risk is ~7-9% of revenue in a severe 10% headcount reduction scenario (including second-order float balance effect per DA correction).

**On float income:**
- Portfolio is LADDERED (3-5 year duration) -- insulates for 2-3 years from cuts.
- Current macro: oil crisis + inflation makes aggressive rate cuts UNLIKELY in near term. FOMC Mar 18 held at 3.50-3.75%.
- Even at 2% yield (recession), float income ~$750M -- still substantial.
- Lower rates stimulate hiring (more PPC), partially offsetting float decline.

**On multiple compression:**
- 20.2x P/E for a company with 50.7% ROIC, 92% retention, 12.5% EPS CAGR is historically cheap.
- But DA correctly noted: if growth settles at 5-6%, 20x P/E is APPROPRIATE for a mid-single-digit grower (compare: PAYX 22.3x, PG 23x, KO 22x).
- I do NOT assume mean-reversion to 29x. I use 18-22x as the sustainable range.
