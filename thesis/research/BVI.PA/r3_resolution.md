## 2. Resolution — Point by Point

### 2.1 Organic Growth: DA mostly wins → 5.5-6.0%

The R1 used 6.5% post-FY2025 BEAT. The DA argued deceleration trend (10.2% → 6.7% → 6.3%) and Marine normalization.

FY2025 came in at 6.5% organic — validating the R1 for THIS year. BUT:
- Marine +16.2% is unsustainable (shipbuilding supercycle peak)
- Consumer Products only +3.5% (China/Taiwan weakness)
- WTO trade forecasts revised down (tariffs)
- Oil ceasefire reduces energy-driven inspection urgency

**Resolution:** 6.0% for FY2026 (give credit for FY2025 momentum), 5.0% normalized thereafter. DA's 5.5% average is more defensible than R1's 6.5% for projections.

### 2.2 EV/EBIT Multiple: DA wins → 15.5-16x

R1 used 17x. DA argued 15.5x with specific deductions:
- Wendel -2x (structural overhang, EUR 2.6B to sell, placements at discount)
- Margin gap -1x (BVI 16.3% vs ITRK 18.1%, SGS 15.3%+)
- Governance -0.5x

FY2025 results showed margin expanding to 16.3% (+51bps), narrowing the gap slightly. But Wendel remains structural — Wendel still at ~21% with years of selling ahead. The Sep 2025 lockup has now expired (mid-March 2026) and Wendel can sell again.

**Resolution:** 16x EV/EBIT (split between DA's 15.5x and R1's 17x, giving credit for margin expansion but Wendel still structural).

### 2.3 Wendel Overhang: DA wins — structural, not temporary

This is the DA's strongest finding after governance. The numbers are clear:
- Wendel still holds ~21% = ~EUR 2.5B to sell
- Placements consistently at discount (Sep 2025: -3.5%)
- Forward sale struck at EUR 27.25 = effective price ceiling
- Buyback EUR 200M vs Wendel EUR 2.5B = absorbs <8%
- 35% voting rights with 21% capital = governance complexity post-exit

**Resolution:** Wendel is a STRUCTURAL discount until position drops below 10%. This persists through 2027-2028 minimum. The multiple reflects this (-1.5 to -2x vs clean peers).

### 2.4 Governance/Integrity: DA wins — critical R1 omission

The DA found documented integrity failures that the R1 body IGNORED:
- China corruption: 53% of workforce identified as having committed corruption
- ISO 17021 conformity assessment procedure violations
- Odebrecht director connection

For a TRUST-based business (certification = trust), this pattern is material. The R1 had a generic KC "major regulatory scandal" but the risk assessment documented specific incidents.

**Resolution:** QS adjusted downward -3 for governance (DA recommendation). QS 70 → 67. Still Tier B. KC strengthened to include accreditation revocation risk.

### 2.5 FV = Consensus = No Edge: Confirmed

R1 FV EUR 33 = analyst consensus EUR 33.40-33.58. No informational edge. The DA correctly flagged this (Error #49 pattern). Our 9/9 DA correction history averaged -16%.

**Resolution:** FV must be below consensus. EUR 29 provides ~12% below consensus — that's our conservatism edge, not informational edge.

### 2.6 FY2025 Results: R1 refresh wins — earnings CONFIRMED strong

FY2025 came in strong post-DA: organic 6.5% (sector-leading), margin 16.3% (+51bps), FCF EUR 824M, EUR 200M buyback. This PARTIALLY invalidates the DA's more bearish growth assumptions for the near term.

**Resolution:** Give credit for FY2025 execution but maintain conservative forward growth (5.5-6.0% normalized) given Marine normalization + tariff headwinds.

## 3. R3 v2 Fair Value Calculation

### Method 1: EV/EBIT Normalized (60% weight)

**Bear case:**
- FY2026 EBIT: EUR 1,050M (5% organic, margin 16.0% — slight compression from Marine normalization)
- Multiple: 14x (Wendel peak selling + tariff uncertainty)
- EV = EUR 14.7B. Net debt EUR 1.7B. Equity = EUR 13.0B / 443M = **EUR 29.35**

**Base case:**
- FY2026 EBIT: EUR 1,120M (6% organic, margin 16.5% continued expansion)
- Multiple: 16x
- EV = EUR 17.9B. Equity = EUR 16.2B / 443M = **EUR 36.60**

**Anti-bullish-bias (60/40):** (EUR 29.35 × 0.60) + (EUR 36.60 × 0.40) = 17.61 + 14.64 = **EUR 32.25**

### Method 2: OEY (40% weight)

FCF FY2025: EUR 824M. Normalized: ~EUR 800M (haircut for working capital timing).

**Bear OEY** (5.5% target, Wendel drag): EUR 800M / 0.055 = EUR 14.5B equity / 443M = **EUR 32.80**
**Base OEY** (4.5% target, quality TIC): EUR 800M / 0.045 = EUR 17.8B equity / 443M = **EUR 40.15**

**Anti-bullish-bias:** (EUR 32.80 × 0.60) + (EUR 40.15 × 0.40) = 19.68 + 16.06 = **EUR 35.74**

### Reconciliation

| Method | Anti-Bias FV | Weight | Weighted |
|--------|-------------|--------|----------|
| EV/EBIT | EUR 32.25 | 60% | EUR 19.35 |
| OEY | EUR 35.74 | 40% | EUR 14.30 |
| **Weighted Average** | | **100%** | **EUR 33.65** |

Mechanical average is EUR 33.65. BUT:
- DA's EUR 29 used more conservative inputs and had specific justification for each deduction
- The governance discount (-3 QS, -0.5x multiple) is real and not in the mechanical calculation
- Wendel overhang structurally prevents the stock from trading at the theoretical FV

**Applying Wendel structural discount (~12%):** EUR 33.65 × 0.88 = **EUR 29.60**

**R3 v2 Fair Value: EUR 29** (rounded down, aligning with DA's recommendation and prior R3).

The FY2025 results were strong enough to maintain EUR 29 (prior R3) rather than revise down further. The R1 refresh to EUR 33 was optimistic — it gave too much credit to a single good year while under-weighting structural drags.

## 8. Verdict

**WATCHLIST at EUR 23 — solid TIC compounder with structural overhang.**

Bureau Veritas is a genuine quality business: sector-leading organic growth (6.5% FY2025), ROIC 19.3% expanding, FCF EUR 824M, regulatory moat. The DA was MODERATE COUNTER — it didn't break the thesis but correctly identified three structural drags: Wendel overhang (years to resolve), governance pattern (documented, material for trust company), and growth deceleration (Marine normalization + tariffs).

The R1 refresh to EUR 33 was optimistic post-FY2025 BEAT. The DA's EUR 28-29 is more defensible. I resolve at EUR 29 — maintaining the prior R3 level. FY2025 strength prevents a downward revision but doesn't justify the R1's upgrade.

At EUR 26.34, the stock offers 9% MoS — insufficient. At EUR 23, it offers 21% MoS with genuine margin of safety against all three structural drags. The 52wL is EUR 24.54 — entry requires a modest new low, plausible if Wendel places another block or macro deteriorates.

**Do not advance to R4 at current price.** Resume if EUR 24 area or after Wendel drops below 15%.

## META-REFLECTION

### Incertidumbres
- The Wendel structural discount (12%) is a judgment call. If Wendel accelerates exit (completes by end 2027), the discount evaporates and FV jumps to EUR 33+. If Wendel gets stuck at 15%+ for years, the drag persists.
- Governance risk is real but unquantifiable. A single certification failure leading to fatalities would crush the stock regardless of valuation. The 53% China corruption finding is concerning for a trust company but hasn't impacted operations — yet.
- The Lotusworks acquisition (EUR 375M, data center QA) announced Apr 6 is POSITIVE and not captured in the R1 refresh. Could add 1-2% revenue growth from data center certification demand.

### Sugerencias
- BVI.PA fits naturally in a TIC basket with ITRK.L (already held). The two have different geographies (BVI: global/France, ITRK: UK-centric) and different margin profiles. If basket formalizes, BVI could enter at EUR 23 as the second name.
