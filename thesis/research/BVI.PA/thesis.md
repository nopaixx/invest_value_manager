## Business Understanding

### What Bureau Veritas Does

Bureau Veritas is one of the "Big Three" global Testing, Inspection, and Certification (TIC) companies (alongside SGS and Intertek). Founded 1828. Provides conformity assessment services across virtually every industry.

**Problem Solved:** Governments and industries need independent third parties to verify regulatory compliance and safety standards. ~60-70% of revenue is MANDATORY (legally required testing). The remaining 30-40% is voluntary but driven by competitive pressure (supply chain requirements, investor demands, ESG reporting).

**Revenue Model:** Asset-light services. Mix of regulatory/mandatory testing, voluntary certification, project-based inspection. ~65-70% recurring or repeat revenue.

## Pricing Power Assessment (European Pricing Power Basket Question)

**Does BVI have genuine pricing power?**

**YES -- but moderate, not exceptional.**

Evidence:
1. **Gross margin 72.2% stable for 4 years** -- No price erosion despite competition

3. **Regulatory mandate creates price inelasticity** -- Customers cannot refuse mandatory testing
4. **Margin expansion** -- 16.0% to 16.3% while growing organically = pricing + efficiency
5. **No client concentration** -- 400K+ clients, no one can negotiate from strength

**Limitations:**
- Voluntary testing (~30-40%) has competitive pricing pressure
- Not as strong as Intertek (18.1% margin) in pricing discipline
- TIC is not a "luxury" pricing power (like Ferrari) -- it is "regulatory toll booth" pricing power

**Conclusion for European Pricing Power basket:** BVI fits as a "regulatory pricing power" play, not a "brand premium pricing power" play. The pricing power is STRUCTURAL (comes from regulation, not brand) and thus highly durable. This is arguably MORE reliable than brand-based pricing power because it does not depend on consumer sentiment.
