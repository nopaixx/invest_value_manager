## Moat Classification: WIDE

### Upgrade Rationale (from NARROW bordering WIDE to WIDE)

FY2025 results confirmed what was previously uncertain:
1. **ROIC trajectory widening:** 14.6% (2021) -> 17.6% (2022) -> 16.2% (2023) -> 19.1% (2024) -> 19.3% (2025). Five consecutive years above WACC (7.1%), with EXPANDING spread (+12.2pp in 2025).
2. **Organic growth 6.5%** = sector-leading. Gaining market share while improving margins = moat IS generating excess returns.
3. **Margin expansion to 16.3%** under LEAP|28 closing gap with peers (Intertek 18.1%).
4. **Gross margin stability at 72.2%** for 4 years = no competitive erosion.

The prior assessment cited only 4 years of ROIC data as insufficient for WIDE. With FY2025 adding a 5th consecutive year of expanding spread AND the structural argument (196-year-old company with regulatory-mandated demand), the evidence base is now sufficient.
