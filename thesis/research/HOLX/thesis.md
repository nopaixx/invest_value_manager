- Current price: $75.41 (99.2% of base deal price)
- Deal premium at announcement: ~46% over pre-leak close
- Expected close: H1 2026
- Regulatory approvals pending

**Why this is NOT investable for our fund:**

1. **Upside capped at $0.59/share (0.8%) on base price** -- trivial return
2. **CVR worth up to $3/share** is contingent on breast health revenue milestones in FY2026/2027 -- speculative, non-tradable
3. **Maximum total return of $3.59/share (4.8%)** even with full CVR -- well below our 12% E[CAGR] minimum
4. **Break risk is real** -- regulatory challenge or financing failure would send stock back toward $52-55 (pre-deal level), a 30%+ loss
5. **This is merger arbitrage, not fundamental investing** -- outside our investment mandate
