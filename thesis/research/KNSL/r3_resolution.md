## DA Challenges — Disposition

### 1. Bear Case MoS 2.1% — Tightest in System History (CRITICAL) — **ACCEPTED**

DA is correct. At the original entry of $310, bear FV $350 gave only $40 cushion. With Jefferies at $312 and Cantor at $360, the "bear case" was essentially the CONSENSUS base case. This is unacceptable for any tier.

**Resolution:** Entry revised $310 → $280. At $280: bear FV $320 (DA-revised) gives 12.5% cushion. Still tight but consistent with ACGL precedent (bear MoS ~15%).

### 2. BVPS Growth 19-20% Assumption (HIGH) — **PARTIALLY ACCEPTED**

DA argues 14-17% is more realistic given ROE normalization (26.4% → 20-22%) and soft cycle. I agree the 19-20% is optimistic but the DA's low end (14%) is too pessimistic — KNSL's expense ratio advantage (20.8% vs 35%) is structural, not cyclical. This protects ROE in soft markets better than peers.

**Resolution:** Use 16% BVPS CAGR (midpoint of DA's 14-17% vs thesis 19-20%). 2027E BVPS: $114 (vs thesis $121, vs DA $112).

### 3. P/B 4.5x vs ACGL 1.5x (HIGH) — **PARTIALLY ACCEPTED**

The 3x premium IS excessive if ROE normalizes to ACGL levels (~20%). But KNSL's expense ratio advantage is wider and more durable than ACGL's. Correct multiple: 3.5-4.0x (not 4.5x thesis, not 1.5x DA comparison).

**Resolution:** Use 3.75x P/B as base case (midpoint of thesis 4.5x and DA-suggested 3.0-3.5x).

### 4. Growth Deceleration — Deliberate or Structural? (MODERATE) — **ACCEPTED as PRIMARY RISK**

New data since R1 (insurance sector refresh Apr 6):
- Jefferies downgraded to UNDERPERFORM Mar 19 (PT $392→$312)
- BMO also downgraded (PT $418→$348)
- E&S market growth: 13.5% → 7.8% → **3% in H2 2025**
- AM Best revised outlook Positive → Stable
- KNSL Q4 non-property growth 10.2% (decelerating from 13.3%)
- Short interest surged +46% to 10.9% (8.8 days to cover)
- CFO sold $1.9M + Director sold $1.5M at $378-380 (above current $345)

**This is the most important DA finding.** The growth deceleration is NOT just property. Non-property growth is also slowing (22% → 13.3% → 10.2% Q4). If casualty pricing peaks in 2026 (as Jefferies argues), total growth could be 3-5% for 2-3 years.

**Resolution:** Model E&S soft cycle as 2-3 year headwind. GWP growth assumption: 5-8% (not 12-15%). This flows through to lower float growth → lower investment income → lower ROE → lower BVPS CAGR.

### 5. QS +15 Adjustment (MODERATE) — **PARTIALLY ACCEPTED**

**Resolution:** Treat as Tier B for sizing/MoS purposes. QS 73 (accept +12 instead of +15: ROE +8, GM gap +3, market position +1). Tier B MoS: 20-25%.
