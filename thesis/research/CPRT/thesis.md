## Business Understanding

### Model of Business

**Problem solved:** When a vehicle is damaged beyond economical repair (total loss), the insurance company needs to dispose of it efficiently to recover salvage value. This requires physical storage (yard space), logistics (towing), technology (online auction platform), and a global network of buyers (dismantlers, recyclers, exporters, dealers). Copart provides this end-to-end solution.

**Revenue model:** Fee-based marketplace. Copart does NOT buy and resell vehicles -- it acts as an agent/consignor. Revenue comes from:
- **Service fees** from insurance companies (consignors): storage, towing, processing, title
- **Auction fees** from buyers: buyer premiums, membership fees, gate fees
- Revenue is ~85% from insurance consignors, ~15% from non-insurance (Blue Car: banks, rental, fleet, dealer)

**Unit economics:**
- Vehicles are consigned, NOT owned -> near-zero inventory risk
- Incremental vehicle through existing yard = very high marginal contribution
- Capital intensity is in LAND (yards), not in working capital or inventory
- LTV/CAC: effectively infinite for insurance contracts (multi-year exclusive agreements, switching costs enormous)
- Network effects: more buyers -> higher ASPs -> more consignors -> flywheel

**Revenue split (FY2025):**
- US: ~82% of revenue ($3.8B)
- International: ~18% ($0.8B)
- Non-insurance (Blue Car): growing 27%+ YoY
