## Business Understanding

### What Gartner Does

Gartner is the tollbooth on enterprise IT purchasing decisions. Every major technology vendor wants to be evaluated in the Magic Quadrant; every CIO reads it before making a $50M+ technology purchase.

**Revenue breakdown (FY2025, $6.5B total):**

| Segment | Revenue | % Total | Growth | Nature |
|---------|---------|---------|--------|--------|
| Research | ~$4.9B | 75% | +2-3% (but +4% ex-federal) | Subscription, multi-year. 95%+ retention. $5.2B CV. Crown jewel. |
| Conferences | ~$800M | 12% | +1-3% | Event-based. Stable. |
| Consulting | ~$850M | 13% | **-13-15% guided** | Project-based. COLLAPSING. CFO: "unprecedented project deferrals." |

### Unit Economics
- Revenue per employee: ~$394K ($6.5B / ~16,500 employees)
- Contract Value: $5.2B (forward visibility)
- Client retention: ~95%+ (was 84% headline, but wallet retention historically >100%)
- Wallet retention: **degrading** -- was 103%, now lower (not explicitly disclosed in recent quarters -- flag)
- Capital intensity: Asset-light (~1% capex/revenue)
- Buybacks: $2B+/year, reducing share count ~8% annually

|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|---------------|-------|

## Why Is It Cheap? (-84% from ATH, -64% from 52wH)

### Phase-by-Phase Decline
1. **2024 H2 - 2025 H1:** SaaSpocalypse selloff + DOGE fears. Multiple compressed from 35-40x to 20-25x P/E. (~-50%)
2. **2025 H2:** DOGE contract losses confirmed. CV growth slowed. Guidance below consensus. (~-20% more)
3. **Feb 3, 2026:** Q4 earnings miss + flat FY2026 guidance + consulting collapse. **Stock fell 31% in ONE DAY.**
4. **Feb-Mar 2026:** Iran war risk-off + continued selling. Stock reached 52wL $139.18.

### Market's Bear Case
1. **AI replaces Gartner advisory** -- ChatGPT/Perplexity can answer CIO questions for free
2. **Consulting is dying** -- 13-15% decline, "unprecedented project deferrals"
3. **Revenue growth is zero** -- guided flat FY2026
4. **Per-seat model at risk** -- if AI reduces IT headcount at client firms, Gartner loses seats
5. **Forrester collapsing** -- seen as validation that the entire advisory model is dying

### My Contra-Thesis

**AI disruption -- LEGITIMATE but OVERSTATED for the core Research segment:**
- The Magic Quadrant is NOT a "search query." It is a PROPRIETARY methodology: hundreds of analyst-hours, vendor briefings, customer references, scoring frameworks. ChatGPT cannot replicate this
- CIOs need ACCOUNTABILITY: "We chose this vendor based on Gartner's recommendation" protects careers. ChatGPT provides no such cover
- Retention 95%+ proves clients are NOT churning to AI -- yet. THIS IS THE #1 METRIC TO WATCH
- AI is simultaneously a TAILWIND: 200K+ AI conversations with clients in 2025. Gartner is THE advisor enterprises consult on AI strategy
- HOWEVER: Consulting IS being disrupted by AI. This is real, structural, and properly concerning

**Flat revenue -- CYCLICAL, not structural:**
- FY2026 includes significant US federal headwinds (DOGE). Ex-federal CV growth is +4%
- Revenue deceleration: 7.9% -> 6.1% -> 3.7% -> ~0.6% guided. Clear trough
- Management expects CV acceleration through 2026
- Enterprise buying cycles lengthened due to macro uncertainty -- this reverses

**Forrester collapsing is BULLISH for Gartner:**
- Competitor dying = market consolidation. Last man standing gets the clients
- Gartner's relative moat STRENGTHENS as alternatives disappear
