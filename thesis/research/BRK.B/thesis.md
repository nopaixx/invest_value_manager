Berkshire Hathaway is a diversified conglomerate built on three pillars:

- $373B cash/T-bills (earning ~5% = $18-19B/yr)

- **QS adjustment magnitude is large (+28 pts).** While each component is quantitatively justified, a 28-point adjustment is at the extreme end. The conglomerate distortion is real and well-documented, but I may be too generous in correcting for it. If the "true" QS is 50-55 (Tier C) rather than 62 (Tier B), the MoS requirement increases.

### Sugerencias para el Sistema

- **Conglomerate QS Module:** The quality_scorer.py tool systematically misprices conglomerates (BRK, L3Harris, Danaher, etc.) because ROIC, FCF margin, and insider ownership metrics are all distorted by holding company structure.
