## Business Understanding

### Modelo de Negocio

Hermes International is a 188-year-old French luxury house operating as a Societe en Commandite par Actions (limited partnership by shares). The family (three branches -- Dumas, Guerrand, Puech -- via H51 holding) controls 67% of shares and ALL management decisions through the commandite structure. This governance moat prevents hostile takeovers, activist pressure, and strategic pivots away from scarcity.

**Problem solved:** Social signaling, craftsmanship heritage, and exclusivity for the ultra-wealthy. A Birkin bag is a store of value, social currency, and investment that appreciates on the secondary market.

### The Scarcity Model (What Makes Hermes UNIQUE)

1. **Production intentionally below demand.** ~5,800 artisans, each trained 2+ years, each bag 15-20 hours by hand. Capacity IS the constraint.
2. **100% DTC distribution.** No wholesale, no department stores, no discounting. Ever.
3. **Allocation system.** Customers invest EUR 5,000-50,000+ in qualifying purchases (scarves, RTW, home) over 1-3 years before being OFFERED the opportunity to buy a Birkin. This inverts the buyer-seller dynamic.
4. **Secondary market premium.** Birkin bags trade at premiums on the secondary market (though this has compressed from 2.2x to 1.4x -- see Risk Assessment).
5. **Veblen pricing.** Price increases of 8-10% annually with ZERO demand erosion. Higher prices increase desirability.

## META-REFLECTION

### Incertidumbres/Dudas
- ROIC declining from 64% to 49% -- is this cash accumulation (benign) or genuine operational degradation? The balance sheet signals say benign (receivables/inventory tightening), but the APAC deceleration contributes. Cannot cleanly separate without detailed invested capital breakdown.
- Birkin resale premium data is from Nov 2025. Has the compression continued, stabilized, or reversed in Q1 2026? More recent data would sharpen the moat assessment.
- The 92% dividend yield from yfinance is a data error. Actual yield ~0.94% (EUR 18 / EUR 1,905). Tool flags correctly.

### Sugerencias para el Sistema
- For luxury companies, add "secondary market health" as a moat indicator (resale premium tracking). This is specific to Veblen goods and would complement the standard 5-source framework.
- Consider tracking generational taste shift data as a long-term moat threat category.
- The WACC divergence between calculated (7.2%) and tool (9.6%) is significant. For net-cash companies with EUR 12.2B cash, the tool's WACC may be overstated because it uses a default Kd of 5.5% on a trivial debt stack. The calculated WACC is more accurate here.

### Anomalias Detectadas
- yfinance dividend yield anomaly persists (92%). Tool correctly flags YIELD_ANOMALY.
- QS tool jumped from 78 (Feb 20) to 88 (Mar 6) solely because EPS data became available. This is a data availability issue, not a fundamental change. Highlights importance of manual adjustment protocol.

### Preguntas para Orchestrator
1. The basket deadline (Apr 4) creates tension with valuation discipline. Recommendation: keep basket alive with RACE.MI only. The theme is multi-year; forcing a purchase of an overvalued stock to meet a 30-day rule would violate first principles.
2. Should the moat assessor's "WIDE (not Ultra-Wide)" classification change the FV? My view: no. The moat is still exceptionally strong. The early erosion signals are 10-15 year threats, not near-term FV adjustments. FV EUR 1,350 stands.
