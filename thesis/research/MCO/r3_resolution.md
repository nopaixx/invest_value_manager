### 3. No Informational Edge vs Consensus (DA HIGH) — ACCEPTED
FA FV $390 is 32% BELOW consensus mean $547. This is actually edge — the market is MORE bullish than us, not less. The DA's framing is backwards. Our edge is NOT that MCO is undervalued — it's that we recognize the premium is excessive at current levels while consensus doesn't. We're looking for a pullback that consensus doesn't expect.

**Resolution: Edge exists in discipline (waiting for pullback), not in price target.**

### 4. Insider Selling = Neutral (DA LOW) — ACCEPTED

**Resolution: Insider signal neutral. Do not use as positive factor.**

### 5. AI Threat to MA Segment (DA MODERATE) — NOTED
MA (47% rev) faces real AI competition. MIS (53% rev) is protected by regulation. This justifies a blended multiple, not a pure-monopoly premium. Already reflected in 60/40 methodology.
