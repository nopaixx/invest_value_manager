## Business Understanding

### What Moody's Does (2-minute version)

Moody's is a financial toll-booth: every time a corporation, sovereign, or municipality issues debt globally, it needs at least 2 credit ratings. Moody's and S&P Global provide ~80% of those ratings. The business splits into two segments:

**Moody's Investors Service (MIS)** -- 53% of revenue, $4.1B FY2025
- Credit ratings on bonds, loans, structured finance
- Revenue grows with debt issuance volume (structural growth as global debt expands)
- Adjusted operating margin: 63.6% (highest in our universe)
- Transaction revenue (new issuance) ~50%, recurring (monitoring fees) ~50%

**Moody's Analytics (MA)** -- 47% of revenue, $3.6B FY2025
- Risk management software, credit research, data, KYC/compliance tools
- 97% recurring revenue (subscription model)
- Decision Solutions (fastest growth, double-digit), Research & Insights, Data & Information
- Adjusted operating margin: ~32-33%, expanding toward 34-35% guided for 2026
- Private credit is a major growth vector (~75% growth in H1 2025)

### Revenue Quality

- **Recurring/Subscription: ~75% of total** (97% of MA, ~50% of MIS from monitoring fees)
- **Deferred Revenue: $1.7B (+9.3% YoY)** -- strong forward visibility
- **FCF Margin: 33.4%** (FY2025), guided $2.8-3.0B for FY2026 (~35-37%)
- **SBC/Revenue: 3.0%** -- moderate, stable

### Unit Economics

Extraordinarily capital-light:
- Capex/Revenue: ~3% (Capex/D&A declining from 2.2x to 1.2x -- investment phase ending)

## Moat Assessment

### Moat Rating: WIDE (among the widest in our universe)

**1. Regulatory Moat (Ratings) -- IMPENETRABLE**
- SEC designates Nationally Recognized Statistical Rating Organizations (NRSROs)
- Only 10 NRSROs exist; only 3 matter (S&P, Moody's, Fitch)
- Basel III, Solvency II, and Dodd-Frank require rated securities for institutional portfolios
- New entrant cannot replicate 100+ years of ratings history and institutional trust
- Switching cost: an issuer who stops using Moody's faces higher borrowing costs (investors demand familiar ratings)

**2. Duopoly Economics**
- MCO + SPGI = ~80% of global ratings market
- Fitch holds ~15% but is private (Hearst) and less profitable
- Pricing power: MCO can raise fees because issuers MUST get rated, and only 2-3 options exist
- Gross margin 74.4% (+4pp expansion in 4 years) confirms pricing power

**3. Data Network Effects (Analytics)**
- Moody's Analytics serves 15,000+ institutions globally
- Risk models built on Moody's data create switching costs (recalibrating models = years of work)
- 97% recurring revenue in MA confirms stickiness
- Private credit expansion (75% growth H1 2025) leverages existing data infrastructure

**4. Brand / Reputation**
- "Moody's rating" is a universally recognized quality signal in capital markets
- 115+ years of history creates institutional inertia
- Government bonds, structured products, and corporate debt all rely on Moody's assessments

### Moat Durability Assessment

| Threat | Probability | Impact | Timeline | Moat Response |
|--------|-------------|--------|----------|---------------|
| New NRSRO gains share | Very Low | Medium | 10+ years | Regulatory + institutional inertia protects |
| AI replaces ratings | Very Low | High | 10+ years | Ratings are trust/regulatory-based, not compute-based |
| Regulation breaks duopoly | Low | Very High | 5-10 years | Post-2008 calls for reform resulted in MORE regulation, not less |
| Private credit bypasses ratings | Low | Medium | 3-5 years | MCO is GAINING from private credit (ratings + analytics for private lenders) |
| Pricing backlash (issuers) | Low | Low | Ongoing | Issuer pays model has faced criticism for decades; no viable alternative emerged |

### Method 1: Owner Earnings Yield (Primary for Tier A -- 60% weight)

```
FCF (FY2025): $2.58B
Depreciation: ~$0.54B
Maintenance Capex estimate: $0.54B x 1.1 = $0.59B
Owner Earnings = $2.58B - $0.59B + $0.54B = $2.53B
(Simplified: FCF ~= OE since capex roughly equals depreciation)

Market Cap: $77B
Owner Earnings Yield = $2.53B / $77B = 3.3%

Expected Growth: 10%
OEY + Growth = 3.3% + 10% = 13.3%
WACC: 11.6%
Spread: +1.7pp (positive but slim)
```

- Forward OEY = $2.9B / $77B = 3.8%

## Kill Conditions

1. **Ratings market share falls below 30%** (currently ~40%). Would signal structural loss of duopoly power. Monitor via NRSRO market share data.
2. **Regulatory change removes or weakens NRSRO designation** -- e.g., new legislation allowing non-NRSRO ratings for institutional portfolios. This would destroy the regulatory moat.
3. **ROIC falls below WACC for 2 consecutive years** (currently 25.6% vs WACC 11.6%). Would signal the business economics have fundamentally deteriorated.
4. **Revenue decline >5% for 2 consecutive quarters** (excluding one-off adjustments). Would signal debt issuance freeze beyond normal cyclicality.
5. **Net Debt/EBITDA exceeds 3.5x** (currently 1.3x). Would signal balance sheet stress, especially dangerous if combined with issuance decline.
6. **Moody's Analytics growth falls below 5% for 2 consecutive years** (currently 9%). MA is the growth engine and diversification away from cyclical ratings. Sustained deceleration would reduce the compounding story.
