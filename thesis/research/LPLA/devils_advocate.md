## Resumen Ejecutivo

The LPLA thesis identifies a genuine secular opportunity (IBD channel consolidation) but contains critical overestimations in three areas: (1) the +35 QS adjustment, the largest ever attempted in this system, lacks adequate self-skepticism and should be +22 to +25, not +35; (2) Commonwealth integration economics are worse than modeled, with $54B in asset defections already realized and full conversion risk ahead; (3) the FV of $365 overestimates normalized earnings and applies a premium multiple that is not justified for a Tier B company in integration mode. My independent bear-case valuation produces $240-260 range. The probability-weighted FV is $295.

## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. QS Adjustment +35 points (Tool 33 to Adjusted 68)

- **The thesis argument:** FCF is distorted by acquisitions, leverage is temporary, EPS CAGR is GAAP-depressed, market position deserves full credit.
- **Evidence in contra:**
  - This is the LARGEST QS adjustment in the system's history. The previous maximum was +20 (ERIE, which was then CUT to +12 by DA). Historical DA tracker shows that large QS adjustments are systematically over-optimistic.
  - **FCF adjustment (+9 points):** Partially valid. But "Adj EBITDA proxy FCF" of $2.28B uses management's adjusted EBITDA, which excludes acquisition-related amortization, SBC, and restructuring. The thesis uses company-defined adj EBITDA without independent verification. True owner earnings are lower. A more conservative proxy: Adj EBITDA ($2.91B) minus maintenance capex ($250M) minus interest ($380M) minus SBC (~$180M) minus amortization of intangibles (~$250M) = ~$1.85B. FCF margin ~10.9%, which scores 5-6/10, not 8/10. **Adjustment should be +5, not +9.**
  - **Leverage adjustment (+8 points):** Using adj EBITDA to calculate leverage is circular when adj EBITDA itself is the contested metric. The debt is REAL ($7.3B). Even at 1.6x adj EBITDA, this is acquisition-funded debt that must be repaid. For comparison: pre-acquisition LPLA had <$3B debt. **Adjustment should be +5, not +8.**
  - **EPS CAGR adjustment (+6 points):** Valid in direction but the "17.8% adj EPS CAGR" cherry-picks FY2023-2025. Include FY2021-2022 (pre-rate-hike) and the CAGR is lower. Also, 4.7M shares diluted the base by ~6%, which the thesis acknowledges but does not adequately penalize. **Adjustment should be +4, not +6.**
  - **Market Position (+8 points):** Valid. #1 IBD is indisputable.
  - **GM Trend (+3) and GM Premium (+1):** Partially valid. The pass-through structure explanation is correct.
  - **NET EFFECT: Justified adjustment is approximately +22 to +25, yielding QS 55-58 (Tier B, lower end).** Not 68.
- **Severidad:** CRITICAL
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Committee should re-evaluate QS at 55-58 range. This changes MoS requirements upward.

### 2. Commonwealth Integration = Base Case Success

- **The thesis argument:** "Atria precedent shows successful execution." Commonwealth retention assumed at 80%+.
- **Evidence in contra:**
  - **653 advisors lost** (22.5% headcount) through Dec 2025
  - **$54B assets defected** (~18% of $305B AUM)
  - Raymond James captured 33% of departures, Kestra 19%, Cambridge 11%
  - LPL's own "90% retention target" is measured by ASSETS, not headcount -- a deliberate misdirection. The thesis reports "80%+ retention target met" for Atria without distinguishing assets vs headcount.
  - Full platform conversion scheduled Q4 2026 -- this is the HIGHEST RISK PERIOD for additional departures as advisors face forced technology migration
  - LPL CEO "not surprised" by departures -- but the synergy model assumed $415M run-rate EBITDA from Commonwealth. If 18-22% of assets leave, synergies are reduced to ~$330-340M (proportional haircut).
  - **Atria is NOT a clean precedent:** Atria was a holding company structure (multiple sub-brands) with different advisor relationships. Commonwealth was a single, tight-knit culture firm. The cultural mismatch is fundamentally different.
- **Severidad:** CRITICAL
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Reduce Commonwealth synergy assumption from $415M to $320-340M. Add KC: "Commonwealth asset retention drops below 78% by Q4 2026 conversion."

### 3. Normalized EPS of $23-25 by FY2027

- **The thesis argument:** FY2025 Adj EPS $20.09 + Commonwealth synergies + organic growth - rate headwind = $23-25.
- **Evidence in contra:**
  - Commonwealth synergies are overestimated (see above). Haircut: -$1.0-1.5/share from thesis
  - Integration costs may extend into 2027 if conversion timeline slips (thesis acknowledges this risk in meta-reflection but does not model it)
  - G&A expenses rising 4.5-7% in 2026 (management guidance) -- not fully reflected in normalized model
  - Share count: 80M diluted is aggressive. If buybacks don't resume until 2027, dilution from ongoing equity comp and potential further issuance could push to 82-83M
  - Rate headwind may be worse: thesis assumes -$0.5-1.0/share, but if Fed cuts 3x (not 2x), client cash revenue impact is -$1.2-1.5/share
  - **My estimate for normalized FY2027 EPS: $20-22** (vs thesis $23-25)
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Use $21 midpoint for valuation, not $24.

### 4. "Wide Moat" Assessment -- Overstated for Current Moment

- **The thesis argument:** 97%+ retention, scale cost advantages, switching costs.
- **Evidence in contra:**
  - The 97%+ retention is LPL's OVERALL historical figure. Commonwealth retention is demonstrably LOWER at 77.5% headcount -- proving that the moat is NOT universal across acquired advisor bases
  - Altruist Corp's AI tax-planning tool (Hazel) and similar fintech challengers are disrupting the value proposition that justifies advisory fees. Multiple analysts downgraded LPLA on "displacement anxiety"
  - Fee compression threat: McKinsey and Deloitte forecast 10-15bp/year fee decline in wealth management. LPL's advisory model charges ~1% AUM -- this is under structural pressure from robo-advisors charging 0.25-0.50%
  - Citigroup specifically cut LPLA citing "recruiting competition" -- the cost of acquiring advisors is rising, which compresses the LTV/CAC ratio the thesis estimates at >10x
  - The moat is NARROWING in the context of AI disruption and fee compression, not widening
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Downgrade moat from "WIDE" to "NARROW -- widening on scale, narrowing on technology disruption." Reduce fair multiple accordingly.

### 5. Fair Multiple of 15-16x Normalized Earnings

- **The thesis argument:** Historical range 10-18x, peers at 12-14x, LPL deserves premium.
- **Evidence in contra:**
  - LPL has NEVER traded at a sustained 15-16x on ACTUAL delivered earnings (vs adjusted/projected)
  - Peer comparison: RJF 14x, SF 12x, AMP 13x. LPL is IN integration mode with elevated debt and execution risk -- this deserves a DISCOUNT to steady-state peers, not a premium
  - Current trailing P/E: 26.7x on depressed GAAP. On normalized $21 EPS: 13.9x. The market is ALREADY pricing close to 14x normalized -- there is minimal upside
  - Integration-era companies historically trade at a 10-15% discount to their peer group. Apply 12-13x, not 15-16x
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Use 13x normalized for base case, 11x for bear case.
