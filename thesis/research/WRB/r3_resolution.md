## 2. Resolution — Point by Point

### 2.1 Soft Market CR History: DA wins — R1 FACTUAL ERROR

The R1 claimed "WRB maintained sub-92% CR through 2017-2019 soft market." The DA found actual data: WRB CR was 94.9-95.9% in Q4 2017-2018. Reinsurance segment hit 109.3%.

This is not a disagreement of interpretation — it's a factual error that overstated WRB's soft market resilience. The true picture: WRB was BETTER than industry (94-96% vs 100%+) but not dramatically so. The "sub-92%" only emerged in the hard market post-2020.

**Resolution:** Soft market CR = 94-96% (not sub-92%). This directly feeds into ROE and FV.

### 2.2 Normalized ROE: DA mostly wins → 16%

- R1 used 18% (75th percentile of 12-23% range)
- DA argued 15-16% (mid-cycle reality: WRB ROE was 12-14% in 2016-2019)
- Post-hard-market ROE of 20-23% is cyclical peak, not normal

The 5-year hard market (2020-2025) was the most favorable P&C environment since 2002-2006. Extrapolating 18% ROE into a soft market is optimistic. However, WRB's investment portfolio yield has structurally improved (record $1.43B NII in FY2025 at 4%+ yields), partially insulating ROE from underwriting cycle. True mid-cycle: 15-17%.

**Resolution:** Normalized ROE = 16% (split between DA's 15% and accounting for structurally higher NII).

### 2.3 Mitsui Sumitomo: Partially DA, partially R1

- R1: strategic floor at ~$70 (Mitsui buying at $70-73)
- DA: Amlin precedent ($1.6B writedown) shows Mitsui can overpay

Both have merit. Mitsui's $3.8B / 15.7% stake IS a real strategic commitment with board seat. But WRB's stock has since dropped to $67 — below Mitsui's avg cost. Mitsui is underwater.

**Resolution:** Mitsui is a POSITIVE signal (strategic validation) but NOT a price floor. If WRB's fundamentals deteriorate, Mitsui won't prevent the stock from falling to $50-55.

### 2.4 Beta: DA mostly wins → 0.75

R1 adjusted from raw 0.36 to 0.65. DA argued 0.80 for cat-exposed insurer. Peer P&C insurers: 0.7-0.9.

WRB's low raw beta reflects low equity market correlation (insurance earnings driven by underwriting/investment, not economic cycle). But cat exposure + social inflation + investment portfolio duration create real systematic risk.

**Resolution:** Beta 0.75 (split between R1's 0.65 and DA's 0.80). Ke = 4.3% + 0.75 × 6% = 8.8%.

### 2.5 No Informational Edge: DA wins

R1 FV $70 = analyst consensus $69.67. We see exactly what the market sees. The "decentralized model" advantage is 55 years old — it's priced in.

**Resolution:** Our edge = discipline (waiting for soft market entry price), not insight. FV must be AT or BELOW consensus.

### 2.6 Soft Market Cycle: DA confirmed by subsequent data

Since the DA (Mar 23), the insurance sector refresh (Apr 8) confirmed:
- Reinsurance Apr renewals: Asia-Pac property cat down -20%
- Social inflation: median nuclear verdict $44M (3x since 2020)
- GL rate increases +5.6-9% (partially offsetting but not fully)
- Combined ratio heading higher (AM Best: 99%+)

The soft market is REAL and ACCELERATING exactly as the DA warned.

**Resolution:** Model CR deterioration from 90.7% (FY2025 record) toward 94-95% by FY2027.

## 8. Verdict

**WATCHLIST at $53 — quality insurer fairly valued entering soft market.**

WRB is a genuinely excellent insurer: 50+ decentralized units, 25.9% Berkley family ownership, record NII, Mitsui strategic validation. The DA was MODERATE COUNTER — it didn't break the thesis but corrected two material inputs (soft market CR factual error, normalized ROE too generous) and confirmed no informational edge.

The R1's $70 FV was at consensus and based on near-peak ROE. The R3 FV of $65 uses mid-cycle ROE (16%) and acknowledges the soft market cycle. At $67 current, WRB is essentially fairly valued — EV = market price.

The opportunity is CYCLICAL: wait for the soft market to bite (2-3 years), CR to rise to 94-96%, and the market to de-rate WRB from P/B 2.6x to ~2.1x. At $53, the market would be pricing soft-market trough — that's when to buy the quality.

**Do not advance to R4 at current price.** Resume if price reaches $55 area.

## META-REFLECTION

### Incertidumbres
- NII structural improvement ($1.43B record) may partially immunize WRB from the soft market. If NII stays above $1.3B, ROE floor is 14-15% even with CR at 96%. This makes the bear case less severe than historical soft markets.
- Mitsui's board seat (Jun 2026 AGM) could signal strategic initiatives (Asia expansion, reinsurance partnership) that create value not in our model.
- The 2026 hurricane season is the wildcard. A major cat event HURTS short-term (losses) but helps medium-term (re-hardens the market, lifting future premiums).

### Sugerencias
- WRB fits naturally alongside GL in an insurance basket. Both are quality US insurers at different points in the cycle. GL is life/health (defensive), WRB is P&C (cyclical). Together they provide diversified insurance exposure.
- The Q1 2026 earnings (Apr 29) will be the first data point on soft market impact. Watch CR trajectory closely.
