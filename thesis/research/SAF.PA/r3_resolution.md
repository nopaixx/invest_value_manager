- **Resolution:** ACCEPT. This is a genuine gap. **Add FX scenario to bear case.** At EUR/USD 1.25 with KO breach: recurring OI drops ~25-30%, bear FV falls to EUR 170-180. Probability: 15-20% (not base case but must be acknowledged). This was the DA's strongest finding.

- **Resolution:** ACCEPT DA's concern. SAF.PA is primarily an **aerospace compounder**, not a defense play. Reclassify to "Aerospace Supply Chain" or keep in Defense basket with explicit note that it's the commercial-aero diversifier within the basket, not a pure defense exposure.
