## Resolution of Key Conflicts

### 1. Q1 Guidance: Timing Issue or Structural Deceleration?

**CIO ruling: Likely 70% timing, 30% structural. Monitor Q1 results (April) as gate.**

Evidence for timing:
- 5% price increase effective Jan 1, 2026, shifting some product revenue from Q1 to Q2+
- Management explicitly stated benefits "increasingly materialize from Q2 onwards"
- FY2025 revenue was $15M above original guidance midpoint (modest execution credibility)
- Full-year FY2026 guidance ($2.89B midpoint) still represents 7% growth

Evidence for structural:
- Revenue CAGR 4.9% vs sector 12-14% — market share loss accelerating
- PANW outpaces in SASE ARR (36% vs CHKP's bundled 10-14%)
- Goodwill grew from 20.3% to 29.5% of assets with no FCF expansion
- New CEO 14 months in with no measurable growth acceleration

**Conclusion:** The Q1 miss is PARTIALLY timing but the underlying growth trajectory is concerning. CHKP is a value play in a growth sector — which is fine, but the thesis should acknowledge this honestly rather than projecting growth acceleration.

### 2. Shwed Selling: Post-Succession Exit or Bearish Signal?

**CIO ruling: Post-succession monetization. MODERATE concern, not HIGH.**

At ~$200M/quarter pace, Shwed drops from ~24% to ~20% over 2 years. This is consistent with a founder transitioning from CEO (stepped down to Chairman in Dec 2024) who built a $17B company and is cashing out gradually.

However: the R1 presenting this as "alignment" without disclosing the selling magnitude is misleading. The honest framing is: "Shwed still owns $3.8B+ worth but is actively reducing at ~$200M/quarter post-succession." This is WEAKER alignment than genuine hold-and-buy patterns.

No 10b5-1 plan confirmation found. If discretionary, more concerning. CHKP should disclose this in the next proxy.
