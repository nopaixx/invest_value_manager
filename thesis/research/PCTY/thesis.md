## Business Understanding

### Modelo de Negocio

Paylocity provides cloud-based Human Capital Management (HCM) software to mid-market US companies (typically 20-1,000 employees, average ~150). The platform covers payroll processing, tax services, time & labor management, HR administration, talent management, benefits administration, and the Community social collaboration hub. Revenue is overwhelmingly recurring (~92% of total) with PEPM (per-employee-per-month) pricing plus implementation fees. The company differentiates via modern UX, API-first architecture, Community engagement platform, and AI-embedded workflows. Distribution is through a direct sales force plus broker referral network (brokers deliver >25% of new business).

**Revenue mix FY2025:** Recurring & Other: $1,471.8M (92.3%), Interest income on client funds: ~$123M (7.7% of total). Total: $1,595.2M.

**Key financial FY2025:**
- Revenue: $1,595M (+14% YoY)
- Recurring revenue: $1,472M (+15% YoY)
- Gross margin: 68.8%
- Operating margin: 19.1%
- FCF: $343M (21.5% margin)
- Adjusted EBITDA: $583M (36.5% margin, or $460M ex-float income = 28.8%)
- Net cash: $140M ($207M cash - $67M debt)
- Client count: ~41,650 (up 7%)
- Revenue retention: >92% (FY2023-FY2025)
- Revenue per client: ~$38,300 (up ~6.5% YoY)

**Q2 FY2026 (Dec 2025):**

- Recurring: $387M (+11.3% YoY)
- Net income: $50.2M
- EPS: $0.92 (beat $0.84 consensus)

**FY2026 Guidance (raised after Q2):**
- Total revenue: $1.732-1.742B (~9% growth)

### Unit Economics

- CAC: Not disclosed. Broker referral channel (>25% of new business) is lower CAC than direct.
- LTV: At ~$38K revenue per client with >92% retention, LTV is approximately $38K / (1 - 0.92) = ~$475K per client.
- Revenue per client growing 6-7% annually from cross-sell/upsell (adding modules: Community, benefits, talent, expense management).
- Payback period: Not disclosed, but SaaS payback typically 12-18 months given the multi-year LTV.

### Capital Intensity

Asset-light: Capex/Revenue ~4-5% (FY2025: ~$75M capex on $1,595M revenue). However, Capex/Depreciation is 3.7x, meaning the company is investing heavily in growth capex beyond maintenance. R&D/Revenue at 12.9% indicates significant product investment. SBC/Revenue declining from 12.5% to 9.0% over 3 years is a positive trend.

### Por Que Esta Barata

**Stock: -45% from 52wH ($201.97), trading at $110.80.**

The market narrative (per sector view and PAYC DA analysis):

1. **Growth deceleration is structural, not temporary.** Revenue growth: 37.8% -> 19.4% -> 13.7% -> guided 9%. The market fears this trajectory continues to 5-6% within 2-3 years, making PCTY a utility-like business.
   - **Mi contra-tesis:** Growth deceleration is real but stabilizing. Q2 FY2026 recurring revenue grew 11.3%, above initial guidance. Company raised guidance. Client growth of 7% is healthy. Revenue per client expanding 6-7% from cross-sell. The deceleration from 37% was the end of a pandemic tailwind; 9-11% is the sustainable organic rate for a business with 92% retention, 7% client growth, and 6-7% ARPC expansion.

2. **AI/SaaSpocalypse risk**: AI reduces client headcount, reducing PEPM revenue.
   - **Mi contra-tesis:** AI may reduce some back-office roles, but regulatory payroll compliance scales with employees. Paylocity is embedding AI INTO its platform (AI Assist, recommendation engines) to capture more wallet share, not lose it. 20% increase in peer recognitions from AI features, 25% of new Community groups from AI recommendations.

3. **Morningstar sector-wide moat downgrades** (ADP Wide->Narrow, PAYX Wide->Narrow, PAYC Narrow->None).
   - **Mi contra-tesis:** PCTY was never rated by Morningstar as having a wide moat. The downgrades affect sentiment but PCTY's actual switching costs (payroll migration complexity, employee habit formation via Community) are intact. 92% retention proves it.

4. **Iran crisis / oil shock / recession risk**: Employment weakness = fewer employees = less PEPM revenue.
   - **Mi contra-tesis:** Valid short-term risk. But payroll is last-cut spending (companies cannot NOT pay employees). PCTY weathered COVID with minimal revenue impact. Float income at elevated rates provides a $120M+ buffer.

5. **Valuation was excessive historically** (traded at 35-50x P/E historically, now at 26x).
   - **Mi contra-tesis:** The compression from 35x to 26x has already occurred. At 26x P/E with 9-10% growth, net cash, and 21% FCF margin, the current multiple is reasonable-to-cheap for the quality profile.
