## META-REFLECTION

### Dudas sobre esta decision
- The SBC-adjusted OEY FV of $24 aligning with market price creates genuine uncertainty. If the market is "correctly" pricing SBC drag, then DOCS is fairly valued at $24 and the upside depends entirely on growth exceeding expectations. My conviction that growth recovers to 8-12% is based on Level 2 evidence (management commentary about "record January bookings"), not Level 1 primary data.
- Only 4 years of public data. Doximity has never navigated a recession as a public company. The moat (85% physician penetration) should be recession-resistant, but advertising budgets tend to get cut in downturns regardless of platform quality.
- The 4.4% gap between current price ($24.07) and entry ($23.00) is narrow. If macro conditions deteriorate or Q4 earnings surprise positively, we may never fill this order. However, discipline at entry is more important than FOMO.

### Debilidades del analisis recibido
- R1 thesis contained a material insider ownership data error (2.7% vs 27%). This was caught by DA but would have led to incorrect conviction assessment if DA had not been run. System improvement: flag dual-class structures in quality_scorer.py.
- No separate moat_assessment.md or risk_assessment.md files were created (only thesis.md). The thesis was comprehensive but having independent moat/risk assessments would have caught the insider data error earlier.
- DCF sensitivity is HIGH (FV spread 67%, terminal value 74.5% of EV). The DCF output should be treated as a range ($33-51), not a point estimate ($40).

### Sugerencias de mejora
1. **insider_tracker.py should flag dual-class structures** and warn when insider ownership appears anomalously low for founder-led companies. This would prevent the 2.7% vs 27% error.
2. Consider a dedicated "Health IT / Digital Health" sector view separate from technology.md -- DOCS, VEEV, HIMS have different dynamics from traditional software.
3. An "SBC penalty framework" in valuation-methods skill would standardize SBC treatment across tech companies and reduce ad-hoc inconsistency.

### Preguntas para Orchestrator
