## Resumen Ejecutivo

The thesis identifies a genuine high-quality business with strong structural tailwinds. However, it OVERSTATES the margin of safety, UNDERSTATES the competitive and governance risks, and CHERRY-PICKS growth data (citing January 2026 re-acceleration while dismissing the Q4 deceleration pattern). The most serious issues are: (1) the Elliott/LSEG activist dynamic creates a LIVE catalyst for forced TW stake sale, which is a material omission from the thesis, (2) IG credit market share is DECLINING, not stable, while Trumid is growing 68% YoY and closing the gap rapidly, (3) insider selling of $26.3M is coordinated across 5 executives on the SAME DAY, not "routine vesting", and (4) the FV of $148-159 leaves insufficient bear-case protection at a P/E of 31.6x for a business where 3 major analysts have DOWNGRADED in the last 6 months. The thesis survives scrutiny on business quality but NOT on valuation.

**Severity: MODERATE COUNTER.** Fair value should be adjusted downward. Entry should be at $105-110, not $119.

## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. "Revenue growth of 12-14% is sustainable for next 3-5 years"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - Revenue growth DECELERATED from 30% (Apr 2025) to 13.3% (Q3 2025) to 12.5% (Q4 2025). The FA uses the FY2025 headline of 18.9% which is backward-looking and masks the deceleration trajectory.
  - Q4 constant currency growth was only 9.9% -- below the FA's 12% base case even ONE quarter after thesis publication.
  - Barclays downgraded specifically citing electronification pace slowing without "more meaningful breakthrough in technology."
  - 2026 expense guidance is $1.1-1.16B (midpoint +11% YoY). If revenue growth slows to 12% while expenses grow 11%, operating leverage DISAPPEARS. The margin expansion narrative breaks.
  - Compression activity (a volume driver) declined 18% YoY in Apr 2025 and 15% YoY in Q3 2025.
  - The FA projects revenue growth of 12% but uses 14% EPS growth -- the 2pp gap requires ongoing margin expansion. If 2026 expense growth matches revenue growth (both ~11-12%), EPS growth converges to revenue growth, not exceeds it.
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** The committee should use 10% revenue growth as base case (not 12%), acknowledging Q4 constant-currency growth of 9.9% as the more recent data point. The FV calculation should be stress-tested at 10% growth.

### 2. "RPM compression is offset by volume growth -- the math works"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - The thesis is correct that FY2025 total revenue grew 19% despite RPM declines. But the DIRECTION of RPM is concerning:
    - Credit RPM: -14%
    - Rates RPM: -5%
    - Equities RPM: -10%
  - This is not a one-year phenomenon. It is structural: as electronification reaches less liquid instruments and more automated protocols (AiEX, streaming), fee per unit traded naturally compresses.
  - MarketAxess is GAINING portfolio trading share (+450bp YoY to 18.6%), which means TW's 66% portfolio trading share claim needs updating.
  - If volume growth decelerates from 23% to 15% (as Jan 2026 suggests) while RPM compression persists at -5 to -14%, the NET revenue growth rate falls to 8-11%, not 12-14%.
  - The thesis uses TAM decomposition: TAM(+5%) + Electronification(+8%) + Share(+2%) + RPM(-4%) = 11-13%. But the +8% electronification contribution assumes the S-curve continues at current pace. Barclays explicitly challenged this. US Treasuries at 70-80% electronic have limited runway. IG credit at ~45% electronic is where the growth was -- but TW's IG share is DECLINING (22.4% to 21.2%).
- **Severidad:** MODERATE
- **Resolucion sugerida:** RPM compression risk is partially mitigated by volume but the committee should model a scenario where RPM decline accelerates (e.g., -8% average across products) combined with volume growth of 15%. This gives net revenue growth of ~7-8%, not 12%.

### 3. "LSEG ownership is a manageable overhang that creates our discount"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - **CRITICAL OMISSION:** Elliott Management built a stake in LSEG in early February 2026 and is ACTIVELY pushing for LSEG to sell its TW stake. Bloomberg reported on Feb 18, 2026 that Elliott is pushing for divestments including the TW stake and a GBP 5 billion buyback. This is not speculative -- it is a LIVE activist campaign by one of the most aggressive activist funds in the world.
  - The FA thesis does not mention the Elliott/LSEG activist campaign AT ALL. It mentions Elliott building a stake in passing but does not connect this to the TW divestiture push.
  - LSEG's TW stake is worth approximately GBP 10 billion ($13.2B). Even a PARTIAL selldown of 10-15% would create $1.3-2.0B of supply overhang.
  - LSEG's ROE is 3.5-4% -- bottom of financial infrastructure peer group -- while ~50% of its profits come from TW. This creates perverse incentives: LSEG may be extracting value from TW through below-market data licensing (Refinitiv data agreement amended Nov 2025 -- related-party transaction with UNKNOWN terms).
  - The thesis frames LSEG as "strategic anchor investor with shared technology." In reality, LSEG is now under activist pressure to SELL this "strategic" asset. The framing is backwards.
  - Insider ownership at 0.2% means management has essentially NO alignment with minority shareholders. If LSEG sells, management's equity stake (~$7M total across all insiders vs $26.3M just sold in Feb 2026) provides zero buffer against governance changes.
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** The committee MUST evaluate the Elliott/LSEG dynamic as a LIVE risk, not a theoretical one. A LSEG selldown is now a realistic possibility within 6-12 months. This should be a specific kill condition: "LSEG announces intent to sell any TW shares." The FV should include a 5-10% governance discount given the 80% voting control, related-party transactions, and activist pressure.

### 4. "Insider selling is routine 10b5-1 vesting liquidation"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - Five executives sold $26.3M on the SAME DAY (Feb 10, 2026), exactly 7 days after Q4 earnings release.
  - CEO Hult sold 121K shares ($14M) -- from a total of 518.7K shares across ALL insiders combined. He sold 23% of total insider holdings in a single day.
  - CFO Furber sold 63K shares ($7.3M).
  - CTO Peterson sold 11K shares ($1.3M).
  - Officer Friedman sold 31K shares ($3.5M).
  - Officer Clack sold 2K shares ($192K).
  - Yes, stock grants were received on Dec 31, 2025. But the SIZE and COORDINATION of sales is notable. All five selling on Feb 10 at ~$115 -- which is BELOW the current price of $119 and well below the 52wH of $152.65.
  - Over 18 months: 0 insider PURCHASES. 32 insider SALES by CEO alone. This is a one-way street.
  - Insider ownership is 0.2% -- among the lowest in our entire portfolio and pipeline. The thesis correctly identifies this but then dismisses it as "structural feature, not red flag."
  - **Counter-argument acknowledged:** These could be 10b5-1 plans set up months before with automatic execution triggers. Without access to the actual plan documents, I cannot definitively distinguish signal from noise. However, the combination of (a) 0.2% insider ownership, (b) $0 insider purchases in 18 months, (c) $26.3M coordinated selling at $115, creates a NET NEGATIVE alignment signal.
- **Severidad:** MODERATE
- **Resolucion sugerida:** This is not a kill condition, but the committee should weight the insider selling as a moderately negative signal. Combined with the LSEG governance risk, the alignment of interests between insiders and minority shareholders is VERY WEAK. This justifies a HIGHER MoS requirement, not a lower one.

### 5. "QS 84 Tier A with WIDE moat 20/25 all 5 sources present STRENGTHENING"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - The QS adjustment of +8 (from 76 to 84) is entirely from Market Position being changed from 0/8 to 8/8. This is a reasonable adjustment -- TW IS the dominant platform. I do not challenge the QS itself.
  - However, the MOAT assessment needs nuance:
    - **IG credit market share DECLINED 22.4% to 21.2%.** This is the most liquid credit market and a bellwether for TW's competitive position. The moat assessor notes market share is "GAINING" overall but this masks the IG decline.
    - **Trumid grew 68% YoY in electronic credit volumes** (largest growth of any platform). Trumid is now within $68M of TW's credit ADV ($8.5B vs TW's $8.57B). The thesis describes Trumid as "small scale (~13% PT share), no rates or derivatives capability." But Trumid's growth rate (68%) dwarfs TW's credit growth rate. The competitive threat is GREATER than depicted.
    - **MarketAxess portfolio trading share grew 450bp YoY to 18.6%.** The thesis claims TW has >66% portfolio trading share per Coalition Greenwich. If MKTX is at 18.6% and growing, the 66% number may be stale or defined differently.
    - **Bloomberg is a coopetition dynamic, not a defeated competitor.** In rates, it is a duopoly. Bloomberg has 330K+ terminals and can bundle execution at zero incremental cost. The thesis dismisses Bloomberg's competitive threat too quickly.
  - The moat is REAL but may be NARROWER in credit than the thesis suggests. Rates moat is strong. Credit moat is being contested.
- **Severidad:** MODERATE
- **Resolucion sugerida:** The WIDE moat assessment for the aggregate business is defensible but should be QUALIFIED: "WIDE in rates, NARROW-to-WIDE in credit." The committee should monitor IG credit market share as a leading indicator of moat health.

### Interpretacion:

The thesis identifies a GENUINE quality business with real structural tailwinds. The business fundamentals are strong -- 67% gross margins, 52% FCF margins, net cash, growing revenue. This is NOT a value trap.

However, the thesis overstates the margin of safety by using an aggressive FV ($148-159) that sits significantly above analyst consensus ($123 median, $130 mean). The growth assumptions (12% base case) are higher than Q4 constant-currency growth (9.9%). The LSEG/Elliott risk is a LIVE catalyst that could create a forced supply event in the near term. And the insider selling pattern, while possibly routine, adds to a weak alignment picture.

The stock at $119 is not OBVIOUSLY cheap for a 31x P/E business with decelerating growth, governance overhang, and an activist pushing for stake divestiture. It may be a good WATCHLIST candidate at $105-110, which would provide genuine MoS against the downside scenarios.
