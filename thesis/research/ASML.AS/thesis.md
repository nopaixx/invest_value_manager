## Business Understanding

### What ASML Does (2-minute test)

ASML is the sole manufacturer of EUV (Extreme Ultraviolet) lithography systems -- the machines that print the most advanced semiconductor chips in the world. Every leading-edge chip from Apple, NVIDIA, AMD, and Qualcomm is manufactured on ASML's EUV machines at fabs run by TSMC, Samsung, and Intel. An EUV system costs EUR 150-350M per unit. Without ASML, the semiconductor industry cannot advance to smaller process nodes (3nm, 2nm, and below).

ASML also makes DUV (Deep Ultraviolet) systems for mature and mid-range chip production, and provides a growing installed base management (service + upgrades) business.

### Revenue Model

| Segment | Description | % of Revenue (est.) | Characteristics |
|---------|-------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| EUV Systems | New EUV lithography machines | ~45-50% | Lumpy, high ASP (EUR 150-350M), growing |
| DUV Systems | Older generation litho machines | ~20-25% | Mature, China-exposed, stable |
| Installed Base Mgmt | Service, upgrades, spare parts | ~25-30% | Recurring, high margin, growing steadily |

**Revenue split by end-market:** Logic/Foundry (~75%), DRAM (~20%), Other (~5%)

### Unit Economics

- Average EUV system price: ~EUR 200M (0.33NA), ~EUR 350M+ (High-NA 0.55)
- ~50 EUV systems shipped per year (growing toward 60-70)
- Gross margin per system: ~50-55%
- Installed base of 200+ EUV systems generates recurring service revenue
- Customer qualification cycle: 12-24 months (massive switching cost)

### Why ASML Dominates (Moat Assessment)

**Moat Type: MONOPOLY (Widest moat in technology)**

1. **Technology monopoly:** ASML is the ONLY company capable of manufacturing EUV lithography systems. No competitor exists. This is not a duopoly or oligopoly -- it is a true monopoly earned through 30+ years of R&D and EUR 10B+ cumulative investment.

2. **Barriers to entry: Insurmountable.**
   - EUR 10B+ cumulative R&D investment in EUV alone
   - Supply chain of 5,000+ suppliers, many sole-source (Carl Zeiss for EUV optics)
   - Decades of process know-how with 40,000+ patents
   - 12-24 month customer qualification cycles
   - A competitor would need EUR 20B+ and 15+ years to replicate -- and ASML keeps advancing

3. **Customer lock-in:** Once a fab is designed around ASML's tools, switching is physically impossible without rebuilding the entire fab. The installed base generates recurring revenue at high margins.

4. **Network effects in the ecosystem:** TSMC, Samsung, Intel all design their process flows around ASML's roadmap. The entire semiconductor ecosystem co-develops with ASML.

5. **Pricing power:** ASML raised EUV system prices from ~EUR 120M to ~EUR 350M+ over 5 years. Customers pay because there is NO ALTERNATIVE.

**Moat durability: 10+ years.** No credible threat to EUV monopoly exists. Chinese NAURA is building DUV-equivalent systems but cannot approach EUV complexity. The next-generation High-NA EUV (0.55) further extends ASML's lead.

## Valuation

### Method 1: Owner Earnings Yield (Tier A Primary -- 50% weight)

```
FCF FY2025: EUR 11.0B
Depreciation FY2025: ~EUR 1.0B (estimated from capex/dep ratio 1.6x)
Maintenance Capex ~ Depreciation * 1.1 = EUR 1.1B
Owner Earnings = FCF = EUR 11.0B (FCF already net of all capex)

Note: FCF is after total capex. Owner Earnings as traditionally defined:
Net Income + D&A - Maintenance Capex - Working Capital changes
= ~EUR 9.6B + EUR 1.0B - EUR 1.1B + positive WC improvement
= ~EUR 10-11B (consistent with reported FCF)

Owner Earnings Yield = EUR 11.0B / EUR 487.4B = 2.26%

OEY + Expected Growth = 2.26% + 10% (sustainable growth) + 0.86% (dividend) = 13.1%
vs WACC = 11.0%
Spread = +2.1pp
```

**Interpretation:** At current price, the total expected return (OEY + growth + dividend = 13.1%) barely exceeds WACC. For a Tier A compounder, I want this spread to be at least 5pp to justify deployment. At EUR 1,256, ASML does not offer sufficient spread.

**What price would give 5pp spread?**
Target total return: 16% (WACC 11% + 5pp)
16% = OEY + 10% growth + 0.86% yield
OEY needed = 5.14%
Price = EUR 11.0B / 5.14% / 388M shares = EUR 55B / 388M = ~EUR 550/share

This is extreme -- it implies nearly a 60% correction. Even if I relax to a 3pp spread:
14% = OEY + 10% + 0.86%
OEY = 3.14%
Price = EUR 11.0B / 3.14% / 388M = ~EUR 900/share

**OEY method suggests FV range: EUR 550-900 depending on required spread.**
For a Tier A monopolist, I will use the lower hurdle (3pp spread) as base: **FV(OEY) = EUR 900.**

ASML's top 3 customers (TSMC, Samsung, Intel) represent ~70-75% of revenue. If ONE major customer delays orders (as Intel has done in its turnaround), revenue impact is significant. This is the structural vulnerability of a monopoly with only 3 major buyers.

- Taiwan risk is existential: if conflict disrupts TSMC operations, ASML loses its largest customer and the entire semiconductor supply chain is disrupted
- Dutch government must balance US pressure with ASML's business interests

## META-REFLECTION

### Incertidumbres/Dudas
- **FV range is very wide (EUR 420-1,050).** The sensitivity to WACC and terminal growth is extreme because terminal value dominates (74.5% of EV). A 1pp change in WACC swings FV by ~EUR 100/share. This makes any point estimate unreliable.
- **Morningstar estimates EUR 1,353 FV.** This is nearly 2x my estimate. The divergence likely comes from (a) lower WACC, (b) higher terminal growth, (c) more aggressive margin expansion, or (d) longer projection period. I cannot reconcile without seeing their model, but I refuse to anchor to their number (Error #49). My assumptions are conservative but defensible.
- **China revenue data is inconsistent.** Some sources say 33% (2025), others say 20% target. The discrepancy matters for the bear case. I used ~20-25% as the normalized post-restriction level.
- **Is my WACC too high?** At 11%, it reflects the cyclical beta of semi-equipment. But ASML's monopoly arguably deserves a lower beta adjustment (its business is more durable than its stock price volatility suggests). If WACC were 9%, FV jumps to ~EUR 900-1,000.
