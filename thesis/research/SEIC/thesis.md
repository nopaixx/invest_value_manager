## TL;DR

SEI Investments is a high-quality financial technology and asset management platform ($1.9T in administered/managed assets) with exceptional ROIC (29%), net cash balance sheet, 16.8% insider ownership, and expanding margins. At $77.01 (down from $81.44 at initial R1), the stock trades at 13.7x P/E -- historically cheap for a Tier A compounder. The low valuation reflects slow organic revenue growth (~5% CAGR) and market skepticism about the Stratos acquisition ($441M). The market implies only ~4% FCF growth -- zero credit for Stratos or alternatives momentum. Applying anti-bullish-bias (60% bear + 40% base), FV = $82, offering 6.5% MoS. The real thesis is that 7% revenue growth + operating leverage + buybacks = 13%+ EPS growth, with eventual multiple re-rating from 13.7x to 16-18x.

## Business Understanding

### What SEI Does

SEI Investments provides technology-driven investment processing, operations, and asset management solutions to financial institutions, advisors, and wealth managers. It operates as the "operating system" for wealth management firms -- they outsource back-office, middle-office, and investment management operations to SEI's platform (SEI Wealth Platform, TRUST 3000).

### Revenue Model (FY2025, $2.3B total)

| Segment | Q4 Revenue | % Total | Op Margin | Description |
|---------|-----------|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Investment Managers | $220.8M | ~36% | 41% | Platform for alternative + traditional asset managers |
| Investment Advisors | $156.2M | ~26% | 46% | Wealth platform for RIAs and advisors |
| Private Banks | $149.8M | ~25% | 19% | Technology for private banks and trust companies |
| Institutional Investors | $72.8M | ~12% | 47% | OCIO solutions for pensions, endowments |
| New Businesses | $8.3M | ~1% | Negative | Stratos, new ventures |

**Revenue mix**: ~60% asset-based fees (tied to AUM/AUA), ~40% platform/processing fees. This creates moderate market sensitivity but with high switching costs.

**Key Scale**: $1.9T in assets administered/advised, $455.4B AUM, $215.9B AUA (Q4 2025). Record net sales events $149.9M in FY2025.

### Unit Economics

- **Client retention**: Very high (multi-year contracts, deep integration). Switching measured in years and millions of dollars
- **FCF Margin**: 24.2% (FY2025) -- excellent for the sector
- **Capital intensity**: Asset-light. Capex/Depreciation at 1.7x. SBC/Revenue at 2.3% (well-controlled)
- **LTV/CAC**: Not disclosed but implied by 95%+ retention and 20+ year relationships. Very favorable

### Margin Structure

| Metric | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | Trend |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| Gross Margin | 52.5% | 51.3% | 53.1% | 53.5% | Expanding |
| Operating Margin | 23.9% | 22.1% | 26.0% | 27.3% | Expanding |
| FCF Margin | 24.7% | 20.2% | 26.6% | 24.2% | Stable |

Operating margin expansion from 22.1% to 27.3% over 3 years: driven by operating leverage on platform business + cost discipline.

### Stratos Acquisition ($441M closed Q4 2025)

- 57.5% stake in Stratos Wealth Holdings (US portion ~$441M; Mexico portion ~$100M expected H1 2026)
- 360+ advisors, $37B+ client assets across 26 states
- Strategic shift: adds advice distribution to technology/operations
- Goodwill increased from 6.3% to 10.9% of assets
- Funded entirely from balance sheet cash (net cash maintained)
- Risk: integration execution, minority interest accounting complexity

### Competitive Position

SEIC is a **niche platform champion**:
- **Wealth Platform**: Competes with FNZ, Broadridge, InvestCloud, Envestnet. Not #1 but deeply entrenched
- **Investment Operations Outsourcing**: Competes with State Street, BNY Mellon. Smaller but more tech-focused
- **Asset Management**: $455.4B AUM. Mid-tier scale
- **Moat type**: Switching Costs (primary) + Scale Economies. Narrow-to-wide moat

## Valuation

### ANTI-BULLISH-BIAS PROTOCOL: Bear FIRST, then Base. Final FV = 60% Bear + 40% Base.

### Method 1: Owner Earnings Yield (50% weight)

```
FCF (FY2025):         $555M
Depreciation:         ~$65M
Maintenance Capex:    ~$72M (Dep * 1.1)
Owner Earnings:       $548M
Market Cap:           $9.4B (at $77.01)
OEY:                  5.8%

OEY + Expected Growth = 5.8% + 7.0% = 12.8%
vs WACC 9.4% = spread of +3.4pp (favorable)
```

**Bear OEY Fair Value** (target OEY 6.0%, minimal re-rating):
FV = $548M / 6.0% = $9.13B / 122.5M shares = **$75**

**Base OEY Fair Value** (target OEY 4.5%, quality compounder):
FV = $548M / 4.5% = $12.2B / 122.5M shares = **$99**

**Anti-Bias FV (OEY)** = 60% * $75 + 40% * $99 = **$85**

### Method 2: DCF (30% weight)

From tool (sensitivity matrix):

| Growth \ WACC | 7.5% | 9.0% | 10.5% |
|--------------|------|------|-------|
| 2.0% | $94 | $73 | $60 |
| 5.0% | $107 | $83 | $68 |
| 8.0% | $122 | $94 | $76 |

**Sensitivity Assessment**: FV Spread 74%, TV 74.5% of EV --> HIGH SENSITIVITY. DCF unreliable as point estimate.

**Bear DCF** (3.5% growth, 10% WACC): ~$66
**Base DCF** (6.5% growth, 9% WACC): ~$88

**Anti-Bias FV (DCF)** = 60% * $66 + 40% * $88 = **$75**

### Method 3: EV/EBIT Normalized (20% weight)

EBIT (FY2025): ~$628M (27.3% * $2.3B)

**Bear EV/EBIT** (11x, trough multiple):
EV = $628M * 11 = $6.91B + $405M net cash = $7.31B / 122.5M = **$60**

**Base EV/EBIT** (15x, justified by ROIC 29% + growth 7%):
EV = $628M * 15 = $9.42B + $405M = $9.83B / 122.5M = **$80**

**Anti-Bias FV (EV/EBIT)** = 60% * $60 + 40% * $80 = **$68**
