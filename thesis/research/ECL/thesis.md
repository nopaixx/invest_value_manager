## Business Understanding

### Model de Negocio

Ecolab provides water treatment chemicals, hygiene products, and infection prevention solutions to institutional and industrial customers worldwide. The business operates on a razor-razorblade model: Ecolab rents proprietary cleaning/dispensing equipment to customers (hotels, restaurants, hospitals, food processing plants, data centers) and then sells the consumable chemicals used in that equipment. This creates:

1. **~90% recurring revenue** — customers cannot switch equipment vendors without costly replacements
2. **Embedded service model** — 25,000+ field service technicians maintain equipment and optimize chemical dosage on-site
3. **Regulatory compliance** — Ecolab's solutions help customers meet FDA, EPA, and local health codes

**Segments (FY2025):**
- **Global Industrial** (~46%): Water treatment, process chemicals for heavy industry, energy, mining
- **Global Institutional & Specialty** (~42%): Restaurants, hotels, hospitals, food processing
- **Global Healthcare & Life Sciences** (~7%): Infection prevention, sterile processing
- **Other** (~5%): Pest elimination, textile care

**Revenue:** $16.1B (FY2025). Organic growth 2-4%, supplemented by Ovivo Electronics acquisition (7-9% reported growth for 2026).

### Unit Economics
- **Customer retention:** >90% for multi-year bundled programs
- **Pricing power:** Value-based pricing strategy — 2-3% annual price increases without material volume loss
- **FCF Margin:** 11.8% and expanding (was 7.6% in 2022)
- **Capital intensity:** Medium — Capex/Depreciation 1.6x (investing in growth above maintenance)

### Por Que Esta "Barata" (Relative Discount)

ECL is not classically cheap — it trades at 37.5x P/E. However, it is 12% below its 52-week high of $309, creating a perceived relative discount. The market narrative:

1. **Revenue growth deceleration** — organic growth slowed from 8% (2023) to 2.2% (2025). Market worries this is structural, not just post-pandemic normalization
2. **Oil/raw material costs** — elevated oil prices ($95-100 Brent) pressure input costs. ECL's chemicals are partially petroleum-derived
3. **Tariff/trade uncertainty** — broad Section 301 probes add cost pressure. ECL implemented trade surcharges but market worries about margin erosion
4. **Premium fatigue** — ECL has always traded at a premium (30-40x P/E), but the premium expanded to 37.5x, making some investors hesitant

**My contra-thesis:**
- Revenue growth is normalizing, not collapsing. Guidance for 3-4% organic in 2026 with acceleration through the year. Ovivo adds another 4-5pp
- Margin expansion is structural, not cyclical: One Ecolab savings ($325M by 2027), value-based pricing, digital tools
- Oil sensitivity is real but manageable — ECL passes through costs via surcharges with lag. Gross margins have EXPANDED even during this oil crisis (37.8% in 2022 to 44.5% in 2025)
- The premium is justified by 90% recurring revenue, wide moat, and 12-15% EPS growth guidance

## Moat Assessment

### Moat Type: WIDE

**1. Switching Costs (PRIMARY — Very Strong)**
- Razor-razorblade: proprietary equipment installed at customer sites creates lock-in
- Multi-year bundled contracts with equipment, chemicals, and service
- Switching requires replacing physical equipment, retraining staff, recertifying regulatory compliance
- Retention >90% across institutional business
- Cost of ECL products is small relative to customer's total operating cost (cleaning chemicals = <1% of restaurant revenue), making price sensitivity low

**2. Scale & Network (STRONG)**
- 25,000+ field service technicians globally — impossible to replicate quickly
- Largest service footprint in water treatment/hygiene globally
- Scale enables R&D spend ($0.5B+/yr) that smaller competitors cannot match
- "Local for local" manufacturing reduces supply chain risk

**3. Regulatory Expertise (MODERATE)**
- Deep expertise in FDA, EPA, OSHA compliance for customers
- Customers rely on Ecolab to navigate complex and evolving regulations
- Regulatory changes often benefit ECL (tighter standards = more demand for compliance solutions)

**4. Intangible Assets (MODERATE)**
- 10,000+ patents
- Brand trust in healthcare and food safety — reputational risk of switching is high for customers
- 100+ years of operational history

**Gross Margin Premium:** +19.5pp vs sector median (44.5% vs 25.0%) — strong moat signal.
**ROIC Persistence:** ROIC > WACC in recent years, trajectory improving (7.9% to 12.7% over 2022-2025).
