## Business Understanding

### Model de Negocio

Qualys provides cloud-based cybersecurity and compliance solutions. Founded in 1999 by Philippe Courtot (deceased 2021), it pioneered cloud-delivered vulnerability management (VM) -- scanning enterprise IT assets for known vulnerabilities and compliance gaps. Today, the Qualys Cloud Platform delivers 20+ security and compliance applications.

**Problem solved:** Enterprises must continuously identify, assess, and remediate cybersecurity vulnerabilities across their IT infrastructure. This is not optional -- regulatory mandates (NIS2, DORA, SEC disclosure rules, FedRAMP) require ongoing vulnerability assessment. A breach can cost $4.45M on average (IBM), while compliance failures trigger fines and legal liability.

**Revenue model:** 100% subscription-based (SaaS). Customers pay annually for platform access and per-asset scanning. Revenue is highly recurring with deferred revenue growing to $401M (+8% YoY).

**Key metrics (FY2025):**
- Revenue: $669.1M (+10% YoY)
- ARR: $697M (+9.5%)
- Non-GAAP Operating Margin: 45%
- Free Cash Flow: $304.4M (FCF margin ~45.5% -- guided low-40% for 2026)
- Net Revenue Retention: ~103% (declining from ~104%)
- Deferred Revenue: $401.1M (8% growth, slightly trailing revenue growth)

### Unit Economics

- **Asset-light model:** Capex is minimal (Capex/D&A: 0.3-0.7x). Nearly all costs are people and cloud infrastructure.
- **FCF Margin: 45.5%** (FY2025) -- among the highest in all of cybersecurity. Guided "low-40%" for FY2026.
- **SBC: $77M / $669M revenue = 11.5%** of revenue. MODERATE for cybersecurity (sector avg 15-25%). Shares outstanding declining ~2-3% annually via buybacks ($183M in 2025, new $200M authorization).
- **SBC-adjusted FCF margin: ~34%** ($304M FCF - $77M SBC = $227M / $669M). Still excellent.
- **R&D: 18.4%** of revenue (declining from 20.7%). Efficient for the sector.

## Por Que Esta Barata

### Narrativa del Mercado

1. **Guidance deceleration** (PRIMARY): FY2026 revenue guidance of 7-8% growth vs 10% in FY2025. The market hates growth deceleration in SaaS more than almost anything.
2. **NRR declining to 103%**: From 104% prior quarter. Signals weak upsell/cross-sell momentum. Below SaaS "best-in-class" NRR of 115%+.
3. **Platformization threat**: Enterprises consolidating to 2-3 platform vendors (PANW, CRWD, FTNT). Qualys as a "point solution" VM specialist risks being bundled away.
4. **SaaSpocalypse contagion**: $800B destroyed in software sector since Feb 2026. Qualys caught in the wave even though VM is not per-seat SaaS.
5. **Hyperscaler competition**: AWS Inspector, Azure Defender, Google Security Command Center offer basic VM capabilities natively.

### Mi Contra-Tesis

**On guidance deceleration:**
- The market is pricing -2.9% FCF DECLINE (reverse DCF). That is a 12.7pp gap vs historical FCF CAGR of 9.8%.
- Even the bear guidance of 7% revenue growth + margin expansion = FCF growth of ~5-8%. The market is pricing in NEGATIVE FCF growth. That is excessively pessimistic.
- FY2025 Q4 billings grew only 6% -- this IS a leading indicator of slower revenue. But "slower" is 7-8%, not negative.

**On NRR at 103%:**
- This is genuinely concerning. Best-in-class SaaS runs 115-130% NRR. Qualys's 103% means almost no net expansion from existing customers.
- HOWEVER: Qualys's model is asset-based (per-IP/per-device), not seat-based. NRR is less relevant -- growth comes from new customers and new assets, not upselling existing seats.
- The better metric is deferred revenue growth (8%) and ARR growth (9.5%) -- both healthier than NRR suggests.

**On platformization:**
- This is the REAL RISK. PANW's Cortex XSIAM, CRWD's Falcon Exposure Management, and FTNT's FortiVM are all moving into vulnerability management.
- BUT: Qualys has 3 billion+ IPs scanned, creating a massive data moat. 10,000+ customers. Switching costs are high (18-36 months to migrate). Compliance-driven demand means enterprises need SPECIALIZED VM, not just a checkbox in a platform.
- Qualys is responding with Enterprise TruRisk Management (ETM) platform -- expanding from VM into CSPM, CNAPP, and risk quantification. TotalCloud now 5% of bookings (from 4%). Agentic AI marketplace launched.
- Gartner MQ Leader in Exposure Assessment Platforms (2025). This is NOT a dying product.

**On SaaSpocalypse:**
- Qualys is NOT per-seat SaaS. It charges per-asset (IPs, devices, cloud workloads). AI replacing employees does NOT reduce the number of assets to scan -- if anything, AI proliferation INCREASES attack surface.
- The SaaSpocalypse narrative is irrelevant to Qualys's pricing model.

**On hyperscalers:**
- AWS Inspector and Azure Defender cover basic scanning. Enterprise-grade VM requires continuous assessment, compliance reporting, prioritization, and remediation orchestration -- where Qualys excels.
- Hyperscaler tools are like Lemonade vs GEICO -- good for simple cases, insufficient for enterprise complexity.
