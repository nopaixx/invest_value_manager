## Business Understanding

### Business Model (2-minute explanation)

Zoetis makes medicines, vaccines, and diagnostics for animals — both pets (~65% revenue) and farm animals (~35%). Spun off from Pfizer in 2013, it is the undisputed #1 player in a $67-78B global market growing 7-11% CAGR.

This is "pharma for animals" but BETTER than human pharma:
- **Slower patent cliffs:** Generics take 20-40% share over 5 years (vs 80%+ overnight in human pharma)
- **Vet relationships:** Direct sales to clinics create sticky switching costs
- **Regulatory moat:** FDA approval takes 5-7 years per product; near-impossible for new entrants
- **Pet humanization:** Secular tailwind — pet spend is non-discretionary, growing 3-5%/yr per pet

Revenue is recurring prescriptions (parasiticides, dermatology) + consumables (vaccines, diagnostics). ~70% of companion animal revenue is highly recurring.

### Key Franchises
| Franchise | 2025 Revenue | Growth | Status |
|-----------|-------------|--------|--------|
| Simparica (parasiticides) | $1.5B | +12% | Best-in-class. Trio surpassed $1B US. Patent to 2032-33. |
| Apoquel/Cytopoint (dermatology) | $1.7B | +6% | Blockbuster. Faces 2 oral JAK competitors intl. Patent to 2032. |
