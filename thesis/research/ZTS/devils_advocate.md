# Counter-Analysis: ZTS (Zoetis Inc.) — R2 Refresh

## Fecha: 2026-03-13

> **CONTEXT: This DA reviews the R1 REFRESH thesis (2026-03-13), which raised FV from $120 (committee R4, Feb 14) to $130 (CIO-corrected from FA's $155). The thesis now recommends entry at $105 with SO gated on Q1 May earnings. Price has dropped further to $114.84 since the Feb DA.**

## Resumen Ejecutivo

The refreshed thesis corrects several errors from the original R1 (CIO caught the Apoquel patent conflation, reduced FV from $155 to $130, added KC for generic erosion). Credit where due. However, the thesis STILL overvalues ZTS by anchoring to $130 when the committee's own $120 FV (Feb 14) remains the most defensible number. The $10 increase ($120 to $130) is unjustified: the fundamentals have NOT improved since the committee decision -- they have DETERIORATED (price fell another 8%, vet visits still declining, Apoquel cliff now 8 months closer). The $130 FV relies on an OEY target yield of 3.3% that is too aggressive for a company facing simultaneous franchise erosion in its two largest growth engines. My independent bear-case valuation (EV/EBIT at 15x current EBIT) produces $102. At $114.84, the stock is in no-man's land: not cheap enough for the risks, not expensive enough to short.

## Phase 0.5: Calibration

**Market anchor:** ZTS at $114.84 implies 12.8% FCF growth for 5 years (reverse DCF). Historical FCF CAGR is 19.9%, but revenue CAGR is only 5.4%. The FCF CAGR was inflated by margin expansion (OP 36% -> 38%) and working capital dynamics. Sustaining 12.8% FCF growth requires either (a) continued margin expansion, which is limited at 38% OP already, or (b) revenue acceleration above 5.4%, which contradicts 2026 guidance of 3-5%. The market is pricing in growth that EXCEEDS what the company is guiding. This is NOT a cheap stock -- it is a fairly-priced stock facing headwinds.

**DA historical calibration:** Median DA correction is -13.0%. Average is -15.7%. All 25 prior corrections were negative. The Feb DA on ZTS was a STRONG COUNTER that reduced FV from $140 to $115-125. The committee settled at $120. This refresh raises it to $130 without new positive data -- this is FV drift upward and should be challenged.

## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. "FV should be $130 (up from committee's $120)" -- UNJUSTIFIED FV INFLATION

- **What changed:** The thesis argues the R3 FV of $120 was "over-penalized by DCF sensitivity" and that the OEY method "properly captures the compounding engine." The CIO reduced the FA's $155 to $130 based on EV/EBIT at 18x.
- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - The committee (Feb 14) weighed 4 methods and arrived at $124 weighted, then applied a -$4 haircut for Librela + Apoquel to reach $120. This was a RIGOROUS process with DA input.
  - Since the committee decision, NOTHING has fundamentally improved. Price fell further (from $126 to $115). Apoquel patent is now 8 months away vs 9 months. Vet visits continued declining. No Librela stabilization data.
  - The ONLY change is the FA used OEY at 60% weight with a 3.3% target yield. But 3.3% OEY for a company with 7% expected growth implies the market should pay ~30x owner earnings. For a company whose two largest growth engines (dermatology + OA pain) are both under pressure, this is aggressive.
  - The FA's own thesis notes Apoquel patent expires Nov 2026, Librela declined -32% US Q4, and guidance is 3-5% growth. A company with these headwinds does NOT deserve a 3.3% OEY target.
  - **Precedent comparison:** The committee's $120 was set AFTER resolving all DA challenges. Raising it to $130 without a committee re-approval is procedurally irregular (Error #62 risk -- thesis FV not matching pipeline output).
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Maintain committee FV of $120 until Q1 2026 data resolves franchise headwinds. The $130 is FV creep.

### 2. "Expected Growth: 7%" -- AGGRESSIVE GIVEN FRANCHISE HEADWINDS

- **What the thesis says:** Revenue ~6% + margin expansion ~1% + buyback accretion ~1% = 8% (header says 7%).
- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - **Management guides 3-5% organic for 2026.** This is the company's OWN assessment.
  - **Apoquel generics launch in ~8 months.** Even with "rational" animal health generic pricing, Apoquel ($650M+ in dermatology franchise) faces first-ever generic competition. The thesis claims "slow erosion" (20-40% over 5 years), but this is an ASSUMPTION, not a fact. No generic oclacitinib has launched before -- the erosion rate is unknown.
  - **Librela declined 32% US Q4 and is accelerating downward.** The thesis says "even at zero, core grows 5-6%" -- but Librela was supposed to be the GROWTH ENGINE for OA pain. Its failure removes a growth vector, it does not simply subtract 4.5% of revenue.
  - **Dermatology already stalling at 1% Q4 growth** -- BEFORE generics. This suggests the franchise has organically peaked.
  - **Vet visits declining 2.8-3.2% in 2025**, with forecasts of continued negative growth through mid-2026. This is a DEMAND CEILING for all companion animal products.
  - **Margin expansion is limited:** OP already at 38%, GM at 71.8%. Further expansion requires either pricing (inflationary, risky with vet visit declines) or cost cuts (limited upside at these margins).
  - **Buyback accretion at value-destructive prices:** Management bought back $3.2B at $140-170. Stock is $115. Future buybacks at these levels destroy value, not accrete it.
  - **Realistic growth estimate:** Revenue 3-5% (matching guidance) + margin 0.5% (limited room) + buyback 0.5% (smaller program to avoid further destruction) = **4-6%**, NOT 7-8%.
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Use 5% expected growth (midpoint of realistic range), not 7%.

### 3. "Apoquel patent cliff: FA claims 2032, CIO corrected to Nov 2026" -- CIO CORRECTION IS RIGHT BUT RISK STILL UNDERWEIGHTED

- **What the thesis says:** CIO correctly caught that original tablet patent expires Nov 2026, chewable formulation patents extend to 2033. KC#7 corrected to track generic erosion.
- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - The CIO correction is accurate on the patent DATES. However, the thesis still treats Apoquel generic risk as a manageable headwind rather than a MAJOR EVENT.
  - Apoquel tablet revenue is approximately $650M. Chewable is a newer formulation with lower penetration. Generic oclacitinib tablets could capture significant share because:
    - Unlike human pharma, there is no PBM/formulary system in vet medicine, BUT cost-conscious pet owners (52% already skipping care) will actively ask for generics.
    - Vet clinics under financial pressure from declining visits have economic incentive to stock cheaper generics.
    - The animal health generic market is evolving -- the "rational pricing" assumption is based on historical data that may not predict the current environment.
  - **The timing is devastating:** Apoquel generics launching in late 2026/2027 WHILE Librela is declining SIMULTANEOUSLY. The thesis discusses each risk separately but does not adequately model the COMPOUND effect of losing growth in both franchises at once.
  - **No ANDA filing data found** -- this means we don't know if anyone has already filed. The uncertainty itself is a risk: if a generic launches in Q1 2027, the dermatology franchise could show negative growth in H1 2027.
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Model Apoquel generic erosion of 15-25% over the first 2 years as base case, not 10-20%. Combined with Librela decline, this means $300-500M revenue at risk across the two franchises.

### 4. "Librela is 4.5% of revenue -- contained risk"

- **What the thesis says:** "Even at ZERO, core grows 5-6%." Librela is dismissed as non-material.
- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - **The 4.5% framing is misleading.** Librela + Solensia = $568M = 6% of revenue. But the OA pain franchise was supposed to be a $2B+ opportunity at maturity. The LOSS is not just current revenue -- it is the forfeiture of a major growth vector.
  - **The mechanism-of-action concern is genuinely alarming.** The Frontiers in Veterinary Science study (peer-reviewed, May 2025) found 9x higher musculoskeletal adverse events. An 18-member expert panel concluded "strong suspicion of causal association" between NGF inhibition and accelerated joint destruction. This is NOT a manufacturing issue -- it is potentially a fundamental problem with the therapeutic approach.
  - **Lenivia (replacement product) targets the SAME NGF pathway.** Lenivia binds to a different site on NGF, but NGF inhibition is the mechanism. If the expert panel is correct that NGF inhibition itself causes joint harm, Lenivia faces the same fundamental risk. The Change.org petition to halt Lenivia/Portela already has traction.
  - **EudraVigilance data integrity concern:** The Frontiers study notes that Zoetis MODIFIED case narratives and diagnoses from what reporting veterinarians originally submitted. This is a corporate culture flag that extends beyond any single product.
  - **Real-world trajectory:** Librela US revenue: ~$180M annualized Q4 2025 (vs $215M Q4 2024 annualized). At this rate, Librela US could fall below $100M annualized by Q4 2026.
- **Severidad:** HIGH (for the growth vector loss + NGF mechanism concern; would be MODERATE if only about current revenue)
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Model OA pain franchise declining to $250-300M (from $568M) by 2027. DO NOT assume Lenivia replaces Librela 1-for-1 until post-marketing safety data from EU/Canada clears the NGF mechanism question.

### 5. "P/E 19x is historically anomalous -- multiple will revert to 25x"

- **What the thesis says:** "Business has never traded at 19x since IPO. Panic pricing for temporary problem."
- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - The historical 35-40x P/E was earned during a period when:
    - Revenue grew 8-12% annually (now 3-5% guided)
    - No major franchise was in decline
    - Vet visits were growing (now declining 2.8-3.2%)
    - No patent cliff was imminent
    - Margins were expanding rapidly (OP 30% to 38%)
  - **The 19x P/E may reflect a PERMANENT re-rating to a slower-growth profile.** If sustainable growth is 5% (not 8%), the appropriate P/E for a healthcare company with this profile is 18-22x, not 25-30x.
  - **BofA downgraded to Neutral** with $135 PT, specifically citing that competitive pressures and Librela challenges have "materially impacted financial results" and they see "limited upside through much of 2026."
  - **Current analyst consensus PT is $148-162** (depending on source), which implies only 25-40% upside. For a stock that formerly attracted $200+ PTs, this is a structural de-rating by the sell-side itself.
  - The thesis assumes multiple reversion as a catalyst with 50% probability and +30% impact. This is too generous. If growth stays at 3-5%, the multiple does NOT revert. The probability of returning to 25x+ in the next 2 years is more like 15-20%, not 50%.
- **Severidad:** MODERATE
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Use 18-20x P/E as base case, not 18-25x. Multiple reversion probability should be 20%, not 50%.
