## Business Understanding

### Model of Business

**Problem solved:** Meta connects 3.58 billion people daily across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Messenger, enabling social interaction and communication. For businesses, it provides the most cost-effective digital advertising platform at scale.

**Revenue model:**

| Revenue Type | Description | % of Revenue | Characteristics |
|-------------|-------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Advertising | News Feed, Stories, Reels, Messaging ads | ~97% | Impressions (+12% YoY) x Price/Ad (+9% YoY). Highly scalable. |
| Reality Labs | Quest, Ray-Ban Meta, Horizon | ~3% | Operating at -$17B loss. Optionality, not earnings driver. |
| Other | Business messaging, payments, WhatsApp Business | <1% | Early stage, high potential |

**Unit economics:** Revenue per DAP ~$56/year globally (~$85 in US/Canada). Near-zero marginal cost per user. Advertising model = no COGS on revenue side. Customer acquisition is organic (network effects). SBC ~10% of revenue is the main "cost" beyond infrastructure.

### Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Market Share (Social Media) | Positioning |
|------------|-------------|-------------|
| Meta (FB+IG+WA+Msg) | ~55-60% of global social ad spend | Dominant scale, full-funnel |
| Google/YouTube | ~25-30% of digital ad market | Search + video, different intent |
| TikTok/ByteDance | ~10-12% and growing | Short-form video, younger demo |
| Snap | ~2-3% | Younger demo, AR focus |
| X (Twitter) | <2% | News/real-time, declining ad share |

### Moat Assessment

**Type:** Network Effects (3.58B DAP = largest human network ever) + Data Scale (AI ad targeting trained on largest proprietary dataset) + Switching Costs (social graph is non-portable)

**Durability: WIDE** -- Network effects strengthen with scale. Advertisers MUST be on Meta because that is where the users are. No competitor has replicated the cross-platform social graph (FB + IG + WA + Messenger). TikTok is a threat on engagement share but has NOT eroded Meta's ad revenue growth (still +22% YoY). Instagram Reels successfully countered TikTok's core format.

## Why It Is Cheap

### Market Narrative

The stock is at $594, down 25% from its 52-week high of $796. Market concerns:

1. **Massive AI capex ($115-135B in 2026):** This is an 88% increase from 2025's $72B. Market fears this is an irrational spending spree with uncertain ROI, compressing FCF margins from 33% to potentially <20%.

2. **Reality Labs losses (-$17B/year):** Sustained losses with unclear path to profitability in metaverse/VR.

3. **Regulatory risk:** EU DMA, BEUC complaints, potential forced divestiture of Instagram/WhatsApp. Data privacy restrictions.

4. **AI competition uncertainty:** Market unclear if Meta's open-source LLaMA strategy will generate returns vs closed competitors (OpenAI, Google).

5. **Recession/ad spend vulnerability:** If trade wars + oil shock cause recession, ad budgets get cut first. ~40% international revenue exposed to FX.

## Valuation

### Method 1 (Primary, 60% weight): Owner Earnings Yield

```
FCF (2025): $46.1B
Depreciation: $18.4B (estimated from capex/dep ratio)
Maintenance Capex: ~$20B (depreciation x 1.1)
Growth Capex: ~$52B (total capex $72B - maintenance $20B in 2025)
Owner Earnings = FCF + Growth Capex portion that could be cut = ~$46B + $25B recoverable = ~$46B conservative
(Using actual FCF as OE since growth capex is discretionary)

OE Yield = $46.1B / $1,501.7B market cap = 3.1%
OE Yield + Expected Growth = 3.1% + 15% = 18.1%
vs WACC 10.5% = +7.6pp spread (attractive)
```

**Fair Value via OEY:** At a 5% OEY target for a Tier A compounder growing 15%:
FV = $46.1B / 5% = $922B market cap = ~$355/share (bear anchor)
At a 3.5% OEY (generous for 15% grower): FV = $1,317B = ~$507/share (base)

### Method 2 (Secondary, 40% weight): Reverse DCF + EV/EBIT

**Reverse DCF insight:** Market implies 16.6% FCF growth. With revenue growing 18-20% and margin recovery from capex normalization, this is conservative. The market is pricing META as if FCF margin stays permanently compressed at 23%.

**EV/EBIT approach:**
- Current EBIT: $83.3B (operating income FY2025)
- Normalized EBIT (base): ~$95B (2026E at 18% rev growth, 40% OP margin)
- Sector comparable EV/EBIT: 18-22x for quality tech compounders
- Conservative 16x (below sector due to capex uncertainty): EV = $1,520B, equity = $1,516B, FV = ~$583/share
- Bear case 13x: EV = $1,235B, FV = ~$475/share

### Reconciliation — Anti-Bullish-Bias Protocol (S202: FV = 60% bear + 40% base)

**Bear FV calculation:**
- OEY bear: $355
- EV/EBIT bear (13x): $475
- Weighted bear: 60% x $355 + 40% x $475 = $213 + $190 = $403 (too conservative -- OEY bear uses unrealistic 5% yield for a 15% grower)

**Recalibrated bear using more reasonable OEY 4% + EV/EBIT 14x:**
- OEY bear: $46.1B / 4% = $1,153B = ~$444
- EV/EBIT bear (14x, $88B normalized EBIT): $1,232B = ~$474
- Bear FV: 60% x $444 + 40% x $474 = $266 + $190 = $456

**Base FV calculation:**
- OEY base (3.2% yield): $46.1B / 3.2% = ~$554
- EV/EBIT base (17x, $95B): $1,615B = ~$621
- Base FV: 60% x $554 + 40% x $621 = $332 + $248 = $581

**Final FV = 60% Bear + 40% Base = 60% x $456 + 40% x $581 = $274 + $232 = $506**

Adjusting upward slightly for the extreme conservatism of double-applying the 60/40 weighting: **FV = $540** (splitting difference between pure 60/40 mechanical result and single-pass bear-weighted calculation).
