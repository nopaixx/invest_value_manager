# Devil's Advocate: CKN.L (Clarkson PLC)

## Date: 2026-03-23

## Resumen Ejecutivo

The R1 thesis (dated 2026-02-19) is partially STALE. FY2025 results are now published (PBT GBP 90.6M, EPS 225.8p, within guidance). The stock has risen from 4095p (thesis date) to 4535p (+10.7%), narrowing the upside to 14.7% vs FV 5200p. Three of five key assumptions survive scrutiny. However, the thesis materially OVERSTATES the margin of safety available, and the Hormuz crisis creates a complex dual-edged dynamic that the thesis did not contemplate. The FV of 5200p is AGGRESSIVE for a cyclical Tier B stock now trading at 21.3x trailing P/E with confirmed margin compression. My independent bear-case valuation produces 3750p, suggesting the stock is trading in a no-man's land: above fair value in a bear case, but below base-case value -- with insufficient margin for the risk profile.

## Assumptions Challenged

### 1. "Through-cycle revenue growth of 3-5% supports FV of 5200p"

- **Evidence against:** FY2025 confirmed revenue decline to GBP 631.4M (-4.5% vs FY2024 GBP 661.4M). This is now TWO data points of deceleration: +3.4% (FY2024), then -4.5% (FY2025). The thesis assumed "3-5% through-cycle growth" but the 5-year revenue CAGR including FY2025 shows the growth was almost entirely driven by the 2021-2022 COVID shipping boom. Normalized FY2021-FY2025 revenue growth is approximately +9.3% CAGR, but this includes a low base (GBP 443M in 2021). The FY2023-FY2025 window (peak-to-trough) shows -2.4% CAGR. The through-cycle growth assumption is plausible at 3-5% but may be optimistic at the upper end.
- **Counterpoint (favorable):** The forward order book INCREASED to USD 244M (from USD 231M), and management stated Q4 2025 showed momentum with early 2026 spot business exceeding prior year levels. The Zuma Labs acquisition adds AI-enabled FFA trading capability.
- **Severity:** MODERATE
- **Resolution:** Through-cycle growth of 3% is better supported than 5%. Use 3% in base case, not 5%.

### 2. "FV 5200p from EV/EBIT 13x and P/E 22x on through-cycle earnings"

- **Evidence against:** The 5200p FV involves rounding UP from a weighted average of 4980p, adding a 4.4% "quality premium." This quality premium is applied ON TOP of already-generous multiples.

  Specific multiple concerns:
  - **EV/EBIT 13x:** The thesis argues "quality broker with market dominance: 12-15x." But the comparable set is questionable. Insurance brokers (MMC at 15-18x) are NOT good comparables -- they have recurring premium revenue, regulatory moats, and far lower cyclicality. Clarkson's 80% revenue is non-recurring commission income on cyclical shipping transactions. A more appropriate comparable is a cyclical industrials broker at 10-12x. Current EV/EBIT is 8.7x (per reverse DCF tool), and using FY2025 operating profit of GBP ~97M (Broking 93.9 + Financial 12.9 + Support 4.8 + Research 10.6 = GBP 122.2M total, less admin = ~97M PBT implies OP around GBP 95-100M), the market is pricing at ~10x current EBIT. This is arguably FAIR for a cyclical broker, not cheap.
  - **P/E 22x:** The thesis uses Clarkson's own 5-year average P/E as the "justified" multiple. But this 5-year average includes the boom years 2021-2024 when the market gave higher multiples because earnings were growing. Using 22x on TROUGH-ADJUSTED earnings is double-counting: you cannot normalize earnings DOWN and then apply PEAK multiples. A 18-20x multiple on through-cycle EPS of 230p gives 4140-4600p -- i.e., the stock is FAIRLY VALUED right now, not 15% undervalued.
  - **Quality premium of 4.4% (4980p rounded to 5200p):** The precedent cited (ADDT-B.ST: max 10% quality premium) was for a Tier A company. CKN.L is Tier B (QS 70 adjusted). Applying a quality premium to a Tier B cyclical is unjustified.

- **Severity:** HIGH
- **Resolution:** FV should be 4400-4800p, not 5200p. At current price of 4535p, MoS is effectively 0-6%, grossly insufficient for a Tier B cyclical.

### 3. "FCF margin compression is cyclical and will recover to 15-17%"

- **Evidence against:** The FCF margin trajectory is: 28.0% (2022) -> 22.6% (2023) -> 16.2% (2024). FY2025 data: free cash resources INCREASED to GBP 232M from GBP 216.3M despite lower profits, which suggests working capital release (favorable) rather than operating improvement. However, the operating profit margin compression is confirmed: Broking operating profit fell from GBP 122.6M to GBP 93.9M (-23.4%) on revenue decline of ~4.5%.

  The CRITICAL insight from the risk assessment is the operating leverage: -4% revenue produced -23% PBT in H1 2025, and the full year confirmed this pattern (revenue -4.5%, PBT -21.4%). This 4.8x operating leverage is structural, driven by 80%+ of costs being staff-related.

  Moreover, the UK's employer NI increase of 1.2% effective April 2025 adds structural cost pressure. Administrative expenses fell only GBP 11.7M (GBP 526.0M to GBP 514.3M, -2.2%) despite revenue falling GBP 30M (-4.5%). The cost base is sticky.

- **Counterpoint (partially favorable):** Management did cut admin expenses GBP 11.7M, showing some cost flexibility. Financial division delivered record profit (GBP 12.9M, up from GBP 5.2M) partially offsetting Broking decline. Research division profit grew to GBP 10.6M from GBP 9.5M (+11.6%).
- **Severity:** MODERATE
- **Resolution:** FCF margin of 15-17% for FY2027 is plausible IF shipping cycle recovers. But the thesis underestimates the severity and stickiness of the cost structure in downturns.

### 4. "The Research division is a 'hidden gem' that provides margin floor"

- **Evidence against:** Research division FY2025 operating profit: GBP 10.6M (up from GBP 9.5M). On a GBP 1.4B market cap, this represents 0.76% of market cap. Even capitalizing Research at 25x earnings (generous for a niche data business), it is worth GBP 265M -- approximately 19% of market cap. It is NOT a hidden gem; it is a small, nice-to-have adjunct to a cyclical commission business.

  The thesis claims "Clarksons Research = Bloomberg Terminal of shipping." This is hyperbolic. Bloomberg has 325,000+ terminal subscribers at ~USD 24K/year each = USD 7.8B+ revenue. Clarksons Research has 15,000 users at ~GBP 1,670/user/year = GBP 25M revenue. The comparison in scale, switching costs, and network effects is not valid. SIN is a useful data product, not a platform monopoly.

- **Severity:** LOW-MODERATE
- **Resolution:** Research is a genuine competitive advantage and provides earnings stability, but its financial impact is modest (4% of revenue, <10% of operating profit). It should be valued as a nice attribute, not as a thesis-changing asset.

### 5. "Market is overly pessimistic -- pricing in -9.8% annual FCF decline"

- **Evidence against (UPDATED DATA):** At the new price of 4535p (up from 4095p at thesis date), the reverse DCF still shows -9.6% implied FCF decline. However, the FY2025 ACTUAL FCF decline was meaningful: operating profit fell ~21%, and FCF was only sustained by working capital release. The market's implied -9.6% annual decline over 5 years is indeed pessimistic (cumulative ~40% decline), but it is not absurdly so given:
  - FY2024 to FY2025: PBT fell 21.4% in ONE year
  - If Hormuz crisis resolves and shipping normalizes, rates could fall further in H2 2026
  - The Red Sea rerouting that boosted ton-miles in 2024 is now complicated by BOTH Hormuz AND Red Sea closures

  The thesis assumes the market is pricing "permanent collapse." But the market may be pricing a multi-year shipping downturn (2025-2027) before recovery -- which is a reasonable scenario given the shipping cycle outlook.

  **Hormuz crisis complication:** The Strait of Hormuz closure (effective March 2, 2026) creates a PARADOXICAL situation for Clarkson:
  - SHORT-TERM POSITIVE: VLCC rates hit all-time highs ($423K/day), massive rerouting increases ton-miles, increased broker demand for complex rerouting/war-risk advisory
  - MEDIUM-TERM NEGATIVE: 22% of global tanker fleet stranded in Middle East Gulf, insurance cover being cancelled, physical trade volumes through Hormuz collapsed 92%. If prolonged, reduces TOTAL shipping activity even as per-unit rates spike
  - Deutsche Bank analyst James Beard noted Clarkson is "well-placed" in this environment
  - Clarksea Index up 20% YTD vs 2025 average
  - Stock has rallied from ~3500p (post-profit-warning low) to 4535p partly on Hormuz tailwind expectations

  **Key question:** Is the current 4535p ALREADY pricing in Hormuz-driven short-term upside? If yes, buying now captures the crisis premium, not the through-cycle value.

- **Severity:** MODERATE
- **Resolution:** Market pessimism was valid at 3500p (post-profit-warning). At 4535p, the stock has already re-rated 30% from the trough. The easy money has been made.

### Interpretation:

The thesis has genuine strengths: Clarkson is an undeniably high-quality business with a dominant market position, fortress balance sheet, and a small but valuable data franchise. The QS adjustment from 83 to 70 was appropriate and well-reasoned. The fundamental analysis was thorough.

However, the VALUATION is the weak point. The FV of 5200p relies on generous multiples (22x P/E, 13x EV/EBIT) applied to through-cycle earnings, plus a quality premium, which collectively overstate value by 10-15%. At the current price of 4535p, the stock has already re-rated 30% from its post-profit-warning trough. The MoS at current price is effectively 0-6% using corrected multiples -- grossly insufficient for a Tier B cyclical with 4.8x operating leverage.

The Hormuz crisis adds complexity: short-term tailwinds are real (Clarksea Index +20% YTD, record tanker rates), but buying during a crisis-driven spike captures temporary premium, not through-cycle value. When the crisis resolves, rates will normalize and the underlying shipping softness will re-emerge.

### Recommendation to Investment Committee:

1. **REDUCE FV from 5200p to 4400-4600p.** Use 18-20x P/E (not 22x) and 11-12x EV/EBIT (not 13x) on through-cycle earnings. Remove quality premium (Tier B does not warrant it).
2. **Entry at 3500p (thesis target) is CORRECT and should be MAINTAINED.** This provides 22-30% MoS vs corrected FV of 4400-4600p, appropriate for a Tier B cyclical. The stock traded there in April 2025 -- it was achievable.
3. **DO NOT BUY at current 4535p.** At best, 3% upside to corrected FV. Insufficient for risk profile.
4. **WAIT for Hormuz crisis resolution.** Post-crisis normalization will likely compress rates and the stock could retrace toward 3800-4000p, providing a more sensible entry.
5. **MONITOR CFO transition.** New CFO appointment is a catalyst to watch (positive or negative depending on quality of appointment).
6. **GATE: FY2025 results (now available) PASSED.** PBT GBP 90.6M was within guidance. H2 weighted recovery confirmed. Forward order book grew. This removes the original HARD GATE but the timing issue remains.

## META-REFLECTION

### Doubts/Uncertainties
- The Hormuz crisis is evolving in real-time (crisis began March 2, 2026). Its duration and resolution are unknowable. If the crisis extends beyond 6 months, Clarkson could benefit enormously from elevated rates, potentially earning GBP 120-140M PBT in FY2026. This would make the stock look cheap at 4535p. I cannot model this scenario with confidence.
- The through-cycle EBIT of GBP 91-94M used by the thesis and my own GBP 82M bear case are BOTH potentially stale if Hormuz reshapes the shipping market for years.
- I could not access Clarkson's FY2025 annual report PDF directly to verify divisional margins, FCF breakdown, or the precise cost structure. My divisional analysis is based on press releases and third-party summaries.

### Limitations of This Analysis
- No access to sell-side research reports (Deutsche Bank, Canaccord Genuity) to understand their models and assumptions in detail
- The FY2025 divisional P&L is derived from aggregated summaries, not direct filing data
- Insider dealing data beyond Cotzias's March 2026 purchase and the April 2025 CEO/CFO sales is limited
- Shipping cycle timing is inherently uncertain and I have no proprietary freight rate forecasting capability

### Suggestions for the System
- The thesis was written on 2026-02-19 but FY2025 results came out on ~2026-03-09. The R2 should have been triggered immediately after FY2025 results as this was the stated HARD GATE. The 2-week delay means the thesis was already partially stale when the Hormuz crisis erupted.
- For cyclical businesses, the valuation skill should explicitly discourage using historical AVERAGE P/E as the "justified" multiple during a downturn. The correct approach is to use TROUGH multiples on NORMALIZED earnings, or MID-CYCLE multiples on TROUGH earnings -- not PEAK multiples on TROUGH earnings.
