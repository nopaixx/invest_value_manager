## 2. Resolution — Point by Point

### 2.1 FV 5,200p: DA wins — multiples too generous

The DA's strongest finding: the R1 applied PEAK multiples (22x P/E from 5-year boom average) to NORMALIZED earnings. This double-counts: you can't normalize DOWN and apply PEAK multiples. Additionally:

- EV/EBIT 13x is generous for a cyclical broker with 80% non-recurring commission revenue. Insurance brokers (MMC 15-18x) have recurring premiums; Clarkson does not. 10-12x is appropriate.
- The 4.4% quality premium (4,980→5,200p) was applied to a Tier B stock. Precedent was Tier A. Rejected.
- R1 FV 5,200p is near the TOP of analyst range (3,484-5,355p). Our FV at consensus top = no edge.

**Resolution:** EV/EBIT 11-12x (split between DA's 10x and R1's 13x). P/E 19-20x (below R1's 22x, above DA's 18x). No quality premium.

### 2.2 Through-Cycle Growth: DA mostly wins → 3%

FY2025 confirmed -4.5% revenue. The 14.3% CAGR was COVID-base inflated. Normalized:
- FY2023-25: -2.4% CAGR (peak-to-trough)
- Order book +6% OCC provides forward support
- Management says early 2026 spot exceeds prior year

But the 3-5% range's upper end requires sustained shipping cycle recovery. With ceasefire 2 weeks (Hormuz may normalize), the Red Sea + Hormuz rerouting ton-mile tailwind UNWINDS. Post-normalization growth is probably 2-4%, not 5%.

**Resolution:** 3% through-cycle (DA's recommendation). 5% only in bull case with sustained energy security theme.

### 2.3 Operating Leverage: DA confirmed by data

-4.5% revenue → -21.4% PBT. 4.8x operating leverage. The risk-identifier had flagged this and was RIGHT. Admin costs are 81.4% of revenue and 80%+ is staff. Sticky in downturns.

**Resolution:** Operating leverage is structural and severe. Must be central to bear case modeling.

### 2.4 Hormuz Crisis: New since R1 — currently INFLATING stock

CKN.L rallied from ~3,500p (post-profit-warning) to 4,706p (+34%) largely on Hormuz crisis tailwind:
- VLCC rates spiked to ATH ($424K/day)
- Clarksea Index +20% YTD
- Rerouting increases ton-miles and broker complexity/demand

BUT: 2-week ceasefire announced Apr 7. If Hormuz normalizes, the rate spike UNWINDS. The stock at 4,706p is pricing in crisis-level rates continuing, not through-cycle economics.

**Resolution:** Current price captures crisis premium. Through-cycle FV must exclude Hormuz tailwind. Buying at 4,706p = buying the crisis premium, not the franchise.

### 2.5 QS 70 Tier B: Confirmed

Both R1 and DA agree on Tier B after the -13 adjustment for broker model flattery. The QS framework correctly captures that 78% ROIC is model artifact (ROE 17.3% is the real metric), 95% GM is standard for brokers, and revenue CAGR was COVID-inflated.

**Resolution:** QS 70 Tier B. MoS 18-20% required.
