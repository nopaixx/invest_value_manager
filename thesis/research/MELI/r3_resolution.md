## DA Challenges — Disposition

### 1. Anti-Bullish-Bias Protocol Override: $2,300→$2,450 (HIGH) — **ACCEPTED**

DA is correct. The FA calculated $2,300 via 60/40 protocol then overrode to $2,450 arguing "inputs are already conservative." This is exactly Error #65 — 25/25 prior DA corrections were negative. The protocol exists to correct systematic bias, not to be overridden when the analyst feels conservative.

**However**, $2,300 itself may be too generous. The DA's independent bear FV of $1,953 uses 30% normalized FCF margin (vs FA's 37.3%). I split the difference:

- FA protocol-compliant FV: $2,300
- DA independent base: $2,638 (16x normalized FCF)
- DA bear: $1,953 (12x normalized FCF)
- Consensus PT: $2,165

**R3 FV: $2,200** — midpoint of $2,300 (FA protocol) and DA range midpoint ~$2,100. This is:
- Below FA's $2,450 by 10.2%
- Below FA's protocol-compliant $2,300 by 4.3%
- Above DA bear $1,953 by 12.6%
- Near consensus $2,165 (+1.6%)

**Why $2,200 not $2,300:** The DA's normalized FCF argument is compelling. Using 37.3% FCF margin on a credit business with 90% YoY book growth overstates sustainable cash generation. 30% is conservative, 34% (3yr avg) is reasonable. At 32% normalized, the OEY shifts downward. $2,200 captures this without fully adopting DA's most conservative assumptions.

### 2. Competition Severity — Temu/Shopee (HIGH) — **PARTIALLY ACCEPTED**

DA presents strong evidence: Temu surpassed MELI in Brazil traffic (Jul 2025, 410M vs 393M). Shopee $10B GMV. JPMorgan downgraded citing Shopee specifically.

**Where DA is right:** Competition is more severe than R1 acknowledged. MELI's margin compression (OP margin 14.6%→11.1%) IS the competitive response — free shipping, 1P commerce, logistics investment. The competitive spending war has no clear end date.

**Where DA overshoots:** Traffic ≠ GMV ≠ revenue ≠ profit. MELI's GMV/buyer ($350) far exceeds Temu's average order ($15-25). Temu serves impulse-purchase, low-ASP segment. MELI serves considered-purchase, high-ASP segment with integrated logistics + payments + credit. The DA's own META-REFLECTION acknowledges this.

**Resolution:** Model margin compression lasting through 2028 (not just 2026-2027). OP margin base case: 12-13% (not R1's 14%). Add KC#7 for market share monitoring.

### 3. Bear FV = Current Price (HIGH) — **ACCEPTED**

R1 bear FV $1,600 vs market $1,636 = zero cushion. This is the most damning finding. At $1,636, if ANY bear assumption materializes, we are underwater from day 1.

**Resolution:** This VALIDATES the $1,500 entry. Do NOT market buy. The entry at $1,500 provides:
- MoS 31.8% vs R3 FV $2,200
- MoS 23.3% vs DA bear $1,953
- $100 cushion below R1 bear $1,600

### 4. QS +8 Adjustment / Tier Classification (MODERATE) — **PARTIALLY ACCEPTED**

DA argues QS should stay at 70 (Tier B) not 78 (Tier A). The +8 for market position is defensible (MELI IS #1 by a wide margin) but creates a classification change that cascades.

**Resolution:** QS 74 (Tier B, upper range). Accept +4 for market position (not +8 — the tool's GM premium 10/10 and ROIC persistence 7/7 already partially capture market dominance). This keeps MELI in Tier B with 20-25% MoS requirement. At $1,500 entry with $2,200 FV: MoS 31.8% — comfortably exceeds Tier B requirement.

### 5. FCF vs Operating Margin Divergence (MODERATE) — **ACCEPTED**

FCF 37.3% vs OP 11.1% = 26pp gap. Credit provisions are real economic costs. OCF/NI 6.1x is extreme.

**Resolution:** Use 32% normalized FCF margin (not 37.3%). This is built into the $2,200 FV.

### 6. CEO Transition (MODERATE) — **NOTED, NOT MATERIAL**

Szarfsztejn 3 months as CEO. Galperin stays as Executive Chairman ($1.1B+ personal stake). Not a departure — it's a planned succession with founder oversight. No KC needed beyond existing monitoring.
