## Business Understanding

### The Business in 2 Minutes

MercadoLibre operates the dominant integrated digital commerce and fintech ecosystem across Latin America, serving 218M+ unique buyers and 72M monthly active fintech users. It solves two massive problems simultaneously:

1. **E-commerce (Mercado Libre):** Provides a trusted marketplace in a region where physical retail infrastructure is fragmented and underpenetrated (e-commerce is only mid-teens % of retail in LatAm vs 20%+ in developed markets). Integrated logistics network (Mercado Envios) handles ~90% of items shipped.

2. **Fintech (Mercado Pago):** Provides digital payments, credit, insurance, and investment products to a region where ~40% of the population is underbanked. Credit portfolio of $12.5B growing 90% YoY. The payments platform processes both on-marketplace and off-marketplace transactions, creating a standalone fintech business.

The dual flywheel: marketplace drives payments adoption (merchants need to accept Pago), payments drive marketplace (buyers use Pago credits to buy on Libre), both drive logistics density, logistics drives customer satisfaction, satisfaction drives repeat purchases.
