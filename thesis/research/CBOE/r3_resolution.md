## Resolution of FA vs DA Disagreements

### 1. S&P DJI License Dependency (DA HIGH) — ACCEPTED, CRITICAL
This was completely OMITTED from the R1 thesis. The DA found that CBOE's crown jewels (SPX/VIX) operate under a LICENSE from S&P DJI expiring 2032 (exclusive) / 2033 (non-exclusive). This is the most important risk factor for the business — the monopoly is a license, not owned IP.

However, context matters: CBOE has renewed this license before (20-year extension in 2013). S&P DJI benefits from CBOE's distribution and volume. The relationship is symbiotic, not adversarial. Risk is royalty repricing, not license loss.

**Resolution: FV $273 → $260 (-4.8%). Add KC#8 (S&P DJI license signals). Monitor royalty disclosures in 10-K.**

### 2. 0DTE Regulatory Risk (DA HIGH) — ACCEPTED PARTIALLY
0DTE at 63% of SPX volume IS concentrated. SEC staff paper and OCC margin add-ons confirm active regulatory attention. However, 0DTE is transformative for hedging — institutional adoption (not just retail) makes an outright ban unlikely. More likely: position limits, margin requirements, or reporting obligations that slow growth but don't kill it.

I model a scenario where 0DTE share stabilizes at 50% (from 63%) = ~15% volume decline = ~$200M revenue impact = $2-3/share EPS impact. This is already partially captured in the bear case.

**Resolution: Bear case adequate. Add KC#9 (SEC formal 0DTE rulemaking). No additional FV adjustment.**

### 3. Revenue Cyclicality (DA MODERATE) — ACCEPTED
FY2023 revenue DECLINED 4.7% when VIX sustained below 15. If Hormuz resolves and VIX reverts to 15-18, volumes could compress 20-30%. The current elevated VIX (23-26) flatters the business. The 60/40 bear/base methodology captures this — the bear case uses lower volume assumptions.

**Resolution: Already in bear case. KC#7 (sustained low VIX) confirmed as monitoring item.**

### 4. Fee Capture Compression (DA MODERATE) — NOTED
Multi-listed RPC declined -21% QoQ. This is real but partially offset by proprietary product growth. The mix shift from multi-listed (commoditized) to proprietary (monopoly pricing) is actually POSITIVE for margins long-term.

**Resolution: Monitor RPC trends. Not FV-impacting yet.**
