## 1. Business Overview

Cboe Global Markets is the world's largest options exchange operator and owner of the most valuable proprietary index products in derivatives: the S&P 500 Index Options (SPX) and the Cboe Volatility Index (VIX). The company operates four US options exchanges commanding ~31% of US options volume, making it the single largest player ahead of Nasdaq (~28%) and other competitors. Beyond US options, Cboe operates North American and European equities exchanges, futures markets, FX trading, a growing data business (Data Vantage), and digital asset infrastructure.

The critical insight about Cboe is its toll-booth monopoly structure. SPX Index options hold a 74% market share of all S&P 500-linked derivatives, and VIX options/futures are exclusively traded on Cboe -- there is no alternative venue. This means that every participant hedging S&P 500 exposure or trading volatility MUST use Cboe's products. Revenue comes primarily from per-contract transaction fees (volume-driven) and an increasingly important recurring data/analytics business (Data Vantage, ~10% of net revenue, growing mid-to-high single digits).

FY2025 was a record year: net revenue of $2.4 billion (+17% YoY), adjusted EPS of $10.67 (+24%), and total options ADV of 18.4 million contracts (sixth consecutive record year). The secular trend toward options adoption is structural -- driven by institutional hedging demand, retail 0DTE trading (57% of SPX volume), and the electronification of fixed income and FX markets internationally. The company operates with a net cash position of $626M, minimal capex requirements, and generates $1.68B in FCF.

## 2. Competitive Position / Moat Assessment

**Moat Rating: WIDE**

Evidence supporting a wide moat:

**Regulatory Monopoly on Proprietary Products.** SPX options and VIX options/futures are proprietary Cboe products. No competitor can offer these instruments. SPX options reached a record 74% market share of all S&P 500-linked derivatives. VIX options set a record ADV of 858,000 contracts in Q3 2025. This is an irreplaceable monopoly that would require decades of regulatory change to dislodge.

**Network Effects and Liquidity Moat.** Options markets exhibit powerful network effects: the more participants trade on a venue, the tighter the spreads, which attracts more participants. Cboe's 31% share of US multi-listed options reflects this liquidity gravity. Switching costs are real -- market makers and institutional traders maintain connectivity, algorithms, and execution infrastructure optimized for Cboe's platforms.

**Data Moat.** Cboe's real-time and historical market data from its proprietary products (SPX, VIX, volatility surfaces) is non-replicable. Data Vantage grew 10% in FY2025, with 90% of growth coming from new units/sales rather than pricing -- indicating genuine organic expansion. This recurring revenue stream is becoming increasingly valuable.

**Switching Costs.** Clearing relationships, regulatory frameworks, technology integrations, and the embedded nature of SPX/VIX in institutional risk management create multi-year switching barriers. Pension funds, insurance companies, and systematic strategies reference VIX and SPX in their mandates and documentation.

**International Expansion.** European equities market share grew from 22.5% to 25.1% YoY, creating new growth vectors where competitors (primarily Euronext, LSE) are entrenched but losing share.

**Threat Assessment:**
- CME competition in index derivatives: CME's E-mini options have ceded share to SPX (now only 26% vs SPX's 74%). The trend favors Cboe.
- Prediction markets (Polymarket, Kalshi): Cboe and CME jointly urged CFTC review of prediction market rules in March 2026. Early-stage regulatory risk but marginal to Cboe's core business.
- Technological disruption: Low. Exchange infrastructure is deeply embedded in financial system plumbing.

## 4. Growth Drivers & Risks

### Growth Drivers

1. **Secular Options Adoption (+++):** Sixth consecutive year of record options volume. 0DTE options (57% of SPX volume) are a genuine innovation driving new participants. Institutional hedging demand growing with market complexity.

2. **Data Vantage Expansion (++):** 10% growth in FY2025. Recurring revenue with high incremental margins. Real-time volatility data is non-replicable. Guided mid-to-high single digits for 2026.

3. **International Expansion (++):** European equities market share 22.5% to 25.1%. Asia-Pacific presence building. Global derivatives adoption lags the US, providing a long runway.

4. **Margin Expansion (+):** Operating margin from 24.5% (2022) to 32.1% (2025). Operating leverage from fixed-cost exchange infrastructure processing higher volumes.

5. **Capital Returns (+):** Conservative balance sheet enables sustained buybacks, supporting EPS growth above revenue growth. Share count declining.

### Risks

1. **Volume Cyclicality (HIGH):** ~75-80% of net revenue is transaction-based. A prolonged period of low volatility (VIX <15 sustained) would compress revenues. FY2023 showed this: revenue declined 4.7% with lower volumes.

2. **Regulatory Risk (MEDIUM):** SEC could increase scrutiny on 0DTE options (systemic risk arguments), impose fee caps on exchange data, or mandate competition in proprietary products. Prediction market regulation is an emerging area.

3. **Concentration in SPX/VIX (MEDIUM):** The crown jewels (SPX 74% share, VIX monopoly) generate a disproportionate share of high-margin revenue. Any regulatory challenge to proprietary index exclusivity would be existential.

4. **Valuation Risk (MEDIUM):** At 28x P/E and 18.5x EV/EBIT, the stock prices in continued strong execution. Mid-single-digit guidance for 2026 after +17% in 2025 suggests normalization -- the market may be pricing in more than guidance implies.

5. **Low Insider Ownership (LOW):** 0.2% insider ownership means management has limited skin in the game. Institutional ownership at 89.4% is standard for this market cap.

6. **Geopolitical/Crisis Impact (VARIABLE):** Paradoxically, market crises BENEFIT Cboe through higher VIX levels and increased hedging activity. The Hormuz crisis and tariff uncertainty are positive for volumes in the near term.

## 8. Kill Conditions

1. **SPX options market share falls below 60%** (from 74% today). Would indicate structural erosion of the monopoly in S&P 500-linked derivatives.

2. **SEC mandates competition in proprietary index products** (SPX/VIX opening to other venues). This would be existential for Cboe's premium business.

3. **Total US options ADV declines >20% YoY for 2 consecutive quarters.** Would signal structural demand decline, not just cyclical volatility normalization.

4. **Data Vantage revenue growth turns negative for 2 consecutive quarters.** The recurring revenue stream is the stability anchor. Negative growth would signal competitive erosion.

5. **ROIC falls below 15% for 2 consecutive years.** Current ROIC of 24% has strong trajectory. A sustained decline to <15% would indicate the business model is degrading.

6. **Net debt exceeds 2.5x EBITDA** (currently net cash). A leveraged acquisition destroying the fortress balance sheet would change the risk profile fundamentally.

7. **0DTE options regulatory ban or severe restrictions.** 0DTE represents 57% of SPX volume. A ban would remove a major growth driver, though the base business would survive.
