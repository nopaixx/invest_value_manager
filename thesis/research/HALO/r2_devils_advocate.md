# Counter-Analysis: HALO (Halozyme Therapeutics)

## Fecha: 2026-02-26

---

## COUNTER-THESIS SUMMARY

The fundamental-analyst thesis presents HALO as a royalty monopoly trading at a significant discount to intrinsic value. However, the thesis systematically understates five material risks: (1) the ENHANZE patent cliff is NOT fully mitigated by Hypercon/Surf Bio -- royalty RATES decline upon patent expiry even if contracts survive, and replacement revenues from new IP won't arrive until 2030+, creating a 3-5 year revenue gap; (2) Goldman Sachs' SELL thesis (PT $56-$58) projects a 38% revenue decline from 2030-2041, requiring $34B in new partner product revenues to sustain current valuation -- this is NOT consensus bullishness; (3) Alteogen has emerged as a genuine competing platform with $1.65B in signed deals (AstraZeneca + Daiichi Sankyo), breaking the "monopoly" narrative; (4) the CMS/IRA draft guidance on combination products could accelerate price negotiations for DARZALEX SC, directly threatening the largest royalty stream; and (5) CEO Helen Torley has sold approximately $10M+ in stock over the last 12 months via 10b5-1 plan while insider ownership sits at only 1.6%. Short interest at 16% of float with 7.5 days to cover signals informed bearish positioning, not just noise. The FA's FV of $110 exceeds the highest analyst PT ($96) by 15% -- without a clear informational edge, this appears to be optimism, not alpha.

## Assumptions Mapped from Thesis

### Critical Assumption 1: QS Adjustment 73 -> 82 (+9 points)

**Thesis claim:** Tool scored 73 (Tier B). Adjusted to 82 (Tier A) based on market position (+5), buybacks (+2), EPS CAGR distortion (+2).

**Adversarial challenge:** The adjustment is directionally justified but likely overstated.

- **Market position (+5):** Partially valid. HALO IS the dominant SC delivery platform. However, Alteogen has signed $1.65B in deals with AstraZeneca ($1.35B) and Daiichi Sankyo ($300M), and separately powers Merck's Keytruda SC. The "undisputed #1 with NO competitor" framing is already outdated. More accurate: #1 with 1 credible competitor gaining rapid traction. Adjustment of +3 (not +5) is more defensible.

- **Buybacks (+2):** Valid. $2.7B in buybacks since 2022 is aggressive. However, buybacks funded partly by debt (net debt $2B) are less impressive than buybacks from excess cash. The buyback is partly financial engineering, partly genuine return. +1.5 is more appropriate.

- **EPS distortion (+2):** Partially valid. GAAP distortion from Surf Bio is real. But note: Halozyme previously took an impairment charge on Elektrofi/Surf Bio technology in Q4 2025 of $285M (acquired IPR&D). This is NOT purely "accounting noise" -- it reflects the reality that acquired IP may not deliver its promised value. The $2.42/share hit from IPR&D expense signals real uncertainty about these acquisitions. +1 is more appropriate.

**Revised assessment:** QS Tool 73 + ~5.5 adjustment = ~78-79 (Tier A, bottom end). This matters because at QS 78, the Tier A categorization is fragile -- any further deterioration drops it to Tier B, which changes the MoS framework.

**Severity: MODERATE**

### Critical Assumption 2: FV $110 (Weighted Average)

**Thesis claim:** OEY $116, DCF adjusted $117, EV/EBIT $116, Reverse DCF midpoint $81. Weighted to $113, rounded to $110.

**Adversarial challenge:** EVERY valuation method in the thesis uses forward 2026E numbers that assume management guidance is accurate. The fundamental risk is in the TERMINAL value and post-2029 trajectory.

**Problem 1: Three methods converge because they're all driven by the same forward FCF estimate ($850M).**

The OEY method uses $850M 2026E FCF. The EV/EBIT method uses 2026E EBIT of $1.08B. The adjusted DCF uses $850M starting FCF growing at 13%. These are NOT independent triangulations -- they are three ways of expressing the same bullish 2026 estimate. The reverse DCF midpoint ($81) is the ONLY method anchored to reality rather than forward estimates.

**Problem 2: The adjusted DCF ($117 after 30% haircut) is unreliable.**

The raw DCF produced $167 before a subjective 30% haircut. If the haircut were 35% (reasonable given patent cliff), FV drops to $109. At 40% haircut, FV drops to $100. The choice of 30% is not justified with evidence.

**Problem 3: Goldman Sachs' model explicitly addresses what the FA's model ignores -- the post-2029 cliff.**

Goldman estimates royalty revenue growth slows to 1% YoY in 2029 and DECLINES thereafter. Their model shows a 38% revenue decline from 2030-2041. Goldman's PT is $56-$58 (SELL). This is not a fringe view -- it's a top-tier investment bank with dedicated pharma coverage.

**Problem 4: Analyst consensus PT is $84.50 (mean), $90 (median), high $96.**

The FA's FV of $110 exceeds the HIGHEST analyst PT ($96) by 14.6%. This means the FA believes it knows something that ALL 8 covering analysts missed. What specifically? The thesis identifies no unique data source.

**Severity: HIGH**

### Critical Assumption 3: "Royalty Platform Monopoly" Moat

**Thesis claim:** WIDE moat, 80% confidence. Only scaled rHuPH20 provider. 130+ patents. EXTREME switching costs.

**Adversarial challenge:**

**The monopoly is BREAKING.**

Alteogen (South Korea) has emerged as a viable competitor with its ALT-B4 / Hybrozyme platform:
- Merck chose Alteogen over Halozyme for Keytruda SC (the world's #1 drug by revenue)
- AstraZeneca signed $1.35B in deals for multiple oncology assets
- Daiichi Sankyo signed $300M for subcutaneous Enhertu
- An unnamed "global pharma" signed an option deal with 2026 license call
- Total signed Alteogen deals: $1.65B+

This means NEW pharma partners now have a CHOICE. The switching costs protect EXISTING formulations (DARZALEX SC, Phesgo cannot switch), but they do NOT protect new deals. Every new partner that chooses Alteogen is revenue Halozyme will NEVER earn.

**The 130+ patents are narrower than presented:**
- Core rHuPH20 composition-of-matter: expires 2027 (US) / 2029 (EU)
- MDASE patents (anti-Merck): under active PTAB challenge
- Hypercon IP: extends to 2040s but is UNPROVEN (no clinical data yet)
- Surf Bio IP: even earlier stage than Hypercon

**Moat reclassification:** NARROW, not WIDE. Existing royalties are protected by switching costs (valid). New business faces genuine competition (Alteogen). Patent estate is shrinking, not growing, for the core ENHANZE platform.

**Severity: HIGH**

### Critical Assumption 4: Revenue +38% Sustainable / $2B+ by 2028

**Thesis claim:** Guided $1.71-1.81B for 2026, $2B+ by 2028. Revenue CAGR 2025-2028: 13% (base case).

**Adversarial challenge:**

The 2026 guidance is credible and likely achievable -- it is driven by DARZALEX SC momentum and argenx VYVGART Hytrulo growth. However, the $2B+ by 2028 target masks a critical transition.

**Goldman Sachs projects:**
- Revenue growth 20% CAGR 2025-2028 (strong)
- Revenue growth SLOWS to 1% in 2029
- Revenue DECLINES starting 2030
- 70% of modeled royalties expire by 2035

The thesis's 13% base case CAGR through 2028 is reasonable. But the thesis then applies this growth rate in DCF projections for 5 years (through 2031), which would span INTO the decline period. This is a fundamental modeling error.

**DARZALEX maturation timeline:**
- DARZALEX SC had $483M in royalties in 2025 (56% of total)
- DARZALEX is already standard of care with 97% US market share in its franchise
- Growth is increasingly from geographic expansion (which has lower royalty rates)
- Biosimilar development is active: Henlius received FDA IND clearance for SC daratumumab biosimilar in February 2026. Celltrion has EMA approval for Phase 3.
- J&J DARZALEX patents extend to mid-2030s, but biosimilar pressure begins affecting pricing/volume by ~2029-2030

**The "$2B+ by 2028" may be peak revenue, not a waypoint.**

**Severity: MODERATE-HIGH**

### Critical Assumption 5: DARZALEX Concentration is Manageable

**Thesis claim:** 56% of royalties from DARZALEX SC. argenx VYVGART diversifying. 15 pipeline programs.

**Adversarial challenge:**

56% revenue concentration in a single drug from a single partner is genuinely high risk, and the thesis understates the compounding nature of DARZALEX-specific risks:

1. **CMS/IRA combination product guidance:** In May 2025, CMS issued draft guidance that could classify SC formulations as "combination products" eligible for Medicare price negotiations based on the ORIGINAL drug's approval date, not the combination approval date. This directly threatens DARZALEX Faspro. The impact: Leerink downgraded HALO to Underperform, stock fell 27%. The thesis DOES NOT MENTION this CMS/IRA risk at all.

2. **EPO patent revocation:** In October 2024, the European Patent Office revoked J&J's co-formulation patent for DARZALEX SC (EP 3827845). While Halozyme claims no impact on royalties through 2032, this creates precedent for further patent challenges in Europe.

3. **Biosimilar timeline accelerating:** Henlius (Shanghai) received FDA IND clearance for SC daratumumab biosimilar in February 2026. This is MUCH earlier than the thesis's "5+ years" estimate.

4. **Royalty rate step-down:** Upon ENHANZE patent expiry (US 2027, EU 2029), royalty rates STEP DOWN to a lower tier. The contracts survive, but at REDUCED rates. The thesis claims "royalties survive patent expiry" without acknowledging the rate reduction.

**The diversification narrative is real but slow.** argenx VYVGART Hytrulo is growing rapidly but from a small base. It will take until 2028-2029 before it meaningfully offsets DARZALEX concentration. During that window, any DARZALEX weakness is disproportionately painful.

**Severity: HIGH**

---

### Critical Assumption 6: Merck PTAB 35% Adverse Probability

**Thesis claim:** 35% probability of Merck winning. Even partial loss is manageable.

**Adversarial challenge:**

The 35% probability is GENERIC (based on general PTAB invalidation rates), not specific to this case. Key considerations:

- Merck filed 14 PGR (Post-Grant Review) petitions against Halozyme patents. The PTAB has already instituted at least one (PGR2025-00046). When PTAB institutes review, it invalidates claims ~60-70% of the time.
- The dispute involves Halozyme's MDASE patents (not the core rHuPH20 composition-of-matter). If Merck wins on MDASE, it establishes that alternative hyaluronidase enzymes (like ALT-B4) do NOT infringe Halozyme's IP, opening the door for ALL future partners to bypass Halozyme.
- The German preliminary injunction is positive but non-binding and jurisdiction-specific.
- This is NOT just about Keytruda SC royalties. It is about whether Halozyme's patent moat extends to ANY modified hyaluronidase or only to rHuPH20 specifically. A Merck win would be a precedent that narrows the moat permanently.

**Revised probability of MATERIALLY adverse outcome:** 40-50% (higher than thesis's 35%). The consequences of loss are also understated -- it's not just $0 from Keytruda, it's validation that alternative enzymes are legal.

**Severity: HIGH**

---

### Critical Assumption 7: Hypercon/Surf Bio Are Platform-Extending

**Thesis claim:** Acquisitions extend IP to mid-2040s. Address patent cliff.

**Adversarial challenge:**

- **Elektrofi (Hypercon) acquisition cost: $750M upfront + $150M milestones = $900M total.** Surf Bio: $300M upfront + $100M milestones = $400M. Combined: $1.3B in acquisitions.
- **Hypercon is hyperconcentration technology, NOT a replacement for rHuPH20.** It is complementary (enables higher drug concentrations for SC delivery) but requires BOTH the technology AND a drug partner willing to reformulate. Two partners expected to initiate Phase 1 by end 2026.
- **Surf Bio is even earlier stage** -- clinic-ready by end 2027 or early 2028.
- **Revenue from these acquisitions:** Halozyme projects first royalties in 2030 for Hypercon. Surf Bio later.
- **The gap problem:** Core ENHANZE royalty rates step down 2027-2029. Hypercon/Surf Bio royalties don't begin until 2030+. There is a 1-3 year VALLEY where the old IP is declining and the new IP hasn't delivered.
- **Impairment risk is real:** The $285M IPR&D charge for Surf Bio in Q4 2025 was recognized because the technology is still in development. If clinical results disappoint, further impairments are possible.
- **Management track record with acquisitions:** Antares Pharma ($5.6B in 2022) has been accretive but primarily for product sales, not royalties. The Hypercon/Surf Bio acquisitions are a bet on unproven technology -- fundamentally different risk profile.

**The $1.3B spent on Hypercon + Surf Bio is NOT a low-risk "platform extension." It is a high-stakes bet on early-stage technology to replace a declining core patent estate. The thesis treats this as risk-mitigating when it is actually risk-ADDING in the near term.**

**Severity: MODERATE-HIGH**

## Independent Bear-Case Valuation (DA Phase 3B)

### Method: EV/EBIT with Terminal Decay (different from FA's primary OEY method)

**Step 1: Near-term value (2026-2029)**
- 2026E EBIT: $1.08B (per thesis, reasonable given guidance)
- 2027E EBIT: $1.15B (royalty rate step-down US begins, partially offset by volume growth)
- 2028E EBIT: $1.20B (peak, DARZALEX SC maturation approaching)
- 2029E EBIT: $1.05B (EU patent expiry, royalty rate step-down in Europe, DARZALEX growth stalls)

**Step 2: Multiple assignment**
- Peers: Royalty Pharma (RPRX) trades at P/E 12.8x, well below sector avg of 30.4x
- HALO forward EV/EBIT: 9.5x currently. With revenue cliff visibility, 10-12x is generous.
- Apply 11x to 2027E normalized EBIT (mid-point of near-term trajectory)

**Step 3: Calculation**
- EV = $1.15B x 11x = $12.65B
- Less: Net debt $2.0B
- Equity = $10.65B
- Shares: 122M (declining with buybacks -- assume 118M by 2027)
- FV = $10.65B / 118M = **~$90/share**

But this IGNORES the terminal decline. Apply a 15% terminal decay discount (Goldman estimates 38% revenue decline 2030-2041):
- Adjusted FV = $90 x 0.85 = **~$76/share**

### DA Bear Case FV: $76

This is conservative but not unreasonable. It assumes:
- Near-term growth materializes (gives credit for 2026 guidance)
- Royalty rate step-downs occur on patent expiry
- No terminal value premium for Hypercon/Surf Bio (unproven)
- Moderate multiple (11x, between current 9.5x and comps 15-25x)
- 15% discount for post-2030 revenue cliff

## Recomendacion al Investment Committee

Before approving this thesis, the committee should require:

1. **Model the CMS/IRA impact.** What happens to DARZALEX Faspro royalties if CMS finalizes the combination product guidance? This alone could reduce DARZALEX royalty revenue by 15-25% starting 2028.

2. **Reduce FV to account for royalty rate step-downs.** The thesis should model royalty revenue at post-patent-expiry rates (lower tier) starting 2027 (US) and 2029 (EU). This likely reduces FV by $10-15.

3. **Explicitly model the 2029-2030 transition valley.** What is EBIT if ENHANZE rates decline, Hypercon hasn't ramped, and DARZALEX growth stalls? If EBIT contracts 15-20% from peak, the stock could trade down to $55-65.

4. **Consider waiting for Merck PTAB clarity (Jun 2026).** A binary risk 4 months away argues for patience. If HALO wins, entry below $80 is likely still available. If HALO loses partially, the stock could fall to $55-60.

5. **If proceeding, use standing order at $60-65 rather than market buy at $70.** This provides MoS against the DA bear case ($76) and accounts for the possibility that the market has priced HALO correctly at current levels.

6. **Adjust FV downward.** Recommended post-DA FV: $88-92 (incorporates royalty rate step-downs, Alteogen competition reality, and terminal decay). At current price of $70.35, this still provides 25-30% upside and E[CAGR] of ~12-13%, which meets Tier A thresholds. But it does NOT support a market buy -- it supports a standing order.
