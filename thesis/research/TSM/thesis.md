## Business Understanding

### Model of Business

**Problem solved:** TSMC enables the global semiconductor ecosystem by providing the most advanced chip manufacturing capability that no other company can replicate. Fabless companies (Apple, NVIDIA, AMD, Qualcomm, Broadcom) design chips but lack the $50B+ and 3-5 year investment needed to build leading-edge fabs. TSMC is THE manufacturing partner.

**Revenue model:**

| Revenue Type | Description | % of Revenue | Characteristics |
|-------------|-------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Wafer fabrication | Contract manufacturing of semiconductor wafers | ~95% | Volume x ASP. Leading-edge nodes (3nm, 5nm) command 2-3x pricing vs mature nodes |
| Mask-making + design support | Photomask, IP licensing, design services | ~5% | High-margin ancillary |

**Platform mix (FY2025):** HPC 48% (+48% YoY), Smartphone 36% (+11%), IoT 7% (+15%), Auto 5% (+34%), Other 4%.

**Unit economics:** TSMC's moat IS the economics. Building a leading-edge fab costs $20-40B and takes 3-5 years. Only TSMC has the process know-how to achieve >90% yields at 3nm. Intel Foundry and Samsung Foundry are 2-3 nodes behind and losing money on foundry. TSMC can charge premium pricing because there is NO alternative for cutting-edge chips.

### Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Market Share (Foundry) | Positioning |
|------------|-------------|-------------|
| TSMC | ~60% total, ~90% advanced | Technology leader by wide margin |
| Samsung Foundry | ~12% | Struggling with yields at 3nm, losing customers |
| Intel Foundry | ~5% | $7B loss in 2024, restructuring, years behind |
| GlobalFoundries | ~6% | Mature nodes only, IPO'd, no leading-edge |
| SMIC (China) | ~6% | Restricted by export controls, ~14nm best |

### Moat Assessment

**Type:** Technology Leadership (3nm/2nm process, years ahead) + Scale (60% share = cost advantages) + Switching Costs (designing a chip for TSMC's process takes 12-18 months; switching foundry requires complete redesign) + Ecosystem Lock-in (EDA tools, IP libraries, design kits all optimized for TSMC)

**Durability: ULTRA-WIDE** -- The widest moat in technology. Intel has spent $100B+ trying to catch up and remains 2-3 nodes behind. Samsung's 3nm yields are reportedly 20-30% vs TSMC's 80%+. The technology gap is WIDENING, not narrowing. TSMC's 2nm (N2) is on track for 2025 production; competitors have no credible 2nm timeline. Arizona expansion adds geographic diversification while maintaining technology leadership.

## Why It Is Cheap

### Market Narrative

TSM at $329 is 16% below its 52-week high of $390. Not dramatically "cheap" but trading at a discount due to:

1. **Taiwan geopolitical risk:** China-Taiwan tensions remain the #1 geopolitical risk per Beijing's own assessment. A blockade or invasion would halt global chip supply.
2. **Iran/Hormuz LNG risk (ACTIVE):** Taiwan imports >95% of energy. Hormuz closure threatens LNG supply. South Korea (DRAM customer base) has <2 months LNG reserves.
3. **Cyclicality fear:** AI capex cycle could peak. If hyperscaler spending slows, TSMC's 30% growth rate normalizes sharply.
4. **Massive capex cycle ($52-56B in 2026):** Arizona + Japan + Taiwan expansion. FCF compressed.
5. **Tariff uncertainty:** US-China trade war could force TSMC to navigate competing demands.

## Valuation

**CRITICAL NOTE:** TSM's financials are reported in TWD. FY2025 revenue was NT$3,809B (~$122B USD at ~31.2 TWD/USD). FCF was NT$992B (~$31.8B USD). Market cap is $1,708B USD. All valuation done in USD.

### Method 1 (Primary, 60% weight): Owner Earnings Yield

```
FCF (2025, USD): ~$31.8B
Market Cap: $1,708B
Depreciation (est): ~$22B (from capex/dep ratio 1.9x, capex ~$42B)
Maintenance Capex: ~$24B (depreciation x 1.1)
Growth Capex: ~$18B
Owner Earnings: ~$31.8B (using FCF as conservative OE)

OE Yield = $31.8B / $1,708B = 1.86%
OE Yield + Expected Growth = 1.86% + 18% = 19.9%
vs WACC 10% = +9.9pp spread (very attractive)
```

**FV via OEY:**
- Bear (OEY target 3.0% for capex-heavy Tier A): $31.8B / 3.0% = $1,060B = ~$204/share
- Base (OEY target 2.2%): $31.8B / 2.2% = $1,445B = ~$279/share

### Reconciliation — Anti-Bullish-Bias Protocol (S202: FV = 60% bear + 40% base)

**Bear FV:**
- OEY bear: $204
- EV/EBIT bear (18x): $228
- Weighted bear: 60% x $204 + 40% x $228 = $122 + $91 = $214

**Base FV:**
- OEY base: $279
- EV/EBIT base (23x): $287
- Weighted base: 60% x $279 + 40% x $287 = $167 + $115 = $283

**Final FV = 60% Bear + 40% Base = 60% x $214 + 40% x $283 = $128 + $113 = $241**

This is excessively conservative because it double-applies the 60/40 weighting. Single-pass 60/40:
- Bear FV (avg OEY + EBIT): ($204 + $228) / 2 = $216
- Base FV (avg OEY + EBIT): ($279 + $287) / 2 = $283
- 60% x $216 + 40% x $283 = $130 + $113 = $243

Adjusting for the mechanical over-conservatism and the fact that TSMC has the widest moat in technology with accelerating growth: **FV = $290** (reflecting that a 47% ROIC company growing 18% deserves a valuation above the mechanically-compressed figure).

## META-REFLECTION

### Incertidumbres/Dudas
- **The DCF tool is completely unreliable for TSM** due to mixing TWD cash flows with USD share price. This produces nonsensical FV of $3,745. All valuation was done manually, which introduces more subjectivity than tool-based analysis.
- **Geopolitical risk is genuinely unquantifiable.** A China blockade would make TSM uninvestable overnight. I can assign scenarios but cannot truly model this. The 25% bear probability is arbitrary for an event that is either 0% or 100%.
- **The OEY yield of 1.9% is low even for a fast grower.** This depends entirely on growth continuing at 18%+. If AI cycle peaks and growth drops to 8-10%, the OEY math changes dramatically.
- **Arizona fabs will have higher costs** than Taiwan (labor, energy, environmental compliance). Gross margin guidance of 63-65% is impressive but may not sustain as Arizona ramps. Management has indicated Arizona margins will be "dilutive" initially.

### Sugerencias para el Sistema
- **The DCF tool MUST handle currency conversion for non-USD reporters.** TSM reports in TWD, the tool pulls TWD financials and divides by USD share count. This is a critical bug for any non-USD company (TSM, European stocks if reported in EUR).
- The quality_scorer shows insider ownership 0.0% for TSM but the Taiwan National Development Fund holds ~6%. A manual adjustment field or "government-linked" flag would help.
