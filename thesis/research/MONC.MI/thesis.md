## Business Understanding

**Moncler brand (~80% of group):** Premium down jackets. Average price EUR 1,000+. Heritage since 1952 (Monestier-de-Clermont, French Alps). DTC growing from 75% to 80%+ of revenue. 270+ stores globally. "Genius" creative program (rotating guest designers) keeps brand fresh without diluting identity.

**Stone Island (~20% of group):** Streetwear/casual luxury. Acquired 2020 for EUR 1.15B. Different customer (younger, urban). EUR 500M+ revenue. Growth engine. Integration ongoing.

**China exposure:** ~30% of group revenue from Greater China region. This is the CRITICAL risk — Chinese luxury spending contracted in 2024-25 as property crisis + youth unemployment + government austerity reduced aspirational spending.

## HIGH Finding #1: Revenue Deceleration Is STRUCTURAL, Not Cyclical

Revenue growth: 27.2% → 14.7% → 4.2%. Each year is LOWER. This isn't a dip — it's a trend.
- Moncler brand is maturing. 270 stores = near saturation in key markets
- China luxury spending won't return to 2021 levels (structural property/demographic shift)
- Stone Island growth masking Moncler brand stagnation
- Management guided "mid-single-digit" for 2025 = confirming deceleration

**Impact on FV:** If sustainable growth is 5% (not 8%), FV drops EUR 66→58 at same multiples. E[CAGR] drops from 25.8% to 22.0%.

## HIGH Finding #2: China 30% Concentration = Geopolitical Binary

- US-China tensions escalating (Section 301 probes, tariff war)
- Chinese consumer boycott of Western luxury brands is precedent (H&M, Nike 2021)
- If China revenue drops 30% (half the customer base doesn't buy): EBIT drops 15-20%
- Bear case: EUR 30-35 (P/E 13-15x on reduced earnings)

**Impact on FV:** Adds EUR 5+ downside to bear case.

## HIGH Finding #3: Oil $100+ Recession Kills Luxury FIRST

- Luxury is the MOST discretionary spending category
- In 2008 GFC, luxury stocks dropped 60-70% (vs S&P -57%)
- In recession: first the poor stop buying, then the middle class, then the aspirational luxury buyer — Moncler's core customer
- Oil at $100+ with FOMC hawkish = recession within 12 months is 30%+ probability

**Impact on FV:** In recession, P/E compresses from 22x to 14-16x. FV drops to EUR 33-38.

## MEDIUM Finding #4: Revenue Growth 8% Assumption Is Generous

DA growth estimate: 5-6% (Moncler brand 3-4% + Stone Island 8-10% blended, minus FX/China headwinds). R1 used 8%. Correction: **use 6%**.

## MEDIUM Finding #5: Stone Island Integration Execution Risk

Acquired 2020 for EUR 1.15B. Growing but OP margin lower than Moncler brand. If Stone Island stalls → group growth drops to 3-4% (Moncler brand only).
