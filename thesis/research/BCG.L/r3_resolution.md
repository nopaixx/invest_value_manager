## Resolution of FA vs DA Disagreements

### 1. QS +12 → DA says +9 → I resolve at +9 (QS 80 Tier A)
DA concedes market position +8 (undisputed monopoly in all 3 Baltic states). DA reduces ROIC persistence to +0 (only 4 years data) and GM trend to +1. I accept the DA's reasoning — 4 years is genuinely short for persistence scoring, and margin compression guidance undermines "stable" trend. **QS 80 Tier A** (barely, but still Tier A).

### 2. Growth 12% → DA says 8-10% → I resolve at 10%
The Estonian auto tax is permanent (not one-time) but auto transactions will partially recover. ARPU growth is real but has ceiling in 7M population. Growth deceleration pattern (18→19→15→7%) predates the tax.

I split: 10% blended (H1 FY2026 7% is trough, recovery to 10-12% realistic but not 15%). Use 10% as base, 7% as bear.

### 3. FV 260 → DA says 205 → I resolve at 215
DA's FV of 205 uses trailing EV/EBIT only. R1's 260 uses forward FCF at 18-20x (too aggressive for a decelerating grower). My resolution:

At 180p: MoS = 19.4% vs weighted FV 215p. Above bear floor 187p by only 3.7% — thin but acceptable for a monopoly with QS 80.
