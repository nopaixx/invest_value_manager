## Business Understanding

### Model de Negocio

Baltic Classifieds Group operates the leading portfolio of 14 online classified portals across Lithuania (71% of revenue), Estonia, and Latvia. Four verticals:

| Vertical | FY2025 Revenue | Growth | Key Brands |
|----------|---------------|--------|------------|
| Automotive | EUR 31.4M (38%) | +14% | Autoplius.lt, Auto24.ee, ss.lv |
| Real Estate | EUR 22.2M (27%) | +23% | Aruodas.lt, KV.ee, ss.lv |
| Jobs & Services | EUR 16.0M (19%) | +15% | CVbankas.lt, CV.ee, GetAPro |
| Generalist | EUR 13.2M (16%) | +5% | Skelbiu.lt, Osta.ee, ss.lv |
| **Total** | **EUR 82.8M** | **+15%** | |

**Revenue model:** B2C subscriptions (52% of revenue, +13% H1 FY2026) from dealers, agents, and employers + C2C listings (39%, +3%) from private sellers + advertising (9%). ~90% of revenue is B2C+C2C = core classified income.

**Unit economics:** Asset-light platform model. Near-zero marginal cost per listing. 57 million monthly visits = ~9 visits per Baltic resident per month. ARPU expansion is the primary growth lever (real estate ARPU +25% in H1 FY2026). Classic "toll booth" economics — everyone who wants to buy/sell property, cars, or find jobs in the Baltics MUST use these platforms.

### Capital Intensity

Virtually zero capex (capex/depreciation rounds to 0.0x across all years). This is a pure software platform. SBC/Revenue declining: 3.2% -> 2.3%. Cash conversion 99%. The business generates enormous free cash flow relative to its size.

### Why It's Cheap (-53% from 52wH)

**Narrative the market believes:**

1. **Growth deceleration:** H1 FY2026 revenue grew only 7% vs 15% in FY2025 and 17-18% historically. The market extrapolates deceleration.
2. **Estonian auto tax:** Estonia introduced a vehicle tax that halved car transactions, directly impacting Auto24.ee. Auto segment was flat in H1 FY2026.
3. **Small cap, small market:** The Baltic states (Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia) are tiny economies (~7M people combined). Total TAM is perceived as limited.
4. **September 2025 guidance cut:** Management warned FY2026 growth would be "3-4% below previous guidance" due to Estonian auto headwinds. JP Morgan downgraded to Underweight Nov 25.
5. **IPO overshoot:** Stock listed at ~200p in 2021, ran to 380p on growth momentum, now reverting.

**My contra-thesis:**

1. **Growth deceleration is temporary, not structural.** The Estonian auto tax is a one-off regulatory shock (comparable to UK stamp duty changes affecting property portals). Management explicitly guides H2 FY2026 revenue growth "higher than 7%" and FY2027 growth "accelerating into double digits." Real estate (+20% H1 FY2026) is offsetting auto weakness. The company is early in its ARPU monetization journey — real estate ARPU grew 25%, still well below Western European classifieds platforms.
2. **Estonian auto tax impact is priced in as permanent but is transient.** Car transactions don't disappear — they repriced. Used car platforms actually benefit from consumers seeking value in pre-owned vehicles. The auto segment maintained revenue flat despite transactions halving = pricing power absorbed the volume decline.
3. **Small market = moat, not limitation.** With ~7M people and BCG controlling ~50% of the online classifieds market with 6-36x leads over competitors, no rational competitor will enter. This is the exact dynamic of AUTO.L in UK autos or RMV.L in UK property — but with even MORE dominance and LESS competitive threat. The TAM is small but BCG captures an increasing share at increasing prices.
4. **The guidance cut was already in the stock.** Stock dropped 20% on Dec 4, 2025 after H1 results. Since then, it's continued lower on momentum/sentiment. The fundamental business is not impaired.

### Moat Analysis

**Type: Multi-layered network effects + winner-take-all dynamics**

1. **Network effects (STRONG):** More listings attract more buyers, which attracts more listings. With 57M monthly visits (9 per Baltic resident per month), the network is deeply entrenched. Competitors cannot bootstrap this.
2. **Winner-take-all (STRONG):** In each vertical/country combination, BCG's brand is 6-36x larger than the nearest competitor. In online classifieds, #1 typically captures 60-80% of the value — BCG is #1 in ALL 12+ vertical/country combinations.
3. **Switching costs (MODERATE):** Dealers/agents have workflow integrations, pricing packages, and performance data on BCG platforms. Switching to a nonexistent competitor has high friction.
4. **Data moat (MODERATE):** BCG owns the listing inventory data. Untu.lt acquisition adds property valuation data. AI cannot generate listings — it can only redirect users who find listings elsewhere (and there IS no elsewhere).
5. **Regulatory moat (EMERGING):** Small market size means regulators don't investigate dominant platforms here (unlike RMV.L's GBP 1B class action in UK). The markets are too small for antitrust action.

**Comparable moat quality to AUTO.L and RMV.L, but with STRONGER competitive position (larger leads) and LOWER competitive threat (no CoStar equivalent entering the Baltics).**

### Anomalias Detectadas
- **ROIC spread only +2.3pp (tool) despite 78% EBITDA margin and 69% FCF margin.** This is because the tool's ROIC calculation divides by invested capital which includes the large goodwill from Apax acquisitions. If we exclude goodwill (which represents the acquisition premium, not the operating capital needed to run the business), ROIC on tangible capital would be dramatically higher — likely >100%. The 13.3% ROIC is therefore an UNDERSTATEMENT of true operating returns.
