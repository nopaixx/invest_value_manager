## Executive Summary

The R1 thesis presents a compelling quality-at-discount narrative for Evolution AB. However, my independent research reveals **three areas of material concern** the thesis underweights: (1) the Black Cube / Bloomberg allegations of games in sanctioned markets (Iran, Sudan, China) represent a legal risk category far beyond a UKGC fine -- this is potential US sanctions exposure, (2) the "insider ownership 39.6%" headline is misleading because Kenneth Dart (25.4%) is the largest shareholder and he is a financial investor, not an operator-founder, and (3) operating margin decline is not "stabilizing" -- it is structurally accelerating as the company trades gray-market revenue (high margin, zero compliance cost) for regulated-market revenue (lower margin, high compliance cost). The thesis survives scrutiny at a lower FV, but the regulatory tail risk is wider and fatter than acknowledged.

## Key Assumptions Challenged

### 1. "Revenue stall is one-time ring-fencing, not structural shrinkage"

**Evidence in contra:**
- The FA attributes 0.2% FY2025 growth to European ring-fencing and Asian cybercrime. But Evolution itself acknowledged that exiting gray/black markets is PERMANENT, not transient. Q1 2025 profit dropped 5.4% specifically from "black market closures."
- Only 47% of end-player activity is in regulated markets (Q4 2025). If the other 53% is permanently inaccessible due to ring-fencing, the addressable market has ALREADY shrunk by roughly half. New regulated markets opening does NOT compensate at the same pace -- regulation takes years; ring-fencing happens in months.
- 8 consecutive quarters of flat live casino revenue (EUR 430-460M/quarter) is not a "blip." It is the new run-rate after stripping out gray market revenue.
- Pragmatic Play holds 15% European market share vs Evolution's 9.5% (per CasinoRank 2025 data). In LATAM, Pragmatic leads at 19.3% vs Evolution trailing. The FA's "45% global share" may overstate Evolution's position in the markets that MATTER going forward (regulated ones).

**Severity:** **HIGH**

The thesis depends on revenue growth resuming at 5-7%. If the addressable market has permanently shrunk and Evolution's market share in regulated markets is lower than the global headline, base-case growth of 6% may be closer to 2-3%. This directly impacts FV by SEK 100-150.

**Resolution suggested:** Committee should demand clarity from Q1 2026 results (April 22) on: (a) what is live casino revenue in REGULATED markets only, (b) what is the YoY growth rate in regulated markets only. If regulated-market growth is <5%, the 6% blended assumption is fantasy.

---

### 2. "Operating margin stabilizing at 58-60%"

**Evidence in contra:**
- Trajectory: 63.5% (2023) -> 61.5% (2024) -> 58.3% (2025). This is accelerating decline, not stabilization.
- Profit per table fell from EUR 1.08M (2024) to EUR 877K (2025) -- a 19% decline. This is deteriorating unit economics, not investment for growth.
- The structural driver is the TRANSITION from gray to regulated: gray-market revenue has near-zero compliance cost (no local studio, no regulatory overhead). Regulated-market revenue requires local studios, compliance teams, local licenses. Every EUR of revenue that shifts from gray to regulated carries higher cost.
- Headcount rose to 22,400+ employees on flat revenue -- operating leverage is now NEGATIVE.
- Management guides 66% EBITDA margin for 2026. EBITDA margin != operating margin. If D&A continues rising (studio expansion), operating margin could fall to 56-57% even if EBITDA holds at 66%.

**Severity:** **HIGH**

The FA uses 58-60% operating margin as the floor. The structural transition to regulated markets suggests the floor could be 54-56%. On EUR 2.1B revenue, each 2pp of margin compression = EUR 42M less operating profit = approximately EUR 0.20/share EPS impact.

**Resolution suggested:** Model a sensitivity where operating margin reaches 55% by 2027 before recovering. This is the more honest "bear" case.

---

### 3. "UKGC investigation likely results in fine + conditions"

**Evidence in contra:**
- The FA treats this as a UK-only issue (3% of revenue). But the Black Cube / Bloomberg allegations (August 2025) escalate this dramatically. Current and former executives were secretly recorded describing how Evolution's games reached Iran, Sudan, and China -- countries under US sanctions.
- Black Cube agents played and withdrew winnings from Evolution games IN IRAN as recently as May 2025.
- This is not just a UKGC issue. If US regulators (DOJ, OFAC) determine Evolution facilitated gambling in sanctioned markets, the consequences could include: (a) exclusion from US regulated markets (where Evolution has ~90% share), (b) criminal prosecution of executives under US sanctions law, (c) Galaxy Gaming acquisition blocked permanently (Nevada/Louisiana regulators are watching).
- Evolution filed a DEFAMATION lawsuit against the law firm, not a factual rebuttal. The company said Black Cube's report was "false and defamatory" but the recordings are video evidence, not allegations.
- The Galaxy Gaming merger has been delayed 18+ months and is still pending Nevada/Louisiana approval. If these regulators become aware of the sanctions allegations, approval becomes highly uncertain.

**Severity:** **CRITICAL**

This is the single most important risk the thesis underweights. The FA's KC#7 (criminal prosecution) already covers this, but the probability assessment is too low. The Black Cube recordings create a realistic path to KC#7 triggering. US sanctions violations carry criminal penalties, massive fines, and market access revocation.

**Resolution suggested:** This risk should be front-and-center in committee discussion. Wait for: (a) Q1 2026 results management commentary on Black Cube allegations, (b) any DOJ/OFAC inquiry announcements, (c) Galaxy Gaming regulatory outcome. If Galaxy Gaming is blocked, that is a strong signal of US regulatory concern.

### 4. "39.6% insider ownership = owner-operator alignment"

**Evidence in contra:**
- The thesis states "39.6% insider ownership" and describes it as founder-aligned. But the actual breakdown is:
  - **Kenneth Dart:** 25.4% (largest shareholder). Dart is a billionaire financial investor (Dart Container family), NOT a gambling industry operator. He took his initial 5% position in August 2024 and rapidly accumulated through swap agreements. His incentives are FINANCIAL (price appreciation, dividends), not operational alignment.
  - **Osterbahr Ventures (von Bahr + Osterberg):** 10.6%. These ARE the founders and genuine owner-operators.
  - **Other insiders:** ~3.6% (CEO Carlesund + board members).
- The "39.6% insider ownership" headline is misleading because Dart's 25.4% is classified as "insider" on some platforms due to his >20% threshold, but he is NOT an insider in the corporate governance sense.
- Dart's aggressive accumulation (5% to 25% in ~16 months) COULD be bullish (smart money sees value) or could signal activist intent (push for breakup, management change, or privatization). Unknown at this stage.
- True founder ownership is ~10.6%, not ~39.6%. This is still respectable but not extraordinary.

**Severity:** **MODERATE**

The thesis overstates alignment. True insider ownership is 10.6% (founders) + ~3.6% (management) = ~14.2%. Kenneth Dart's 25.4% is smart-money validation but not operational alignment. The thesis should separate these categories.

**Resolution suggested:** Reclassify: "Founder ownership 10.6%, Management ownership ~3.6%, Financial investor (Dart) 25.4%." Kenneth Dart's accumulation is actually a modest bullish signal but does not provide the governance comfort the thesis implies.

---

### 5. "US iGaming only 8/50 states = massive runway"

**Evidence in contra:**
- As of April 2026, ZERO new states have legalized iGaming since Maine (which went live). The 8-state count is unchanged.
- Illinois has SB1963 introduced but not passed. Massachusetts HB 4431 was "tabled for study" in 2026 -- effectively dead for this session. New York has Addabbo's bill but faces strong opposition from land-based casino interests.
- The US legalization story has STALLED since 2023-2024. Sports betting expanded rapidly, but iGaming faces much stronger opposition from land-based casinos who see it as direct cannibalization.
- Even if Illinois passes (60% probability per FA), one new state adds incremental revenue but does not transform the growth trajectory. Illinois online casino market estimated at USD 200-400M in year 1 -- Evolution's 90% share = EUR 180-360M GGR, of which Evolution earns ~10% commission = EUR 18-36M revenue. That is 1-2% of total revenue.
- The FA's "15-20% Americas growth" assumption requires MULTIPLE new states. If only Illinois opens in 2026-2027, Americas growth is more like 8-12%.

**Severity:** **MODERATE**

The US runway is real but the PACE of legalization is slower than the thesis implies. The FA should model a scenario where only 1 new state opens by 2028 (conservative) and check if the thesis still works.

**Resolution suggested:** Reduce Americas growth assumption to 10-15% (from 15-20%). This reduces blended growth from 6% to 4-5%.
