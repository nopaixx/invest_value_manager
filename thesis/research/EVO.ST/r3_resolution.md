## 2. Resolution — Point by Point

### 2.1 Black Cube / Sanctions: DA wins — CRITICAL, R1 omission

This is the DA's most important finding and the R1's most serious gap. Bloomberg reported in Aug 2025 that:
- Current and former Evolution execs were secretly recorded describing games reaching Iran, Sudan, China
- Black Cube agents played AND WITHDREW WINNINGS from Evolution games in Iran (May 2025)
- These are US-sanctioned markets — DOJ/OFAC jurisdiction applies

Evolution's response: defamation lawsuit against the law firm. NOT a factual rebuttal.

**Why this matters enormously:**
- US sanctions violations carry criminal penalties (IEEPA: up to $1M per violation + 20 years imprisonment)
- Evolution has ~90% share in US regulated iGaming — ANY DOJ action could revoke US market access
- Galaxy Gaming acquisition stalled 18+ months, likely because Nevada/Louisiana regulators are aware
- This is NOT a "fine risk" — it's a market-access risk in Evolution's most important growth market

**Resolution:** This is a HARD GATE. No standing order until ONE of:
(a) DOJ/OFAC confirms NO investigation (unlikely to publicly state)
(b) Galaxy Gaming approved by Nevada (signals regulatory comfort)
(c) 12+ months pass from Bloomberg report (Aug 2025) without action = fading risk
(d) Q1 2026 management explicitly addresses sanctions compliance with verifiable evidence

Timeline for (c): Aug 2026. For (b): unknown. For (d): Apr 22, 2026 Q1 call.

### 2.2 Revenue Growth: DA mostly wins → 3-4%

The R1 attributed 0.2% FY2025 to "one-time ring-fencing." The DA showed:
- 8 consecutive quarters flat in live casino (EUR 430-460M/quarter) = new run-rate
- 53% of player activity in unregulated markets — permanently being ring-fenced out
- Pragmatic Play LEADS Evolution in regulated EU (15% vs 9.5%), LATAM (19.3%), Canada (18.6%)

**However:** The R1 correctly identifies Americas as the growth engine. US iGaming at 90% share with 8/50 states is real. Even at slower pace (1 new state per year), Americas growth of 10-15% drives blended 3-5%.

**Resolution:** Blended growth 3-4% (not 6%). Americas 10-15%, Europe flat-to-declining, Asia shrinking from ring-fencing. The R1's 6% required both Europe recovery AND rapid US legalization — neither is confirmed.

### 2.3 Operating Margin: DA mostly wins → 56% floor, not 58%

The trajectory (63.5%→61.5%→58.3%) is structural, driven by gray→regulated transition. Each EUR of regulated revenue costs more (local studios, compliance, licenses). Profit per table -19% YoY confirms deteriorating unit economics.

**However:** Management guides 66% EBITDA margin for 2026. If they achieve this, OM stabilizes ~57-58% after D&A. The margin decline DECELERATES as the gray→regulated transition completes (ring-fencing is finite).

**Resolution:** OM 56-58% base case (split R1's 58% and DA's 55%). The transition has ~2 more years to run before a new equilibrium.

### 2.4 Insider Ownership: DA wins — reclassify

Kenneth Dart (25.4%) is a financial investor, not a founder/operator. Real founder ownership: Osterbahr Ventures 10.6% + management ~3.6% = 14.2%.

**Resolution:** Reclassify. QS adjustment: insider ownership should reflect 14.2% (still 5/5 per tool at >10%), but the governance narrative changes from "extraordinary owner-operator alignment" to "solid founder alignment + smart money validation."

At SEK 620, the stock offers 4.6% MoS vs R3 FV — insufficient for Tier B with CRITICAL tail risk. At SEK 490, the sanctions risk is partially priced and the quality provides genuine margin of safety.

**The investment question is simple: do you believe DOJ/OFAC will act on the Black Cube evidence?**
- If YES (>30% probability) → Do not buy at any price. KC#7/#8 will trigger.
- If NO (<30% probability) → SEK 490 is a gift for this quality. 13.5% E[CAGR] + sanctions clearing = 20%+ upside.

I assess DOJ/OFAC probability at 15-25%. This is a LOW but non-trivial probability of an existential outcome. The HARD GATE (Galaxy Gaming approval or 12-month aging) provides a natural resolution mechanism. If Galaxy Gaming is approved by Nevada, it signals regulatory comfort. If blocked, it signals the opposite.

## META-REFLECTION

### Incertidumbres
- The Black Cube evidence (video recordings of execs, play-from-Iran) is the most asymmetric risk in our entire pipeline. If it leads nowhere, the stock re-rates 30-50%. If it leads to DOJ action, the stock drops 40-60%.
- Kenneth Dart's 25.4% stake IS smart money. His accumulation from 5%→25.4% in 16 months at higher prices is bullish — he knows the sanctions risk and is buying anyway. This is the strongest counter-argument to the bear case.
- Pragmatic Play's market share data (CasinoRank) may not be directly comparable to Evolution's — different measurement methodologies. The "9.5% EU share" for Evolution may understate its live casino dominance while including RNG where it's weaker.

### Sugerencias
- EVO.ST should be tracked on a separate "binary/regulatory" watchlist alongside EXEL and LNTH. These are not compounders — they are quality businesses with identifiable binary risks.
- The Galaxy Gaming regulatory outcome is the best leading indicator for sanctions risk. Monitor Nevada Gaming Control Board decisions monthly.

### Anomalias
- A business with 30% ROIC, 54% FCF margin, and 39% total ownership concentration at P/E 10.8x is EXTREMELY unusual. Either the market is deeply wrong (buying opportunity) or there is a hidden risk the market knows (sanctions). The DA found the hidden risk. The question is probability.
