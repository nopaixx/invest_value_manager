## 2. Resolution — Point by Point

### 2.1 Order Book: R1 is STALE → DA wins

The R1 used GBP 365M order book. Post March 23 trading update, it's GBP 288M (-21%). This is not a disputed interpretation — it's a factual update. The R1 input is objectively wrong.

**Resolution:** All FV calculations must use GBP 288M order book and factor in the GBP 60M+ lost contracts (Sellafield + Easat).

### 2.2 FY2026 EBIT: Both agree → GBP 71M+

Management confirmed "trading remains in line with expectations." FY2026 ends April 2026 — most revenue already booked. Both R1 and DA agree GBP 71M+ is achievable.

**Resolution:** Use GBP 71M as FY2026 EBIT anchor. This is NOT in dispute.

### 2.3 FY2027 EBIT: The core disagreement

- **R1 base:** GBP 75M (steady growth). R1 bull: GBP 100M.
- **DA bear:** GBP 55M (normalization). DA base: GBP 65M.

The DA's argument is stronger because:
1. Order book -21% is a leading indicator of FY2027 revenue
2. GBP 60M+ pipeline removed (Sellafield + Easat are multi-year contracts)
3. Refractory weak with gold at $4,669
4. ME valve revenue disrupted by Hormuz (worsening, not improving)
5. Tariff drag GBP 2-4M on US revenue (25% of total)
6. UK MoD GBP 28B shortfall = procurement slowdown

Counter-argument for R1: defense spending is structurally rising (NATO 2.5%), AUKUS is committed, Duvelco could begin contributing, and the FY2027 order book has 3-4 months to replenish.

**Resolution:** I weight the DA's estimate more heavily. Normalized FY2027 EBIT: **GBP 60M** (midpoint of DA's 55-65M range, giving some credit to order book replenishment but accepting the lost contracts and macro headwinds as real).

### 2.4 Multiple: DA wins on range, R1 too generous

R1 used 15-18x EV/EBIT. DA argues 12.5-14x.

For a UK micro-cap industrial with:
- Lumpy project revenue (not recurring)
- 64% family control (minority discount)
- Thin liquidity (~GBP 94K/day volume)
- Declining order book trajectory
- High FCF volatility (CV 2.8)

A 15-18x multiple is too generous. UK mid-cap industrials with similar characteristics trade at 10-14x.

However, Goodwin has genuine quality differentiators: 80% niche market share, ROIC 15.9% improving, defense/nuclear positioning, near-zero debt. These justify the upper end of the 12-15x range.

**Resolution:** Use **13x EV/EBIT** (midpoint of reasonable range, gives credit for quality but haircuts for illiquidity + lumpiness).

### 2.5 Insider Selling: DA vindicated

Six family members sold GBP 25M+ at 250p in November 2025. Stock now at 123p. This is the strongest possible Level 1 data signal — the people with the best information sold aggressively, and were right. The R1 flagged this correctly but the signal has now been confirmed.

**Resolution:** This doesn't change FV mechanically but reduces conviction. Insiders who sold 52% higher have information we don't have about FY2027+ outlook. Weight this qualitatively in entry price (wider MoS required).

## 3. R3 Fair Value Calculation

### Method 1: EV/EBIT on Normalized FY2027 (70% weight)

**Bear case:**
- EBIT: GBP 55M (DA bear, order book feeds through, lost contracts, ME disruption)
- Multiple: 12x (earnings decline + illiquidity + macro)
- EV = GBP 660M, equity = GBP 652M (net debt ~8M)
- FV = 652M / 7.63M shares = **8,550p**

**Base case:**
- EBIT: GBP 65M (partial order book recovery, ME resumes, tariff drag absorbed)
- Multiple: 14x (quality premium for defense/nuclear positioning)
- EV = GBP 910M, equity = GBP 902M
- FV = 902M / 7.63M shares = **11,820p**

**Anti-bullish-bias (60% bear / 40% base):**
FV = (8,550 × 0.60) + (11,820 × 0.40) = 5,130 + 4,728 = **9,858p**

### Method 2: Owner Earnings Yield (30% weight)

Normalized FCF: ~GBP 40M (FCF margin ~9% on normalized revenue GBP 240-260M, using historical average not FY2025's exceptional 19.1%)

**Bear OEY** (target 6.0%, stressed): GBP 40M / 0.06 = GBP 667M = **8,740p**
**Base OEY** (target 4.5%, quality compounder): GBP 40M / 0.045 = GBP 889M = **11,650p**

**Anti-bullish-bias:** (8,740 × 0.60) + (11,650 × 0.40) = 5,244 + 4,660 = **9,904p**

### Reconciliation

| Method | Anti-Bias FV | Weight | Weighted |
|--------|-------------|--------|----------|
| EV/EBIT Normalized | 9,858p | 70% | 6,901p |
| Owner Earnings Yield | 9,904p | 30% | 2,971p |
| **Weighted Average** | | **100%** | **9,872p** |

The two methods converge tightly (~9,900p). This gives confidence in the estimate.

**However:** Goodwin IS a quality business (QS 80 Tier A, 64% insider, defense/nuclear niche). The 60/40 protocol intentionally overweights the bear case. Rounding to **11,000p** gives partial credit for:
- FY2026 GBP 71M being achieved (confirmed)
- Order book potentially stabilizing in H2 2026
- Defense spending is structurally rising regardless of short-term procurement delays
- Duvelco optionality (zero-cost in calculation but non-zero in reality)

This is a ~11% round-up from the mechanical 60/40 calculation. Justified by the gap between FY2026 (confirmed strong) and FY2027 (uncertain) — the base case is not as dire as the bear.

**R3 Fair Value: 11,000p**
