**Business:** Fortinet is the global leader in network security appliances (FortiGate firewall, #1 market share 35%+). Revenue model: ~67% services (recurring subscriptions, FortiGuard security updates, support) + ~33% product (appliance sales). Transitioning to Unified SASE (cloud security) and Security Operations (SecOps), growing at 24-40% -- these are the next-gen growth vectors. FCF machine: 32% FCF margin, net cash position, $2.2B FCF in FY2025.

**Why cheap:** Stock dropped from $111 to $75 (-32%) driven by: (1) SaaSpocalypse narrative dragging all software names -- FTNT is cybersecurity infrastructure, NOT per-seat SaaS, making the selloff misapplied; (2) Earlier billings growth deceleration fears (now resolved: Q4 billings +18% YoY, above high-end guidance); (3) Multiple compression from ~45x to ~25x P/E despite accelerating fundamentals. Q4 FY2025 results were strong across the board (revenue +15%, EPS beat by 14%, record margins).

**Bull:** Cybersecurity is structurally non-discretionary spend. SASE/SecOps growing 25-40%. Firewall refresh cycle accelerating. AI-driven security demand creates new TAM. $10B+ buyback authorization. Fundsmith holding = quality signal.

**Bear:** Multiple has de-rated for a reason -- growth decelerating from 20%+ to 12-15% range. Product revenue lumpy (hardware cycle dependent). Competition from Palo Alto Networks and CrowdStrike intensifying in SASE. Founder-CEO Ken Xie owns 17% but governance is less scrutinized.

**KCs:**
1. Revenue growth decelerates below 10% for 2 consecutive quarters (indicates loss of competitive position)
2. Unified SASE ARR growth drops below 15% (indicates failure of cloud transition)
3. FCF margin compresses below 25% sustainably (indicates pricing pressure or cost escalation)

**Verdict:** WATCHLIST at $75. Proceed to R1. Strong quality compounder (Tier A adjusted) at reasonable valuation. Entry at current $75 offers ~19% MoS and ~16% E[CAGR], which meets the Tier A deployment criteria. The SaaSpocalypse selloff has created a mispricing -- FTNT is cybersecurity infrastructure, not the commodity SaaS being disrupted by AI. Near 52wL with just-reported record Q4 results is an attractive setup.
