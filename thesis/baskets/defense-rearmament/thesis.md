## Thematic Thesis

Multi-decade structural rearmament driven by three converging forces:

1. **NATO 5% GDP target** (Hague Summit Jun 2025) — European defense budgets to roughly
   TRIPLE over 9 years. Germany alone EUR 82.69B, targeting 3.5% GDP by 2029.
2. **Russia-Ukraine conflict persistence** — no resolution in sight, forcing sustained
   procurement of ammunition, vehicles, air defense, drones.
3. **Iran/Hormuz crisis** (Operation Epic Fury Feb-Mar 2026) — elevated global threat
   perception, accelerating defense spending commitments.

This is NOT a cycle — it's a **regime change** in global defense spending. Global defense
hit $2.63T in 2025 and will grow 5-7% CAGR for 15+ years.

**CONTRARIAN ANGLE:** Most EU defense stocks trade at 30-85x P/E (momentum, not value).
The fund's edge is finding quality at reasonable valuations. BAH at P/E 11.8x is the
widest quality-value gap in the sector. SAF.PA at P/E 18.5x is the cheapest quality
European name.

## Why This Theme Wins Over 10+ Years

- NATO 5% GDP commitment = structural demand floor with political lock-in
- Record backlogs provide 5-10 year revenue visibility (BAE GBP 83.6B, Dassault EUR 46.6B, RHM EUR 63B)
- Industrial capacity constraints create pricing power for incumbents
- Defense spending is counter-cyclical (increases during recession/geopolitical stress)
- Ammunition consumption in Ukraine creates recurring demand base

## Shared Competitive Dynamics

- EU defense is a government-relationship business — incumbents have structural advantages
- US defense primes (LMT, RTX, NOC, GD) are Tier C quality — low margins, near ATH, eliminated
- European names have higher growth (spending from low base) but higher multiples
- BAH is the exception: US govcon IT at P/E 11.8x, crushed by DOGE fears (contrarian entry)
- SAF.PA: CFM duopoly with GE for jet engines — structural moat, QS 73 Tier B, P/E 18.5x
