## Thematic Thesis

Toll-booth monopolies in data, analytics, and professional information. These companies
own irreplaceable data assets or market infrastructure that every participant MUST use.

1. **Irreplaceable data assets** — Credit ratings (SPGI duopoly), bond trading (TW network
   effects), legal/tax databases (WKL.AS switching costs), financial data (MORN indices).
2. **Secular drivers** — Global AUM growth (7-10% CAGR), regulatory complexity increasing,
   passive investing growth, professional information consumption, electronification of markets.
3. **WIDE moats** — Regulatory barriers (ratings), network effects (exchanges/trading),
   data lock-in (indices, legal/tax databases), and switching costs (embedded workflows).

## Shared Competitive Dynamics

- All three positions are WIDE-moat monopolists in their respective niches
- Revenue is highly recurring (WKL.AS 78%, TW subscription + transaction, MORN license-based)
- Pricing power is structural: customers MUST use these services
- AI is an enhancer (not disruptor) for professional information businesses

## Shared Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Global AUM contraction | Moderate | Volume decline for TW, MORN | Subscription revenue provides floor |
| Regulatory breakup of monopolies | Low | Structural disruption | Regulatory moats are self-reinforcing |
| AI disruption of data models | Low-Moderate | Business model challenge | These companies own the DATA that AI needs |
| High sector multiples (P/E 28-35x) | High | Limited upside | Market knows quality; accept lower MoS for WIDE moats |

## Basket Kill Conditions

1. Global AUM declines >15% sustained 2+ years
2. Major regulatory restructuring of credit ratings industry
3. 2+ positions hit individual KCs simultaneously

## Correlation Assessment

- **MODERATE correlation:** All benefit from financial market activity and AUM growth
- **LOW sub-sector correlation:** Bond trading (TW) vs legal/tax info (WKL.AS) vs financial data (MORN)
- **Diversification:** US (MORN, TW) + EU (WKL.AS) geographic split
