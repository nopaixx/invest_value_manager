## Thematic Thesis

UK digital platforms with dominant market positions in their verticals, commanding
>50% market share and generating ROIC well above WACC. The shared thesis is:

1. **Structural offline-to-online migration** continues in UK financial services,
   auto, and retail — these platforms capture increasing share of transactions.
2. **Network effects create natural monopolies** — AUTO.L has 75% UK auto classifieds,
   MONY.L is #2 price comparison (MoneySupermarket + MoneySavingExpert), IHP.L is #1
   independent adviser platform (Transact).
3. **Asset-light models generate exceptional FCF** — 30-50% FCF margins, enabling
   dividends + buybacks without capital needs.
4. **Temporary valuation dislocation** — all 3 trade below intrinsic value due to
   sector-specific headwinds (AI disruption fears, dealer rebellion, governance).

## Shared Competitive Dynamics

- UK regulatory environment is stable for digital platforms (FCA, CMA)
- Competitors are fragmented — no Amazon/Google-scale threat to these niches
- Switching costs are moderate (MONY.L) to high (IHP.L platform migration)
- All three benefit from UK financial literacy driving comparison shopping
