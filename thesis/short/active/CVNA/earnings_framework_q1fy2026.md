## The Single Question That Matters

**Did EBITDA margin stabilize or continue compressing?**

The thesis rests on margin deterioration: EBITDA margin fell from 12.0% (Q1 2025) to 9.1% (Q4 2025) — a 290bps decline in 3 quarters. If Q1 reverses this (seasonal recovery to 11%+), the "diseconomies of scale" thesis weakens. If compression continues (<9%), the thesis strengthens.

Management will frame any result as "deliberate investment." The market will decide whether investing is creating value or destroying it.

## PIK-to-Cash Transition — The Hidden Catalyst

CVNA's $5.6B debt includes PIK (payment-in-kind) notes that convert to cash interest mid-2026. This adds ~$500M+/year in cash costs that AREN'T in current FCF.

| Status | Impact | Timing |
|--------|--------|--------|
| On schedule (mid-2026) | +$125M/quarter cash cost starting Q3 | FCF compresses immediately |
| Delayed/restructured | Positive for CVNA — buys time | Weakens short thesis |
| Accelerated | Even worse for CVNA cash flows | Strengthens short thesis |

**Q1 earnings call commentary on PIK transition is CRITICAL.** If management acknowledges the cash hit, market may reprice. If they downplay it, the Q3 surprise will be worse.
