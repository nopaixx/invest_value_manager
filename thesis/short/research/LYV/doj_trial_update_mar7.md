## DOJ Trial Status (as of Mar 7, 2026)

### Timeline
- **Mar 2**: Trial began in NYC federal court (Judge Arun Subramanian)
- **Mar 3**: Jury selection completed
- **Mar 4**: Opening arguments — DOJ: industry is "broken"; LYV: "fierce, lawful competitor"
- **Expected duration**: 6 weeks (verdict ~mid-April 2026)

### Key Developments
1. **Case NARROWED in pretrial ruling**: Judge Subramanian cut claims that LYV operates a nationwide concert booking monopoly. This is FAVORABLE for LYV — reduces scope of potential remedy.
2. **Plaintiffs**: DOJ + 40 states + DC. Massive coalition = political pressure to pursue aggressively.
3. **Core allegation remaining**: Ticketmaster monopoly in primary ticketing + vertical integration with venues/promotion. DOJ seeks forced divestiture of Ticketmaster.
4. **LYV defense**: Market is competitive; LYV won contracts through quality, not coercion. Points to AXS, SeatGeek, DICE as competitors.
