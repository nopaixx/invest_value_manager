## Section 1: Why the Price Is Unsustainable

### 1.1 Narrative Supporting Current Price

The market narrative is:
1. **Crypto is going mainstream** -- BTC ETFs, institutional adoption, Trump pro-crypto administration
2. **Coinbase is diversifying** -- Deribit acquisition ($2.9B), stablecoin revenue, Base L2 chain, stock trading
3. **Regulatory clarity** -- SEC dropped lawsuit (Feb 2025), Crypto Task Force formed
4. **"Everything Exchange"** -- expanding beyond crypto trading into derivatives, prediction markets, stock trading

### 1.2 Why the Narrative Fails

**Revenue remains cyclically dependent on crypto prices and volumes:**

| Year | Revenue | BTC Avg Price | Delta |
|------|---------|---------------|-------|
| 2021 | $7.8B | ~$47K (peak) | Crypto mania |
| 2022 | $3.2B (-59%) | ~$28K | Crypto winter |
| 2023 | $3.1B (-3%) | ~$28K | Trough |
| 2024 | $6.6B (+113%) | ~$62K | BTC ETF rally |
| 2025 | $7.2B (+9%) | ~$85K | Late cycle |

**The pattern is unmistakable:** Revenue tracks BTC price with ~0.85+ correlation. BTC is now at ~$68K (down from $108K Nov 2024 peak), and COIN's Q4 2025 already showed a $667M net loss with revenue -31% YoY.

**Transaction revenue still dominates:** FY2025 transaction revenue was $4.1B (57% of total $7.2B). Even "subscription and services" revenue at $2.8B is not truly recurring -- it includes blockchain rewards (down -29.5% YoY in Q4), interest income (rate-dependent), and stablecoin revenue (USDC volume-dependent). Q1 2026 guidance of $550-630M sub/services is DOWN from Q4's $727M.

**Deribit is NOT a silver bullet:**
- Cost $2.9B ($700M cash + 11M shares = 4.4% dilution)
- Derivatives volumes are ALSO crypto-cycle-dependent
- Integration risk in a declining market
- Goodwill rose from 5.1% to 14.1% of total assets -- acquisition-dependent growth

### 1.3 Reverse DCF Analysis

The reverse DCF shows the market implies only 3.7% FCF growth for 5 years at 9% WACC. This seems modest, BUT:
- The tool uses trailing FCF of $2.4B (FY2025) as base
- In the 2022 crypto winter, FCF was NEGATIVE $1.6B
- If crypto cycle turns (as it is), FCF could easily revert to $0-1B
- The "3.7% implied growth" is from an INFLATED base

**True implied expectation:** The market prices sustained $2.4B+ FCF, which requires sustained crypto trading volumes near 2024-2025 peak levels. BTC at $68K and falling does NOT support this.

### 1.4 Historical Precedent

2021-2022 cycle: Revenue fell 59% ($7.8B to $3.2B). Stock fell from $342 to $35 (-90%).
The current setup is structurally similar:
- BTC peaked Nov 2024 at $108K, now $68K (-37%)
- Trading volumes declining (consumer spot -6% QoQ, institutional -13% QoQ in Q4)
- BTC ETF outflows of $9B in 4 months
- US-listed Bitcoin ETFs became NET SELLERS in 2026

```
COIN price $197 depends on:
  --> Sustained $7B+ annual revenue depends on:
    --> Crypto trading volumes near 2024-25 levels depends on:
      --> BTC price above $80K depends on:
        --> Institutional demand (ETF flows) BUT: $9B outflows in 4 months
```

**Weakest link:** BTC price and trading volume. Both are already declining. The dependency chain collapses from the bottom up.

**Additional fragilities:**
1. **SBC remains elevated:** 11.7% of revenue in FY2025 (was 49% in 2022, trending down but still high vs mature companies)
2. **Receivables growing faster than revenue:** Receivables +39.7% vs revenue +9.4% -- a classic warning signal
3. **Net loss in Q4 2025:** $667M loss despite being "peak cycle" -- what happens in a trough?
4. **ROIC < WACC:** 13.7% vs 21.8% (beta 3.71 reflects extreme volatility). The business does NOT create value above its cost of capital.

### Incertidumbres/Dudas
- The Deribit acquisition adds $2.9B in goodwill and could genuinely diversify revenue if derivatives prove more cycle-resilient than spot trading. I cannot assess this with certainty.
- The "Deferred Revenue" line in narrative_checker showed $80B (2022) to $197B (2023) -- these are likely CUSTOMER CUSTODY ASSETS mislabeled by the tool as deferred revenue. This distortion makes balance sheet analysis unreliable from automated tools.
- Trump's crypto-friendly stance is genuinely novel -- prior crypto winters didn't have US government support. This COULD create a floor that didn't exist before.

### Sugerencias para el Sistema
- The quality_scorer.py flags "FCF data distortion" for financial services -- this is important for COIN where customer crypto holdings flow through the balance sheet. The tool handled this correctly by using operating margin as proxy.
- The narrative_checker "Deferred Revenue" for COIN is almost certainly customer custody liabilities, not true deferred revenue. The tool should handle financial services/exchanges differently.
- For crypto-related stocks, we should create a `world/sectors/crypto-exchanges.md` sector view before proceeding beyond S1.

- COIN EPS CAGR shows 0/10 despite going from -$11.81 to +$4.85 -- the tool correctly penalizes because the starting point was negative (cannot calculate CAGR from negative). This is fair for the score but masks the profitability recovery.
- Beta of 3.71 produces WACC of 21.8% -- this makes virtually ANY company look like it destroys value. The WACC is defensible (crypto IS that volatile) but makes DCF completely unusable.
