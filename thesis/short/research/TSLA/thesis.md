## Business Understanding

### Model of Business

Tesla operates across four segments:

1. **Automotive (76% of revenue, declining):** Designs, manufactures, sells EVs. Models 3/Y (volume), S/X (legacy), Cybertruck (niche). Revenue $72.1B in 2025 (-5.8% YoY). Auto gross margin ~18% and compressing. Automotive credits ($2.8B in 2025) inflate profitability.
2. **Energy Generation & Storage (13.5% of revenue, growing):** Megapack utility-scale storage, Powerwall residential, solar. Revenue $12.8B in 2025 (+27% YoY). 29.8% gross margin. This is the hidden gem.
3. **Services & Other (10.5% of revenue):** Supercharging, insurance, body shop, used cars. Growing but low margin.
4. **Real Options (no revenue yet):** FSD/Robotaxi, Optimus humanoid robot. These are what the market is primarily pricing.

### Unit Economics -- Automotive

- **ASP declining:** Price cuts throughout 2023-2025 to defend volume. Failed -- volume still declined.
- **Gross margin trajectory:** 25.6% (2022) -> 18.2% (2023) -> 17.9% (2024) -> 18.0% (2025). Stabilized at much lower level.
- **Operating margin collapse:** 17.0% (2022) -> 9.2% (2023) -> 7.9% (2024) -> 5.1% (2025). Continuing to compress.
- **EPS collapse:** $4.02 (2022) -> $4.73 (2023) -> $2.23 (2024) -> $1.18 (2025). Down 75% from peak.

### Why the Price is Unsustainable (Core Short Thesis)

The market is pricing Tesla as if it were a high-growth tech monopoly. The data shows an auto manufacturer losing market share with declining margins.

**What the price requires (Reverse DCF):**

## Structural Fragility Analysis (Error #46 Compliance)

This section addresses Error #46 directly: Is there STRUCTURAL fragility, or is this just "Tesla is expensive"?

### Fragility #1: Auto Business is a Commodity with Eroding Moat

Tesla's auto moat was: (a) first-mover EV, (b) Supercharger network, (c) software/OTA, (d) brand aspirational status.

**Current erosion:**
- **First-mover:** BYD now sells 38% more BEVs globally. 100+ Chinese EV brands active. Tesla went from 20%+ global BEV share to <15%.
- **Supercharger:** NACS standard adopted by legacy OEMs means Tesla's network advantage is being shared. It becomes a revenue source (positive) but loses exclusivity (negative for moat).
- **Software/OTA:** Every OEM now has OTA updates. VW, BMW, Hyundai all have advanced infotainment/ADAS.
- **Brand:** Catastrophically damaged. Brand value down 58% from peak. Yale study estimates 1M+ lost US sales from Musk's political activities. EU registrations down 39% in 2025.
- **Gross margin -17pp vs sector median:** Tesla's 18% GM is BELOW the auto sector median of 35%. This is NOT a premium brand margin -- it's a volume manufacturer margin.

**STRUCTURAL, not cyclical:** Brand damage from DOGE/political polarization is not a one-quarter issue. As CNN reported, "some of the damage will be stained forever in Europe and the US."

### Fragility #2: China Collapse is Structural

- Tesla China NEV sales declined 4.8% YoY in 2025 while the market grew 40%+
- Tesla's China market share: 6% vs BYD's 27.2%
- Geely (12.2% share) now has 2x Tesla's China presence
- BYD targeting 1.3M overseas units in 2026 (+24%) -- directly attacking Tesla's remaining strongholds
- Chinese consumers increasingly prefer domestic brands for EVs (national pride + better value + faster innovation)

**This is irreversible.** Tesla cannot out-compete BYD on cost (BYD is vertically integrated), cannot out-innovate dozens of Chinese brands simultaneously, and is losing the brand war in its largest international market.

### Fragility #3: Dependence on Unproven Future Businesses

The entire valuation gap ($37 auto value vs $397 stock price) rests on:

**Robotaxi/FSD (~$200-500B of implied value):**
- FSD is still legally a driver-assistance system, not autonomous driving
- Robotaxis available in only 2 US cities (Austin, SF Bay) -- with company employees present
- Goldman Sachs estimates only $115M robotaxi revenue in 2027 and 300 vehicles in 2026
- Musk has promised "next year" for FSD since 2016 -- Wikipedia maintains an entire article cataloging his missed predictions
- Regulatory approvals in most jurisdictions remain years away
- Waymo is ahead in actual deployment (operational in multiple cities without drivers)
- Liability framework unresolved

**Optimus Robot (~$50-200B of implied value):**
- As of Feb 2026, Gen 3 robots are "not doing useful work" (Musk's own admission on Q4 2025 call)
- "Still very much in the R&D phase"
- Consumer sales targeted for end of 2027 (shifted multiple times)
- No demonstrated ability to perform useful tasks autonomously
- Boston Dynamics, Figure AI, and others are competitors with different approaches

**Energy Storage ($50-80B reasonable value -- but already in the stock):**
- This is the ONE genuinely strong business. $12.8B revenue, 29.8% margins, 48% deployment growth
- But $50-80B is already generous. At 4-6x revenue for a hardware business = $51-77B
- The market is pricing energy + auto + everything at $1,489B. Even with $80B energy value, the gap is enormous
