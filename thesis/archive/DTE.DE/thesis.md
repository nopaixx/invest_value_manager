## POST-MORTEM

### What Went Right
1. Entry at EUR 26.88 was well-timed (Jan 26, near 52wL area).
2. TMUS as hidden value driver thesis was correct -- TMUS CMD on Feb 11 validated.
3. QS upgrade from 48 to 61 confirmed operational improvement.
4. Multiple profitable trims locked gains progressively.
5. Total return ~22-25% in 31 days on remaining shares.

### What Could Improve
1. Original thesis overclaimed quality (10/10 custom vs QS 48 tool). Framework v3.0+ corrected this.
2. Revenue CAGR overstated (4% claimed vs 1.8-2.4% actual).
3. Position was oversized initially (11.3% of portfolio), required multiple trims.
4. E[CAGR] of 6.7% was lowest in portfolio -- should have been rotated sooner per P16.

### Lesson
DTE.DE demonstrates successful P16 rotation discipline: even profitable positions should be exited when E[CAGR] is lowest in portfolio and pipeline has higher-return alternatives. The position funded rotations into multiple Tier A compounders (ADBE, NVO, MONY.L, LULU, AUTO.L, BYIT.L, MORN, IHP.L, HLNE, DOCS).
