# Counter-Analysis: BZU.MI (Buzzi SpA)

## Fecha: 2026-03-06

## Resumen Ejecutivo

The thesis is fundamentally sound in its assessment of Buzzi as a high-quality cyclical business with exceptional family alignment and best-in-class margins. However, the R1's verdict of WATCHLIST at EUR 49.54 was correct -- and the stock has since fallen 12% to EUR 43.62, vindicating the cyclical peak concern. The through-cycle EBIT normalization of EUR 850-900M is the thesis's most critical and debatable assumption. FY2025 data shows US revenue declined 7%, margins compressing, and cement demand in the US declining for a second consecutive year. The thesis survives the counter-analysis, but with important adjustments to fair value and risk assessment.
