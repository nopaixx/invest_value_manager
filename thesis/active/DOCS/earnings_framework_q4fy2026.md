If pharma is choosing to spend on CRM/operations (Veeva) over marketing (DOCS), that's not a temporary MFN delay — it's a permanent budget reallocation. Q4 DOCS results will either confirm or deny this.

**If NRR drops below 110%, the math changes fundamentally:**
- Growth assumption drops from 8% to 5-6%

- E[CAGR] drops from 16% to 8-11%
- At <12% E[CAGR], DOCS fails the Tier A threshold

**The position is currently 3% underwater ($23.69 vs $24.41 cost).** In a FAIL scenario (FV $20-22), the loss would be EUR 80-130 on a EUR 820 position = -10 to -16%. That's ~1.3-1.5% of portfolio. Painful but not catastrophic.
