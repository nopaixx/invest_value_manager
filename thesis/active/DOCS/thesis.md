## Business Understanding

### What Doximity Does

Doximity operates the largest physician social network in the US, connecting 85%+ of US physicians (>2M healthcare practitioners, >3M registered members). The platform is the "LinkedIn for doctors" but with critical differentiation:

1. **Clinical workflow tools**: HIPAA-compliant messaging, e-faxing, telehealth, electronic signatures
2. **Physician news feed**: Curated medical articles, CME credits, drug reference databases
3. **AI tools**: DocsGPT (AI assistant for physicians), Scribe (clinical note generation), AI drug lookup
4. **Residency navigator**: Used by 97% of medical students for residency matching

**Revenue Model**: The physician network is FREE for doctors. Revenue comes from:
- **Pharma marketing modules (~90% of revenue)**: Pharmaceutical companies pay to reach physicians through targeted digital ads, sponsored content, peer influence modules, rep-triggered messaging
- **Hiring solutions (~5%)**: Medical groups pay to find clinicians through the platform
- **Telehealth/other (~5%)**: Health systems pay for workflow tools

**Key insight**: Doximity is an ADVERTISING business monetizing a professional network -- structurally similar to LinkedIn/Meta, not to SaaS. This means: (a) extremely high margins (90% gross margin), (b) network effects are the moat, (c) revenue depends on pharma ad budgets, not per-seat SaaS pricing.

### Unit Economics

- **LTV/CAC**: Exceptional. Physician acquisition is essentially FREE (organic adoption through residency, peer referral, workflow utility). Customer acquisition cost is primarily sales team for pharma clients.
- **Net Revenue Retention**: 112% TTM (117% for top 20 clients). Strong but decelerating from 118% in Q2.
- **Large customers ($500K+)**: 126 (up 10% YoY), represent 84% of revenue -- HIGH concentration in large pharma accounts.
- **Revenue per large customer**: ~$4.3M TTM ($642M * 0.84 / 126). Growing with upsell.

## Moat Assessment

### Network Effects (PRIMARY MOAT) -- WIDE

Doximity has the most powerful network effect in healthcare professional networking:

- **85%+ of US physicians** on the platform (>2M healthcare practitioners)
- **97% of medical students** use Doximity for residency matching (pipeline lock-in)
- **Winner-take-all dynamics**: Physicians go where OTHER physicians are. No one joins the #2 physician network
- **Pharma advertisers go where physicians are**: If 85% of target audience is on Doximity, pharma MUST advertise there
- **Self-reinforcing**: More physicians -> more advertiser revenue -> more investment in tools -> more physician engagement -> more physicians

**Competitive landscape**:
- **Sermo**: Small competitor (~800K physicians claimed), privately held, focused on surveys not workflow
- **LinkedIn**: Generic professional network; lacks HIPAA compliance, drug databases, CME credits, residency matching
- **Epocrates (Athenahealth)**: Drug reference tool only, not a network
- **UpToDate (Wolters Kluwer)**: Clinical reference, not a social/advertising platform
- **Veeva (VEEV)**: CRM for pharma sales reps; complementary to Doximity, not competitive

**Moat width**: WIDE. No competitor has >10% of physician market. The cost/effort to replicate 85% physician penetration is effectively infinite -- you would need every hospital, medical school, and residency program to adopt your platform. Doximity built this over 13 years.

### Switching Costs (SECONDARY MOAT) -- MODERATE

- Physicians use Doximity for daily workflow (messaging, e-fax, telehealth, credentialing)
- Institutional integrations (hospitals, residency programs) create organizational lock-in
- BUT: individual physicians could theoretically use multiple platforms
- More accurately: switching costs are LOW for individual physicians but HIGH for the pharma advertising ecosystem

### Data Moat (TERTIARY) -- DEVELOPING

- Doximity has the largest dataset of physician engagement, prescribing patterns (via newsfeed interaction), and professional connections
- DocsGPT trained on this data + medical journals + drug databases
- If AI tools become standard clinical workflow, the data moat deepens significantly

### Overall Moat Rating: WIDE (18/25 estimated)

The network effect at 85%+ penetration is a near-insurmountable moat. The key risk is not competition from another network, but rather from pharma companies finding alternative channels to reach physicians (e.g., direct sales, other digital channels). However, the digital shift in pharma advertising is a TAILWIND, and Doximity's targeting capabilities are superior to generic digital advertising.
