## 2. NEW MATERIAL THREAT: AMAZON AUTOS UK LAUNCH (2026)

**This was NOT in the original thesis and represents a potential KC-level development.**

Amazon Autos is targeting UK launch by end of 2026. Director Steve Johnson stated "it's not if, but when."

**Key facts:**
- UK is first of five European markets
- US model: FREE for dealers (no subscription, no revenue share on sales)
- Current US partners: Hyundai, Mazda, Subaru, Ford, Kia (new). Used cars in 130 US cities
- Features: finance quotes, warranties, F&I, inventory management
- Similar to AutoTrader's Deal Builder product but built on Amazon's traffic moat
- Suppliers being lined up. Timeline: by end 2026

**Why this matters:**
- Amazon offers dealers FREE listings vs AutoTrader GBP 2,994/month (GBP 35,928/year)
- Amazon's consumer traffic dwarfs AutoTrader (Amazon UK has ~400M+ monthly visits vs AutoTrader ~80-85M)
- Launch timing coincides with peak dealer discontent over pricing/Deal Builder
- If Amazon captures even 5-10% of dealer listings, it breaks the "must-have" monopoly argument
- HOWEVER: Amazon Autos US launch has been modest. Car buying is high-consideration, not impulse. AutoTrader's inventory data, provenance checks, and financing integrations are not easily replicated. Amazon's free model may not be sustainable long-term.

**Framework implication:** Amazon Autos UK is a MEDIUM-TERM threat (12-24 months), not an April 2026 issue. But it NARROWS the moat outlook and increases the strategic importance of the April pricing event. If dealers see Amazon as alternative, pricing power erodes FASTER than our base case assumes.

**Proposed NEW Kill Condition #10: Amazon Autos UK captures >5% of UK dealer listings within 12 months of launch.**
