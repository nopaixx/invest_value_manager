## Moat Classification: NARROW-TO-WIDE

### Moat Sources

#### 1. Switching Costs (STRONG)

**Domain Lock-In:** Once a business registers its domain with GoDaddy, the switching cost is non-trivial:
- DNS propagation delays (24-48 hours of potential downtime)
- Email disruption (many SMBs use domain-linked email via GoDaddy)
- Integration complexity (website, hosting, SSL, commerce all on one platform)
- Customer inertia -- 85%+ domain renewal rate proves stickiness
- Psychological cost -- small business owners are not technical and fear breaking their web presence

**Quantitative evidence:**
- Domain renewal rate: 85%+ consistently
- Customer retention: implied >85% from deferred revenue growth tracking revenue growth
- Net revenue retention > 100% (ARPU growing as customers adopt more products)

#### 2. Scale Advantages (MODERATE-STRONG)

**Platform economics:**
- 20M+ customers provide massive distribution advantage for new product launches
- 82M+ domains under management = largest registrar globally
- Scale enables lower per-unit cost for domain registration, hosting infrastructure, and support
- Advertising efficiency: GoDaddy brand recognition among SMBs is unmatched

**Quantitative evidence:**
- Gross margin 63.6% vs sector median 55% = +8.6pp premium
- Operating margin expanded from 12.6% to 23.0% in 3 years while serving the same customer base = operating leverage at scale

#### 3. Network Effects (WEAK-MODERATE)

**Limited direct network effects** -- one customer's domain doesn't make the platform better for another. However:
- Data advantage from 20M+ SMBs helps train Airo AI for better website generation
- Marketplace effects in commerce (GoDaddy Payments, commerce tools) have nascent network effects
- "Airo drives 50% of new paid subscriptions" suggests AI recommendations improve with more data

#### 4. Brand / Intangible Assets (MODERATE)

- GoDaddy brand recognition among micro-SMBs is very high (Super Bowl advertising legacy)
- First mover / top-of-mind for "I need a website/domain" searches
- ICANN-accredited registrar with regulatory standing
- NOT a luxury brand -- the brand is "accessible and easy" which is somewhat replicable

### Moat Durability Assessment

**Threats to moat:**

1. **AI website builders (Wix AI, Squarespace, Replit):** Medium threat. These compete on the website builder piece but NOT on the domain registration / DNS backbone. GoDaddy's response (Airo) is competitive. The moat is the integrated platform, not any single product.

2. **Social media replacing websites:** Low-Medium threat. Instagram/TikTok shops partially substitute for standalone websites, but businesses still need a domain for credibility, email, and SEO. The trend is ADDITIVE (social + website), not substitutive.

3. **DIY coding via AI agents:** Low threat for GoDaddy's target market. Micro-SMBs (pizza shops, hair salons, plumbers) will NEVER vibe-code their own site regardless of how easy AI makes it. They want one-click solutions.

4. **Cloudflare / Namecheap on domains:** Low-Medium. Price competition on domain registration is real but commoditized at the low end. GoDaddy's advantage is the BUNDLE (domain + hosting + website + commerce + payments), not domain pricing alone.

5. **Alternative web presence (.ai domains, decentralized DNS):** Very low. These are niche alternatives that don't threaten the mainstream domain market in any relevant timeframe.

## Key Moat Risk: AI Disruption Depth

The central question for moat durability is whether AI changes the DELIVERY mechanism (website building) or the NEED (web presence). My assessment:

- **The need for web presence is not going away.** Even in an AI agent world, businesses need a discoverable online identity. Domains, email, and hosted content remain the foundation.
- **The delivery mechanism IS changing** -- AI makes website creation faster and easier. But this BENEFITS GoDaddy (Airo reduces customer acquisition cost and time-to-value) as much as it threatens them.
- **The moat anchor is the domain + customer relationship, not the website builder.** GoDaddy could lose 100% of website builder market share and still have a viable business generating $3B+ from Core Platform alone.

**Moat verdict: Narrow-to-Wide, anchored in switching costs and scale, with domain registration providing a toll-road-like base business. The moat is more durable than the market's AI fear implies.**
