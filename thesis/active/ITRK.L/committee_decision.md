## META-REFLECTION

### Dudas sobre esta decision

1. **FCF decline is the elephant in the room.** FY2025 FCF was GBP 392M vs FY2024's 462M -- a -15% decline. FCF margin dropped to 11.4% from 13.6%. The thesis and R3 resolution were built on FY2024 data (462M base). The margin EXPANSION narrative is real on operating margin (17.0% up from 16.4%), but FCF tells a different story. This divergence between operating margin improvement and FCF decline needs explanation -- it could be capex timing, working capital, or restructuring cash costs excluded from "adjusted" figures. If H1 2026 confirms FCF recovery, thesis is intact. If FCF continues declining, this becomes a value trap.

2. **Cash position post-buy.** EUR 124 remaining is very tight. This limits ability to react to any opportunity or add to existing positions. The MONY.L sell planned for Mar 26 (~EUR 716) would resolve this, but if that doesn't execute, we're stuck at 1.2% cash.

3. **Orphan position without basket.** ITRK.L would be the only TIC position. Per P17, a single position is not a basket. Could form a "Quality Defensives" basket with 2-3 more candidates, but that's aspirational, not current. This should not block the buy, but it means ITRK.L is a standalone thesis without thematic reinforcement.

4. **Edge quality is MODERATE.** The DA correctly identified Error #49 -- our FV is near consensus. The R3 correction improved this somewhat, but 4700p is still within the analyst consensus range (4500-6610p). Our edge is primarily behavioral (patience to buy at 3592p) and timing (EU AI Act catalyst). This is real but not strong.

### Debilidades del analisis recibido

1. **R1 thesis was pre-FY2025 results.** The fundamental_analysis.md uses FY2024 data and H1 2025 projections. FY2025 actuals are now available (reported Mar 3) and show lower FCF than projected. 3. **Company-reported vs tool ROIC discrepancy persists.** Company says ROIC >21% (FY2025). Tool shows 17.0%. This 4pp gap was flagged by both moat-assessor and DA but never resolved. Both confirm moat, but the absolute level matters for calibration.

2. **FCF vs operating margin divergence should be a standard check.** When operating margin expands but FCF declines, this is a yellow flag that should be systematically investigated. Add to the risk-identifier standard checklist.
