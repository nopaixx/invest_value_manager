## Finding-by-Finding Resolution

### Finding #1 (HIGH): AI Disruption Expanding

**Resolution: ALREADY PRICED — committee range incorporated 15-25% revenue at risk.**
- OpenEvidence $12B is concerning for UpToDate (27% of revenue). But $12B VC valuation =/= $12B in real adoption. Thrive/DST fund many high-valuation AI companies.
- CoCounsel 1M users is mostly legal segment, not tax/compliance. WKL's exposure to pure legal is ~10% of EBIT.
- FY2025: ZERO customer defections, 6% organic growth. AI disruption is a 2027-2030 thesis.
- The committee priced this at EUR 72 (vs R1 EUR 94 = -23% AI haircut). The re-eval to EUR 80 was for FY2025 CONFIRMATION of resilience, not ignoring AI.
- **Action:** KC#13 added (OpenEvidence hospital contracts). No FV change.

### Finding #2 (HIGH): Buyback Value Destruction

**Resolution: ACKNOWLEDGED as backward-looking weakness.**
- EUR 524M lost on 2025 buybacks is real and concerning for capital allocation quality.
- 0.1% insider ownership is structural for Dutch large-caps — not unique to WKL.
- 2026 buybacks at EUR 68 are highly accretive if thesis is correct (FV EUR 80 = 17% upside on buyback capital).
- Total capital returns (EUR 1.57B) exceeding FCF (EUR 1.35B) is debt-funded. KC#12 added (net debt >2.5x).
- **Action:** Note. QS Tool 72 already reflects imperfect capital allocation (Capital Allocation 5/10). No FV change.

### Finding #3 (HIGH): Peer Discount Unexplained

**Resolution: PARTIALLY ACCEPTED — discount reflects real concerns, not pure opportunity.**
- WKL at 12.7x vs RELX 20.8x and TRI 33.3x.
- Likely factors: (a) higher AI vulnerability perception in health/legal segments, (b) CEO transition, (c) Amsterdam listing discount vs London/NYSE.
- Our FV (EUR 80) does NOT assume multiple expansion to peers. We use WKL's own 12.5-13x as base.
- If even partial re-rating occurs (to 15x), FV would be EUR 95+. This is upside optionality not in the thesis.
- **Action:** No FV change. The thesis doesn't depend on re-rating.

### Finding #4 (MODERATE): Receivables Anomaly

**Resolution: YELLOW FLAG — monitor, not actionable yet.**
- +23% receivables vs +3.5% revenue is a valid concern. OCF/NI declining 1.5x→1.3x.
- Possible benign: timing, enterprise mix, FRR divestiture effects.
- Possible concerning: aggressive recognition, customer payment difficulties.
- Cannot resolve without earnings transcript access.
- **Action:** Monitor in Q1 2026 results. If persists → escalate to KC. Do NOT add to position until explained.

### Finding #5 (MODERATE): FV Instability

**Resolution: ACKNOWLEDGED — commit to EUR 80 absent material new data.**
- 4 revisions (94→72→85→72→80) reflects Error #61 correction journey, not analytical weakness.
- EUR 80 is now grounded in: committee range + FY2025 actuals + DCF cross-check.
- **Action:** No further FV revisions until Q1 results or material event.
