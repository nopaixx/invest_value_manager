## Resumen Ejecutivo

The thesis has improved since the original R2 (Feb 11) -- FV was corrected from EUR 94 to EUR 72 (committee), then to EUR 80 (re-eval post-FY2025). FY2025 results confirmed 6% organic growth, validating the core operational thesis. However, five material concerns persist: (1) Morningstar's moat downgrade to NARROW on 20-year AI disruption horizon validates our committee's classification, (2) EUR 1.1B buybacks at EUR 134 avg were massive value destruction (stock now 49% lower), (3) new CEO Caywood is 8 days into the role during the company's worst crisis, (4) OpenEvidence raised $250M at $12B valuation targeting UpToDate's clinical CDS market, and (5) receivables grew 23% vs revenue 3.5% -- a quality-of-revenue anomaly not addressed in the thesis. The EUR 80 FV is defensible but sits at the optimistic end of a reasonable range. Verdict: **MODERATE COUNTER.**

## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. "AI threatens only 8-10% of revenue -- workflow layer is protected"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - The original thesis estimated 8-10% revenue at risk. The committee revised this to 15-25%. Since the committee decision, several developments have intensified the threat:
  - **Thomson Reuters CoCounsel** reached 1 MILLION users across 107 countries (Feb 25, 2026). This is no longer a pilot -- it is mainstream adoption. CoCounsel now includes "Deep Research" and autonomous document review capabilities (agentic workflow, not just information retrieval).
  - **Harvey AI** was squeezed between incumbents (TR, LexisNexis) AND Anthropic (which can handle operational legal work at foundation model scale). Harvey's $190M ARR demonstrates real enterprise willingness to pay for AI legal tools.
  - **OpenEvidence** raised $250M Series D at $12B valuation (Jan 2026, led by Thrive Capital/DST Global). This is "ChatGPT for Doctors" -- a direct competitor to UpToDate's clinical decision support. UpToDate is 27% of WKL revenue. New competitors iatroX, Glass Health, and Vera Health are also entering the space. Vera Health's clinical accuracy now EXCEEDS OpenEvidence's.
  - **Fortune** (Mar 4, 2026) distinguished two types of legal AI: "co-pilot" (incumbent-embedded, where WKL plays) and "co-worker" (autonomous agent, where Anthropic plays). The article frames this as a fundamental split, not a distinction WKL can ignore.
  - The thesis's clean "information vs workflow" separation is weakening. Agentic AI is entering the workflow layer -- Thomson Reuters "Ready to Review" automates 1040 tax prep workflow, not just tax research.
  - **FY2025 data point:** 6% organic growth confirmed. No customer defection evidence. BUT: the AI disruption thesis is about 2027-2030, not 2025. Current organic growth does not falsify the threat.
- **Severidad:** **HIGH** (downgraded from CRITICAL in original DA because FY2025 confirmed no near-term impact, but OpenEvidence at $12B valuation is a material escalation in the health segment)
- **Resolution sugerida:** The 15-25% revenue-at-risk range from the committee remains appropriate. The thesis's EUR 80 FV should incorporate a higher AI risk premium than implied.

### 2. "FV EUR 80 is conservative -- DCF supports EUR 88"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - The FV history is concerning: EUR 94 (R1) -> EUR 72 (committee) -> EUR 85 (ghost, unsupported) -> EUR 72 (corrected) -> EUR 80 (re-eval S143c11). Four revisions in one month. Instability in FV estimates suggests uncertainty, not precision.
  - The re-eval justified EUR 80 as "top of committee range (67-80), conservative vs DCF EUR 88." But the committee set EUR 72 as BASE, not EUR 80. Using the TOP of the committee range as the new FV is not conservative -- it is selectively optimistic.
  - **DCF tool output (today):** Base case EUR 88.42 at default parameters. Bear case EUR 66.64. The market at EUR 68.52 is between the tool's bear and base -- suggesting the market prices in a moderately negative scenario, not the catastrophic one the thesis claims.
  - **Morningstar FV:** EUR 120 (reduced from EUR 150 after moat downgrade to NARROW). This is 50% above our EUR 80. The gap is huge -- but Morningstar uses a lower WACC assumption (~7% implied) and higher growth.
  - **Analyst consensus PT:** EUR 130-137 (avg), range EUR 73-183. Our EUR 80 is at the extreme LOW end of the analyst range. This either means we see risks others don't (edge) or we're being excessively conservative.
  - **EV/EBIT context:** WKL at 12.7x vs RELX 20.8x, TRI 33.3x. Even applying a "narrow moat + AI risk" discount, 12.7x for a business growing 6% organically with 73.5% gross margins and 84% recurring revenue seems excessively cheap. The peer discount may reflect WKL-specific concerns the thesis hasn't fully explained.
- **Severidad:** **MODERATE**
- **Resolution sugerida:** EUR 80 is within a defensible range. The committee range of EUR 67-80 remains valid. The risk is not that EUR 80 is wrong but that the FV keeps migrating. If the next catalyst (Q1 results, new CEO strategic plan) is negative, EUR 80 will be revised down again.

### 3. "New CEO Caywood will execute well"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - Caywood became CEO on February 27, 2026 -- 8 days ago. She has ZERO track record as group CEO.
  - She came from WKL Health (one of five divisions). Her appointment of Tejas Shah (ex-McKinsey) as Chief Strategy & Innovation Officer signals that the board recognizes strategic uncertainty.
  - The FY2025 results (Feb 25) and CEO transition (Feb 27) happened within 48 hours. The results were McKinstry's last output. Caywood's influence on results will not be visible until H1 2026 at earliest.
  - Positive signal: Caywood's immediate priorities include "accelerating AI-powered innovation, expanding partnerships, strengthening commercial capabilities." This is the right rhetoric. But rhetoric != execution.
  - The share buyback decision (EUR 500M for 2026, EUR 100M already completed) was announced alongside FY2025 results. At current prices (EUR 68.52 vs EUR 134 avg for 2025 buybacks), these buybacks are more accretive. But it also means management expects the stock to stay low long enough to execute EUR 500M.
  - Risk: new CEO + new strategy officer + AI crisis = potential for strategic pivots that could be value-destructive. The EUR 500M buyback commitment could constrain M&A flexibility if an AI acquisition opportunity arises.
- **Severidad:** **MODERATE**
- **Resolution sugerida:** Monitor closely. First 12 months are critical. The thesis should have an explicit KC: "If Caywood makes an acquisition >EUR 500M in first 12 months, re-evaluate immediately."

### 4. "Buybacks are value-accretive capital allocation"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - EUR 1.1B buybacks in 2025 at EUR 134.06 average. Stock now EUR 68.52. That is EUR 524M of value destruction (49% loss on buyback capital).
  - 0.1% insider ownership means management bore virtually NONE of this loss personally. Zero skin in the game.
  - 2026 buyback of EUR 500M at current ~EUR 68 would reduce share count by ~7.3M (~3.2%). IF the stock recovers, these buybacks will have been excellent. IF the stock continues falling, this is more value destruction.
  - The thesis claims EUR 2.52/share dividend (+8%) as a positive. But dividend + buyback = EUR 1,068M + EUR 500M = EUR 1.57B total capital return vs EUR 1,348M FCF. The company is RETURNING MORE THAN IT GENERATES in FCF, funded by debt. Net debt is already 2.0x EBITDA.
  - Adjusted net financing costs guided to increase to ~EUR 110M in 2026 (from EUR 86M in 2025) -- a 28% increase. The cost of the buyback debt is visible in the P&L.
- **Severidad:** **HIGH**
- **Resolution sugerida:** The 2025 buybacks at EUR 134 were clearly value-destructive. The 2026 buybacks at EUR 68 are potentially value-creative but depend on the thesis being correct. The committee should add a KC: "Net debt >2.5x EBITDA" (tighter than the current 3.5x).

### 5. "Receivables growth is normal"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - Narrative checker flags: Receivables grew 23.0% while revenue grew only 3.5%. This is a 19.5pp gap.
  - This anomaly was not mentioned in the thesis, the re-eval, or the committee decision.
  - Possible benign explanations: timing of invoicing, mix shift toward longer payment terms for enterprise contracts, one-time effects from FRR divestiture.
  - Possible concerning explanations: channel stuffing, customer payment difficulties, aggressive revenue recognition, quality of revenue deteriorating.
  - OCF/Net Income declined from 1.5x (2022-2024) to 1.3x (2025) -- consistent with receivables growing faster than revenue. Cash conversion is declining.
  - Goodwill at 49.9% of total assets (up from 46.2% in 2022) adds to balance sheet quality concerns.
- **Severidad:** **MODERATE**
- **Resolution sugerida:** Investigate this in the earnings transcript. If management provides no explanation, this is a yellow flag. If it persists in H1 2026, it becomes a KC.
