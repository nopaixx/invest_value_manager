## Resumen Ejecutivo

The thesis identifies a genuinely high-quality business with strong structural tailwinds, and the moat assessment (WIDE 20/25) is well-supported. However, the position was opened at $124.59 vs R3 FV of $140, providing only 12.4% MoS -- thin for a 31.6x P/E stock where growth is decelerating. The February 2026 data ($3.1T ADV, +23.4% YoY) confirms volume strength but does NOT address the core bear concerns: (1) RPM compression is structural and accelerating across multiple asset classes, (2) LSEG confirmed it has "no plans for disposals" but Elliott's activist campaign remains a live overhang, (3) three major analysts downgraded in the last 6 months while consensus PT of $130.46 sits BELOW our FV of $140, (4) insider selling of $26.3M on a single day (Feb 10) with zero offsetting buys remains unexplained, and (5) constant currency Q4 growth of 9.9% is the most recent quarterly data point and sits BELOW the thesis's 12% base case. The business quality is not in question. The PRICE PAID for that quality is.

**Severity: MODERATE COUNTER.** FV of $140 is defensible but represents the UPPER bound, not the midpoint. At $124.59, the position offers limited margin of safety relative to the risks.

## Asunciones Clave Desafiadas

### 1. "Revenue growth of 12% is sustainable (base case)"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - Q4 2025 constant currency growth: 9.9% -- BELOW the 12% base case assumption
  - Revenue growth trajectory: 30% (Apr 2025) -> 13.3% (Q3) -> 12.5% (Q4). The deceleration is real.
  - Feb 2026 ADV: $3.1T (+23.4% YoY) is strong on volume but RPM compression means revenue growth lags ADV growth significantly. If RPM decline averages -5% to -8%, revenue growth = ADV growth minus RPM = 23.4% - 6.5% = ~17% maximum, and more likely 12-15% given product mix.
  - 2026 expense guidance $1.1-1.16B (midpoint +11% YoY) narrows the margin expansion story. If revenue grows 12% and expenses grow 11%, operating leverage is only +100bps, not the +130bps/yr trend the thesis assumes.
  - Barclays downgraded (Dec 2025) specifically citing electronification pace slowing. Morgan Stanley cut PT from $159 to $148. Rothschild Redburn downgraded to Neutral.
  - US Treasuries at 70-80% electronic penetration have LIMITED incremental electronification runway. The easy growth is DONE in the largest product segment.
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Committee should stress-test at 10% growth (closer to Q4 CC actual of 9.9%). At 10% growth, OEY method produces FV of ~$135-140; at 8% growth, FV drops to ~$120-130.

### 2. "LSEG ownership is manageable -- creates the discount"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - LSEG confirmed "no plans for disposals" per Feb 2026 preliminary results. This is POSITIVE vs the prior DA's concern.
  - HOWEVER: Elliott Investment Management built a LSEG stake and is pushing for strategic changes. As LSEG's crown jewel (providing ~50% of LSEG profits while LSEG's own ROE is 3.5-4%), TW is the obvious asset to monetize if LSEG needs to unlock value for Elliott.
  - LSEG's integration of TW into Workspace (H1 2026) creates value for LSEG's distribution but potentially commoditizes TW's execution. The question is who captures the margin from this integration.
  - Related-party data licensing agreement amended Nov 2025 -- terms not public. If below arm's-length, this is ongoing value extraction from TW minority shareholders.
  - Insider ownership of 0.2% means management alignment with minority shareholders is negligible. LSEG IS the effective "insider" and their interests do not necessarily align with TW minority shareholders.
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** The LSEG risk is structural, not transient. It should be factored as a permanent governance discount of 5-8% on FV. This alone would reduce FV from $140 to $130-133.

### 3. "Insider selling is routine vesting liquidation"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - $26.3M sold on a SINGLE DAY (Feb 10, 2026) by 5 executives: CEO Hult ($14M), CFO Furber ($7.3M), Officer Friedman ($3.5M), CTO Peterson ($1.3M), Officer Clack ($192K)
  - This was 5 days after Q4 earnings release (Feb 3) -- within the first available trading window post-earnings
  - Zero insider PURCHASES in 18 months. Not a single executive has bought shares with their own money.
  - Total insider ownership is only 0.2% ($54M across all insiders at current prices). The $26.3M in sales represents nearly half of total insider holdings.
  - Even if these were pre-planned 10b5-1 sales, the ABSENCE of any offsetting buys is the signal. Management has chosen not to accumulate equity in the company they run.
  - CEO sold 121K shares, leaving 518.7K total across all insiders. At the current trajectory of selling, insiders will own effectively nothing within 2-3 years.
- **Severidad:** MODERATE
- **Resolucion sugerida:** Insider selling alone is not a kill condition, but when combined with (a) low ownership, (b) zero buys, and (c) coordinated timing, it reduces confidence that management sees significant upside from current levels. This is a NEGATIVE qualitative factor, not a thesis-invalidator.

### 4. "RPM compression is offset by volume growth -- the math works"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - Credit RPM: -14% (Q4 2025). This is not fee compression at the margin -- this is structural pricing erosion driven by dealer migration from variable to fixed plans.
  - Rates RPM: -5%. Equities RPM: -10%. Compression across ALL product lines.
  - The thesis correctly notes FY2025 revenue grew 19% despite RPM declines. But the TRAJECTORY matters: if ADV growth decelerates from 23% to 15% while RPM compression persists at -7% average, net revenue growth falls to 8-10%.
  - MarketAxess portfolio trading share grew +450bp YoY to 18.6% (Coalition Greenwich), narrowing TW's lead. Trumid growing 68% YoY in credit. Competition is intensifying in TW's most RPM-sensitive segment.
  - The shift from variable to fixed fee plans (as noted in Q4 earnings) means TW captures LESS upside from volume growth. Fixed plans are sticky but cap revenue per relationship.
  - Feb 2026 variable fees were $2.04 per million (Q4 2025 level). No recovery in per-unit pricing.
- **Severidad:** MODERATE
- **Resolucion sugerida:** RPM compression is the slow-burning structural risk. It does not invalidate the thesis but narrows the growth band. Revenue growth is increasingly a VOLUME story, and volume growth requires both market activity AND electronification penetration to sustain 15%+ levels.

### 5. "FV of $140 provides adequate margin of safety at $124.59"

- **Evidencia en contra:**
  - MoS at current price: ($140 - $124.59) / $140 = 11.0%. For a P/E 33x stock with growth decelerating, this is THIN.
  - Analyst consensus PT: $130.46 (14 analysts). Our FV of $140 is 7.3% ABOVE consensus. Where is our informational edge?
  - The R1 thesis started at $155-159. The original R2 cut to $108 bear / consensus $130. R3 resolved at $140. The position was opened at $124.59 -- only $15.41 below FV.
  - Bear case DCF (growth 3.5%, WACC 10.5%): $143.72 per tool. This means our FV of $140 is BELOW even the bear case DCF. However, the bear case DCF uses trailing FCF of $1.06B which includes a year of 19% revenue growth -- if growth truly decelerates to 8-10%, forward FCF will be lower.
  - At $124.59, E[CAGR] = ($140/$124.59)^(1/3) - 1 + 0.3% = 4.2% + 0.3% = 4.5% from rerating alone. Including 10% growth: total E[CAGR] ~14.5%. This is decent but requires the $140 FV to be correct AND growth to sustain 10%+.
  - RISK: If FV is actually $130 (consensus) and growth is 8%, E[CAGR] drops to ~9.6%. At 8% E[CAGR], this is a BOTTOM-TERCILE position that should be a rotation candidate.
- **Severidad:** HIGH
- **Resolucion sugerida:** The committee should recognize that $140 is the CEILING of defensible fair value, not the center. A more conservative estimate of $130-135 would be consistent with (a) consensus, (b) the governance discount, and (c) the growth deceleration.

### Dudas/Incertidumbres
- The Feb 2026 ADV of $3.1T (+23.4% YoY) is genuinely strong and partially counters the growth deceleration narrative. However, ADV growth does NOT equal revenue growth due to RPM compression. I could not find Feb 2026 revenue data (only available at Q1 earnings in April). This is the single biggest uncertainty: is the volume strength translating to revenue?
