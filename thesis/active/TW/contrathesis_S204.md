## Why I Could Be Wrong About TW

### 1. RPM Compression Is Structural, Not Cyclical (HIGH severity)

**The thesis says:** Revenue per million (RPM) is declining because of competition and
electronic trading commoditization, but this is offset by ADV (volume) growth.

**The counter:** RPM declined -14% in credit and -5% in rates in Q4 2025. This is NOT
cyclical — it's the natural endgame of electronification. As more trading moves electronic,
bid-ask spreads compress, and each trade generates less revenue. TW makes MORE trades
but earns LESS per trade. Eventually, volume growth cannot outrun price erosion.

**Evidence:** Total revenue grew +18.9% in Q4, but strip out ADV growth of +26% and the
PRICE component is negative. TW is running faster just to stay in place.

**Implication:** FV $135 assumes 10% growth. If RPM compression accelerates (e.g., -20%
in credit), growth drops to 6-7% even with record ADV. FV drops to $110-115.

### 2. Insider Selling — $37M From Entire C-Suite (HIGH severity)

**The thesis says:** Likely 10b5-1 automatic plans (Feb 10 cluster = compensation vesting).

**The counter:** Even if 10b5-1, the SCALE matters. CEO Hult sold $23.1M in 30 days.
CFO Furber $7.3M. CTO Peterson $1.3M. The entire leadership team is reducing exposure.
10b5-1 plans are PRE-SCHEDULED — but management CHOOSES when to set them up. Setting up
large 10b5-1 plans = management doesn't see significant upside.

**Comparison:** HLNE insiders are BUYING $3.2M (opposite signal). DOCS insiders are doing
small 10b5-1 sells (2K/month). TW insiders are doing MASSIVE 10b5-1 sells.

### 3. LSEG Overhang — 44% Stake (MEDIUM severity)

**The thesis says:** KC#9 monitors LSEG orderly reduction. Elliott activism at LSEG
could force LSEG to sell TW stake.

**The counter:** LSEG owns 44% of TW. If Elliott forces LSEG to divest, 44% of TW
shares hit the market. Even orderly selling creates persistent overhang that suppresses
the stock price for 12-18 months. This is NOT a tail risk — Elliott is actively engaged
at LSEG NOW.

### 4. Valuation Already Fair (MEDIUM severity)

**The numbers:** P/E 32.9x at $124. Reverse DCF implies -3.9% FCF growth (vs +23% historical).
This looks cheap BUT: the historical FCF CAGR of 23% was driven by electronification
tailwinds that are MATURING. US Treasury market is already 60%+ electronic. Credit is
40%+. The low-hanging fruit is picked. Future growth requires entering NEW markets
(munis, repos, private credit) where TW has no established position.

**Implication:** P/E 32.9x for a company with slowing growth + RPM compression +
management selling is not obviously cheap. It may be fairly priced.

### 5. Only 1 Fund Holder (LOW severity but notable)

Cantillon Capital holds 8.2% of their portfolio in TW. No other quality fund. Compare
to SPGI (7 funds) or ADBE (6 funds). If Cantillon exits, TW has ZERO quality fund support.
