The thesis survives because: (a) market prices -9.8% FCF decline which is clearly excessive, (b) FY2025 was record revenue/EBITDA confirming no decline, (c) total shareholder return ~10% (dividend + buyback) provides floor, (d) insurance premium recovery expected 2026.

## Finding-by-Finding Resolution

### Finding #1 (HIGH): AI Disruption LIVE
**DA claim:** Tuio + Insurify ChatGPT apps launched. MONY -13.8% in one day. BCG places auto marketplaces in "Breached" category.

**Resolution: ACCEPTED as material risk, NOT as immediate thesis-breaker.**
- MONY responded proactively (own ChatGPT app for car/broadband/van). This is defensive but shows adaptation.
- Current adoption data: unknown. UK adoption likely lags US. FCA regulatory environment creates friction for AI insurance intermediation.
- The 190p FV was already set POST the -13.8% AI selloff. The market has priced this risk.
- **Action:** Monitor quarterly. If >5% of UK insurance quotes flow through AI agents within 12 months → revise FV to 150-160p. Existing KCs cover this.

### Finding #2 (HIGH): P/E Re-Rating May Never Come
**DA claim:** BofA Underperform 173p. Historical 15-20x P/E may be permanently gone if market correctly prices structural disruption.

**Resolution: ACCEPTED — thesis does NOT assume P/E re-rating.**
- The 190p FV uses current depressed multiples (11-12x), not historical 15-20x. P/E expansion is upside optionality, not base case.
- If P/E stays at 11-12x: FV 190p still holds because it's based on normalized earnings, not multiple expansion.
- BofA 173p = market price = says "fairly valued now." We disagree by 10%. Not a large gap.
- **Action:** No FV change. Already conservative.

### Finding #3 (HIGH): Google AI Overviews
**DA claim:** 14% of transactional queries now have AI Overviews. Threatens MONY's Google traffic.

**Resolution: PARTIALLY ACCEPTED — SuperSaveClub is the hedge.**
- SuperSaveClub (2.1M members, 2x LTV) creates direct customer relationships bypassing Google.
- Martin Lewis / MoneySavingExpert (21.7M monthly visits) provides branded traffic independent of Google search.
- However, 80% of SSC members are existing users converted, not new customer acquisition. The brand moat helps retention but doesn't solve new customer acquisition if Google changes.
- **Action:** Monitor Google traffic share. Critical metric for H1 2026 results.

### Finding #4 (HIGH): Compound Risk
**DA claim:** AI + Google + premium cycle hitting simultaneously. Thesis treats as independent.

**Resolution: PARTIALLY ACCEPTED.**
- The premium cycle is RESOLVING (EY forecasts insurer losses 2026 → premiums rise). This is a tailwind developing.
- AI and Google risks ARE correlated (both reduce comparison site traffic). The thesis should acknowledge this compound effect.
- However: at 172.9p, the market is pricing ALL THREE risks. We're buying the compound discount, not ignoring it.
- **Action:** Note. No FV change.

### Finding #5 (HIGH): Narrow Upside
**DA claim:** 172.9p → 190p = only 10% upside. Total return case (yield), not growth.

**Resolution: ACCEPTED — reframe position as yield anchor.**
- E[CAGR] 12.5% = lowest in portfolio. Growth contribution: 2%. Yield contribution: 10.5% (dividend 7.3% + buyback 3.2%).
- This IS a yield position, not a growth compounder. The E[CAGR] framework understates the total return (buyback yield not fully captured).
- P16 (Perpetual Rotation): MONY is the weakest position by growth profile. FIRST candidate to fund a higher E[CAGR] opportunity.
- **Action:** Flag as FIRST ROTATION CANDIDATE. Do NOT ADD. HOLD for yield while awaiting better deployment target.
