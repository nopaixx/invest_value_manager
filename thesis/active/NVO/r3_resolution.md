## Finding-by-Finding Resolution

### Finding #1 (HIGH): FCF Collapse Is Structural
**DA claim:** OEY used DKK 72B Owner Earnings; actual 2025 FCF was DKK 29B. OEY-derived FV overstated 30-40%.

**Resolution: PARTIALLY ACCEPTED.**
- The OEY calculation IS stale — this is correct. DKK 72B was projected, not actual.
- However, the $50 FV was NOT derived from fresh OEY. It came from the scenario bridge ($66 -> $50) which applied percentage adjustments for CagriSema failure, share loss, and probability reweighting.
- The capex surge (DKK 60B, 4.1x D&A) is predominantly GROWTH capex — manufacturing capacity for GLP-1 at global scale. This converts to future revenue capacity. Treating it as permanent FCF destruction overstates the bear case.
- **Action:** Switch to P/E as primary valuation method during capex cycle. OEY revisitable when capex normalizes (~2028). P/E at 16x normalized EPS DKK 21 = DKK 336 = ~$46.
- **FV impact:** -$3 (from $50 to $47)

### Finding #2 (HIGH): Wegovy Pill = Franchise Maintenance
**DA claim:** 14-16.6% weight loss at $149-299/month is margin-dilutive and cannibalistic, not a growth catalyst.

**Resolution: PARTIALLY ACCEPTED, but already priced in.**
- The margin dilution concern is valid — oral is lower ASP than injectable.
- But the thesis base case ALREADY assumed "Wegovy pill grows, CagriSema niche" — this IS franchise maintenance by design, not a bull case catalyst.
- The pill expands the addressable market: needle-phobic patients, primary care convenience. Not purely cannibalistic.
- 50K scripts/week without denominator context is a valid DA critique. Need total franchise data to assess cannibalization vs expansion.
- **Action:** No FV change. The base case already incorporated this as maintenance, not growth. The concern is valid but not incremental to the thesis.
- **FV impact:** $0 (already baked in)

### Finding #3 (MODERATE): $50 FV Still Optimistic
**DA claim:** Central estimate $42.50 using bear assumptions (3% growth, 9% WACC, 15x P/E).

**Resolution: PARTIALLY ACCEPTED — DA too bearish on inputs.**
- DA's 9% WACC is excessive for big pharma (sector 6-8%). Using 7.5% (+0.5% uncertainty premium over thesis 7%).
- DA's 3% growth is too pessimistic. Even without CagriSema premium, Ozempic/Wegovy franchise + TAM growth supports 5% normalized.
- DA's 15x P/E assumes "pharma troubled" — but NVO has 22% ROIC, $10B+ revenue, global scale. 15x is appropriate for generic pharma, not for a franchise leader in a growing TAM. 16x is fair compromise.
- **The DA itself admitted** its P/E base case (17x x DKK 22 = DKK 374 = ~$51) "converges with the thesis $50."
- **Action:** Resolve at $47, splitting DA bear ($42.50) and thesis ($50). P/E method: DKK 21 x 16x = $46. Scenario method: $46.25. Both converge.
- **FV impact:** -$3 (from $50 to $47)

### Finding #4 (MODERATE): Competitive Moat Eroding
**DA claim:** Duopoly under threat from Amgen MariTide, Roche CT-388, Viking, Pfizer. Patent cliff in ex-US.

**Resolution: ACCEPTED as probability adjustment.**
- Competitor pipeline is real but 2-3 years from approval. Not immediate threat to 2026-2027 thesis horizon.
- Patent cliff in ex-US (India, Canada, China, Brazil) IS near-term — already impacting 2026 guidance.
- **Action:** Increase bear probability from 30% to 35%. This is already reflected in resolved scenario weights.
- **FV impact:** Captured in scenario reweighting (-$0.50)

### Finding #5 (LOW): No Insider Buying
**DA claim:** All 5 recent transactions are sells. No buying despite 50%+ decline.

**Resolution: NOTED, not actionable.**
- Danish disclosure regime is different from US. Novo Foundation (28.1%) is the alignment mechanism, not individual insider trades.
- Yellow flag but not thesis-changing. Foundation hasn't been actively buying, which is more concerning.
- **FV impact:** $0
