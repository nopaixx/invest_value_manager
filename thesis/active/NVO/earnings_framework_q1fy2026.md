## The Two Questions That Matter

**1. How severe is the revenue decline?**

Management guided -5% to -13% sales decline for FY2026. That's an enormous range. Q1 will reveal where in that range we're tracking. If -5% → trough is shallow, recovery possible. If -13% → structural deterioration, FV needs revision.

**2. Is Wegovy pill creating real revenue or just prescriptions?**

NVO claims 50K scripts/week. S284 correction: IQVIA retail shows 38K peak, PLATEAUED since mid-Feb. Oral requires 70x more API than injectable at $149/month vs $1,350/month = margin destruction. Q1 is the first quarter with meaningful oral Wegovy contribution. Does it show up in revenue? And at what margin?

## Wegovy Pill Reality Check (S284 Corrections)

The thesis originally said "50K scripts/week exceeding expectations." S284 found:

| Claim | Reality |
|-------|---------|
| 50K scripts/week | **Management-sourced.** IQVIA retail: 38K peak, PLATEAUED since mid-Feb. |
| Fastest drug launch | Directionally true but 90% are cash-pay at $149/month ($16K/yr). |
| Revenue contribution | Unknown — $149/month × 38K/week = ~$290M/yr run rate. BUT: 70x API requirement = margin destruction. |
| Competitive advantage | **~3-month window** before orforglipron (if approved). Small molecule = structurally better economics. |

**What Q1 must show:** Not just prescriptions but REVENUE. If 38K scripts/week translates to <$100M in Q1 revenue, oral is volume without value. If >$200M, it's real.

**Margin question:** Management has NOT disclosed oral Wegovy gross margin separately. If oral GM is <50% (vs injectable ~76%), every script moved from injectable to oral DESTROYS value. This is the "own goal" risk (KC#6): Novo's own oral success cannibalizes its higher-margin injectable.

## What I'm MOST Worried About

**The margin destruction scenario.** Wegovy pill at $149/month with 70x API requirement could have gross margins of 30-40% (vs injectable 76%). If Q1 shows oral revenue growing while total gross margin compresses below 72%, it means Novo is WINNING on prescriptions but LOSING on economics. The market at P/E 10x may already be pricing this — but if confirmed, P/E could de-rate further to 8x (= $29, below our cost).

**Orforglipron is the existential threat.** If Lilly gets FDA approval for a small molecule oral GLP-1 with no food restrictions, no bioavailability penalty, and conventional manufacturing economics — Wegovy pill's 3-month head start becomes meaningless. The oral GLP-1 market becomes Lilly's to lose, not Novo's to win.

**At 7% allocation and $36.40 cost, max loss in CRISIS scenario ($30 FV) = EUR 160 (1.7% of portfolio).** Painful but survivable. The trim was the right preparation.
