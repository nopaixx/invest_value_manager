## Finding-by-Finding Resolution

### Finding #1 (HIGH): Moat = Regulatory Capture Being Removed
**DA claim:** Italy/Brazil/Turkey pattern shows governments systematically removing the regulatory protection that sustains Edenred's 41% EBITDA margins. The moat IS regulatory capture.

**Resolution: PARTIALLY ACCEPTED — moat is regulatory-DEPENDENT, not purely regulatory capture.**
- The DA is correct that regulation is a moat component. Italy caps, Brazil interchange limits, Turkey antitrust — this IS a pattern.
- However, the moat also includes: network effects (60M users, 2M merchants, 1M corporates), switching costs (employer payroll integration), scale (45 countries, no competitor has comparable geographic reach), and data/float (EUR ~3B managed float).
- QS 62 Tier B already reflects this compromised moat. A QS 75+ (Tier A) stock would have a regulation-independent moat.
- The DA's point that margins are "closer to Visa/MA than Worldline/PayPal" because of regulatory protection is the strongest argument. If regulation normalizes globally, margins converge toward 25-30%.
- **Action:** Accept that 20-25% probability of secular margin compression exists. Already reflected in current FV (EUR 29 vs original EUR 38.4 = -24.5% haircut). No further FV change.

### Finding #2 (HIGH): 2027-28 Guidance Credibility Damaged
**DA claim:** Management cut guidance 10-16pp one week after CMD. Their 2027-28 guidance (+8/+12% EBITDA) may be similarly unreliable.

**Resolution: ACCEPTED — weight guidance at 70% probability.**
- Valid point. The CMD-to-decree sequence damaged forward guidance credibility.
- However: the 2026 cut was triggered by a SPECIFIC external event (Brazil decree). The 2027-28 guidance assumes Brazil impact is absorbed. If that assumption holds (likely — 2026 is the absorption year), recovery is plausible.
- Autonomous Research's EUR 17.50 Underperform is the counter. They specialize in financial services.
- **Action:** Growth rate set at 6% (vs guidance-implied 8-12%) to reflect ~70% probability. KC#6 (2027-28 guidance <5%) remains the key forward gate. If 2027 delivers <5% EBITDA growth, thesis fundamentally weakened.

### Finding #3 (MODERATE): KC#1 Should Be DORMANT Not CLEARED
**DA claim:** France chose transparency over caps THIS TIME, but: 8% social levy discussed, Banque de France oversight replacing dissolved National Commission, fiscal pressure on French treasury.

**Resolution: ACCEPTED — reclassify KC#1 to DORMANT.**
- The DA is correct. "Cleared" implies zero future risk from France. The reality: France chose the lighter path but retained regulatory tools. BdF oversight is more powerful than the previous body.
- DORMANT = risk exists but no active trigger. Will re-evaluate if: (a) levy proposal enters legislative process, (b) BdF announces fee review, or (c) next French fiscal consolidation targets meal voucher tax benefits.
- **Action:** Update thesis KC#1 status: CLEARED → DORMANT.

### Finding #4 (HIGH): Margin Compression Not Modeled in FV
**DA claim:** If EBITDA margin compresses from 41% to 30% over 5 years, FV = EUR 25.40. This is the shorts' core thesis.

**Resolution: ACCEPTED as explicit scenario, 20% probability.**
- This is the strongest DA contribution. The thesis had no explicit margin compression scenario.
- The margin compression scenario requires: (a) regulatory normalization in 3+ major markets simultaneously, AND (b) competitive entry at scale (iFood-Alelo in Brazil + fintech in France/Italy), AND (c) no pricing power offset from new products (mobility, corporate expenses).
- 20% probability is appropriate: Italy already regulated (margins held at segment level), Brazil decree is the worst-case template. But 41% → 30% requires Italy+Brazil+France+rest simultaneously.
- At 20% probability, impact on EV: 0.20 × (EUR 25.40 - EUR 29.0) = -EUR 0.72. This is within the existing FV range and doesn't warrant a change to the EUR 29 rounded figure.
- **Action:** Document as explicit scenario. If H1 2026 shows group EBITDA margin below 37% (vs ~41% FY2025), escalate probability to 35% and revise FV.

### Finding #5 (HIGH): Position Sizing 18.4% in Tier B Violates P9
**DA claim:** Largest position in portfolio is a Tier B stock. P9 (Quality Gravitation) says portfolio should aspire to Tier A.

**Resolution: ACCEPTED as tension, NOT as action.**
- The DA is correct on principle. 18.4% in Tier B is not what P9 envisions.
- However: E[CAGR] 21.7% (with corrected 6% growth) is HIGHEST in portfolio. P16 (Perpetual Rotation) says rotate FROM worst TO best E[CAGR]. EDEN is the best, not the worst.
- The tension resolves as: EDEN is first rotation candidate IF a Tier A opportunity with E[CAGR] >22% appears. Until then, the position earns its weight through expected return, not quality score.
- Do NOT add further. 18.4% is the ceiling, not the floor.
- **Action:** Hold. Document P9 tension. Set automatic trim trigger: if any Tier A pipeline candidate achieves E[CAGR] >22% with approved thesis, EDEN is the funding source.
