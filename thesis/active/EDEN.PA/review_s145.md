## 6. Status Determination — Reasoning from Principles

### Precedents Consulted

From `decisions_log.yaml`:
- **EDEN.PA sizing history**: 5 ADDs to ~19% of portfolio. This is our LARGEST position by far. Original sizing decisions were made at higher conviction; current conviction is MEDIUM.
- **RACE.MI BUY (Mar 5)**: Tier A at MoS 11.9%, E[CAGR] 13.5%. EDEN at MoS 35.2% and E[CAGR] 22.5% has dramatically better risk/reward.
- **SHEL.L HOLD/EXIT precedent**: We held SHEL.L with weak thesis and eventually sold. EDEN's thesis is NOT weak — FY2025 was record, guidance maintained.

### Reasoning

**Why HOLD (not EXIT):**
1. **Thesis INTACT.** FY2025 record results. 2027-2028 guidance confirmed. France reform resolved positively. No KC triggered.
2. **MoS 35.2% is substantial** for Tier B. Precedents show we accept MoS 20-25% for Tier B. 35% provides significant buffer.
3. **E[CAGR] 22.5%** is among the highest in the portfolio. Selling this to hold cash (3% return) would be destructive.
4. **Reverse DCF confirms extreme pessimism.** Market implies -15% FCF decline for 5 years. Even in bear case, this is unrealistic.
5. **Kill conditions improving**, not deteriorating. France risk removed, leverage at 0.9x, dividend increased.

**Why NOT ADD:**
1. **Position already ~19% of portfolio.** This is our largest holding by a significant margin. Adding more increases concentration risk beyond what is prudent for a Tier B position (P1, P3).
2. **Conviction MEDIUM** (not HIGH). The regulatory environment in Italy + Brazil is real and ongoing. SI at 21% signals smart money disagrees.
3. **Sizing precedent:** Even our highest-conviction Tier A positions (ADBE, NVO) were 3-5% initial. EDEN at 19% is 4x normal sizing. No precedent supports adding further.
4. **Macro environment:** Iran crisis, oil at $92, recession risk 40-55%. Conservative positioning is warranted with existing large position.

**Why NOT TRIM:**
1. **No thesis deterioration.** FY2025 was better than expected. Trimming after GOOD news contradicts P6 (sell requires argument).
2. **E[CAGR] 22.5% is too high to trim.** Would need to identify a BETTER use of capital, and nothing in the pipeline at current prices offers comparable risk-adjusted return.
3. **Timing:** Selling during Iran-induced market stress when EDEN is relatively insulated would be poor timing. The 6.9% dividend provides real carry.
4. **The 19% position size is a concern but not urgent.** If thesis weakens or a materially better opportunity arises, then trim is justified. Currently neither condition is met.

### Decision: **HOLD**

Conviction: **MEDIUM** (unchanged)
Watchpoints: Brazil implementation timeline (May-Nov 2026), SI trajectory, 2027 guidance at next update, 3rd market regulation
