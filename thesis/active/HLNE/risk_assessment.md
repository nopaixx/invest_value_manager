## Top 3 Riesgos Criticos

### 1. Private Credit Meltdown -- Evergreen Contagion Risk (CRITICAL -- NEW, NOT IN ORIGINAL THESIS)

- **Categoria:** Fundamental / Macro
- **Descripcion:** The Blue Owl crisis (Feb-Mar 2026) triggered an industry-wide panic in semi-liquid/evergreen private credit funds. Blue Owl permanently gated its $1.6B OBDC II fund after 200%+ surge in redemption requests. The contagion has spread: Blackstone BCRED saw $3.8B (7.9%) redemption requests; BlackRock restricted withdrawals on its $26B HPS Lending Fund; Morgan Stanley's North Haven fund faced 10.9% repurchase requests and fulfilled only 45.8%; Cliffwater's $33B fund faces 7% withdrawals. JPMorgan is marking down software loan collateral, triggering de-risking cascades. Morgan Stanley predicts a "significant private credit shakeout on par with Covid losses."
- **HLNE-specific exposure:** Hamilton Lane's SCOPE fund (Senior Credit Opportunities Fund) offers MONTHLY liquidity in private credit -- the EXACT structure (semi-liquid wrapper around illiquid assets) that caused Blue Owl's collapse. SCOPE targets floating-rate, senior secured loans, which is higher quality collateral than Blue Owl's book, but the liquidity mismatch architecture is identical. If retail/HNW investors lose trust in semi-liquid private credit broadly, SCOPE faces elevated redemptions regardless of credit quality.
- **Evidencia:**
  - NPR (Mar 19): "Private credit could lead to big trouble on Wall Street." U.S. banks lent ~$300B to private credit companies (Moody's).
  - CNBC (Mar 17): "Private credit off-ramp emerges as investors look to cash out."
  - Morgan Stanley expects direct lending default rates to reach 8%, approaching Covid peak.
  - AI disruption of software sector (15-25% of private credit portfolios) = structural collateral risk.
  - HLNE management (Erik Hirsch) calls it a "private credit witch hunt" and says fears are "overblown" -- this is both a mitigant (insider knowledge) and a risk (management dismissing legitimate structural concern).
- **Probabilidad:** Media-Alta (55%) -- contagion is actively spreading as of Mar 2026. The question is whether it reaches HLNE's specific funds or remains concentrated in lower-quality lenders.
- **Impacto si materializa:** SEVERE. If SCOPE or other Evergreen funds gate or face net outflows: (a) Evergreen AUM growth drops from +70% to potentially negative, (b) KC#4 triggered within 2-3 quarters, (c) narrative shift from "democratization" to "liquidity trap" destroys the growth premium, (d) P/E compression from 17.8x to 12-14x = stock to $60-75 range (-25% to -40%).
- **Mitigante:** HLNE's SCOPE invests in senior secured (top of capital stack), unlike Blue Owl's riskier exposure. HLNE has $339M cash + minimal leverage (0.1x Net Debt/EBITDA). Management is actively defending positioning. Insider cluster buy $3.24M at $100-108 suggests management does NOT see fund-level stress.
- **Kill condition?:** YES. **Suggested KC#8: "HLNE Evergreen funds report net outflows or impose redemption gates in any quarter."** If triggered, run EXIT Protocol immediately.

### 2. PE Fundraising Drought Is Structural, Not Cyclical (CRITICAL)

- **Categoria:** Fundamental
- **Descripcion:** PE fundraising has declined for four consecutive years -- the weakest since 2020. Only 388 funds raised $310B through Q3 2025 vs 905 funds/$585B full-year 2024. This is not a cycle, it is an industry maturation event. Bain & Company (Senior Partner, Global PE Practice): "This is a 5+ year problem." McKinsey 2026: Shifts in deployment, returns, and fundraising are "more likely STRUCTURAL features of a maturing industry." LP overallocation at record levels (20-30%). Distributions at only 6% of AUM (vs 10yr avg 14%) = LPs cannot recycle capital. 78% of distributions concentrated in mega exits, leaving mid-market stagnant.
- **HLNE-specific impact:** HLNE's advisory business ($860B AUM) depends on institutional LPs committing to new funds. If commitments stall for 3-5 years, advisory fee growth stalls. The thesis projects management fee growth of 12-14%; in a structural drought this drops to 5-7%. The counter-narrative (semi-liquid fundraising +204% since 2023) partially offsets, but semi-liquid is the segment now under stress from contagion risk (#1 above). These two risks are CORRELATED.
- **Probabilidad:** Alta (65%) -- 4 years of data + multiple independent research firms (Bain, McKinsey, Cambridge Associates) confirming structural nature.
- **Impacto si materializa:** Management fee growth drops from 12-14% to 5-7%. FRE growth moderates to 5-8%. At P/FRE 14x (trough multiple), FV = $95 = roughly current price. Expected return drops below 12% threshold. Position becomes rotation candidate.
- **Mitigante:** HLNE is GAINING market share (FEAUM +11% vs industry flat). Semi-liquid vehicles growing as partial offset. Exit environment improving (+80% YoY in 2025 per McKinsey). PE is maturing, not dying.
- **Kill condition?:** The existing KC#1 (FEAUM negative 2Q) and KC#2 (fee revenue decline YoY) partially capture this. No additional KC needed, but MONITORING threshold should be tightened: if FEAUM growth decelerates to <5% for 2 consecutive quarters, begin EXIT evaluation.

### 3. Mega-Manager Competition in Private Wealth (HIGH)

- **Categoria:** Competitivo
- **Descripcion:** Blackstone private wealth AUM reached $290B (tripled in 5 years). KKR is "pushing deeper into wealth distribution." Apollo, Ares, and others launching competing Evergreen products. Citi partnered with BX/KKR/Blue Owl for private wealth distribution -- HLNE was EXCLUDED. The mega-managers can subsidize Evergreen product fees from their trillion-dollar platforms: BX/KKR/APO can offer 50bps products vs HLNE's 67bps. HLNE at $146B total AUM cannot match this pricing power.
- **Probabilidad:** Alta (70%) -- the trend is already underway, with BX/KKR/APO all actively building.
- **Impacto si materializa:** Medium. HLNE's addressable TAM share shrinks. Fee rate expansion reverses (67bps toward 55-60bps). KC#7 (mega-managers >30% of addressable TAM) approaches. However, the overall TAM is growing rapidly ($700B Evergreen industry, projected $3.6T+), so HLNE can grow in absolute terms while losing relative share.
- **Mitigante:** HLNE's 30-year data moat ($18T+ private markets data) is genuinely unique and defensible. Specialist vs generalist positioning. First-mover in private wealth advisory channel. RIA/broker-dealer relationships where HLNE is embedded. Not all wealth channels are served by mega-managers.
- **Kill condition?:** Existing KC#7 covers this. Monitor competitive commentary at Q4 earnings (May 2026).

## Additional Material Risks

### 4. Oil Crisis -> Recession -> PE Cascade (HIGH)

- **Categoria:** Macro / Geopolitica
- **Descripcion:** Oil WTI at $96.77, Brent ~$103. Iran-US war Day 14+, Hormuz mined. If sustained $95+ for 4+ weeks: recession probability 45-55%. Recession triggers: PE exits freeze -> distributions collapse -> LP overallocation worsens -> fundraising drought deepens -> HLNE's advisory fee growth stalls -> incentive fees go to near-zero -> PE AUM marks down (3-6 month lag) -> management fee base shrinks mechanically.
- **Probabilidad:** Media (40-50%) -- baseline scenario is extended war 3-4 weeks, oil $80-95. Full recession = lower probability but rising.
- **Impacto:** At beta 1.49, HLNE falls ~45% in a -30% S&P scenario ($99 to ~$55). This is the HIGHEST beta in the portfolio. In a recession, PE fundraising drought deepens dramatically. Incentive fees go near-zero. The stock would trade at 10-12x trough FRE.
- **Mitigante:** 72% of revenue is contractually locked management fees. Fortress balance sheet (0.1x leverage). Private markets actually gain share during downturns as institutions increase alternatives allocations (secular trend). But timing is terrible for near-term performance.

### 5. SBC Dilution Eating Shareholder Returns (HIGH)

- **Categoria:** Financiero
- **Descripcion:** Revenue CAGR 24.7% vs EPS CAGR 10.7% = 14pp gap. SBC doubled to 4.4% of revenue in FY2025 (from ~2%). This is not modest dilution -- it is STRUCTURAL value transfer from shareholders to employees. True owner earnings growth may be closer to 7-8%, not the 10% assumed in the thesis header.
- **Probabilidad:** Alta (80%) -- SBC is a structural feature of the comp model, unlikely to reverse.
- **Impacto:** Medium. Reduces E[CAGR] by ~2-3pp if growth assumption drops from 10% to 7-8%. At 7-8% growth with current price, E[CAGR] falls to ~9-10%, below deployment threshold.
- **Mitigante:** High insider ownership (10.6%) means insiders bear the dilution too. SBC aligns employee interests. Revenue growing fast enough to absorb SBC for now.

### 6. Robbins Geller Investigation into Hamilton Lane Private Assets Fund (MEDIUM -- NEW, NOT IN THESIS)

- **Categoria:** Legal
- **Descripcion:** Robbins Geller Rudman & Dowd LLP launched an investigation into Hamilton Lane Private Assets Fund in July 2025, investigating potential breaches of fiduciary duties. This is one of HLNE's flagship Evergreen products. While Robbins Geller is known for ambulance-chasing (they investigate many companies that never result in lawsuits), the investigation specifically targets an Evergreen fund -- which is HLNE's primary growth engine -- during a period of industry-wide scrutiny of semi-liquid fund structures.
- **Probabilidad:** Baja-Media (25%) -- most RG investigations never result in formal action. However, in the current regulatory environment (SEC specifically targeting "retailization" of private funds in 2026 exam priorities), this could gain traction.
- **Impacto:** Medium. If formal suit filed: (a) legal costs, (b) reputational damage to Evergreen fundraising, (c) regulatory scrutiny intensifies, (d) narrative amplifies Blue Owl contagion. If SEC takes interest, impact escalates to HIGH.
- **Mitigante:** No SEC formal action. No specific allegations public. HLNE has clean regulatory history. Could be routine plaintiff-firm activity.
- **Kill condition?:** Not standalone, but if combined with KC#8 (Evergreen gating), this becomes part of a CRITICAL composite risk.

The beta creates a feedback loop that makes the stock MORE volatile than the underlying business justifies.
