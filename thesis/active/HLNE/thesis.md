## Business Understanding

### What Hamilton Lane Does

Hamilton Lane is one of the world's largest private markets investment management firms, founded in 1991 and headquartered in Bala Cynwyd, Pennsylvania. It provides solutions across the entire private markets spectrum:

1. **Discretionary AUM ($146B):** Direct management of private equity, private credit, real assets, and infrastructure funds. Revenue from management fees (0.67% blended fee rate) and incentive fees (10-20% of profits above hurdle).

2. **Advisory AUM ($860B):** Non-discretionary advisory services where HLNE advises institutional investors on private markets allocation, manager selection, and portfolio construction. Revenue from advisory fees (lower fee rate, but massive scale).

3. **Evergreen Platform:** Open-ended, semi-liquid funds targeting private wealth investors (HNW, RIAs). This is the key growth engine -- Evergreen AUM grew 70% in Q3 FY2026. Part of the broader "democratization of private markets" mega-trend.

4. **Data & Technology:** HLNE's proprietary database covers $18T+ of private markets data from 30+ years. Used internally for manager selection and externally as a competitive moat. Recently partnered with YCharts to bring benchmarks to advisors.
