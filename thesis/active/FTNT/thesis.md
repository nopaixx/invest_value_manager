## Business Understanding

### What Fortinet Does

Fortinet is a cybersecurity platform company founded in 2000 by brothers Ken and Michael Xie. The company designs, develops, and sells cybersecurity solutions centered around the FortiGate next-generation firewall (NGFW), powered by proprietary purpose-built ASICs (Application-Specific Integrated Circuits). This hardware advantage delivers 5-10x better price/performance than competitors using general-purpose CPUs, creating a structural cost moat that is effectively impossible to replicate without billions in R&D investment and 20+ years of iteration.

The business model has evolved from a pure firewall appliance vendor into a converged networking + security platform. FortiOS, a single operating system, unifies firewall, SD-WAN, SASE, ZTNA, EDR, SIEM, OT security, and more into one platform. This is critical because the cybersecurity industry is undergoing platformization: enterprises average 83 security tools from 29 vendors and are consolidating toward 2-3 platform vendors. Fortinet's single-OS architecture makes it the most natural consolidation target, particularly for organizations that want both networking and security from one vendor.

### Revenue Model

The services revenue is highly recurring, composed of:

- **FortiCare:** Technical support and maintenance contracts. Near-100% attach rate.
- **FortiSASE/SecOps/OT subscriptions:** Newer, faster-growing categories.

**Deferred Revenue: $7.1B** ($3.64B current + $3.48B non-current) -- this is 104% of trailing revenue, providing exceptional visibility. Deferred revenue grew 15% YoY.
