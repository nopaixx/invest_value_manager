## Business Understanding

### What Fortinet Does

Fortinet is a cybersecurity platform company founded in 2000 by brothers Ken and Michael Xie. The company designs, develops, and sells cybersecurity solutions centered around the FortiGate next-generation firewall (NGFW), powered by proprietary purpose-built ASICs (Application-Specific Integrated Circuits). This hardware advantage delivers 5-10x better price/performance than competitors using general-purpose CPUs, creating a structural cost moat that is effectively impossible to replicate without billions in R&D investment and 20+ years of iteration.

The business model has evolved from a pure firewall appliance vendor into a converged networking + security platform. FortiOS, a single operating system, unifies firewall, SD-WAN, SASE, ZTNA, EDR, SIEM, OT security, and more into one platform. This is critical because the cybersecurity industry is undergoing platformization: enterprises average 83 security tools from 29 vendors and are consolidating toward 2-3 platform vendors. Fortinet's single-OS architecture makes it the most natural consolidation target, particularly for organizations that want both networking and security from one vendor.

### Revenue Model

**FY2025 Revenue: $6.8B (+14% YoY)**

| Segment | FY2025 Revenue | % of Total | Growth |
|---------|---------------|------------|--------|
| Product (FortiGate + appliances) | $2,218M | 33% | +16% |
| Services (FortiGuard + FortiCare) | $4,581M | 67% | +13% |
| **Total** | **$6,800M** | **100%** | **+14%** |

The services revenue is highly recurring, composed of:
- **FortiGuard:** Subscription security services (threat intelligence, IPS, sandboxing, web filtering, etc.). Attached to every FortiGate appliance. Duration: 1-5 year contracts.
- **FortiCare:** Technical support and maintenance contracts. Near-100% attach rate.
- **FortiSASE/SecOps/OT subscriptions:** Newer, faster-growing categories.

**Deferred Revenue: $7.1B** ($3.64B current + $3.48B non-current) -- this is 104% of trailing revenue, providing exceptional visibility. Deferred revenue grew 15% YoY.

**Billings: $7.55B (+16% YoY)** -- billings are the leading indicator. Q4 billings of $2.37B grew 18%, showing acceleration. Unified SASE billings grew 40% in Q4. This acceleration is a key signal.

### Unit Economics

- **Gross Margin: 81.3%** (FY2025) -- expanding from 75.4% in FY2022. The ASIC advantage means Fortinet's COGS on hardware is materially lower than competitors, and as services mix increases (higher margin), gross margin expands.
- **SBC/Revenue: 4.3%** (FY2024) -- CRITICALLY LOW for cybersecurity. Peers: CRWD ~20%, PANW ~18%, ZS ~25%+, S ~60%. This is one of the most underappreciated advantages. SBC-adjusted FCF is close to headline FCF, unlike peers where 15-25% SBC creates massive real dilution.
- **FCF Margin: 32.5%** (FY2025, $2.21B) -- consistent 30-33% range over 4 years. High quality FCF with minimal SBC distortion.
- **Operating Margin: 35.5%** non-GAAP (FY2025) -- expanding from 21.9% in FY2022. Significant operating leverage as services mix grows.
- **Share Count: 748M diluted** (Q4 2025 vs 764.6M FY2025 average) -- active buyback program. $1.38B remaining authorization through Feb 2027. Shares DECLINING despite tech sector norm of dilution.

### Capital Intensity: Asset-Light

- Capex/Revenue: ~5% (capex includes data center buildout for FortiSASE)
- Minimal working capital needs (deferred revenue > receivables)
- Capital allocation: aggressive buybacks ($10.25B cumulative authorization), no dividend, strategic R&D investment
- Net Cash: $2.6B ($2.5B cash + $1.4B investments - $1.0B debt)
