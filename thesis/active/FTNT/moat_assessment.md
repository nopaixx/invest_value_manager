# Moat Assessment: FTNT (Fortinet, Inc.)

## Fecha: 2026-02-26

## Clasificacion: WIDE

Fortinet possesses a wide economic moat built on the combination of three reinforcing advantages: proprietary ASIC-based cost advantage, high customer switching costs embedded in the Security Fabric platform, and a data-driven network effect from the largest installed base of firewalls globally. The ROIC persistence (consistently 40-84% over the last 5+ years vs WACC ~10%) provides overwhelming quantitative confirmation. Morningstar independently rates FTNT as Wide Moat, corroborating this assessment.

## Confidence Level

**HIGH (85%).** The combination of proprietary ASIC technology, deeply integrated platform switching costs, and the largest firewall installed base in the world creates a self-reinforcing moat system. The ROIC evidence (40-84% over 5 years, consistently 4-8x WACC) is among the strongest quantitative confirmation of moat I have encountered. The primary uncertainty is the pace of cloud-native adoption, which could erode the hardware advantage over 10-15 years -- but Fortinet's SASE transition and the durability of hybrid architectures provide significant mitigation.
