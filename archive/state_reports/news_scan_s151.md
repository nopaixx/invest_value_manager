### NVO — Novo Nordisk (MATERIAL x2)

**1. FDA Warning Letter — March 10, 2026**
- Classification: **MATERIAL**
- Source: FDA (Tier 1), CNBC/CBS/NBC (Tier 2)
- Details: FDA issued warning letter dated March 5, citing "serious violations" of adverse event reporting requirements. During a 2025 PADE inspection, FDA found: (a) 3 unreported deaths including a suicide on semaglutide, (b) internal procedures allowing rejection of adverse events if staff believed them unrelated, (c) improper invalidation of cases due to "missing" patient identifiers that were present in source documents. Products affected: semaglutide, liraglutide, nedosiran, estradiol. Company has 2 weeks to respond.
- KC Impact: Does NOT trigger any existing KC directly. FDA warning letters are procedural — they require corrective action but do NOT affect drug approval/sales. No product recall, no sales suspension.
- Thesis impact: MINOR negative — reputational, not operational. Novo acknowledged receipt and "welcomes further dialogue." No financial impact expected unless FDA escalates (consent decree, which is rare). Monitor response.
