## Why I Could Be Wrong

### 1. MFN Clauses Are a Pricing CEILING (HIGH severity)

**The thesis assumes** Doximity grows pharma ad revenue at 8%/yr because the physician network is a monopoly.

**The bear case:** 16 of top 20 clients have Most Favored Nation clauses = guaranteed lowest price. This means:
- Doximity CANNOT raise prices above the lowest contracted rate
- As more clients demand MFN, pricing power ERODES, not grows
- 126 large customers ($500K+) = 84% of revenue. If 5-6 major accounts renegotiate MFN terms downward, revenue per client DECLINES
- Growth thesis depends on new client acquisition AND upselling existing — MFN blocks the upselling
- Q4 guidance +4% (vs +10% prior quarter) may reflect MFN renegotiation cycle, not sandbagging

**Impact if MFN structural:** Growth 8%→3-4%. FV $30→$22. At $24.37, we'd be ABOVE fair value.

**Probability: 30%.** The highest-probability bear case for DOCS.

### 2. Client Concentration = Existential Risk (HIGH severity)

**126 clients at $4.3M average = 84% of revenue.** Losing just 6 clients = -5% revenue overnight.

**Why this matters more than it seems:**
- Pharma M&A is accelerating (LLY-Morphic, AZN-Alexion model). When pharma merges, digital ad budgets CONSOLIDATE. Two clients at $4M each become one at $5M (not $8M).
- IRA/GLP-1 competition is compressing pharma R&D budgets. Marketing is first cut.
- If 2-3 major pharma companies decide to build internal physician outreach (like LLY's direct HCP program), they leave Doximity entirely.

**Probability of losing 5+ major clients in 24 months: 15%.** Low but NON-ZERO and would be catastrophic for a company with 84% concentration.

### 3. Q4 Guidance +4% — Not Sandbagging, Genuinely Decelerating (MEDIUM severity)

**The thesis assumes** management sandbagged Q4 ("record Jan bookings" suggests they beat).

**The bear case:** What if +4% IS the real trajectory?
- FY2024: +25%. FY2025: +13%. Q3 FY2026: +10%. Q4 guide: +4%.
- Deceleration is CONSISTENT and ACCELERATING downward
- Each quarter BEATS by 2-3pp → actual ~6-7%, but the TREND is clearly decelerating
- At 6% sustainable growth: FV = $22-25 (P/E 15-18x for slow-growing SaaS with 90% GM)

**Probability: 25%.** If Q4 actual confirms <8%, this becomes the base case.

### 4. The "85% Monopoly" Has Limits (MEDIUM severity)

**The thesis claims** 85%+ of US physicians are on Doximity = monopoly.

**The bear case:** Being ON Doximity ≠ USING Doximity. Engagement metrics matter:
- 85% registered ≠ 85% DAU. Most physicians check it like email — sporadically
- Pharma advertisers pay for REACH, not registrations. If engagement declines, ad effectiveness drops, pharma spends less
- Telehealth revenue declining as COVID normalizes
- New entrants: UpToDate (Wolters Kluwer, already OUR position), Medscape, specialized networks

**Probability of moat narrowing: 15% over 3 years.** Network effects are real but engagement drives monetization.
