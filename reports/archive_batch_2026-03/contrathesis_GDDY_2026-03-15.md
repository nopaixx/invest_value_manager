# Contrathesis: GDDY (GoDaddy) | 2026-03-15

> Buying Mar 26 at EUR 720. FV $130. Price $81.22. E[CAGR] 25.2%. R4 APPROVED.

## Why I Could Be Wrong

### 1. SMB Recession Kills New Registrations (MEDIUM-HIGH severity)

**The thesis assumes** 80%+ domain renewal rate holds in any recession (proven 2008, 2020).

**The bear case:** Oil $100+ → recession → SMB failures SPIKE. Small businesses don't just stop renewing — they CLOSE. In 2008, US small business closures rose 40%. If GDDY's 21M customer base includes 3-4M marginal businesses (restaurants, freelancers, micro-retailers), a severe recession could:
- New registrations: -25 to -30% (vs thesis -15%)
- Renewal rate: drops to 70% (vs thesis 78%)
- A&C revenue: -15% (advertisers pull back)
- Combined revenue impact: -8 to -12%

**At -10% revenue:** FV compresses from $130 to $105-110. At $81 entry, still 30% MoS. E[CAGR] drops to ~16%. Survivable.

**Probability: 25%.** Dependent on oil crisis → recession materializing.

### 2. Starboard Value Exits (KC#5, CLEAR)

**The thesis assumes** activist pressure from Starboard keeps management disciplined on margins.

**The bear case:** Starboard bought at ~$95. If recession fears mount, Starboard could EXIT at $80-85 (small loss, cut risk). A major activist exit = negative signal + selling pressure. Starboard held ~6.5% = ~$700M position. Liquidation over 2-3 weeks would depress price 5-10%.

**Probability: 15%.** Activists exit when thesis changes, not on market moves alone. But $81 is below their entry.

### 3. AI Disruption of SMB Websites (5-year structural risk)

**The thesis assumes** $15/yr domains are the last thing cut because businesses need web identity.

**The bear case:** AI tools (Wix AI, Squarespace AI, Meta business pages) are reducing the need for dedicated websites. A restaurant can operate on Google Business Profile + Instagram without a domain. Over 5 years:
- Domain demand could flatten as AI-generated social presence replaces websites
- GDDY's hosting/website builder revenue (not just domains) could compress
- This doesn't kill domains but caps TAM growth

**Probability: 10% over 5 years.** Domains are infrastructure — social media changes but URLs persist. Low probability but worth monitoring.

### 4. Insider Selling Pattern (KC#8, MONITORING)

**The thesis notes** CAO sold 1,310 shares at $90.15 (Mar 10). Individual sell = noise. BUT:
- CEO Aman Bhutani: 48 sells, 0 buys in 2 years
- CFO Mark McCaffrey: regular seller
- No insider has bought open-market in 2+ years

**Bear interpretation:** Management is systematically diversifying away from GDDY. If the business was truly undervalued at $81, wouldn't SOMEONE buy? The 10b5-1 defense applies but the PATTERN (zero buys across entire C-suite for 2+ years) is concerning.

**Probability of being a problem: 20%.** Most likely tax/diversification. But absence of ANY buying is a yellow flag.

### 5. Leverage Risk in Downturn

**GDDY has $3.4B of net debt** (ND/EBITDA ~2.3x). In recession:
- EBITDA compresses → leverage ratio worsens
- If EBITDA drops 15% → ND/EBITDA jumps to ~2.7x
- Not dangerous but limits financial flexibility (no buybacks in downturn, constrained M&A)

**Probability of stress: 15%.** Well within covenants but constrains counter-cyclical capital deployment.
